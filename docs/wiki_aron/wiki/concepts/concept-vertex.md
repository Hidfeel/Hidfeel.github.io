# 顶点（vertex）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**顶点（vertex）** 是比昂（Wilfred Bion）用来指称**观察所由之出发的立足点／视角**的术语。其核心主张是：任何心理现象都只能从某个顶点被看见，因此**改变顶点即改变所见**——不是看见同一个东西的另一面，而是看见另一个东西。

这一概念在贝蒂·约瑟夫（Betty Joseph）手中被发展为一项临床工具。她指出分析师与病人各自持有一个顶点：分析师持有「他所希望达成的心理改变之理论」，病人则「意识上希望改变，却畏惧其平衡感受到任何扰动」——而后者才是病人的顶点。治疗任务因此不是替换病人的顶点，而是让两个顶点相遇，同时**不让病人失去与其经验性自体的接触**。

由此产生约瑟夫最具标志性的临床动作：把注意力从**诠释的内容**移到**诠释被如何接收、使用或中和**。同一份临床材料，从「内容顶点」看与从「过程顶点」看，会得出完全不同的结论。

## 一、比昂的定义：顶点是「每个人所认为最佳的组织」

Govrin 引比昂 1970 年的原句：

> As Bion (1970) describes: "the psycho-analyst and the analysand each have a vertex (or vertices) which, if it were known, would indicate the organization each regarded as best" (p. 127).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Concept of Vertex」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 127）

这句话里最容易被忽略的是复数的可能性——**vertices**。一个人可以有多个顶点，而顶点之间的切换，正是分析过程中发生的事。

同时要注意，顶点的定义项不是「观点」而是**组织（organization）**：顶点标示的是一个人把自己的心理生活组织成什么样，以及他认为**哪一种组织是最好的**。

## 二、约瑟夫的双顶点：两个顶点，两种「最佳」

约瑟夫把比昂的定义落实为一对临床可操作的规定。

**分析师一侧的顶点：**

> In Joseph's formulation (1992), the analyst holds "a theory or theories of desirable psychic change, of what he hopes he may achieve in his work, which is his vertex,"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Concept of Vertex」（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 237）

**病人一侧的顶点：**

> while the patient "consciously wishes to change, but dreads any disturbance to his sense of equilibrium, the way in which he deals with anxieties and defences, the organization which he regards as best—this is his vertex" (p. 237).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 237）

这一对规定解释了临床上的一个结构性僵局：病人的顶点**包含了**「意识上希望改变」与「畏惧平衡受扰」两件事。因此分析师若只听见前者，就会把后者的运作误读为阻抗或攻击——而那其实是病人正在保护他「所认为最佳的组织」。

**治疗任务的规定：**

> The therapeutic task becomes finding ways for these two vertices to come together without the patient losing contact with their own experiencing self.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章

约瑟夫自己的措辞更严格——病人必须**同时**在两边：

> As Joseph (1992) explains: "The patient must be able to remain within his own personality organization, looking at things from his own perspective, and yet able to have some part of the self allied with the analyst and thus to look at and begin to recognize his way of operating" (p. 237).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 237）

## 三、从内容顶点到过程顶点：约瑟夫的转向

这是本概念在临床上真正锋利的地方。Govrin 先给出古典克莱因派的**内容顶点**：

> Classical Kleinian technique, as exemplified by Hanna Segal and inherited from Klein herself, operates on the assumption that "the deeper the layers of the unconscious reached, the richer and the more stable the therapeutic result" (Segal, cited in Blass, 2011, p. 1151).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Shift from Content to Process」（引 Segal，转引自 Blass, 2011, p. 1151）

以及其技术形态——过去到现在的移情诠释：

> Klein's technical legacy was built upon "past-to-present transference interpretations in order to effect psychic change" (p. 1117).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Aguayo, 2011, p. 1117）

约瑟夫的偏离正在于此：

> Where Klein had emphasized the content of interpretation—what was said—Joseph became fascinated by something more elusive: how interpretations were received, used, or discarded by patients. This shift marked her revolutionary departure from the classical tradition.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章

她所注意到的现象，是分析师最容易错过的一类：

> Joseph noticed something that Klein had perhaps been too busy interpreting to see: patients often seemed to agree with interpretations while simultaneously rendering them useless.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章

而她的技术决断，是**放弃**那个内容顶点的核心操作：

> Joseph gradually "set aside directly linking the patient's past with the present, compelled now by making direct contact with her patients" (Aguayo, 2011, p. 1117).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Aguayo, 2011, p. 1117）

Govrin 对约瑟夫整个倾听姿态的描述，几乎就是「移动顶点」的现象学：

> Where Klein and Segal might hear the echoes of ancient phantasies reverberating through the consulting room, Joseph attends to something far more immediate and perhaps more disturbing: the precise way her patient is trying to seduce her into becoming a particular kind of analyst. This shift in listening represents not merely a technical refinement but a fundamental reimagining of what constitutes the most urgent psychological truth in any given moment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Betty Joseph and the Music of Interpretation」

## 四、临床材料：Mr. A. 与《致命的自负》——同一份材料的两种读法

### （一）材料

> Mr A was coming down a spiral staircase, carrying a pile of books. He had to hurry to go to a meeting and so put a book down on each step. Afterwards he went back to collect them, but found that one was missing, and felt sure that it had been taken. It was by H, expensive and important to him.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 239）

Mr. A. 是一位三十多岁的僵化强迫症男性，因被一位女同事拒绝而陷入严重焦虑与抑郁。

### （二）内容顶点会怎么看

从内容顶点出发，这份材料会被读作象征：螺旋楼梯、逐阶放置的书、被拿走的昂贵而重要的书、作者 H。读法指向丧失、被剥夺、以及与女性／分析师有关的阉割性主题。**这种读法在本组 raw 文献中没有被写出**——Govrin 只呈现了约瑟夫的读法，并明确说约瑟夫不这么做：

> Joseph doesn't rush to interpret the symbolic meaning of books or staircases. Instead, she tracks how Mr. A. uses the dream material to maintain his psychological equilibrium.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Clinical Illustration: Mr. A. and the Fatal Conceit」

### （三）过程顶点怎么看

约瑟夫的诠释（内容顶点看来也成立）是：

> When Joseph interprets that she is "stealing away something very precious from him— his omnipotence, his arrogance—and leaving him full of anxieties," Mr. A. suddenly remembers the title of the missing book: *The Fatal Conceit*

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 240）

但约瑟夫真正追踪的是**接下来发生了什么**——病人如何处置这个诠释：

> Joseph notices something crucial about Mr. A.'s response pattern. Initially, he seems to understand her interpretations, but then his cooperation "became sterile, with him repeating more or less what I said, 'knowing' exactly what was meant, until all movement was paralysed, and a kind of flattened, empty atmosphere was created" (Joseph, 1992, p. 242).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 242）

以及最关键的观察——**当会谈真的要活起来时，病人逃走了**：

> Most tellingly, when the session threatens to become genuinely alive with meaning, Mr. A. shifts to speaking about organizing a memorial for a dead writer—fleeing into grandiosity rather than experiencing the anxiety of real contact. As Joseph observes: "But then there was flight to mania, a somewhat exaggerated and inappropriate idea of a memorial at his workplace. The analyst's perspective is lost and the patient goes 'high'" (Joseph, 1992, p. 242).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 242）

**结论对照表：**

| | 内容顶点 | 过程顶点 |
|---|---|---|
| 关心的问题 | 这个梦**意味着**什么？ | 病人**用**这个梦做了什么？ |
| 材料单位 | 象征（书、楼梯、H） | 会谈气氛的**变化序列** |
| 「同意诠释」被读作 | 洞见的达成 | 一种**中和（neutralization）**的操作 |
| 关键时刻 | 说出正确诠释的那一刻 | 诠释被接收、被重复、被抽空的那一刻 |
| 治疗终点 | 更深层的无意识被触达 | 病人能同时留在自己之内、又从分析师视角看自己 |

Govrin 在第二章结尾给出了「中和」这一术语：

> Joseph reveals how patients neutralize even accurate interpretations

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Conclusion: Truth, Kindness, and the Limits of Interpretation」

并在进入约瑟夫之前就点出了这一研究问题：

> It was Betty Joseph, working within the Kleinian tradition, who would address these questions by shifting attention from the content of interpretation to the process of how interpretations are received and used—or neutralized—by patients.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Later Technical Developments in the Kleinian School」

## 五、移动顶点才能听见「中和」：约瑟夫的技术规定

### （一）听什么：不只是词语，还有气氛与压力

> She emphasizes the importance of monitoring "not only what is being said—the actual words, stories being told—but also to the atmosphere of the session; the pressures on and expectations and anxieties that are being aroused in the analyst" (Joseph, 1992, p. 238).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Music of Process」（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 238）

### （二）Blass 对约瑟夫独特贡献的概括

> As Blass (2011) identifies, Joseph's unique contribution is her attention to "the way in which the patient is inviting the analyst to enact and thus maintain his (the patient's) inner dynamics in their current state of equilibrium" (p. 1150).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Blass, 2011, p. 1150）

这一句把「顶点」与「活现（enactment）」接在了一起：**病人是在邀请分析师进入某个顶点**，而那个顶点的作用正是维持病人当前的平衡。分析师若察觉不到这一邀请，就已经在顶点之内了——这正是「必须移动顶点才能听见」的确切含义。

> Rather than interpreting what the patient thinks or feels about their past, she interprets how they are actively organizing the analytic relationship in the present moment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Music of Process」

### （三）真洞见 vs 智性顺从：顶点错位的判据

这是约瑟夫最著名的一处区分。假的情形：

> Joseph (1992) makes a crucial distinction between genuine insight and intellectual compliance: "If, however, the patient loses contact with his experiencing self, and projectively identifies with, and takes over, the analyst's understanding … then that very understanding becomes an intellectual thing" (p. 243).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Challenge of True Psychic Change」（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 243）

真的情形：

> This is fundamentally different from true understanding, which occurs when "the patient can both remain in contact with what is going on inside himself, while seeing that experience from the analyst's perspective" (p. 243).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 243）

注意这一判据与第二节的「治疗任务」是同一条：**两个顶点同时成立**。病人一旦放弃自己的顶点、完全住进分析师的顶点，理解就退化为智性之物——这正是 Mr. A. 身上发生的（「重复我所说的，'知道'确切的意思，直到一切运动都被瘫痪」）。

所需的能力，约瑟夫给了它一个名字：

> She demonstrates that true psychic change requires maintaining what she calls "a kind of emotional third ear tuned inwards" (Joseph, 1992, p. 238), attending not just to what patients say but to what they do with what is said to them—to the music of the session rather than its lyrics.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 238）

## 八、与其他概念的关系

- **与诠释（interpretation）**：顶点决定了「什么算是一个诠释」——同一句话，从内容顶点看是洞见，从过程顶点看可能是防御操作。见 [[concepts/concept-interpretation]]。
- **与活现（enactment）**：Blass 的概括把顶点与活现直接扣连——病人邀请分析师进入某个顶点，以维持其内在动力的平衡。见 [[concepts/concept-enactment]]。
- **与移情（transference）**：Govrin 指出，1960–70 年代克莱因派的第三个趋势正是「投射性认同被更直接地用于分析移情，日益强调分析师—病人关系中的活现。这一发展在约瑟夫的工作中尤为重要」。
- **与负性能力（negative capability）**：负性能力要求分析师松动自己的顶点——不预先知道该从哪里看。见 [[concepts/concept-negative-capability]]。**本条为【推断】，raw 文献中二者从未并置。**
- **与 O**：顶点管「怎么看」，O 是「所看之物中不可穷尽者」。遴选事实同时是两者的前身／中介。**本条为【推断】。**
- **与涵容（containment）**：顶点管观察的立足点，涵容管观察之前的接收。二者在比昂处是不同层次的工具。见 [[concepts/concept-containment-reverie-alpha]]。**本条为【推断】。**
