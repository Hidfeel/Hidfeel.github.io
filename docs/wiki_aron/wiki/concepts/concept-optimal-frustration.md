# 最佳挫折与蜕变内化（optimal frustration, transmuting internalization）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**最佳挫折（optimal frustration）**与**蜕变内化（transmuting internalization）**是科胡特为「分析如何治愈」给出的机制性答案。命题可以压缩为一句话：**精神分析通过铺设心理结构而治愈**，而结构铺设的燃料是恰到好处的挫折——分析师通过共情调谐暂时满足病人的发展性需求，却不可避免地无法完美满足；正是在这可承受的失望中，病人把原本由分析师执行的**自体客体功能**逐步转为自己的内在结构。

「最佳」二字是整套机制的枢纽：**过多则成为创伤（重复早年的环境失败，而非促成内化），过少则没有内化的动力（病人停留在自体客体的供给中，无需要自己供给自己）**。这是一套**剂量模型**，也是它后来饱受质疑的根源。

本页必须处理两个层次的批评。**内部批评**（Govrin 从科胡特自己的临床材料中读出）：蜕变内化「只是命名了而非解释了」这一炼金术；科胡特的材料实际显示，结构建立更像是**回收**一直存在、只是无法被动用的能力，而非内化外部功能。**外部批评**：剂量模型预设分析师可以掌握「正确的量」，从而把治疗行动重新集中到分析师的操作上，而忽略了意义的**共构性**——后者在 Govrin 所引 Stern 场论与 Wolf／Lichtenberg 的双向自体客体理论中得到明确支持。

## 一、机制陈述：结构如何被铺设

Govrin 的转述从科胡特的著名命题开始：

> Kohut's (1984) formulation that "psychoanalysis cures by the laying down of psychological structure" (p. 98) through optimal frustration and transmuting internalization offers a theory of therapeutic action that fundamentally reconceives the nature of psychic change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 98）

前提是被带入分析的**未满足的发展性需求**：

> According to this view, the patient enters analysis with unmet developmental needs, seeking archaic forms of support from the analyst—mirroring that confirms their grandiosity, idealization that provides strength and calm, twinship that offers essential sameness. The analyst, through empathic attunement, temporarily fulfills these needs, yet inevitably cannot do so perfectly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

机制本身：

> The mechanism Kohut proposes is transmuting internalization: through repeated cycles of empathic connection followed by tolerable disappointment, the patient gradually converts external selfobject functions into internal psychological structure.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

其逐步累积的过程被描述得极为具体——**每一次最佳挫折催化一次微小的自我供给（self-provision）**：

> When the analyst's understanding falls short, the patient must momentarily provide their own self-soothing. When the idealized analyst proves human and fallible, the patient begins to access their own sources of strength. Each optimal frustration catalyzes a small act of self-provision that gradually accumulates into autonomous capacity.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】完整的因果链是：**共情连接 → 可承受的失望 → 病人暂时自己供给自己 → 微小动作反复累积 → 自主能力**。注意其中的计量性词汇（tolable、optimal、small、gradually accumulates）——这套机制本质上是**定量**的。

## 二、为什么「最佳」是关键

科胡特文本中「最佳（optimal）」的约束，在本批资料中是通过**其失败案例的反证**得到展示的。Govrin 记录的治疗目标两端各有明示：

**下游目标（非独立，而是学会使用自体客体）**——防止「过少挫折」变成永久依赖：

> The cure, then, is not independence from selfobjects but what he describes as "the patient's newfound ability to seek, recognize, and draw on selfobjects outside therapy" (Kohut, 1984, p. 120).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 120）

而治疗的**学习曲线**正建立在分析师失败与修复的节律上：

> The patient who can only accept perfect understanding learns, through the analyst's failures and repairs, to make use of imperfect but good-enough responsiveness. The patient who cannot bear to need anyone discovers, through the reliability of the analytic relationship, that dependence need not lead to traumatic disappointment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

**上游约束（过多挫折即创伤）**则在下面的临床片段中被鲜明地反证。

## 三、临床：科胡特的失败与自我修正

这是本组文献中最完整的一段科胡特临床材料（Kohut, 1984, p. 182），也是理解「最佳」二字的钥匙。

**情境**：科胡特一次缺席之后，病人急剧恶化：

> Following an absence, Kohut's patient experienced a severe deterioration—painful head sensations, quasi-paranoid accusations about the harshness of people's voices, and even taking his television to a repair shop to check if it had been tampered with.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（参 Kohut, 1984, p. 182）

**科胡特的第一次处理（认知正确，治疗失败）**：

> Kohut initially offered what seemed like empathic interpretations: he suggested that the patient's worsening was paradoxically part of his improvement, that increased emotional openness had exposed him to new anxieties. While cognitively correct, these interpretations only temporarily relieved the patient's distress before he returned to attacking Kohut as lacking all understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

**关键的发现——正确的诠释本身就是追加的创伤**：

> What Kohut gradually came to grasp was that his interpretations, however accurate, constituted an additional trauma. The patient experienced them as coming "only from the outside"—words without real understanding, thereby repeating the essential trauma of his early life.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

**修正**：

> The crucial therapeutic shift occurred when Kohut (1984) stopped defending the correctness of his interpretations and instead allowed himself to see things "exclusively in his way and not at all in my way" (p. 182).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 182）

> This represents a radical revision of analytic technique. The analyst must accept the patient's criticism not as transference distortion to be interpreted but as psychological truth to be embraced.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】这一片段对「最佳挫折」的剂量模型有直接后果：**诠释的准确性不构成治疗价值，反而可能构成创伤**。因此「挫折是否最佳」不可能由分析师按内容正确性来计量——它取决于病人那一侧的经验结构（是否感到被从内部理解）。这为第六节的当代质疑埋下了伏笔。

## 四、Govrin 的内部批评（一）：最佳挫折不是「分析师的小小失误」

Govrin 从上述材料中读出了一个对剂量模型的**决定性松动**：

> This clinical sequence reveals how optimal frustration operates at multiple levels simultaneously. The initial frustration—Kohut's absence—triggered archaic needs and primitive anxieties. But the more significant frustration emerged in the gap between Kohut's cognitive understanding and emotional attunement.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

> This suggests that optimal frustration involves something more complex than the analyst's minor, inevitable failures. It encompasses the entire dimension of otherness that analysis must navigate—the fact that even the most devoted analyst remains fundamentally separate, seeing through their own eyes, shaped by their own history.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

并由之改写内化的方向：

> The patient builds structure not by internalizing what the analyst provides but by developing ways to bridge this irreducible gap.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

> When Kohut (1984) notes that understanding "substitutes for the de facto fulfillment of the patient's need" (p. 102), he points toward this paradox: the need for perfect mirroring gives way to the capacity to sustain oneself despite imperfect recognition.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 102）

【综合】这里的改写是**结构性的**：如果最佳挫折就是「分析师的他者性本身」，那么它既不是分析师可以施加的，也不是可以调节的——**它已经是分析情境的既定事实**。剂量模型因此失去操作意义：不存在一个可调的旋钮，只存在一个不可消除的间距（irreducible gap）。

## 五、Govrin 的内部批评（二）：蜕变内化只是命名，不是解释

Govrin 的第五章第五节标题即为「The Paradox of Structure Building」，其开篇是本页最重要的方法论批评：

> The process by which optimal frustrations become psychic structure remains, in Kohut's account, fundamentally mysterious. He speaks of "transmuting internalization," but this term names rather than explains the alchemy by which relational experiences become autonomous capacities. How does the repeated experience of being understood become the ability to understand oneself? How does the analyst's recognition of mistakes become the patient's capacity for self-reflection?

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Paradox of Structure Building」

替代性解释——**回收，而非内化**：

> Kohut's clinical material suggests that structure building might be less about internalization than about discovering capacities that were always present but couldn't be accessed without the safety of the analytic relationship.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

> The lawyer-patient's ability to laugh, to express emotion, to relax—these weren't newly created capacities but recovered possibilities that had been sacrificed in the service of psychological survival.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

> The analyst doesn't give the patient something new but creates conditions in which the patient can reclaim what was always theirs. The selfobject functions that the analyst provides don't become internalized; rather, they make it possible for the patient to recognize and utilize selfobject experiences that were always available but couldn't be trusted.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】这是一个**范式级**的翻转，且与温尼科特传统完全一致（参见 [[concepts/concept-holding-going-on-being]]、[[concepts/concept-breakdown-deadness]]）：治疗不是「给予所缺」，而是「创条件使病人收回己有」。对照表：

| 维度 | 科胡特的蜕变内化模型 | Govrin 读出的回收模型 |
|---|---|---|
| 结构来源 | 外部自体客体功能被转化为内部 | 一直存在的能力被重新取用 |
| 分析师的贡献 | 提供功能，然后撤回 | 提供**安全条件** |
| 机制 | 内化（摄入—转化） | 可及性（信任—取用） |
| 隐喻 | 炼金术、营养代谢 | 回收、赎回 |
| 治疗的方向 | 从外到内 | 从不可用到可用 |

那么「蜕变内化实际发生在哪里」？Govrin 给出的答案落在**误解与修复的坩埚**中：

> The transmuting internalization occurred through the patient discovering, within the crucible of misunderstanding and gradual repair, his own capacity to maintain self-cohesion even when profoundly misrecognized.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

## 六、当代质疑：剂量模型是否忽略了意义的共构性

这是本页必须回答的第二个问题。质疑有三条独立来源。

**（一）Wolf 与 Lichtenberg：自体客体经验是双向的。** 若治疗双方**互为**自体客体来源，则「分析师按剂量供给—病人内化」这一单向图式不成立：

> "During an ongoing treatment both patient and therapist communicate verbally and nonverbally, thereby providing a basis for selfobject experiences for the other" (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533）

> This mutual influence means that interpretation can never be a one-way delivery of truth from analyst to patient. It's always a collaborative creation, shaped by both participants' contributions.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

**（二）Stern 的场论：改变来自两人之间的、不属于任何一方的新颖性。** Govrin 在第七章第五节转述的这一立场，是「共构性」最有力的表述：

> This view places interpretation firmly in the realm of ontology rather than epistemology. As Bromberg notes, interpersonal novelty allows the self to grow because it is unanticipated by both persons, organized by what takes place between two minds, and belongs to neither person alone (Stern, 2023, p. 1145).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节（引 Stern, 2023, p. 1145；内引 Bromberg）

> Stern emphasizes that this movement occurred precisely because neither could say why it happened—the fact that they did something unexpected together is the point (Stern, 2023, p. 1145).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节（引 Stern, 2023, p. 1145）

【综合】「**两人共同做了一件意料之外的事，这就是要点**」——这一表述无法被剂量模型容纳：剂量模型预设分析师知道自己在给什么、给多少；而这里的关键恰恰是**双方都不预期**。

**（三）温尼科特／埃谢尔一侧：治疗行动的模型是「新机会」，不是「正确剂量」。** 埃谢尔把温尼科特式的治疗性退行描述为带来**矫正过去经验与情绪发展的新机会**：

> Thus, in Winnicottian terms of regression, the analyst's interconnected "presencing" can be seen as enabling the transformation of the patient's withdrawal and massive self-defensive organization—in this context, the perversion—into regression to dependence in treatment, which carries with it a new opportunity for correcting the patient's past experiences and for emotional development (Winnicott 1954b, 1964, 1988).

— Eshel, *Withness*，临床例证

而温尼科特一侧对分析师专业能力的定义是**环境性调谐**而非剂量控制：

> Rather than interpretive virtuosity, it requires what we might call "environmental attunement"—the capacity to provide the kinds of relational conditions within which the patient's own developmental processes can resume.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

【综合】两条传统的分歧可以精确地表述为：**科胡特的问题**是「给多少挫折才是最佳的」；**温尼科特—埃谢尔的问题**是「什么样的环境能让发展过程重新启动」。前者把分析师置于**施予者**位置，后者把分析师置于**条件提供者**位置。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：治疗性改变是**内化外部功能**（科胡特的剂量／内化模型），还是**回收本有能力**（Govrin 读出的回收模型）／**共构新意义**（Wolf-Lichtenberg、Stern）？
> **A 方立场**：科胡特——「psychoanalysis cures by the laying down of psychological structure」(1984, p. 98)，机制为「through repeated cycles of empathic connection followed by tolerable disappointment, the patient gradually converts external selfobject functions into internal psychological structure」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章）
> **B 方立场（内生于科胡特自己的材料）**：Govrin——「this term names rather than explains the alchemy」；「The selfobject functions that the analyst provides don't become internalized; rather, they make it possible for the patient to recognize and utilize selfobject experiences that were always available but couldn't be trusted」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章）
> **C 方立场**：Wolf 与 Lichtenberg / Stern——意义与自体客体经验是**双向共构**的，「belongs to neither person alone」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五、七章）
> **备注**：A 与 B 的矛盾**发生在科胡特文本内部**——B 方完全以科胡特自己的临床材料为据，因此不是外部否定，而是**内在不一致**：科胡特的理论语言（内化）与他的临床发现（回收）不匹配。C 方则是**范式差异**，与 A 方不可通约：C 方取消了「分析师单方面施予」这一图式本身。临床后果差别巨大——按 A 方，分析师需要估算剂量；按 B 方，分析师需要提供安全；按 C 方，分析师需要**对不预期之物保持开放**。Govrin 明确站在 B/C 一侧，并判科胡特「couldn't quite walk through」他自己打开的门。
