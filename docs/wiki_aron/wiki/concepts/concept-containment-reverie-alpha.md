# 涵容、遐想与 α功能（containment, reverie, alpha-function, beta-elements）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]、[[raw/ogden-ontological-psychoanalysis]]、[[raw/eshel-withness]]

## 摘要

比昂（Wilfred Bion）的理论机制始于**一次临床失败**。在〈攻击联结〉（"Attacks on Linking", 1959）中，他承认自己曾以「例行诠释（routine interpretations）」回应病人投射进来的死亡恐惧——这些诠释不带情绪在场、不提供真正的涵容（containment），因而被病人体验为**断然拒绝（outright rejection）**。这一失败成为比昂重构诠释的起点：分析师的工作不是交付理论洞见，而是在**紧急时刻**说出一句已经被涵容过的诠释；诠释与涵容不可分割。

机制的具体形态是：病人把无法承受的原始感官印象——β元素（beta-elements）——投射给分析师；分析师以**遐想（reverie）**这一清醒梦式的接收状态接住它，经由**α功能（alpha-function）**将其转化为可被思考、可被梦、可被储存的**α元素（alpha-elements）**，再以可被重新内摄的形式返还。整个过程一旦分析师因自身焦虑而关上接收通道，就退化为病人早年的创伤复演：一个不可用、不接收的客体。

本页还需处理一处细微而关键的差别：分析师的心智究竟被理解为**转化装置**（加工、消化、代谢异物的器官），还是被理解为**被唤起的接收者**（被 O 所唤起、与之合一的在场）。两种读法在原始文献中都有文本依据，且彼此张力未解。

## 一、起点：一次临床失败与「例行诠释」

比昂在〈攻击联结〉的「对正常程度投射性认同的否认」一节中，罕见地公开了自己的失误。Govrin 的复述如下：

> Bion recounts how a patient attempted to project overwhelming fears of death into him, in the hope that the analyst's mind could contain, modify, and eventually return them in a tolerable form.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Interpretation in Wilfred Bion's Writings」

而比昂实际给出的，是另一回事：

> Instead, Bion realized in hindsight, he had responded with what he calls "routine interpretations"— interventions that carried no emotional presence, offered no true containment, and were thus experienced by the patient as an outright rejection.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

比昂本人的原话点明了这一失误的谱系——它重复而非转化了病人的婴儿期创伤：

> This patient had had to deal with a mother who could not tolerate such feelings and evacuated them instead

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1959, p. 312，原文着重）

失败的临床后果是**升级而非缓解**：

> instead of finding relief through containment, the patient felt that his emotional pain had intensified after being "evacuated" by the analyst. The urgent communication of unbearable psychic states met with an obstruction, and this led the patient to escalate the forcefulness of his projective identifications

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

比昂由此得出的区分，是整套机制的伦理内核：

> The true error, Bion insists, lay in his misinterpretation: he had treated the patient's projection as aggression, whereas in fact it signaled an urgent appeal for survival through containment

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1959, pp. 312–313）

## 二、从失败到理论：诠释与涵容不可分割

这一认识直接改写了「诠释是什么」：

> For him, the analyst's specific action is not the delivery of theoretical insight, but *a spoken interpretation at the point of urgency*, following the patient's projection of raw, unbearable anxiety into the analyst.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

而说出这句话的前提，是一种放弃记忆与欲望的接收姿态：

> The analyst must wait in a state that abandons expectation, memory, or desire, in order to receive these projected parts of the patient and contain them, rather than defensively rejecting them

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1992/1967）

唯有在此姿态中，诠释才成为**联结（link）**而非排泄：

> Only in this receptive stance can the analyst's interpretation function as a true *link*—restoring the patient's capacity to think and to experience continuity of being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Govrin 用一句话收束了这一转化：

> Bion's own clinical failure crystallized his conviction that interpretation is inseparable from containment: to speak effectively at the right moment, the analyst must first suffer, bear, and metabolize the patient's unbearable states, transforming what would otherwise remain destructive evacuations into the beginnings of meaning.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> **【原文】** 需要如实标注：在 Ogden 的 *Ontological Psychoanalysis* 全篇中，`containment` 与 `projective identification` 两个词**均未出现**（经 Grep 核实，命中数为 0）。Ogden 是用 `reverie`、`alpha-function`、`dreaming` 来表述同一机制的。因此「涵容」一词在 Ogden 篇上的缺席，是他本人的术语选择，不是机制的缺席。

## 三、β元素与 α功能：从「梦工作」到「消化」隐喻

比昂把弗洛伊德的梦理论倒转过来：梦不只是通往无意识的「王道」，而是现实借以变得**可被精神消化**的机制本身。

> This reversal of the traditional psychoanalytic formula challenges the directional assumption embedded in Freud's famous dictum "where id was, there ego shall be." Instead, Bion suggests a circular process whereby conscious material must be subjected to dream-work to render it suitable for psychic storage and transformation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

从「梦工作-α」到「α功能」，是一次理论的扩展：

> Bion's shift from dream-work-α to alpha-function represented a broader theory: how the mind transforms raw experience (beta elements) into elements suitable for thought and dreaming (alpha elements).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Ogden 给出了本组文献中对 α功能最清晰的定义性表述——他明确把它读作一个**隐喻**：

> I view Bion's(1962a) theory of alpha-function as a metaphor for the transformation of beta-elements (raw sense impressions that are bodily responses to experience, but which do not yet constitute meaning, much less being oneself) into alpha-elements

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章「Bion's Contributions to」

α元素的下游，是梦思与做梦：

> Alpha elements are linked with one another in the process of producing "dream-thoughts," which in turn are used in the process of dreaming. Dreaming is the psychic event in which the individual becomes a subject experiencing his own being.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章

α功能停摆的代价是双重的——不只是失去意义，而是失去「自己活着且真实」的感觉：

> When, in severe forms of psychopathology ..., alpha-function ceases to process sense impressions, not only does the individual lose the capacity to create meaning, he also loses the capacity to experience himself as alive and real.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章

Govrin 则把这一机制铺展为比昂的**消化隐喻**：

> Just as the alimentary system breaks down food into forms that can nourish the organism, dream-work processes raw experience into elements that can be stored, combined, and utilized for psychological development.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

其病理对应物，是那些「接触着现实却几乎无法使用现实」的病人：

> The failure of this digestive function creates what Bion observed in psychotic patients—individuals who seem to have contact with reality but can make remarkably little use of it. They possess the raw material of experience but lack the apparatus to transform it into psychically nutritious elements.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

于是分析师的位置被重新定义：

> The analyst working with such patients must function as what might be called an auxiliary digestive system, processing the patient's raw communications through their own dream-work apparatus and offering back transformed elements that the patient can potentially incorporate.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 四、遐想（reverie）：母亲的答案，分析师的清醒梦

比昂在《从经验中学习》第十二章末尾自问自答：

> Bion asks: "When a mother loves the infant, what does she do it with?" He replies: "Leaving aside the physical channels of communication my impression is that her love is expressed by reverie"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1962）

婴儿的 α能力，系于母亲的遐想：

> The development of the infant's alpha capacity depends fundamentally on the mother's capacity for reverie, which enables her to receive any psychic object from the infant and submit it to the process of transformation through her own alpha-function.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Parsons 把 analyst 一侧的对应状态描述为**内在澄明**：

> Parsons (2021) describes reverie as "a way for the analyst to bring into resonance all the levels of awareness of his being" (p. 72) to be available for the patient's experience resulting in an "inner clarity" (p. 72) like the openness of water or sky.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Parsons, 2021, p. 72）

Ogden 的定义则把遐想安放在**存在状态**（state of being）的层面——这是他把比昂读作本体论思想家的核心证据：

> Reverie (waking-dreaming) is a state of being that entails making oneself unconsciously receptive to experiencing what is so disturbing to the patient (or infant) that he is unable to "dream" (to do unconscious psychological work with) the experience. The analyst's (or mother's) reveries, waking dreaming—which often take the form of his most mundane, quotidian thoughts ...—constitutes a way in which the analyst (or mother) unconsciously experiences something like the patient's (or infant's) unthinkable, undreamable experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章

而返还的形式也有严格规定——**从**遐想中说，而不是**关于**遐想说：

> In the analytic setting, the analyst makes available to the patient the transformed (dreamt) version of the patient's "undreamt" or partially dreamt experience by speaking (or relating in other forms) from, not about, reverie experience

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章（参 Ogden, 1994）

这也解释了为什么同一句诠释可能是完全不同的干预。Parsons 的例子指出，差别不在显在内容，而在**分析师心智中的出发点**：

> The difference, Parsons (2021) demonstrates, lies not in manifest content but in what he calls the point of departure in the psyche of the analyst (p. 75). When the analyst employs alpha-function through reverie, the interpretation must evolve from A to what he terms B2—the same words as the mechanical formulation but emerging from a fundamentally different transformational process.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 五、第一处细微差别：不能替病人做梦

比昂机制内部有一道容易被抹掉的界线：分析师做梦，不等于**替**病人做梦。

> The analyst must not dream for the patient— such an approach would constitute a form of psychic intrusion that replicates the very projective identification processes that keep the patient imprisoned in their pathology.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> This distinction—between dreaming for the patient and dreaming the session—represents one of Bion's most subtle clinical insights. The analyst's reverie must remain genuinely their own while simultaneously providing the containing function that allows the patient's fragmented material to cohere. It is a delicate balance between engagement and separateness, between receptivity and autonomy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Govrin 还给出了遐想的负面定义——它不是温柔的幻想：

> Reverie is not gentle fantasy but dwelling within the unbearable—sharing unformed existence while resisting both premature meaning-making and collapse.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

在更困难的配置下，α功能可以完全在意识阈值之下运作。Cassorla 对边缘性病人的观察给出了这一层：

> His observations of "chronic enactments"—those seemingly stagnant periods when analyst and patient appear trapped in unproductive collusions—demonstrate how implicit alpha-function operates below the threshold of conscious awareness. During these periods, "unconscious exchanges occur between the dyad, in which the analyst provides implicit alpha-function to the patient, little by little recovering the traumatized parts" (p. 161).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Cassorla, 2008, p. 161）

## 六、第二处细微差别：转化装置，还是被唤起的接收者？

这是比昂接受史上一道未被消解的张力。两种读法在原始文献中各有直接文本支撑。

**读法 A｜分析师的心智是转化装置。** 重心在「加工」：分析师接收异物，用自身的 α功能将其代谢为可思考之物，再返还。文本依据是 α功能隐喻与消化隐喻（见上文第三节），以及「辅助消化系统」的表述。Ogden 对 α功能的 metaphor 读法与此一致。

**读法 B｜分析师的心智是被唤起的接收者。** 重心在「被唤起」：分析师并不回忆、并不加工，而是让自己的心智被那个未知之物所唤起，并在合一状态中成为它。Ogden 对遐想的定义（"making oneself unconsciously receptive"）已经朝向这一极。Eshel 所引的 Grotstein 则把这一点推到最彻底处：

> the necessity for the analyst to experience his own inner version of what the analysand is suffering from; to "*become*" the analysand's anguish and agony. Bion (1967, 1970) termed this phenomenon "transformation in O within the analyst."

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」（引 Grotstein, 2010, p. 25，原文着重）

而 Eshel 所引比昂本人的原句，是这一读法最硬的文本支点：

> With this [O] the analyst cannot be identified; he must *be* it

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27，原文着重）

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：分析师在此机制中的心智，是**转化装置**（加工并返还异物的器官），还是**被唤起的接收者**（被 O 唤起并与之合一的在场）？
> **A 方立场**：Ogden 与 Govrin 读作转化装置——「I view Bion's(1962a) theory of alpha-function as a metaphor for the transformation of beta-elements ... into alpha-elements」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章）；分析师「must function as what might be called an auxiliary digestive system」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章）。
> **B 方立场**：Ogden 的遐想定义与 Eshel 所引比昂原句读作被唤起的接收者——「Reverie (waking-dreaming) is a state of being that entails making oneself unconsciously receptive」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章）；「With this [O] the analyst cannot be identified; he must *be* it」（Eshel, *Withness*，引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）。
> **备注**：**部分可调和**。A 方描述的是机制的功能侧（β→α 的转化如何发生），B 方描述的是分析师的状态侧（以何种存在方式才能发生）。但张力并未完全消解：若严格采取 B 方，「转化」一词本身即失去意义——因为「成为 O」与「加工 O」是两种不可通约的动作。比昂晚期的 O 概念明显加重了 B 方，见 [[concepts/concept-transformation-o-to-k]]。本条为【综合】。

## 七、当通道被关上：焦虑作为失败的诊断指标

本组文献给出了三种「关上通道」的判据——它们分别位于机制的不同层面。

**（一）焦虑是拒绝做梦的信号（Govrin / 比昂）。**

> The analyst's anxiety, in this framework, becomes a diagnostic indicator of their refusal to engage in the dreaming process. When the analyst experiences anxiety during a session, it may signal that they are defending against the primary process thinking that would allow them to metabolize the patient's communications.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

对应的三联公式：

> The formula "not (dream) = resist = not (introject)" captures the interconnected nature of these defensive operations

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1992）

**（二）遐想的背景噪音消失（Ogden，Jim 案例）。** Ogden 在自己身上观察到一个可辨认的物理事件——遐想的「边缘视野」整体熄灭：

> I noticed that the usual background noise of my mind—the thoughts that came and went, the "peripheral vision" of reverie, even the bodily feelings of my heart pumping, my breath moving, were absent.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五章「Do You Watch TV?」

**（三）分析师撤退、沉默，并以话语自我保护（Eshel，P. 案例）。** 这是「关上通道」最完整的临床样本：

> I was thinking to myself: What is the point of all the hard work, of this entire analysis ...? And I withdrew, fell silent.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Clinical Illustration」（引 Eshel, 2005, pp. 1089–1090）

病人**立刻**察觉：

> And I realized he sensed and knew that I had *abandoned* him, left him wounded and lost on the battlefield, and had gone off to save myself.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Clinical Illustration」

而分析师的自我判词更锋利——她的话同时也是自我保护：

> I felt that my words were also an attempt to protect myself from the intensity of this violent, repetitive desperation to which I had no answer.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Clinical Illustration」

以及最彻底的一条：

> no attempt on my part to understand and interpret had any significance, meaning, or impact.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Clinical Illustration」

**（四）涵容经由相互贯通而发生（Eshel）。** 与上述失败相对，Eshel 给出了涵容的正面定义：

> It is my view that containing evolves through the interconnectedness of patient and analyst's psyches, whereby the analyst's psyche is used as an area of experiencing and transformation for the patient's projected, split-off, unbearable experiences

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「The Be(com)ing of a New Dimension」

以及她对自身所受比昂吸引力的自述：

> his formulation of the fundamental transformative capacity of the object—the primal mother or the analyst (through reverie, alpha-function, and containing of the patient's unbearable, projected experiences)

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「Be(com)ing a Psychoanalyst」

## 八、这一机制的准备工作与伦理重量

比昂对分析师的状态有非常具体的要求：

> Bion's (1992) observation that "the analyst must have plenty of sleep" (p. 120) if they are to dream the session without sleeping reflects his understanding that the analyst's psychological state directly affects their capacity to provide the containing function that analysis requires.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> cultivating what Bion (1965) calls "analytically trained intuition" (p. 18)

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> The analyst must be "tough and resilient enough to be regarded and treated as insane while being sane"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1991）

比昂同时对这套机制的滥用设有警戒：

> His warning about the dangerous doctrine opening the way for the analyst who theorizes unhampered by the facts of practice reveals his concern that psychoanalytic theory might become detached from clinical reality

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1965）

伦理结论因此不是修辞：

> The analyst's capacity to transform beta elements into alpha elements carries enormous responsibility—they become, in effect, co-creators of the patient's psychic reality. This is why the quality of the analyst's reverie, their capacity to maintain authentic engagement with unconscious processes, becomes not merely a technical consideration but a moral imperative.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 九、与相邻概念的关系

| 相邻项 | 关系 | 差别 | 详见 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 诠释（interpretation） | 诠释在此机制中是**产物**而非起点 | 诠释必须「被涵容过」才是联结，否则是排泄 | [[concepts/concept-interpretation]] |
| 遴选事实（selected fact） | 同为比昂的「从混沌到秩序」工具 | 遴选事实是**发现**，诠释是**修复**；遴选事实后来演化为 O | [[concepts/concept-transformation-o-to-k]] |
| O 与转化 O→K | 本机制的晚期形态 | β→α 属「知识中的转化」；O 中的转化更靠下、更未分化 | [[concepts/concept-transformation-o-to-k]] |
| 负性能力（negative capability） | 本机制所需的分析师姿态 | 放弃记忆、欲望、理解，是接收的前提条件 | [[concepts/concept-negative-capability]] |
| 顶点（vertex） | 观察本机制时所用的立足点 | 顶点管「怎么看」，涵容管「怎么看之前先接住什么」 | [[concepts/concept-vertex]] |
| 抱持（holding） | 同属环境供给谱系 | 抱持偏环境侧，涵容偏代谢侧；Eshel 强调二者**都不够** | [[concepts/concept-holding-going-on-being]] |
| 同在（withness） | Eshel 对涵容的下探版 | 涵容在「之间／之内」的位置之争 | [[concepts/concept-withness]] |
