# 解离与自体状态（dissociation and self-states）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**解离与自体状态（dissociation and self-states）** 是菲利普·布隆伯格（Philip Bromberg）与关系取向的核心概念。其主张是：心灵**不是统一的整体**，而是由多个**自体状态（self-states）**构成的集合；这些状态之间可能处于保护性隔离，尤其当创伤僵化了经验之间的正常流动时。

这一模型的临床后果是彻底的：**心理健康不在于整合为单一自体，而在于能够在诸状态之间流动、同时保持连续性。** 分析师的任务因此从「诠释无意识冲突」转为「识别并照护病人的多重自体状态」——他听的是**自体状态的切换**，而非无意识内容；他的目标是**促进诸状态之间的沟通**，而非使无意识成为意识。

本页还处理两个延伸议题：（一）这一模型与弗洛伊德「压抑—无意识」地形学模型的关系——本批资料**未出现**布隆伯格本人对该替代关系的直接表述，只提供可被引文支撑的对照；（二）它与什洛米特·亚德林-加多特（Shlomit Yadlin-Gadot）**真理轴（truth axes）**模型的呼应——后者把自体状态直接写入其理论，却又**明确不同意**布隆伯格关于自体状态是「零碎的、无限的、情境性的」这一立场。

> ⚠️ **重要预告**：本页第八节如实记录——**「standing in the spaces（站在间隙之中）」这一常被引作布隆伯格核心口号的短语，在 `raw/` 六份文献中检索为 0 命中**，本页不予引用。

## 一、定义：心灵是自体状态的集合

Govrin 对布隆伯格「革命性视野」的概述，以一句关于**心灵结构**的断言开场：

> The mind, in Bromberg's vision, operates as a collection of self-states that may exist in protective isolation from one another, particularly when trauma has rigidified the normal flow between different aspects of experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「The Dissociative Gap as Clinical Reality」

治疗行动的定义随之改变——不是诠释性揭示，而是**共同穿越解离经验**：

> Philip Bromberg's (2010) central insight concerns what he terms "minding the dissociative gap", that is, the recognition that therapeutic action emerges through the shared navigation of dissociated experience rather than through traditional interpretive unveiling.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

其最直接的临床翻案是：**看起来像阻抗的东西，其实是保护性解离**：

> The analyst's task becomes radically different when viewed through this lens. Rather than interpreting unconscious conflicts, the analyst must recognize and work with the patient's multiple self-states, understanding that what appears as resistance often represents the protective dissociation of "not-me" aspects of experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】「not-me（非我）」是这一模型的术语枢纽：被解离的不是「被禁止的愿望」，而是**不能被承认为自己的那部分经验**。

## 二、它取代了什么：与「压抑—无意识」地形学模型的对照

### 2.1 弗洛伊德一侧的原文

弗洛伊德在 1900—1915 年间运作于**地形学模型（topographical model）**，其诠释工作是考古学式的：

> Between 1900 and 1915, Freud operated with what he called the "topographical" model of the mind, dividing mental life into three regions: the conscious, the preconscious, and the unconscious.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「The Structural Revolution」

> Under the topographical model, interpretation functioned much like archaeological excavation, with the analyst tracing associations back to latent wishes and uncovering repressed contents (Freud, 1900, pp. 277–309; Freud, 1915a, p. 173). The primary aim was to bring hidden material into consciousness, with resistance manifesting mainly as censorship or repression.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

### 2.2 解离模型一侧的结构性主张

Govrin 把关系取向的重构归纳为四个维度，其中**解离**被明确指派为**精神组织的结构**——即它占据的正是地形学模型中「压抑」所占的位置：

> Through examining four interrelated dimensions of this reconceptualization—enactment as the medium of unconscious communication, dissociation as the structure of psychic organization, the analytic third, and self-disclosure as the vehicle of authentic engagement…

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章导言

> ⚠️ **本批资料的限制（必须如实标注）**
> **议题**：解离／自体状态模型是否**取代**了弗洛伊德的「压抑—无意识」地形学模型？
> **可被引文支撑的部分**：（a）弗洛伊德地形学模型以**压抑**为阻力之主要形式（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章，见上）；（b）关系取向把**解离**重新指派为「the structure of psychic organization」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章导言）；（c）布隆伯格本人把治疗行动定义为「shared navigation of dissociated experience **rather than** traditional interpretive unveiling」。
> **无法被引文支撑的部分**：**布隆伯格本人（或任何一位关系取向作者）在本批资料中，并未直接说出「解离取代压抑」或「以自体状态取代地形学模型」这类句子。** 布隆伯格的原文（《Minding the dissociative gap》等）不在 `raw/` 中，全部内容经 Govrin 转述。
> **备注**：因此本节的标题表述为「**它取代了什么：与……的对照**」而非断言。对照本身成立且有双方引文，但「取代」这一动词属**【推断】**，需显式标注。

### 2.3 一个旁证：解离作为「尚未被思考者」的领域

埃谢尔在其相互贯通模型中，把解离与「未被思考者」连写，作为分析所要抵达的层面——这是本批资料中少数直接处理解离的理论句：

> Within the deep interconnected existence of their psyches, an actual, nonlinear (synergic and transcendent), new possibility is created and present, of getting in touch with, experiencing, knowing, containing, and effecting hitherto unknown, dissociated, unthinkable aspects of being and relating (Eshel 2001, 2002, 2004a, 2004b, 2005a, 2006, 2008, 2009a, 2009b).

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「THE BE(COM)ING OF A NEW DIMENSION」

她还把解离列入自己与病人共同穿越的临床地形：

> Thus, I went with my patients on a psychoanalytic serendipitous journey through "*black holes*," through feelings of deadness, sleepiness, dissociation, petrification and silences, yearnings and longings, into the depths of perversion, and the enigma of telepathic dreams (Eshel 1998a, 1998b, 2001, 2004a, 2004b, 2005b, 2006, 2008, 2009a, 2009b).

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「THE BE(COM)ING OF A NEW DIMENSION」

## 三、技术后果：听自体状态的切换

这是该模型在临床上最具体、也最具操作性的部分。**分析师要听的东西变了**：

> Bromberg's approach demands a fundamental reconsideration of interpretive technique. The analyst must listen for self-state shifts rather than unconscious content, attending to sudden changes in topic, affects, or ways of being that signal movement between different organizations of experience (Bromberg, 2010).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「Metaphor, Cooccurrence, and Symbolic Transformation」

**目标也随之改变**——不是使无意识成为意识：

> The goal becomes facilitating communication between self-states rather than making unconscious material conscious.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

### 3.1 「冲突语言」的识别

布隆伯格最具标志性的技术贡献，是识别病人使用的**伪冲突语言**。当病人说「我努力想感到自信而不是害怕」，那个「而不是」暴露的不是冲突，而是**抹除**：

> Bromberg illustrates this with his description of working with patients who use "conflict language" (2010, p. 23) while actually being dissociated. A patient might say they are trying hard to feel confident instead of afraid but cannot seem to get it right. Bromberg recognizes that the phrase "instead of" represents false conflict language—the patient is not actually experiencing internal conflict but is attempting to obliterate one self-state to please the analyst.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

其干预方式是把那个**不在场的自体状态**请回场内，而不是解释恐惧：

> His intervention addresses this directly: he might observe that there seems to be another part of them behind the scenes that does not like what he just said, and that they appear to be trying to keep their relationship safe by staying away from those unruly feelings. Rather than interpreting the fear as resistance to confidence, he recognizes two legitimate self-states that need to coexist rather than one eliminating the other.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】「two legitimate self-states that need to coexist rather than one eliminating the other（两个都合法的自体状态需要共存，而非一个消灭另一个）」——这是健康标准最明确的陈述：**健康＝共存，不是整合为单一自体。**

### 3.2 所需的分析师姿态：一种新的倾听立场

> This shift requires what Bromberg calls a different "listening stance" (2010, p. 20)—one that attends to the inherently confusing nature of clinical process rather than seeking to organize it into coherent understanding. The analyst must tolerate not knowing while remaining present to the patient's struggle to integrate disparate aspects of experience. Interpretation, when it occurs, emerges from this shared process of discovery rather than from the analyst's theoretical understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文校勘】原文此处页码写作 "(1910. p. 20)"，系 PDF 转换导致的排版错误，正确年份应为 2010（同段前后均引 Bromberg, 2010）。本页按 2010 标注并在此说明。

## 四、羞耻、承认与治疗行动

羞耻在这一模型中被赋予**结构性的信号功能**——它标示出哪些经验必须保持为「非我」，以维系依恋联结：

> Bromberg's emphasis on shame as a central organizing force in dissociative processes has profound implications for interpretive work. Shame often represents the affective signal that certain aspects of experience must remain "not-me" to preserve attachment bonds.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「Shame, Recognition, and Therapeutic Action」

治疗关系由此成为一个**实验室**，其实验对象是新的承认形式：

> The therapeutic relationship becomes a laboratory for experimenting with new forms of recognition that do not provoke the shame-dissociation cycle. The analyst's interpretive task involves recognizing the patient's dissociated self-states without inadvertently inducing shame through premature integration attempts. This requires extraordinary sensitivity to the patient's capacity to tolerate self-reflection at any given moment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

> The interpretation must emerge from a place of genuine recognition rather than theoretical understanding, as only such recognition can provide the safety necessary for integration to occur.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】「premature integration attempts（过早的整合尝试）」——**整合本身在此成为风险**，这与古典模型把整合当作治疗目标形成最尖锐的对立。

## 五、隐喻、共现与象征转化（唐纳德·斯特恩的延展）

斯特恩（2009）把布隆伯格推进到象征过程：治疗任务是让**共现（cooccurrence）**被认出并发展为隐喻性理解：

> Stern's (2009) exploration of metaphor and cooccurrence extends Bromberg's thinking into the realm of symbolic process. The therapeutic task involves creating conditions where cooccurrences can be recognized and developed into metaphorical understanding. This process cannot be forced through interpretation; it emerges through the gradual softening of dissociative barriers as different self-states become more comfortable with each other's existence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

而分析师自己承受解离的能力，是这一过程的**模型**：

> The analyst's capacity to tolerate and work with their own dissociative reactions becomes crucial, as it models the possibility of holding contradictory aspects of experience simultaneously.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

## 六、与「真理轴」模型的呼应：亚德林-加多特

本组资料中最值得注意的跨文献呼应，是亚德林-加多特把**自体状态直接写入**她的真理轴理论。她的核心命题是：**轴、自体状态、现实图像与被经验的真理，四者结合成一个「真理轴」**：

> In each reality resides a characteristic self-state that is guided, directed, and motivated by its truth. *The joining together of epistemic assumption, characteristic self-state, image of reality, and experienced truth form a truth axis*. In that sense, a truth axis is a multidimensional mental domain.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「From Truth Notions to Truth Axes」

诸轴**并存**、各造一个现实、各造一个自体——这正是「诸自体状态并存」的另一种说法：

> The various truth axes coexist in consciousness as prisms through which various realities are construed, each giving rise to a different experience of self and world.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

> The vulnerable subjective self of inner truths, to which experiences of shame, exposure, and fear of annihilation are linked, differs from the matter-of-fact, realistic self of the correspondent axis. The self of the Intersubjective axis is the one supported by agreement of its community, or lost within it, the one experiencing anxieties of alienation and questions of belongingness.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

她也把**解离**接进这个框架——不是被压抑，而是**被关闭（foreclosed）**：

> Personality and experience render some axes dominant, while others remain foreclosed and dissociated.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，摘要

以及**诸轴互不取消**这一与「共存」同构的主张：

> Usually, one lives according to directives of various different truth axes simultaneously. Life, as we experience it, eclipses any one register, and is perpetually given in multiple significations.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

### 6.1 但这是一次**明确的反驳**，不是简单的呼应

亚德林-加多特在引用布隆伯格之后，立刻**点名不同意**关系取向／后现代立场对自体状态的规定：

> Recurring patterns of world experience create different states of self in the form of balancing mechanisms (Bromberg, 2009; Mitchell, 1991; Rowan, 2010). The various states of the self are not perceived here as sporadic, infinite, or situational, which is the accepted position of many relational and postmodern writers (e.g., Bromberg, 2009; Gubrium & Holstein, 1994; Rowan, 2010). Rather, the different self-states are understood as subject to general alignments that correspond to the different truth axes; these alignments are universal and may be defined and described.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：**诸自体状态的性质——是零碎、无限、情境性的，还是受普遍排列（对应诸真理轴）所规范的？**
> **A 方立场**：**关系取向／后现代立场**（布隆伯格、米切尔、Rowan、Gubrium & Holstein，经亚德林-加多特转述）。自体状态是「sporadic, infinite, or situational」——由「recurring patterns of world experience」产生的「balancing mechanisms」，不被普遍结构所规定。Govrin 一侧的对应表述是：心灵「operates as a collection of self-states that may exist in protective isolation from one another」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章）。
> **B 方立场**：**亚德林-加多特**。自体状态「are understood as subject to general alignments that correspond to the different truth axes; **these alignments are universal and may be defined and described**」——即存在**有限、可定义、普遍**的排列（对应六条真理轴），而非无限的情境性碎片。
> **备注**：**可调和度低，属认识论层面的分歧**。二人对「多重自体并存」完全一致；分歧在于**多重性有无普遍结构**。值得注意的是方向性：**亚德林-加多特在此比关系取向「更古典」**——她主张可定义的普遍排列，接近结构论；关系取向主张不可总括的情境性。这与她把「解离」改写为「foreclosed（被关闭）」一致：**轴是被关闭的，不是被分裂出去的。**

## 七、批评：解离转向的代价

Govrin 对这一转向提出了最实质的一击——它可能**以关系真实性换取分析严密性**，且这笔交易是否划算尚未可知：

> Yet Bromberg's emphasis on mutual enactment and shared dissociation raises intriguing questions about the nature of analytic understanding itself. The risk inherent in Bromberg's approach may be the gradual erosion of what makes psychoanalysis distinctively analytic. When the analyst becomes so focused on managing dissociative enactments and facilitating self-state communication, the capacity for sustained thought about unconscious process may atrophy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「Critical Reflections on the Dissociative Turn」

> The analyst's mind, constantly alert to relational nuance and affective shifts, may lose the contemplative quality that allows for deeper pattern recognition across time. While Bromberg would argue that this represents an evolution beyond outdated models of analytic neutrality, critics might suggest that it represents a fundamental departure from the reflective stance that enables genuine psychological insight.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

> The question remains whether therapeutic effectiveness requires the analyst to sacrifice a certain kind of analytic rigor in favor of relational authenticity, and whether such sacrifice ultimately serves or undermines the therapeutic process.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章
