# 团体性人格（corporate personality）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/symington-act-of-freedom]]

## 摘要

**团体性人格（corporate personality）**是内维尔·西明顿（Neville Symington）在《分析师的自由行动作为治疗性改变的动因》中提出的概念：分析师与病人在某一层面上共同构成一个**单一系统**，可称为一个"实体"；从两人进入所谓分析的那一刻起，他们就共同处在一个**错觉系统**之中，双方都被卷入。融合经由双方的**超我**而发生——"**正是通过超我，团体性人格才得以实现**"。

这一模型带来三项重述：**移情与反移情**不再是分属两人的现象，而是同一系统的两个部分、一个统一体，是"分析工作缓慢消解的**共享错觉**"；**阻抗**不再仅位于病人，而是一个双方共同卷入的系统；**超我**则借助塔尔科特·帕森斯（Talcott Parsons）的扩展被理解为**内化的共同文化**——不仅是道德标准，而是共同文化的全部成分。

由此，精神分析的功能被界定为催化**自我对自我的接触（ego to ego contact）**——人格中"非团体性的、个人的、个体性的"区域；这被明确置于与**宗教**相反的方向上，因为宗教的中心社会功能是"把人结合进团体性实体"。戈夫林（Aner Govrin）评价说，这一概念**预示了后来的关系／场域概念**。关联概念见 [[concepts/concept-analytic-act-of-freedom]]。

## 一、概念的提出：分析师与病人共构单一系统

> I think at one level the analyst and patient together make a single system. Together they form an entity which we might call a corporate personality. From the moment that patient and analyst engage in what we call an analysis the two are together part of an illusory system. Both are caught into it

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

西明顿同时反驳了"分析师只是一面镜子"这一近期文献立场，称之为"**严重的低估**"：

> Recent literature stresses that the analyst is not just a mirror but this is a gross understatement. The analyst is lassoed into the patient’s illusory world. He is more involved in it, more victim to it than the average social contact. As the analytical work proceeds the analyst slowly disengages himself from it

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

注意这里的关键顺序：**卷入在先，脱离在后**。分析工作的可能性不在于避免卷入，而在于"缓慢地把自己从中脱离出来"。

## 二、超我作为内化的共同文化

团体性人格经由超我而实现：

> It is through the super egos that corporate personality is effected. When the patient first comes to the analyst’s consulting room it is probable that a fusing takes place of the analyst and patient via the superegos of each

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

为了支撑这一模型，西明顿借用社会学家帕森斯对弗洛伊德超我概念的扩展——**弗洛伊德聚焦道德标准虽"深刻正确"，但视野"过窄"**：

> the place of the superego as part of the structure of the personality must be understood in terms of the relation between personality and the total common culture, by virtue of which a stable system of social interaction on the human levels becomes possible. Freud’s insight was profoundly correct when he focused on the element of moral standards. This is, indeed, central and crucial, but it does seem that Freud’s view was too narrow. The inescapable conclusion is that not only moral standards, but *all the components of the common* *culture* are internalized as part of the personality structure. Moral standards, indeed, cannot in this respect be dissociated from the *content* of the orientation patterns which they regulate

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」（引 Parsons, 1952, p. 23）

错觉性感受的构成因此不只是家庭的道德，还包括阶级、民族忠诚与内在的（尤其是破坏性的）冲动：

> These illusory feelings in the patient are partly the internalized values of the family of origin, of his class and national allegiances together with the impulses, especially the destructive ones, from within. The impulses from within are strengthened and supported by the cultural values

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

分析早期的典型状态是双方一同被这一**个人—文化错觉**所攫住：

> At the beginning of the analysis (and often for a long time), the patient and analyst are held in thrall by the power of this personal–cultural illusion. This is possible because the patient and analyst become part of a system through which communication takes place. In his passive role where he does not assert his own view of the world, the analyst allows himself to be swept into the personal– cultural contents of the patient’s superego and interprets within that framework. Analyst and patient are part of a system and are joined through the superego parts of their personalities

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

## 三、移情与反移情是共享错觉

> In this way transference and countertransference are two parts of a single system; together they form a unity. They are the shared illusions which the work of analysis slowly undoes

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

> Transference and countertransference are emotional expressions of this fusion

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

## 四、阻抗是一个系统，而非仅位于病人

这是团体性人格模型最具临床后果的推论。西明顿明确拒绝把阻抗定位于病人：

> At the same time there is another process at work, in both the analyst and the patient, whose goal is to sabotage the analysis. This process is located in the superego and makes use of illusions and cultural myths as its instrument. We call this process resistance but I have wanted to emphasize that this is a system in which both analyst and patient are involved, not something that is just located in the patient

— Symington, *Freedom*，「结论」

> If this model is accepted, then it follows that within the corporate personality there is a process of resistance and transference occurring in the whole entity, in other words in the patient and analyst. There is, however, also a process of analysis occurring in both persons, in the total entity. The process of analysis is the guarantee that there can be movement out of a locked situation

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

完整的系统描述——**共享错觉 + 共享阻抗 + 与之搏斗的精神分析过程**，三者同时在两人之中运作：

> So in the corporate entity there is a shared illusion or delusion (transference/ countertransference) and shared resistance and there is also a process which we call psychoanalysis which fights a slow but persistent battle in both against the shared resistance and illusion

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

一个后果：**既有的理论立场既可能被阻抗过程所用，也可能被良性的精神分析过程所用**：

> Received theoretical positions may be used by the resistant process, as they also may be used by the benign psychoanalytical process. The patient and analyst as a corporate entity are involved in these two processes

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

与之相连的是他对"古典技术"的批评（同时声明绝非轻蔑技术本身）：

> I hope it will not be inferred that I am scorning analytic technique; this would be the very opposite of what I am intending to say. After all the soul of analytic technique is to free analyst and patient from the normal social constraints and so favour development of the inner world. The problem is when ‘classical technique’ becomes the agent of a new social constraint

— Symington, *Freedom*，「临床例证三之后的反思」

## 五、精神分析催化「自我对自我的接触」；与宗教的方向相反

> Psychoanalysis is a pro-cess which catalyses the ego to ego contact: that area of the person-ality that is non-corporate, personal and individual. In this way psy choanalysis is working in the opposite way to religion, whose central social function is to bind people together into corporate entities

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

维系二人的虚假信念被比作**微观的社会黏合剂**：

> This belief that held both together is that social glue in microcosm that binds together the numerous communities and groupings of society

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

自我对自我的接触之所以被称为**革命**，是因为新的现实与新的成长由此开始：

> This type of contact is a revolution because new reality, new growth begins. In fact, it is the only true revolution that does occur within human affairs

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

需要注明：此处是把宗教作为**社会功能的类型学对照**，而非对其信仰内容的批评。

## 六、洞见的行动：错误观念位于超我，新概念须被输入超我

理解的行动植根于最具个人性的自我，错误观念位于超我；洞见被表达于诠释的那一刻，幻象在**双方**同时被驱散：

> The act of understanding is rooted in what is most personal, in the ego, but the false ideas are located in the superego. At the moment of insight, expressed in interpretation, the illusions or false ideas are banished both in analyst and in patient

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

随后还有第二步——**超我必须被重写**：

> Because, subsequent to this personal act of understanding new concepts have to be imported into the superego in order that the latter agency can now reflect the new changes that have taken place in the ego

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

这与 x 现象的机制描述完全吻合：**属于超我的感受遮蔽了属于自我的感受**：

> In all the instances of x-phenomenon that I have given, the analyst’s personal feelings have been shrouded by illusory feelings, emanating from the patient’s unconscious superego. This could be formulated by saying that the feelings belonging to the superego have cloaked the feelings belonging to the ego

— Symington, *Freedom*，「理论建构」

## 七、分离需要触及分析师的核心感受

> In order to separate, the patient needs to get access to the analyst’s core feelings. His interpretations need to flow from here to as great an extent as possible if the patient is to be able to separate. This is most especially true for the psychotic patient whose fusion at the superego level is greatest and whose need for ego to ego contact is also greatest

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

病人对此有锐利的检验——他说的到底是**我**所思所感，还是精神分析传统的既成教条（即仅仅是一个超我内容）？

> It greatly concerned one patient whether what I said to him was what *I* thought or felt or was just a received dictum of the psychoanalytical tradition and therefore just a superego content

— Symington, *Freedom*，「洞见行动」

## 八、形而上学结论：人既是个体，又是团体性实体的部分

全文末段同时是对整个学界的批评：

> With the exception of Winnicott I think that most analysts operate on the assumption that people are separate entities. I think that the x-phenomenon and the particular form of knowledge that it must imply means that people are individuals and yet part of a corporate entity

— Symington, *Freedom*，「结论」

> Because we are parts of a corporate entity then as soon as analyst and patient come together in the same room there is an immediate adaptation and fusing. The corporate entity instantly establishes itself. Socially this occurs when two people meet, but, in this case, ego-to-ego contact is kept to a minimum, so also the joint illusion is kept to a minimum. In psychoanalysis the latter is enhanced but only, so to speak, so as to hypercathect it and work through it and give place to the personal

— Symington, *Freedom*，「结论」

最后一句给出了精神分析设置的奇特逻辑：**融合被增强，恰恰是为了对它作过度投注、修通它，并让位给个人性的东西**。

## 九、戈夫林的评价：预示后来的关系／场域概念

> Symington’s concept of the “corporate personality”—the fused entity that analyst and patient create at the superego level— anticipates many later relational concepts (Symington, 1983, p. 288). Both participants become trapped in shared illusions comprising the patient’s internalized family values and destructive patterns. The analyst’s task is not to interpret these patterns from the outside but to achieve freedom from within them, and this internal liberation somehow communicates itself to the patient before any words are spoken

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第二节「转向主体性：内在自由作为治疗行动」

亚德林-加多特（Shlomit Yadlin-Gadot）在转述 x 现象时也保留了这一概念的核心结构：

> Symington suggests an elaborate explanation for the “x-phenom- enon” involving unconscious communication and an illusory corporate personality system shared by patient and analyst and dominated by elements of superego. The analyst’s act of freedom brings to consciousness the imperatives of this system and the ability to question them

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

## 十、⚠️ 矛盾标注：共同实体是"待消解的错觉"还是"生成的意义场"？

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：分析二元共构的那个"第三物"，其价值是**负面的**（待消解的错觉系统），还是**正面的**（意义生成的场域）？
> **A 方立场**：西明顿——共同实体位于**超我**层面，由共享错觉与共享阻抗构成，是"分析工作缓慢消解"的对象；治疗目标是**从中脱离**，走向自我对自我的接触。他并且把维系二人的信念称为"社会黏合剂的微观形态"。
> **B 方立场**：关系／场域取向（奥格登的分析性第三方）——共同空间是**生成的**：「This third is not simply the sum of two subjectivities, but rather a dialectical creation that simultaneously preserves and negates the separateness of each participant」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「分析性第三方：作为涌现属性的诠释」）。在此，诠释"从第三方内部涌现，而非从外部施加于它"。
> **备注**：部分可调和，但存在真实的重心差异。戈夫林把西明顿读作关系概念的**先驱**，暗示二者是连续谱；但价值符号相反——西明顿把共同实体视为**病人病理的社会化延伸**（须被修通并"让位给个人性的东西"），关系取向则视之为**意义的产地**。差异的根源在于：西明顿的"共同"锚定在**超我**，关系取向的"共同"锚定在**主体间场域**。建库时应保留这一分歧，不宜互相化约。
