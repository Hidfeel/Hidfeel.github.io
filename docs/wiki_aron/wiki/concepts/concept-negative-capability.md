# 负性能力（negative capability）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]、[[raw/ogden-ontological-psychoanalysis]]、[[raw/eshel-withness]]

## 摘要

**负性能力（negative capability）** 源自济慈（John Keats），经比昂引入精神分析，指一种「能够处于不确定、神秘、怀疑之中，而不急躁地追求事实与理性」的能力。在比昂的框架里，它与**三条戒律**直接绑定：分析师必须放弃记忆、欲望与理解——不是因为这些东西无用，而是因为它们会让分析师**预先知道**本应被当下遭遇的东西。

负性能力因此不是一种技术，而是一种**分析姿态**：等待而非求知，接收而非解码，在场而非解释。它在本组文献中有三条清晰的延伸线索：Govrin 把它与比昂的 O 与诠释克制相连；Ogden 用温尼科特的「等待病人创造性地抵达理解」表述同源立场（**但 Ogden 全篇未出现 negative capability 一词**）；Ferro 与 Civitarese 则把它发展为「在黑暗中一起绊倒」的场域技术。它与温尼科特的**抱持（holding）**有明确亲缘性：两者都是环境侧的供给，而不是内容侧的解释；差别在于，抱持偏供给的持续性，负性能力偏分析师的**克制**。

## 一、济慈的原句，与比昂的引入

Govrin 在「O」一节的末尾给出了负性能力的出场语境——它是**诠释克制**所要求的纪律：

> This interpretive restraint requires a particular kind of discipline from the analyst. Rather than rushing to fill silences with understanding or to resolve confusion with explanation, the analyst must learn to tolerate states of not-knowing that may feel professionally uncomfortable. The analyst must develop what Keats calls "negative capability"—the capacity to remain in uncertainty and doubt rather than irritably reaching after fact and reason.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O and the Limits of Language in Bionian Interpretation」

紧接着是比昂所引的济慈原句：

> As Bion (1970) writes "When a man is capable of being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact and reason" (125).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 125）

Govrin 在全书的章节导览中，把负性能力放在比昂全部工作的枢纽位置：

> His concept of "O"—the unknowable ultimate reality of emotional experience—challenged analysts to abandon their interpretive certainties in favor of what he called "negative capability." As we explore in detail, Bion's approach turns the analyst from an expert decoder into a participant in a shared journey toward meaning that neither analyst nor patient can predict in advance.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，「The Organization of This Book」

而在第三章的结论处，负性能力被提升为比昂留给后世最根本的东西：

> Perhaps most significantly, his work suggests that the future of psychoanalytic interpretation lies not in developing increasingly sophisticated theoretical frameworks but in cultivating the analyst's capacity for negative capability—the ability to remain present with uncertainty while trusting in the emergence of meaning from authentic engagement.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Conclusion: Interpretation as Faith」

**一个必要的限定**（这决定了该概念的定位）：

> This does not mean abandoning all theoretical knowledge or clinical judgment, but rather holding these tools lightly, ready to set them aside when they become obstacles to genuine encounter.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 二、三条戒律：放弃记忆、欲望与理解

负性能力不是一句态度宣言，它有具体的操作规定。第一条表述出现在比昂对「例行诠释」失败的回应中：

> The analyst must wait in a state that abandons expectation, memory, or desire, in order to receive these projected parts of the patient and contain them, rather than defensively rejecting them

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1992/1967）

Ogden 用他自己的语言给出了同一条戒律，并说明了**为什么**——记忆指向已不存在的东西，欲望指向尚未发生的东西，两者都不可知：

> Bion insists that, as psychoanalysts, we must shed the desire to understand and instead engage as fully as possible in the experience of being with the patient. ... because memory is what we think we know based on what no longer exists, and is no longer knowable. And we must renounce "desires for results, 'cure,' or even understanding"

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章「Bion's Contributions to」（引 Bion, 1967, p. 137）

而两者的共同后果，被比昂表述为对**直觉**的阻碍：

> Memory of what we think we know and desire for understanding of what has not yet occurred (and consequently unknowable) are both a "hindrance to the psychoanalyst's intuition of the reality [of what is occurring in the present moment of a session] with which he must be at one"

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章（引 Bion, 1967, p. 136）

Govrin 把三条戒律合在一起，作为「成为 O」的纪律条件：

> It requires what he calls "becoming O" rather than knowing about O—a transformation in the analyst's state of being that creates conditions for genuine encounter with psychological reality. This is not a technique that can be taught through instruction but a capacity that must be cultivated through discipline—the systematic suspension of memory, desire, and understanding in favor of presence with the immediate, evolving truth of the analytic situation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

在第三章的另一处，这一姿态被命名为**生产性的不确定**：

> The analyst working in a Bionian framework learns to distinguish between interpretation that opens and interpretation that closes ... This requires what we might call productive uncertainty—the ability to remain genuinely not-knowing while maintaining therapeutic presence and authority.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

以及它最朴素的定义——分析师不知道自己将说什么：

> This is perhaps Bion's greatest gift to analytic thinking: the recognition that our most sophisticated tools for creating meaning are also our greatest obstacles to encountering truth. The selected fact emerges not through clever interpretation but through the analyst's willingness to be genuinely surprised by what emerges in the space between two minds struggling toward understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> **【推断】** 本组 raw 文献中**没有**出现比昂「Notes on Memory and Desire」的原文引文；三条戒律的内容来自 Govrin 的转述（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章）与 Ogden 的两处引 Bion 1967。因此「三条戒律」这一提法本身在本 Wiki 中属【综合】，其英文表述不可直接归给比昂本人。

## 三、与 Ogden「等待病人创造性地抵达理解」的关系

**【综合，附缺席标注】** 经 Grep 逐字核实，`negative capability`、`Keats`、`uncertainties, mysteries, doubts` 在 Ogden 的 *Ontological Psychoanalysis* 全篇中**均未出现**（命中数为 0）。Ogden 是用**等待（waiting）**这一同源词汇表述同一立场的。

他引温尼科特那句著名的自责：

> It appalls me to think how much deep change I have prevented or delayed … by my personal need to interpret. If only we can wait, the patient arrives at understanding creatively and with immense joy, and I now enjoy this joy more than I used to enjoy the sense of having been clever. [Winnicott 1969,p.86]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章（引 Winnicott, 1969, p. 86）

并把这句直接转成了技术指令：

> While in epistemological psychoanalysis the analyst's role centrally involves conveying in the form of interpretation the analyst's understanding of the leading edge of anxiety in the present moment of the analysis, in a predominantly ontological psychoanalysis the analyst had better "wait" (Winnicott 1969,p.86) before conveying his or her understandings to the patient

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章

而「等待」的理由，与负性能力的理由完全同构——重点不在所得的知识，而在**抵达**这一经验本身：

> From the perspective of ontological psychoanalysis, it is not the knowledge arrived at by patient and analyst that is the central point; rather, it is the patient's experience of "arriv[ing] at understanding creatively and with immense joy," an experience in which the patient is engaged not predominantly in searching for self-understanding, but in experiencing the process of becoming more fully himself.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章

Govrin 在第七章对 Ogden 的转述，把这层伦理含义点破——**诠释的热情本身可以是暴力的**：

> Winnicott's confession that his personal need to interpret had prevented or delayed deep change reveals the potential violence of epistemological enthusiasm

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节「From Knowing to Being—The Ontological Turn」

### 两个临床样本：不问、不说

**（一）L 女士：不问。** Ogden 的反事实反思，是「等待」最精确的操作性说明：

> Had I asked, "What's frightening you?" or "Tell me more," I think that the patient very likely would have felt that I was backing away from the intensity of her feeling by asking her to engage in secondary process thinking aimed at finding reasons and explanations, as opposed to experiencing the patient's way of introducing herself to me (telling me who she was at that moment).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五章「Of Course You Are」

**（二）Mr. C.：不说。** 面对一个梦，Ogden 拒绝给出他的理解：

> I had not the slightest inclination to talk with Mr. C. about my understanding of the dream. I said to him, "What a wonderful dream that was"

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五章「What do you want to be when you grow up?」

Govrin 的评注：

> This response honored the patient's achievement of self- acceptance, celebrating being rather than seeking understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节

> **【推断】** 把 Ogden 的「等待」与比昂的「负性能力」接为同一主张，在本组 raw 文献中**没有直接文本支撑**：两者从未在同一文本中被并置，Ogden 也未引用济慈或比昂 1970 年那段话。这一接合属于结构同构的推断，依据是 Govrin 第七章把两者放在同一节（「From Knowing to Being—The Ontological Turn」）中论述。

## 四、与温尼科特「抱持」的亲缘性

负性能力与抱持同属**环境侧**供给，而非内容侧解释。三处文本支撑这一亲缘性。

**（一）Ogden 引温尼科特：治疗发生在两个游戏区的重叠处；若不能游戏，先处理不能游戏这件事。**

> Psychotherapy takes place in the overlap of two areas of playing, that of the patient and that of the therapist. The corollary to this is that where playing is not possible then the work done by the therapist is directed towards bringing the patient from a state of not being able to play into a state of being able to play. [Winnicott 1971a,p.38, italics in the original]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章（引 Winnicott, 1971a, p. 38，原文着重）

以及与之配套的母亲／分析师一侧的承诺——**绝不提出那个问题**：

> it is a matter of agreement between us and the baby that we will never ask the question: ' Did you conceive of this [object] or was it presented to you from without?' The important point is that no decision on this point is expected. The question is not to be formulated. [Winnicott 1971c,p.12, italics in the original]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二章（引 Winnicott, 1971c, p. 12，原文着重）

这与负性能力的同构性是显见的：二者都以**悬置判断**为条件。

**（二）Eshel：抱持与涵容同属一个「相互贯通的关联」所提供的品质，而它明确对立于诠释。**

> It is an interconnected rather than interactive relatedness, a primary quality of presence that provides experience-near attunement, holding, containing, and protection, rather than interpreting patient–analyst relationships and especially patient–analyst separateness.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「The Be(com)ing of a New Dimension」

**（三）Govrin 论 Bollas：抱持性的涵容本身即发挥诠释的功能。**

> Christopher Bollas takes this liberation in a radical direction: the analyst's freedom enables complete devotion to creating a holding environment for patients whose psychic organization is collapsing, with this devoted containment itself functioning as interpretation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第三节「Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**但抱持也不够。** Eshel 在引 Green 时明确指出，在最深的状态中，所需的「远不止于抱持或涵容」；她自己的落点是悲悯性的抱持：

> there is no end unless the bottom of the trough has been reached, unless the thing feared has been experienced in a compassionate holding, within the analyst's "presencing" and interconnecting with the patient's gripping, unbearable agony, devastation, and death.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Concluding Thoughts: Unless the Bottom Has Been Reached」

> **【推断】** 温尼科特本人对 holding 的定义**未出现在本组 raw 文献中**（Govrin 第四章论温尼科特，但 holding 一词在该书仅出现于论 Bollas 与论温尼科特遗产的两处索引性表述；Eshel 两篇中 holding 均作修饰语出现）。因此本节的「亲缘性」论断为【推断】，其文本依据仅是上述三处对 holding 的用法，而非温尼科特的原定义。

## 五、Ferro 与 Civitarese：不甘于不知的技术

Ferro 与 Civitarese 把比昂的这一姿态发展为场域方法论，而且直接使用了济慈的原句：

> Rather than maintaining a position of knowing, the analyst must cultivate the willingness to dwell in uncertainty without irritably reaching after fact and reason (Civitarese, 2023, p. 21).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Technical Implications and Clinical Method」（引 Civitarese, 2023, p. 21）

关键在于，它不只是耐受，而是**主动的信**：

> This requires not only tolerance for not-knowing but active faith that the analytic couple, stumbling together in the dark, might create illumination through their very stumbling.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Technical Implications and Clinical Method」

遐想在此从「分心」翻转为工作的本质：

> The analyst's reverie becomes not a distraction from the work but its very essence—the medium through which beta elements are transformed into alpha elements that can be thought and dreamed

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Ferro & Di Donna, 2005, p. 93）

而诠释的成功标准也随之改变——它不安装真理，它培育做梦的条件：

> Civitarese emphasizes that successful interpretation does not install truth, but rather nurtures conditions for further dreaming (Civitarese, 2023, p. 26). The most effective interpretations, in this view, are often those that interpret nothing directly but instead create conditions for new meanings to emerge.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Ethical and Philosophical Dimensions」

Ferro 一侧的对应表述，是**未饱和诠释**——其反面被命名为一种智性暴力：

> This technical approach reflects his belief that premature interpretive closure—what he describes as attempts to reveal true truth—functions as a form of intellectual violence that forecloses possibility rather than opening it

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Ferro's Theoretical Foundation」（参 Ferro, 2006b, p. 1）

以及这一立场的伦理内核——**分析师必须冒险**：

> the analyst must risk not knowing, risk being changed by the encounter, and risk discovering that carefully cultivated theoretical frameworks might prove inadequate in the face of this particular person's suffering.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Ethical and Philosophical Dimensions」

## 六、区分：负性能力不是什么

| 被混淆者 | 与负性能力的差别 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 无知 / 没听懂 | 负性能力是**主动悬置**可用的知识，而非缺乏知识 | 「holding these tools lightly, ready to set them aside」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章） |
| 诠释节制（timing） | 节制关乎**何时**说；负性能力关乎**是否以知晓为姿态** | 「not the knowledge arrived at ... but the patient's experience of arriving at understanding」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章） |
| 被动 / 无为 | 它是高强度的接收状态，需要纪律与准备 | 「a capacity that must be cultivated through discipline—the systematic suspension of memory, desire, and understanding」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章） |
| 抱持（holding） | 抱持偏环境供给的持续性；负性能力偏分析师的克制 | 见本页第四节；**本区分为【推断】** |
| 涵容（containment） | 涵容是**机制**（β→α 的转化）；负性能力是使该机制得以运作的**姿态** | 见 [[concepts/concept-containment-reverie-alpha]] |

## 七、与相邻概念的联络

- **与 O**：负性能力是接近 O 的姿态条件；O 是负性能力所朝向者。Govrin 把二者直接扣在一起（见本页第一节）。
- **与遐想／α功能**：分析师若处在「急躁地追求事实与理性」的状态，接收通道即关闭，遐想无从发生。见 [[concepts/concept-containment-reverie-alpha]]。
- **与顶点（vertex）**：负性能力要求分析师**松动自己的顶点**——不预先知道该从哪个立足点看。见 [[concepts/concept-vertex]]。
- **与未饱和诠释（unsaturated interpretation）**：负性能力在言语层面的直接产物。Ferro 的表述见本页第五节。
- **与同在（withness）**：Eshel 的同在把「不急躁」推到极致——分析师不是等待理解，而是**在其内与之共苦**。见 [[concepts/concept-withness]]。
