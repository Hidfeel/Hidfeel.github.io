# O 与转化（O, transformation O→K, at-one-ment）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]、[[raw/eshel-withness]]、[[raw/ogden-ontological-psychoanalysis]]

## 摘要

**O** 是比昂（Wilfred Bion）晚期最晦涩也最核心的符号，指分析情境中的**终极实在**——不可知、不可被直接把握、只能通过「成为」而非「知晓」来接近之物。与它配套的另外两个术语是 **at-one-ment**（与 O 合一）与 **transformation O→K**（从 O 到可被言说的知识 K 的转化）。三者的共同主张是：分析的目标不是让病人获得关于自己的知识，而是让分析师与病人一道**成为**那个实在。

本页处理四层内容：O 的定义与「不可知」的严格含义；从 K 到 O 的重心转移及其技术要求（放弃记忆、欲望、理解）；at-one-ment 与宗教性 atonement 的刻意区分；以及 O→K 在两处当代临床中的用法——Ogden 把这一转向纳入他所谓的**本体论精神分析**（ontological psychoanalysis），Eshel 则把她的「同在（withness）」明确下探至 Vermote 三分模型中的第三区，即「O 中的转化」。

**必须先行标注一点**：经 Grep 逐字核实，作为比昂术语的 `O`、`at-one-ment`、`containment`、`enactment`、`projective identification` 在 Ogden 的 *Ontological Psychoanalysis* 全篇中**均未出现**（命中数为 0）。Ogden 是用他自己的词汇——存在状态、做梦、遐想、"at one"——表述同一转向的。因此本页第五节对 Ogden 的处理，是【综合】而非【原文】。

## 一、O 是什么：不是待解码的内容

Govrin 给出的定义性表述如下：

> O represents the thing-in-itself of the analytic encounter, the ultimate reality, or "invariance"—the unknowable essence that underlies all psychoanalytic phenomena but can never be directly apprehended

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O and the Limits of Language in Bionian Interpretation」（参 Sandler, 2005, p. 178）

与传统精神分析概念的关键差别，在于 O **刻意保留神秘**：

> Unlike traditional psychoanalytic concepts that seek to make the unconscious conscious, O deliberately preserves mystery as an essential element of human experience. It is not something to be discovered or decoded, but rather approached with what Bion calls "awe" and "reverence"—emotional states that acknowledge the limits of rational understanding while remaining open to transformation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Govrin 在全书导论处的概括，把 O 与负性能力直接扣在一起：

> His concept of "O"—the unknowable ultimate reality of emotional experience—challenged analysts to abandon their interpretive certainties in favor of what he called "negative capability."

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，「The Organization of This Book」

## 二、从 K 到 O：成为（becoming）取代知晓（knowing）

比昂用 **Klink** 一词标记传统的认识论工作域：

> Traditional interpretation operates within what Bion calls the "Klink"—the domain of knowing, learning, and understanding. The analyst gathers information about the patient's history, observes patterns, applies theoretical frameworks, and offers interpretations designed to increase insight.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

对这一工作域的诊断是：它「熟悉得令我们安心」，而这份安心恰恰是障碍：

> This approach, while valuable, ultimately remains trapped within the realm of what can be consciously grasped and verbally articulated. There is something deeply familiar about this mode of working—it satisfies our need to understand, to make sense, to feel competent in our professional role. Yet Bion suggests that this very familiarity may be precisely what prevents us from encountering the more elusive dimensions of psychic reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

转移本身不是技术调整，而是注意力的重新定向：

> The movement from K to O represents more than a technical adjustment; it constitutes a fundamental reorientation of analytic attention. Where K seeks to know about the patient, O requires that we become present with what the patient is becoming.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> Bion's approach requires a fundamental shift from K to O—from knowing to being. Bion thought that the analyst's concern is not with what can be known about the patient but with what the patient is becoming in the present moment

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1967）

在语言的层面，这一转移有具体的可辨认形式。Govrin 给了一对对照句：

> Consider the difference between saying "You seem angry because your mother didn't validate your needs" and saying "Something fierce is stirring in the room right now." The first interpretation operates within the Klink, connecting present experience to past understanding through causal explanation. The second attempts to stay closer to O, acknowledging the emergence of something that cannot yet be fully grasped or explained.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

其技术后果是**不饱和的诠释**：

> These less saturated interpretations can create space for the patient's own language to emerge, allowing them to approach their experience without the constraint of predetermined meanings.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 三、at-one-ment：与 O 合一，不是赎罪

比昂的 hyphen 是刻意的。Govrin 明确点出这一点：

> This understanding leads us to examine one of Bion's most profound concepts: atonement, or at-one-ment. The hyphenated spelling is deliberate, emphasizing not religious expiation but a state of being at one with ultimate reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

比昂原句：

> The central postulate is that atonement with ultimate reality, or O, as I have called it to avoid involvement with existing associations, is essential to harmonious mental growth

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1967, p. 145）

与传统的洞见／理解目标相比，这是另一类目标：

> Rather than achieving knowledge about unconscious conflicts or developmental failures, the analytic process seeks to achieve a state of being that harmonizes with reality as it actually is, rather than as we wish it to be or fear it might be.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

那句被反复引用的语法创新，把「knowing」换成「being」：

> Reality cannot be known but must be "been"—like potatoes that can be grown or eaten but not sung. The analyst must shift from knowing about the patient's reality to being with it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

Eshel 引比昂的原句，是这一主张最硬的文本支点——分析师与 O 之间，**认同都不够**：

> With this [O] the analyst cannot be identified; he must *be* it

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27，原文着重）

Eshel 的转述与她自己的用法：

> Later on, Bion (1970) goes further and presents his profound conception of at-one-ment and the analyst's being and becoming O, the unknown and unknowable, ultimate emotional reality of the patient

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」

> I wish to propose here the notion of *psychoanalytic* "*withness*"—the being there, within the experience of suffering, becoming at-one with it, in deep patient–analyst interconnectedness.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」

## 四、transformation O→K：被唤起，而非被回忆

Eshel 借 Vermote 的三区模型，为 O→K 提供了本组文献中最清晰的坐标。**第二区**正是涵容—遐想—α功能所在的层次：

> 2. Transformation in Knowledge—container-contained, reverie, dream-work, alpha-function (Bion, Marty, de M'Uzan, Bollas, Botella and Botella, Ogden, Ferro);

— Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」（引 Vermote, 2013）

**第三区**才是 O 的地盘：

> 3. Transformation in O, when dealing with the most non- thought, unknown, undifferentiated zone of psychic functioning (Winnicott, Milner, late Bion, late Lacan). Rather than epistemological exploration (knowing), the focus here is on the unknown and unknowable, ultimate, emotional reality-O, the primacy of being at-one with the patient's emotional reality, and a lived, new experience.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」（引 Vermote, 2013）

在此之下，比昂原句的确切含义才显现出来：

> For me, it is in this zone of analytic functioning that Bion's (1970) radical words acquire their full meaning: "at-one-ment with it [O] and evolution.... In practice this means *not* that the analyst recalls some relevant memory but that a relevant constellation will *be evoked during the process of* *at-one-ment with O*, the process denoted by transformation O → K"

— Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」（引 Bion, 1970, pp. 30, 33，原文着重）

**这是 O→K 最要紧的一点**：分析师不是回忆起某段相关的记忆，而是让一个相关的星座**在合一的过程中被唤起**。记忆属于 K，被唤起属于 O→K。

Eshel 的自我定位随之明确：

> In my view, the emerging dimension of the analyst's "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness that I am proposing reaches down to this third, most undifferentiated and fundamental analytic functioning and transformation in O.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」

而这一维度**超越主体间性**：

> "Presencing" and interconnectedness go beyond the level of interactions and patient–analyst relationships (object or subject relationships), even beyond intersubjectivity, to offer the opportunity for getting in touch with basic (environmental) relatedness and formative experiences of being and becoming

— Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」

在分析师一侧，O 中的转化有另一个名字。Eshel 引 Grotstein：

> the necessity for the analyst to experience his own inner version of what the analysand is suffering from; to "*become*" the analysand's anguish and agony. Bion (1967, 1970) termed this phenomenon "transformation in O within the analyst."

— Eshel, *Withness*，「Compassion」（引 Grotstein, 2010, p. 25，原文着重）

## 五、Ogden 如何把这一转向纳入本体论精神分析

**【综合，附缺席标注】** 如上所述，Ogden 全篇未出现 `O`、`at-one-ment` 等词。但他把同一转向命名为**本体论精神分析**，并把比昂列为其两位主要缔造者之一：

> A radical change has occurred, rather unobtrusively, in the theory and practice of psychoanalysis in the course of the past 70 years, a change for which, until recently, I have not had a name. That transformation involves a shift in emphasis from epistemological (pertaining to knowing and understanding) psychoanalysis to ontological (pertaining to being and becoming) psychoanalysis.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章「Epistemological and Ontological Psychoanalysis」

> I view Freud and Klein as the founders of a form of psychoanalysis that is epistemological in nature, and I consider Winnicott and Bion as the principal contributors to the development of ontological psychoanalysis

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章

> As I read Bion, throughout his opus, he is principally an ontological thinker.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章「Bion's Contributions to」

Ogden 用他自己的词汇重述了「从 K 到 O」：

> being has supplanted understanding; the analyst does not come to know or understand or comprehend or apprehend the reality of what is happening in the session, he "intuits" it, he becomes "at one" with it, he is fully present in experiencing the present moment.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章

> because memory is what we think we know based on what no longer exists, and is no longer knowable. And we must renounce "desires for results, 'cure,' or even understanding"

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三章（引 Bion, 1967, p. 137）

治疗作用的对应定义也落在同一处：

> therapeutic action characterizing ontological psychoanalysis involves providing an interpersonal context in which forms of experiencing, states of being, come to life in the analytic relationship that were previously unimaginable by the patient

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一章

Govrin 在第七章对 Ogden 的转述，明确把两者接上：

> For Bion, analysts don't come to know what's happening in sessions but "intuit" it, becoming "at one" with it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节「From Knowing to Being—The Ontological Turn」

以及那句被 Govrin 反复引用的结论：

> Our interpretations matter not because they reveal hidden truths but because they create new possibilities for being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节

> **【推断】** 「Ogden 的本体论精神分析 = 比昂的 O 之转向」这一等式，在本组 raw 文献中**没有直接文本支撑**：Ogden 从未使用 O 这一符号，Govrin 也只在转述 Ogden 时使用 "at one"（动词形式）而未使用 O。两者在结构上同构（知晓→存在／生成），但是否可视为同一主张，需另行取证。

## 六、O 与诠释：诠释是 O 演化中的实际事件

比昂把诠释从「关于实在的陈述」改写为「实在演化中的事件」：

> The clinical implications become clear when Bion states that "the interpretation is an actual event in an evolution of O that is common to analyst and analysand" (Bion, 1970, p. 27). This transforms our understanding of what happens when we speak in the consulting room. The interpretation does not describe or explain reality—it participates in reality's evolution. It is not a statement about truth but an enactment of truth's emergence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

会谈内的表述与会谈外的表述因此**价值不同**：

> This is why Bion insists that "formulations of the events of analysis made in the course of analysis must possess value different from that of formulations made extra-sessionally" ... The living interpretation, offered in the heat of the analytic moment, conducts transformation in O rather than mere knowledge about O.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 26）

这解释了临床上的一个常见现象：

> This distinction helps to explain why interpretations that seem theoretically correct can fall completely flat, while others that emerge spontaneously from the analyst's state of at-one-ment can transform both participants. The difference lies not in the content of the words but in the quality of being from which they emerge.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

以及成为 O 之后的认识论后果：

> The analyst who achieves this state discovers that "in so far as the analyst becomes O he is able to know the events that are evolutions of O"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

支撑这一切的是**信（faith）**，而信的对象不是任何具体命题：

> But this "becoming" requires a particular discipline—what Bion calls faith, "faith that there is an ultimate reality and truth—the unknown, unknowable, 'formless infinite'"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 31）

代价则是悖论耐受与循环往复：

> As Bion observed, drawing on the mystic tradition, those who have contacted O must develop a capacity to tolerate paradoxes.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> the discoverer must be prepared to find that he has started another round of group oscillations. Persecution↔Depression

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1997, p. 285）

## 七、谱系：遴选事实（selected fact）如何演化为 O

O 不是凭空出现的。Govrin 给出了它的前身：

> Although Bion stopped writing explicitly about the selected fact after 1965, this does not mean he abandoned the concept. The selected fact evolved and developed into the concept of O, which made its debut in "Transformations." The connection between these concepts is profound: the selected fact is present indirectly in his concept of O, and contact with O is really the only way one could possibly intuit the selected fact.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

遴选事实的原初定义， borrowed from Poincaré：

> The analyst, like Poincaré's mathematician confronting disparate mathematical elements, searches for that which might suddenly "introduce order where the appearance of disorder reigned"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（参 Bion, 1962）

而它与诠释的分工，预示了后来 K／O 的分工：

> The selected fact belongs to the realm of discovery—it is something the patient must find for themselves ... Interpretation, however, serves a different master: repair rather than discovery.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

## 八、评估标准的转移

若目标从洞见变为 at-one-ment，那么「分析是否成功」的判据也必须随之改变：

> Progress cannot be measured simply in terms of symptom reduction or increased self- understanding, though these may certainly occur. Instead, the analyst must attend to subtler indicators: the patient's growing capacity to tolerate uncertainty, their increasing ability to remain present with difficult emotions, their developing faith in the possibility of truth beyond immediate understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：与 O 接触时，分析师被唤起的究竟是**其自身的主体性过去经验与记忆**，还是**其在病人主观现实中的嵌入**？
> **A 方立场**：Ogden（经 Eshel 转述）强调分析师自身的主体性过去经验与记忆——「I differ from Ogden in the emphasis that he places on the analyst's own subjective past experiences and memories, which are evoked in the analyst's mind in the session.」（Eshel, *Withness*，「What Kind of Analytic Presence and "Withness" Would That Be?」）
> **B 方立场**：Eshel 伸向分析师嵌入病人的主观现实——「I reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality, especially as a necessary, functioning presence.」（Eshel, *Withness*，同节）
> **备注**：**不可通约为同一技术指令**。两者共享「移情与反移情不是彼此回应而生的分离实体，而是单一主体间性整体之诸面向」这一前提（Eshel 明言与 Ogden 共享此点），但在分析师的着力点上分道。此分歧同时是 [[concepts/concept-withness]] 与 [[concepts/concept-analytic-third]] 的核心分界线。注意：本条 A 方立场系 Eshel **转述** Ogden，Ogden 原文中并无对应原句，故 A 方为【综合】。

## 九、术语位置速查

| 术语 | 含义 | 与之相对者 |
|---|---|---|
| O | 终极实在、不可知之物；只能「成为」不能「知晓」 | K（可知之域）／Klink |
| K / Klink | 知晓、学习、理解的域；传统诠释在其中运作 | O |
| at-one-ment | 与 O 合一（**非**宗教性的 atonement 赎罪） | 关于 O 的知识 |
| transformation O→K | 从 O 到可被言说之知识的转化；分析师**不回忆**而**被唤起** | 回忆、联想、理论套用 |
| transformation in O within the analyst | 分析师内部的 O 中转化（Grotstein 语） | 反移情诠释 |
| 遴选事实（selected fact） | O 的前身；属发现域，非修复域 | 因果解释（cause） |
