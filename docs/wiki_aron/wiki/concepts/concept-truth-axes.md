# 真理轴（truth axes）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/yadlin-gadot-truth-axes]]

## 摘要

施洛米特·亚德林-加多特（Shlomit Yadlin-Gadot）在《真理轴与自体的转化》（*Truth Axes and the Transformation of Self*，2017）中提出的核心创见是：**精神分析中的诸种真理不是彼此竞争的候选答案，而是心灵的组织原则**。她把每一种真理概念设想为心灵中的一个组织原则，并构成其"确定性的阿基米德点（Archimedean point of certainty）"；每一条这样的原则拣选并组织知觉、情感与思想中的离散元素，生成一个**独特的现实意象**，以及栖居其中的**可定义的自体（self hood）**。她称之为"真理轴（truth axes）"。

这一构想建立在一次根本翻转之上：**真理满足的不是"事态（states of affairs）"，而是"心境（states of mind）"**。真理问题的根源是心理性的——人需要确定性、安全与消除怀疑。由此，作者在她所谓的三个秩序（实在、命题、意识约束）之外加入第四个秩序：**作为思考者的主体的心理需要**。

临床上，治疗目标从"判定何者为真"转为**让分析二元不坍缩进单一轴**，并促成轴间运动的"流动"而非"受阻"；分析者的权威则由"知真理"转为**认识论专长（epistemic expertise）**。本页是 [[concepts/concept-truth-types]] 的上位框架页。

## 一、起点：从"真理是一"到真理的碎裂

作者把弗洛伊德定位为实在论认识论的锚：他坚持精神分析是科学、真理是一。此后认识论从实在论滑向主观唯心论，单一的对应真理碎裂为多重真理：

> Freud adhered to the idea that psychoanalysis is a science and that truth is one. The transition from a realistic epistemology to an epistemology of subjective idealism in psychoanalytic thought was accompanied by the splintering of the “one” realistic truth into a multiplicity of truths: realistic-correspondent, ideal, subjective- existential, intersubjective, coherent, and pragmatic truths

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

本文不打算在这些认识论之间做取舍，而是**为多元认识论与多元真理开辟理论空间**。学派之争被诊断为：各家其实都承认多种真理在起作用，分歧在于赋值与对过程的解释：

> What is clear from this short discussion is that analysts from different psychoanalytic schools acknowledge the different truths that influence mental life generally and analysis particularly. But they ascribe their value differently, often as dictated by their epistemological convictions, and interpret differently the processes that are set in motion when pur- suing a particular truth

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理概念与精神分析理论」／「真理与认识论的关系及价值问题」

## 二、核心翻转：真理作为需要（truth as need）——满足的是心境而非事态

这是全文最关键的一步。作者明确宣称本文要"把真理描绘为一种按照人对确定性的需要而运作的心理动力学"：

> this paper will depict truth as a psychic dynamic that functions in accord with the human need for certainty as it expresses itself across critical dimensions of the subject’s life

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

哲学史的追问被判定为：真理问题的答案是**心理学性质的**。

> The vital continuance of the search for truth begs the question of its intractability. What drives this search? What are its origins? The history of philosophical thought suggests that the answer to this question is clearly of a psychological nature: Human beings have a need for certainty, security, control, and the elimination of doubt

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

> Contrary to naive intuition, and many philosophical positions, this implies that what truth satisfies are not states of affairs, but rather states of mind. If until now the search for truth involved three orders—reality, propositions, and the constraints of human consciousness—we now add a fourth order: that of the psychologi- cal needs of the subject-as-thinker

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

拉康（Jacques Lacan）的格言被用来点明视角的转移：

> This is neatly captured in the clever aphorism proposed by Jacques Lacan (1998): For the physi- cist, where there is smoke there is fire, but for the psychoanalyst where there is smoke there is a smoker

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

> The need for truth is an overarching need for certainty and it expresses itself across the different dimensions of the subject’s life

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

作者用四条现代路线论证"对真理的执着具有心理学性质"：尼采（求真由恐惧驱动）、美国实用主义（真理是改善人类福祉的手段）、海德格尔（真理反映人控制世界、尤其是控制死亡的需要）、列维纳斯与德里达（把形而上学与暴力关联，真理是虚幻纯洁性的范式例证）。

> Nietzsche’s (1886) critique of metaphysics is accepted as a prime modern origin of the line of thought that led to the dismantling of our naive belief in metaphysics inasmuch as it aspires for ab- solute truth on the one hand and explores the immense human attachment to this absolute truth on the other. In Nietzsche’s view, the quest for truth is driven by our fears

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

## 三、六种范式真理及其哲学来源

> In a comprehensive review of the history of the idea of truth, I identify six paradigmatic notions of truth (Yadlin-Gadot, 2016): Correspondent, Coherent, Ideal, Intersubjective, Subjective-Exis- tential, and Pragmatic truths (for clarity, I shall denote them with capital letters)

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

| 真理类型 | 哲学源头（原文标注） | 认识论归属 |
|---|---|---|
| 对应真理（Correspondent truth） | Aristotle (350 BCE) | 实在论（realistic epistemology） |
| 融贯真理（Coherent truth） | Spinoza (1677)、Hempel (1935) | 唯心论（idealistic epistemology） |
| 理想真理（Ideal truth） | Plato (380 BCE)、Russell (1961) | 客观唯心论（objective idealism） |
| 主体间真理（Intersubjective truth） | Husserl (1983)、Orange/Stolorow/Atwood (1998) | 主体间认识论 |
| 主观-存在真理（Subjective-Existential truth） | Kierkegaard、Elleray (2007) | 主观唯心论（subjective idealism） |
| 实用真理（Pragmatic truth） | James (1907, 1912) | 实用主义 |

**对应真理**——真值由命题与外部、独立于心灵的事实之间的对应关系确定：

> The Correspondent conception of truth, introduced by Ar- istotle (350 b ce), maintains that truth values are established by correspondence between a proposition and an external mind- independent fact. Correspondent truth is anchored in a realistic epistemology that posits the subject’s ability to know the mind- independent world by means of his or her senses

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

**融贯真理**——真值由某一信念与整个信念系统的相容性决定，并导致真理的"本质性软化"（只在**程度上**为真）：

> The Coherent conception of truth as, for example, in Spinoza’s thinking (1677), determines truth values by examining the com- patibility of a belief with a whole system of beliefs. A coherent theory of truth, rooted in an idealistic epistemology, leads to an “essential softening” of truth. A belief is true to the degree that it coheres with other beliefs

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

**理想真理**——柏拉图式，真值由具体信念或经验个案与相应的永恒法度/形式（eternal decrees or forms）的相容性决定：

> The Ideal conception of truth, as in Plato’s (380 b ce) thought, determines truth values by the compatibility between particular beliefs or empirically perceived cases and their corresponding eternal decrees or forms. It is situated in the realm of “objective idealism” that claims the existence of a mind-independent order inaccessible to the senses and partially known by means of labori- ous mental processes

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

**主体间真理**——客观性由人际一致（明示或更多时候默会）所确立：

> The Intersubjective conception of truth regards objectivity as established by interpersonal agreement, sometimes explicit, often implicit. This truth reflects the logic of intersubjective epistemol- ogies, whereby the world achieves its true transcendence through the presence of a foreign subjectivity

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

**主观-存在真理**——真理被视作高度个人化的、体现主体所体验到的本真性（authenticity），经验先于任何本质或概括：

> The Subjective-Existential conception of truth, seeds of which can be found in the work of Kierkegaard, perceives truth as highly personal, embodying the subject’s experienced authenticity. Here, experience is prior to any essence or generalization

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

**实用真理**——关注一个观念在信奉者生活脉络中的有用性；真理是过程性的：

> Pragmatic truth concerns the usefulness and practicality of an idea in the context of the believer’s life. A true belief is one that has been proven valid by the compatibility between its pre- dictions and its results. Here, truth is process based, and tomor- row, today’s truth may be no more than an opinion

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「六种真理概念」

认识论与真理相互关联却不等同：每种认识论"自然产生"一种主导真理（实在论认识论是对应真理的自然家园），但在临床上**只偏爱那对它而言原初的那一种**：

> Epistemologies may recognize various truths, but they favor the truth that is primary to them. In the context of psychoanalysis, this valuing of truths is reflected in the definition of therapeutic objectives and methodologies

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理与认识论的关系及价值问题」

## 四、什么是「轴」：四者结合构成一条轴

从真理概念到真理轴的关键一步，是把四个要素绑定为一个整体：

> Truth axes are posited as organiz- ing principles of the psyche aimed at achieving stable images of reality across critical dimensions of the subject’s life. Personality and experience render some axes dominant, while others remain foreclosed and dissociated

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

> I will present each of these truths as reflecting an organizing principle in the psyche and as constitut- ing its Archimedean point of certainty. Each organizing principle selects and organizes disparate elements of perception, emotion, and thought, and enforces certain rules of combination among them. Every organization of this kind produces a distinct image of reality, together with a definable self hood that resides within it. These images sometimes overlap but are often incompatible and generate tensions within the psyche. I term these organiza- tional principles “Truth Axes.”

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

因为需要是复数的，世界意象也就必然是复数的——每一意象只捕获经验的一个可能而不完整的侧面，其"有机的真理"充当其中的确定性之点：

> But as we have various needs we form various images of the world in accordance with them. Thus, we remain with different needs rendering their “inter- pretation”’ of the world that do not necessarily overlap or cohere. Each image of reality captures a possible, yet incomplete, aspect of experience. Its organic truth serves as a point of certainty within it. In each reality resides a characteristic self-state that is guided, directed, and motivated by its truth

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「从真理概念到真理轴」

严格定义如下——**认识论预设＋特征性自体状态＋现实意象＋被体验的真理**，四者结合即构成一条轴：

> The joining together of epistemic assumption, characteristic self-state, image of reality, and experienced truth form a truth axis. In that sense, a truth axis is a multidimensional mental domain

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「从真理概念到真理轴」

轴并非只在高层的世界观层面运作，而是贯穿从感觉到人生观的**全部心理活动层级**：

> The truth axes, their products, and their motivational forces exist on all levels of mental activity, from basic experiences of sensation and emotion, to ground-level representations (memory, imagination, etc.), and then to outlooks on life, both intuitive and formulated (e.g., in language). They give rise to the subject’s interpretations of the world, as well as to his or her experience of himself or herself

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「从真理概念到真理轴」

## 五、六轴的需要根源与必须克服的发展危机

> Each organizational principle provides for a deep emotional need that motivates its formulation. These needs unify each axis in a particular logic and motivate its development

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

| 轴 | 需要根源 | 生成的现实 | 发展挑战（须克服的危机） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 对应轴 | 与外部现实保持接触以提升生存机会 | "事实的"现实，依赖感官信息、观察与掌握现实约束 | 须能建立内在现实对立于外在现实，区分并比较二者（Freud, 1923） |
| 融贯轴 | 信念、期待与行为倾向之间的相容与和谐；身份、连续性与规律性 | 在个人身份与被感知的外部性两个层面上的凝聚一致的现实 | 首先须有"在思想之间建立连结"的能力——比昂的"link"；须对抗以"让思想保持分裂与混乱"来消解痛苦的防御倾向 |
| 理想轴 | 对完美与完整、对赋予指导与意义者的需要；克服可厌、可憎与任意之物 | 我们所向往并信仰的现实，提供指导与动机 | 须曾经历允许正向镜像与理想化、并能被内化为人格功能的关系（Kohut） |
| 主观-存在轴 | 忠诚于自己，把自己置于中心舞台作为规划人生与决定意义的标准 | 私人的、常常隐藏的真理所构成的主体-本真现实 | 依赖使"真"自体得以生成与存在的复杂过程；唯有相对这一本真自体，存在-本真之轴才能形成（Winnicott, 1975） |
| 实用轴 | 按未来收益组织经验；感到自己在达成目标上是有效的；体现主动施为，克服无助感 | 容纳我们的目标与利益的现实意象；我们在其中作为主动的、发起性的施动者存在 | 需要灵活的良心（按自利原则行事）；须曾有"影响现实"的体验，加上功能弹性与处理模糊性的能力（James, 1907） |
| 主体间轴 | 与周遭之人的联结、共享现实的深刻需要 | 我们与周遭之人共享的现实 | 需要一个"其存在与构成现实的能力等同于自己"的主体在场；须放弃海洋性自恋、把他人从客体地位释放出来、确立其为主体（Benjamin 承 Winnicott 1969 与黑格尔主奴辩证法） |

**各轴生成的现实意象（原文逐条）**——表中"生成的现实"一栏的出处：

> The Correspondent truth axis creates a “factual” reality, en- abling us to negotiate the mind-independent reality around us. In this reality we rely heavily on sense information, observe, and try to master the realistic constraints in which we live our lives. The Coherent truth axis produces for us a cohesive and consistent reality at the level of both personal identity and perceived exter- nality. This axis often functions to downplay the significance of facts and tendencies that do not cohere with one’s core beliefs about self and world. The Ideal truth axis embodies a reality to which we aspire and in which we believe. It exists within us and at times outside of us. We move toward its realization in many things we do and find guidance within it. The Pragmatic truth axis cre- ates an image of reality that accommodates our objectives and interests. Here, we may plan and decide in ways that determine what may best enhance our well-being. We exist within it as active, initiating agents. The Subjective-Existential truth axis produces a person’s subjective-authentic image of reality, containing his or her private, often hidden truths. Finally, the Intersubjective truth axis creates the reality we share with those around us

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

**各轴需要根源（原文逐条）**：

> At the root of the Correspondent axis is the need to be in touch with external reality so as to enhance the chances of survival, through explaining, responding to, and anticipating events that influence the exchanges between a person and the mind-indepen- dent world around him or her

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> At the root of the Coherent axis of truth is the need for compat- ibility and harmony among a person’s beliefs, expectations, and behavioral tendencies. Coherence allows the smooth transition among the various activities that the subject is engaged with and allows him or her to create a sense of identity, continuity, and regularity across time

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> Humanity’s need for perfection and completeness, for that which bestows guidance and meaning, and allows overcoming the repulsive, the abhorred, and the arbitrary, is at the root of the Ideal axis of truth. Ideal truths give people a sense of harmony and beauty, serving as both directives and sources of motivation

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> The Subjective-Existential axis arises in the subject’s need to be loyal to himself or herself, to be positioned at center stage as a criterion for planning his or her life and determining meaning without subordinating himself or herself to a universal concept of subject or truth

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> The organization of experience in terms of future benefit is the basis for the Pragmatic axis of truth. This axis addresses the need to feel effective in one’s ability to achieve one’s goals in the world. It embodies active agency and overcomes the experience of helplessness and inevitability

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> At the root of the Intersubjective axis of truth is the profound need for a connection with those around us and a shared reality with them

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

**发展挑战（原文三例）**：

> The establishment of a Coherent axis demands, first and fore- most, the ability to connect between thoughts. A key concept here is Bion’s (1970) “link,” central in his theory of the development of thought. Linking concerns the ability to connect between a thing and a thought and between one thought and another. Linking is possible when an individual is prepared and able to fight against a defensive tendency to annul pain by leaving thoughts split and in disarray

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> The establishment of an Intersubjective axis demands the pres- ence of a subject whose existence and ability to constitute real- ity is equivalent to one’s own. Jessica Benjamin, following Win- nicott (1969) and the dialectic of Hegel’s (1807) Master and Slave, formulates the complex emotional process required in order to renounce oceanic narcissism, release the other from his or her status as object, and establish him or her as a subject

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

> A Pragmatic axis of truth necessitates a flexible conscience that allows a person to behave in accordance with principles of self-benefit. In order for this axis to exist it is essential to have had the experience of influencing reality, together with functional flexibility and the ability to deal with vagueness

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、心理需要与发展挑战」

## 六、诸轴如棱镜并存：语法差异、主导—封禁、情境激活

**（一）棱镜式并存**：

> The various truth axes coexist in consciousness as prisms through which vari- ous realities are construed, each giving rise to a different experi- ence of self and world

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

诸轴不只生成不同的世界，也生成**不同的自体**——各自联结着不同的焦虑（羞耻与湮灭、异化与归属、罪疚与满足）：

> The vulnerable subjective self of inner truths, to which experiences of shame, exposure, and fear of annihilation are linked, differs from the matter-of-fact, realistic self of the cor- respondent axis. The self of the Intersubjective axis is the one sup- ported by agreement of its community, or lost within it, the one experiencing anxieties of alienation and questions of belonging- ness. The self that scrutinizes itself against its ideals and senses either guilt or satisfaction is once again a self that differs from the pragmatic one that aims to calculate and understand the ways reality could benefit it

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

**（二）互不取消，各有语法**。作者明确反对关系与后现代作家把自体状态视为"零散、无限、情境性"的立场：

> The various states of the self are not perceived here as sporadic, infinite, or situational, which is the accepted position of many relational and postmodern writers

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

> Rather, the different self-states are understood as subject to gen- eral alignments that correspond to the different truth axes; these alignments are universal and may be defined and described

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

语法差异的具体例证：

> Thus, in the Coherent theory, elements are ordered and checked for truth value not by correspondence to reality, but by inner con- sistency. The Subjective-Existential axis, on the other hand, is not subject to rules of logic. It not only allows for paradoxes, but also establishes itself through the tensions within them. The present is dominant in the Subjective-Existential axis, negligible in the Ideal axis, and is but a step toward the future in the Pragmatic axis of truth. Sense data is pertinent in the Correspondent axis, is irrel- evant for the Ideal axis, and provides a base for common significa- tion in the Intersubjective axis

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

**（三）主导—封禁与情境性激活**：

> The six organizing axes exist in the psychic space of every per- son in different forms and degrees of dominance. Each person may have one or two dominant axes, whose relation to other axes may vary considerably

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

> Every organizing axis can have constructive or pathological expressions but, in both cases, the underlying logic of truth will be apparent. Of course, a person does not always respond to events with the same logic of truth. Different contexts arousing different needs may activate one or another of the axes at particular times. Usually, one lives according to directives of various different truth axes simultaneously

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

**四条共同预设**（从真理概念到真理轴的过渡段）：

> First, one must give up the idea that truth is one and absolute. Second, one must keep in mind that truth is inextricably tied up with the subject and determined by his or her needs. Third, truth is not static; it is not a specific point of reference regarding a particular fact or statement. Instead, it is a dynamic product of a long and gradual process of ordering. Fourth, different notions of truth do not in- validate each other. Each notion relies, in one way or another, on the way that another notion captures a different aspect of reality

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「从真理概念到真理轴」

## 七、临床意涵

### 7.1 治疗目标：不坍缩进单一轴，促成轴间「流动」

> Axes of truth are not conceptualized as resting places, even if they may in fact fulfill such a function from time to time. Rather, the axes are viewed as having the potential to create continuous movement of images and meanings

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴与精神分析临床」

> I suggest that, among other factors, this space may be constituted by the evolving shared ability of the ana- lytic dyad not to gravitate toward and collapse into one particu- lar axis of truth. Instead, patient and analyst develop the ability to analyze the way in which movement between axes is enabled, whether it “flows” or rather gets “blocked.”

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴与精神分析临床」

治疗的具体内容是**描绘诸现实、承认其张力、避免再创伤**：

> In analysis, we can help our patients understand and articulate the different realities they inhabit. It may well be that, while do- ing so, we will discover the tensions and the difficulties that arise from the different intersections, tensions, contradictions, and in- compatibilities among these realities. We grant legitimacy to the various truths, admitting that life is too complex to be accounted for by one reality alone. We avoid retraumatization of our pa- tients who, in the past, may have had their Subjective-Existential truths banned, their actual experiences ignored, their Pragmatic truths dismissed on moral grounds, their Ideal truths scoffed at, and so on

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴与精神分析临床」

### 7.2 分析者的权威：由「知真理」转为认识论专长

> Traditionally, the analyst based his or her authority on a truth which he or she knew, mastered, and employed. The analyst’s au- thority was undermined in the postmodern era by the relinquish- ing of a single truth. I ascribe here the analyst’s authority to his or her epistemic expertise, that is, his or her ability to know the epistemic layout of the psyche, recognize particular configura- tions in it, and make democratic and pluralistic use of a variety of languages

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴与精神分析临床」

分析者**不必中立**，只需觉察自己的偏好与认同：

> The analyst is not expected to be neutral in his or her relation to the different languages, to equally identify with them, or to be devoid of natural preferences and tendencies. The analyst’s chal- lenge consists in being aware of these tendencies and their signifi- cance

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴与精神分析临床」

### 7.3 真理轴是临床直觉的形式化

> The therapist is attentive, even if not consciously, to various kinds of truth. While the therapist listens, he or she automatically and unconsciously considers a number of possible meanings in what is heard

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理轴、诸现实与自体状态」

> The concept of the truth axes is, in a way, a formalization and articulation of part of our intuitive movements as therapists. This movement reflects our intuitive understanding of the way images of reality are constituted

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「结论」

### 7.4 诸理论构成"理论弧"而非竞争对手

> From the perspective I present here, each psychoanalytic theo- ry may be viewed as focused on one or two truths or epistemolo- gies, creating a deep analysis of their evolution, from the level of need to the level of health, pathology, and therapeutic objectives. From this vantage point, the different psychoanalytic theories are not seen as competing, but as creating a theoretical arc that paral- lels the epistemic multiplicity of the mind

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「结论」

同一逻辑落到诸轴自身：它们**互补而非竞争**，各自表达不同的需要与相应的恐惧和欲望。这是"互不取消却常不相容"的正面表述：

> I have stressed that these truths are not in competition with each other. Rather, they are complementary, arising from different needs and expressing their associated dreads and desires

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「结论」

### 7.5 治疗与伦理的合流

> Acknowledging that all truth axes express basic needs and are determined by them draws together the dimensions of the therapeutic and the ethic

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「结论」

> The therapeutic objective here is not necessarily to resolve conflicts. Our work often consists of creating a space that may contain various versions of world, self, and experience, acknowl- edging and recognizing their validity, though they often do not cohere

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「结论」

## 八、三个临床案例

三个案例共享一个方法论前提——**多重发生（multiple happening）**：

> All that we experience in the world is transcribed in our psyche in several discrete registers. Anything we experience is, in that sense, a multiple happening

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「临床例示」

### 8.1 悖论：温尼科特的中年男性（"你在谈论阴茎嫉妒"）

案例直接引自温尼科特《游戏与现实》（1971, pp. 72-73）。温尼科特对一位长程分析陷入僵局的中年男性说：

> I am listening to a girl. I know perfectly well that you are a man but I am listening to a girl, and I am talking to a girl. I am telling this girl: ‘You are talking about penis envy.’

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」（引 Winnicott, 1971, pp. 72-73）

病人在短暂停顿后立刻点出了这一诠释所制造的矛盾——按常理，把男人认作女人只能意味着疯狂：

> After a pause the patient said: ‘If I were to tell someone about this girl I would be called mad’

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」（引 Winnicott, 1971, p. 72）

温尼科特随后把"疯狂"揽到自己身上，从而同时拆毁了第二条对应真理（疯癫的定义）：

> I said: “It was not that you told this to anyone; it is I who see the girl and hear a girl talking, when actually there is a man on my couch. The mad person is myself.”

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」（引 Winnicott, 1971, p. 73）

就对应真理与融贯真理的语法而言，"你是个女孩"字面上是假且无意义的；常规解法是隐喻化或范畴切换。温尼科特**拒绝任何这类"安排"**：

> Winnicott, however, abstains from any such “arrangement,” thus challenging Correspondent logic, which is to say, not everything that is false according to Correspondent truth is false categorically and in all possible ways. In this way, he creates space for the introduction of additional truths into the analytic space

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」

但他**并不放逐对应轴**：

> The authoritarianism of Correspondent truth has been under- mined. Its grammatical rules are no longer all-pervasive in the psyche. However, Winnicott does not try to banish Correspon- dence, only to bring it into dialogue with other axes

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」

> After articulating his paradoxical intervention, Winnicott returns to Correspondence, looking for biographical events that had a role in creating his patient’s conflicts

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」

> Winnicott volunteers to hold on to this “hot potato” of contradic- tion and paradox and later of madness, taking upon himself the risk of moving among truths, a move experienced here as border- ing on madness and reiterating the mother’s madness

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「悖论」

### 8.2 自由行动：西明顿的 M 小姐

> The case cited in Symington’s (1983) “The Analyst’s Act of Free- dom as Agent of Therapeutic Change” illustrates once again the therapeutic effect involved in movement among truth axes, trig- gered by the analyst’s intervention. The case illustrates the po- tency of deconstructing an entrenched, Pragmatic truth by means of Correspondence

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

M 小姐主要沿**实用轴**被建构：她栖居在一个自己无法影响的现实中。西明顿的自由时刻是一个**由对应轴决定的念头**闯入：

> Symington’s moment of freedom involved a Correspondently determined thought that entered his mind: “Why can’t Miss M pay the same as all my other patients?” This thought was “startling” for the analyst, not a part of the “furniture of his mind.” This is mostly because the Correspondent axis was not the one structur- ing the reality of the analytic dyad

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

> What followed the interpretation may actually be understood as an enactment of a Pragmatically related drama

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

治疗效力来自**双重影响**：诠释内容造成的"结构性认识论混乱"，加上分析者自身运动与意图的示范——他示范了"效能"本身：

> The startled and crying patient is engulfed in structural epistemic confusion. But what also exists in the session is the impact of the therapist’s movement and intention. Effectively, the therapist models effectiveness. He is not afraid to act

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

> This combined influence undermines the formerly entrenched Pragmatic truth, and Mary experiences the new possibility of existing as an active agent in the world

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「自由行动」

作者的反讽性提醒：把自由时刻理想化为主体间事件，可能把"平淡、清醒的对应"引入我们深爱的诗学领域。

### 8.3 粘附之网：作者本人的 Bill 案例

Bill，三十多岁，分析中浮现一位有魅力的男性戏剧老师——Bill 从十三岁起与之发展出性关系。作者使用了"虐待（abuse）"一词，效果戏剧性；Bill 缺席两次并提出终止。应作者要求，他写信说明原委——这是本案例中主观-存在真理的直接陈述：

> He wrote me that the relationship with this man was the place he had felt most loved and appreciated in his life. Ever since, no one could share or understand the extent to which degrading this relationship was a degradation of himself, of his judgment, of love itself. I wrote back that I realize that I had thrown the baby out with the bath- water

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「主体间真理与理想真理的粘附之网」

> Bill returned to therapy, and through a prolonged struggle with myself, I wrenched myself from the way I had rejected his Subjective truth by subjecting it to my own truths. The next two years in therapy dealt mostly with his shame and anger, not with the “abuse” but with his environment’s inability to respect the meaning this sexual relationship had held for him. I had to loosen my hold on several truths that had a firm hold on me in order to work in this direction

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「主体间真理与理想真理的粘附之网」

> Acknowledging the Intersubjective and Ideal truths that find such relationships abusive, he could surrender his long-cherished resentment and anger toward people close to him who had objected to this relationship. He no longer felt he had to avoid intimacy in order to avoid this painful negation of his truest feelings, now that they had been acknowledged

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「主体间真理与理想真理的粘附之网」

案例以未回答的问题收尾：

> I write these questions and leave them unanswered. They remain so as testimony to the intensity of conflict among different truths

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「主体间真理与理想真理的粘附之网」

   — Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「真理作为需要」

   — Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言
