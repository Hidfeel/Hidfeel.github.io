# 活现（enactment）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**活现（enactment）** 指分析师与病人**无意识地共同演出**一段关系剧本——分析师在其中不是旁观者，而是**参与者**。它是当代精神分析最重要的范式转变之一：从「分析师观察病人的移情」到「分析师发现自己**已经在其中**」。

这一转变的实质是**无意识沟通的媒介**发生了改变：它以**活过的经验（lived experience）**而非象征表象来传递。因此活现的评价也发生了历史性的反转——它不再主要被当作需要消除的**技术失败**，而成为通往无意识的通道与治疗行动的发生地。与之同步的是**反移情**概念的三级演进：从**干扰**（需要被排除）→ **工具**（用于理解病人内部世界）→ **共同建构**（分析师自身即场域的一部分，且对其中的角色「部分盲视」）。

本页最后处理与奥芙拉·埃谢尔（Ofra Eshel）的关系：她的**临在（presencing / presencing with-in）**概念**正是**针对重度活现（massive acting out, acting in and enactment）而提出的；但与关系取向不同，她在活现中做的事不是诠释分析关系，而是**留在其中**（staying with-in, communing with it）。

## 一、定义：无意识地共同演出

Govrin 给出活现之所以成为关系取向核心的理由——它抓住了传统诠释永远抓不住的东西：

> The concept of enactment has become central to relational psychoanalysis precisely because it captures something that traditional interpretation could never quite grasp: the way unconscious communication occurs not through symbolic representation but through lived experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「Enactment: The Unconscious in Action」

安东尼·巴斯（Anthony Bass, 2003）区分了**小写**的日常活现与**大写 E** 的活现（capital-E Enactments）——后者在一段时间内**占据**整个分析活动：

> Anthony Bass (2003) makes a crucial distinction between ordinary enactments—the quotidian mutual influences that texture every analytic hour—and what he terms capital-E Enactments, those moments when "unconscious psychic elements in patient and analyst mobilize our full, heightened attention and define, and take hold of, analytic activity for periods of time" (p. 657).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Bass, 2003, p. 657）

而活现的**入口**恰恰是分析师的技术失手：

> These Enactments, Bass suggests, often emerge through "mistakes, slips, and blind spots that serve as doors through which the analyst and the patient are transported into realms of personal encounter and self-experience that might otherwise remain inaccessible" (660).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Bass, 2003, p. 660）

【原文】「门（doors）」这个隐喻是本节的关键——它是本批资料中与「通往无意识的皇家大道」最接近的表达式（详见第二节的资料校正）。

最重要的是**分析师的位置**：

> Rather than standing outside the patient's dynamics to interpret them, the analyst is inevitably drawn into enacting complementary roles.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

菲利普·布隆伯格（Philip Bromberg）则从解离角度给出互补定义——活现是**双方**同时被锁进解离过程：

> Enactments represent moments when both analyst and patient become locked into dissociative processes, each defending against "not-me" aspects of experience by unconsciously inducing these aspects in the other.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「The Dissociative Gap as Clinical Reality」

> Bromberg's approach recognizes the enactment as a co-created dissociative field where both participants are simultaneously enacting and disowning aspects of shared emotional reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

## 二、评价的转变：从「治疗失败」到「无意识的媒介」

Govrin 把关系取向的重构列为**四个相互关联的维度**，活现居首，并被直接命名为**无意识沟通的媒介**：

> Through examining four interrelated dimensions of this reconceptualization—enactment as the medium of unconscious communication, dissociation as the structure of psychic organization, the analytic third, and self-disclosure as the vehicle of authentic engagement—we can begin to understand how the relational approach transforms interpretation from an epistemological exercise into an ontological encounter.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章导言

治疗行动因此**不在**于分析师对活现的理解与解释：

> The therapeutic action emerges not from the analyst's capacity to understand and explain these enactments but from both participants' ability to survive and metabolize them together. The most profound therapeutic moments often occur precisely when both participants' defensive organizations temporarily collapse, creating space for something genuinely new to emerge.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

最强的一句出现在 Mitchell 案例的结尾——**共同穿越活现的经验，本身就是诠释工作**：

> The interpretation doesn't explain the enactment; rather, the lived experience of navigating the enactment together, with all its uncertainties and ambiguities, constitutes the interpretive work itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

### 2.1 ⚠️ 资料校正：「皇家大道」的说法在本批资料中**无据**

须如实说明：**「活现是通往无意识的皇家大道（royal road）」这一表述，在 `raw/` 六份文献中检索不到。** 「royal road」在本批资料中共出现三次，全部与**梦**相关：

> "The interpretation of dreams," Freud famously declared, "is the royal road to a knowledge of the unconscious activities of the mind" (Freud, 1900, p. 608).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「The Birth of a Method」

> His vision of interpretation as the "royal road" to the unconscious set both the possibilities and the problems that subsequent thinkers would inherit.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

> The dream is no longer simply the "royal road" to the unconscious but becomes the very mechanism by which reality itself becomes psychically digestible.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（比昂章）

【推断】因此，本页把「评价转变」表述为「从技术失败到**无意识沟通的媒介**」，这是**有引文支撑**的说法；「皇家大道」一说属外部文献的常见转述，在本 Wiki 中**不采用为引文**。

### 2.2 残留的「失败」用法：慢性活现

不过「活现＝停滞」的旧义并未消失。在比昂传统中，卡索拉（Cassorla, 2008）仍把**慢性活现（chronic enactments）**当作需要被理解的胶着状态，尽管其价值已被重估：

> His observations of "chronic enactments"—those seemingly stagnant periods when analyst and patient appear trapped in unproductive collusions—demonstrate how implicit alpha-function operates below the threshold of conscious awareness. During these periods, "unconscious exchanges occur between the dyad, in which the analyst provides implicit alpha-function to the patient, little by little recovering the traumatized parts" (p. 161). Chronic enactments serve multiple functions: avoiding trauma revival, using the analyst as protective shield, employing the analyst's alpha-function, and making time for collaborative restoration of injured mental parts.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Cassorla, 2008）

【综合】同一术语在两种传统中的色调差异值得记录：**比昂—后比昂传统把慢性活现读为「隐性 α 功能的作业现场」；关系取向把活现读为「双方共同存活下来并被代谢之物」。** 二者并非矛盾，而是把活现放在不同的治疗行动模型里。

## 三、与反移情概念的演进关系：干扰 → 工具 → 共同建构

活现之所以可能成为核心概念，前提是**反移情**的地位已经翻转了三次。

**第一级：干扰。** 埃谢尔自述其受训经历，第一任督导的态度是这一阶段的标本：

> Anne-Marie concentrated, for example, on the interpretations and understanding of the here and now of the analytic situation and related to counter- transference issues; my first supervisor had focused exclusively on transference and considered countertransference an impediment to the analytic work.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「BE(COM)ING A PSYCHOANALYST」

**第二级：工具。** 克莱因（Melanie Klein）的名言把反移情限制为**认识自己的工具**，而非认识病人的工具：

> While acknowledging that the patient's projections can profoundly affect the analyst, she maintains a cautious stance: "I have never found that the countertransference has helped me to understand my patient better; if I may put it like this; I have found that it has helped me to understand myself better" (Klein, 2017b, p. 103).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

> Klein criticized contemporary uses of projective identification that focus on analyzing the analyst's feelings, insisting instead that the analyst's central task is to use countertransference only as a tool for understanding the patient's internal world, not as an object of exploration in its own right.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章

**第三级：共同建构。** 刘易斯·阿伦（Lewis Aron, 1992）一句话拆掉了整个权威建筑——分析师不只是受影响，而是**活现的完全参与者**，且对其中的角色**部分盲视**：

> The analyst is not only impacted by his own unconscious process in the formulation of any interpretation, not only a full participant in any analytic enactment, but partially blind to the role he plays in helping the patient to understand both of these processes. (p. 654)

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 654）

其总纲即那句被本 Wiki 反复引用的话：

> The transformation of the relational approach rests on a deceptively simple recognition articulated by Lewis Aron (1992): "interpretation is best thought of as the quintessential container and purveyor of intersubjectivity between patient and analyst" (p. 475).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 475）

【综合】三级演进的内在逻辑是：**反移情先是「噪音」（干扰）→ 再是「信号」（工具）→ 最后是「场域本身的成分」（共同建构）**。活现概念只有在第三级才可能成立——因为只有当分析师的反移情被视为**参与**而非**观察**时，「共同演出」的说法才有意义。

## 四、分析师「醒来」：发现自己已在其中

范式转变的心理实感，是分析师**事后的惊觉**。Govrin 用一句格言概括：

> We might say that every interpretation is a kind of accidental self-portrait, painted while the artist believes he is capturing someone else's likeness.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章导言

Mitchell（1988）则从正面给出了「必须进入」的条件——**不进入，治疗就不真正开始**：

> As Mitchell (1988) noted, unless the analyst affectively enters the patient's relational matrix and discovers themselves within it—becoming charmed by the patient's entreaties, shaped by the patient's projections, and antagonized and frustrated by the patient's defenses—the treatment never fully engages and a certain depth is lost. This formulation challenges traditional notions of analytic neutrality and positions the analyst as an inevitable participant whose very participation becomes the vehicle for therapeutic change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Mitchell, 1988）

「醒来」之后的**叙述冲动本身也可能是防御**——这是 Govrin 最尖锐的一处观察：

> The confidence with which Mitchell later explains his motivations may itself be defensive, turning a moment of genuine uncertainty into a theoretical explanation. These ambiguities are not failures of the relational approach, but rather its most honest acknowledgment: that every interpretation, even those emerging through enactment, remains irreducibly ambiguous, open to multiple meanings that can never be fully resolved.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

### 4.1 唐纳德·斯特恩（Donnel Stern）的案例：以耐受自己的羞耻换取新知觉

> Both were locked in a mutual enactment—she presenting an impossible perfection while he absorbed the shame of professional inadequacy. The breakthrough came not through a brilliant interpretation but through Stern's authentic recognition of the patient's underlying loneliness—a recognition that emerged from his willingness to tolerate his own shame and inadequacy long enough for a new perception to form.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Stern, 2009）

> The interpretation was co-created through the relational process rather than delivered from outside it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

### 4.2 埃谢尔的案例：一次完整的「撤出—察觉—返回」序列

埃谢尔第二年的片段是「醒来」过程最完整的临床记录。先是**无法承受的厌恶与撤出**：

> although he had already recounted far more violent and bizarre scenes—perhaps because of this inundating, wretched cheapness. I was thinking to myself: What is the point of all the hard work, of this entire analysis, the great investment of money and years? Better go to a whore every day, get beaten up for five minutes, pay 100 shekels, and be done with it. And I withdrew, fell silent. Then I noticed that he became very agitated, in sharp contrast to his former quiet and detached manner.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「CLINICAL ILLUSTRATION」

【原文校勘】该句前半在源文件中被 PDF 页眉碎片污染，作「the first time in this analysis, I felt great tiredness and **For** disgust」——句首的 "For" 被转换器移位至 "and" 与 "disgust" 之间。为免讹误，本页引文自 "although" 起录，并在正文以中文转述前半句：**分析师在本次分析（四年余）中首次感到巨大的疲倦与厌恶**，尽管病人此前叙述过更暴力、更怪异的场景——「也许是因为这种淹没性的、可鄙的廉价」。

随后是**察觉**——病人已经知道她撤出了：

> And I realized he sensed and knew that I had *abandoned* him, left him wounded and lost on the battlefield, and had gone off to save myself.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「CLINICAL ILLUSTRATION」

最后是**返回那个位置**，而不是解释它：

> Thus I returned to this despicable, despairing, and desperate place, his and mine, and said: "You are so desperate because you felt that I'd given up. And when we both give up, there's nothing more to hold on to. It's really very despairing, *but we are going on*." He lay back quietly, tears in his eyes for the first time in analysis (perhaps in his life), and said, "Death can be so cheap. You should lock me up inside the treatment."

— Eshel, *Withness*，「CLINICAL ILLUSTRATION」

【原文】注意「his and mine（他的、也是我的）」——**活现的位置不是病人的，也不是分析师自己的，而是二人共有的那处**。这是「共同建构」最简明的临床证据。

## 五、Mitchell 与 Ed：一个完整的活现案例

Mitchell（2000）忘记了一次为配合 Ed 牙科急诊而约定的清晨会谈，Ed 在严寒中等待。这一**失误**开启了整段活现：

> The enactment that Mitchell describes began with his forgetting an early morning appointment he had scheduled to accommodate Ed's dental emergency. Finding Ed waiting in the bitter cold, Mitchell apologized and hurried them inside. Ed's response—"So, you're a creature of habit"—initiated a complex dance around guilt, reparation, and the possibility of ordinary human fallibility. When Mitchell charged Ed one-third of the usual fee for their abbreviated session, Ed was furious, experiencing this as petty and ungenerous. Mitchell's decision not to waive the fee emerged from his recognition that doing so would constitute "appeasing him, buying him off"—perpetuating rather than transforming Ed's internal world where guilt must always be erased through reparative action rather than borne as part of human experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Mitchell, 2000）

其理论要点是**诠释的位置**：

> What makes this vignette particularly instructive is how it demonstrates that interpretation in the relational model emerges through rather than about enactment. Mitchell doesn't simply interpret Ed's reaction; he participates in creating a new relational experience where guilt can be tolerated rather than evacuated, where imperfection doesn't require compensation, where two people can fail each other and remain in a relationship.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】「**通过**活现而非**关于**活现（through rather than about）」——这是活现概念在技术上最凝练的表述。

## 六、与埃谢尔「临在」的关系：临在正是为重度活现提出的

埃谢尔两篇都明确说明，**临在**概念的提出语境**就是**活现：

> I first developed this approach with regard to massive acting out, acting in, and enactment, arguing that the fate of these acting situations in the therapeutic work is determined largely by the analyst's willingness and ability to give him- or herself over to "being there," staying within the intense impact of the acting situation and communing with it (Eshel 1998a, 1998b).

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

> I began to develop this approach with regard to massive acting out, acting in and enactment, arguing that the fate of these acting situations in the therapeutic work is determined largely by the analyst's willingness and ability to give himself or herself over to being with-in there, staying with the intense impact of the acting situation and communing with it (Eshel, 1998a, 1998b).

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「THE BE(COM)ING OF A NEW DIMENSION」

定义本身也把「活现」写进了分析师的任务：

> *Presencing* is essentially the analyst's deep acceptance of the necessity of becoming an embedded, fundamental, and sustaining functioning presence within difficult treatment situations and enactments—thereby experiencing, withstanding, processing, and gradually transforming, from within, the repetitive cycle of pathological self–other relations and defenses.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「THE BE(COM)ING OF A NEW DIMENSION」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：**在活现中，分析师该做什么？**
> **A 方立场**：**关系取向**（Govrin 转述 Mitchell / Bass / Stern）。治疗行动来自「both participants' ability to survive and metabolize them together」；诠释「emerges **through** rather than **about** enactment」，最终产生**共同建构的诠释**（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章）。
> **B 方立场**：**埃谢尔**。临在是「a primary quality of presence, a multiple-function presence or deep availability that focuses on experience-near attunement, receptive capacity, holding, containing, and protection, **rather than proffering interpretations of the analytic relationships and especially of patient–analyst separateness**」（Eshel, *Withness*，首节）。I 篇的平行表述：「**rather than interpreting patient–analyst relationships and especially patient–analyst separateness**」（Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，「THE BE(COM)ING OF A NEW DIMENSION」）。
> **备注**：**部分可调和，但重心确实不同**。二者都反对「从外部解释活现」；分歧在于活现被穿越之后**要不要／能不能被诠释**。埃谢尔的答案是明确的**不诠释分离性**——因为诠释分离性会把分析师重新放回「之间」，而她要留在「之内」。她引博拉斯（Bollas, 1987）为这一立场背书：「The psychoanalyst's countertransference task within this transference is to allow himself to be assumed by the patient and not to interpret unless the patient needs it」（Eshel, *Withness*，注释 1，引 Bollas, p. 256）。**这是与「之间／之内」位置之争同源的分歧，而非独立争议。**

## 七、批评：活现范式的代价

### 7.1 格林伯格（Jay Greenberg）与米尔斯（Jon Mills）：热情与不对称

> Jay Greenberg's (2001) critique provides a necessary counterpoint to the enthusiasm surrounding analyst self-disclosure. His observation that relational case examples invariably portray analysts who "throw away the book" in moments of crisis raises important questions about whose needs are being served. In a profession under siege, analysts may be particularly vulnerable to what Greenberg calls "therapeutic zeal"—the wish to rescue, to heal, to transform through personal engagement rather than analytic understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Greenberg, 2001）

> Greenberg's most provocative suggestion is that the relational emphasis on creating novel experiences may actually foreclose deeper engagement with the patient's psychic reality. When the analyst acts quickly to provide a corrective experience, they may prevent both participants from fully confronting the depth and intensity of the patient's transference. Jon Mills (2017) extends this critique, arguing that excessive emphasis on mutuality risks ignoring the fundamental asymmetries that structure the analytic relationship—primarily that the patient seeks help and that the analyst is being paid as an authority.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Greenberg, 2001; Mills, 2017）

### 7.2 解离转向的代价：沉思能力萎缩

Govrin 对布隆伯格路线最实质的一击，是它可能在整体上削弱精神分析的独特性：

> The risk inherent in Bromberg's approach may be the gradual erosion of what makes psychoanalysis distinctively analytic. When the analyst becomes so focused on managing dissociative enactments and facilitating self-state communication, the capacity for sustained thought about unconscious process may atrophy. The analyst's mind, constantly alert to relational nuance and affective shifts, may lose the contemplative quality that allows for deeper pattern recognition across time.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「Critical Reflections on the Dissociative Turn」
