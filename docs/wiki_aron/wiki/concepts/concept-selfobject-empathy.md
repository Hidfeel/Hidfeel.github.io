# 自体客体与共情沉浸（selfobject, empathic immersion）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]、[[raw/eshel-withness]]

## 摘要

**自体客体（selfobject）**与**共情沉浸（empathic immersion）**是海因茨·科胡特（Heinz Kohut）自体心理学的一对核心概念。它们的革命性在于一次**双重倒置**：共情不再被视为通往诠释的 preparatory 步骤，而**本身即是**首要的观察工具与治疗工具；分析师不再被病人体验为一个可被诠释的独立客体，而被体验为**病人自身自体的延伸**。

Govrin 指出，共情是精神分析中第一个真正打破了「治疗行动＝导向洞见的诠释」这一保守大厦的概念。但科胡特的革命有一个致命的内在断裂：**它是临床的，不是认识论的**。他把共情界定为**替代性内省（vicarious introspection）**，试图以此保全传统分析的客观观察姿态——尽管他自己的临床工作已经证明这种客观性既不可能也不可欲。Govrin 的名言是：科胡特「**从临床的前门把分析师的主体性迎了进来，又在理论上试图把它从后门送出去**」。

本页还需处理一个必须精确交代的归属问题：**「解释只是追溯性的虚构」这一表述是 Govrin 的读法**（第五章第四节的标题），而非科胡特本人的自述；本批资料中并无科胡特自称其解释为「虚构」的引文。详见第四节。

## 一、历史位置：美国语境中的创伤疗愈传统

Govrin 首先给出了一个容易被忽略的历史事实——科胡特与温尼科特在美国并非并行的两条线，而是**先后承接**的：

> It is crucial to understand that in the United States of the 1970s and 1980s, Donald Winnicott's work remained largely unknown outside specialized circles. In this intellectual landscape, Heinz Kohut emerged as the most faithful representative of what I have termed in the previous chapter the "trauma healing" tradition in American psychoanalysis

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology's Approach to Interpretation」

其革命性的具体所指被 Govrin 说得非常明确：

> The concept of empathy became the one of the first notions in psychoanalysis to genuinely rupture the conservative edifice that had long equated therapeutic action with interpretation leading to insight.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

而整套重构的枢纽句是：

> This reconceptualization unleashed a cascade of transformative insights: empathy was recognized as the fundamental medium of psychological existence. The self could only be understood as functioning within its matrix of selfobjects—we exist as complete beings solely through our connection with others, embodying a profound intersubjective reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】**自体＝在自体客体矩阵中运作的组织**，这是科胡特对「自体」的定义方式。它与笛卡尔式自足主体的决裂是彻底的：自体不是实体，而是**关系性组织**。

## 二、共情作为首要工具：替代性内省

科胡特 1959 年的论文〈内省、共情与精神分析〉奠定了这一主张，并把共情同时立为**观察方法**与**治疗行动**。Govrin 复述了「共情沉浸」的操作性定义：

> Here, the analyst's primary task is not to challenge defenses or to expose unconscious wishes but to enter empathically into the patient's subjective world. This requires what Kohut (1984) describes as "empathic immersion" in the patient's experience—a process of vicarious introspection that allows the analyst to grasp the patient's inner reality from within rather than observing it from without.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」

其对立面被明确点名——**不是**挑战防御，**不是**揭露无意识愿望。这是与克莱因—比昂主线最直接的冲突点（参见 [[concepts/concept-immediate-interpretation]]）。

分析师作为自体客体的一面，被科胡特写成了方法论原则：

> This is why Kohut insists that "the analyst's primary tool must be sustained, finely attuned empathy—not interpretation as revelation of hidden drive conflicts" (Kohut, 1984, p. 115).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 115）

> When Kohut (1984) describes the analyst's task as grasping the patient's "perception of his psychic reality and accepts it as valid" (p. 173), he points toward something that exceeds the spatial metaphor he employs.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 173）

### 一个关键的自我瓦解：共情沉浸走向「丰产的误解」

Govrin 用 Ernest Wolf 记录的一个著名片段，展示了「理解阶段」实际生效的方式**恰恰不是理解**。病人为了赶来会谈而闯红灯，科胡特的回应是——「you're a damn fool!」：

> In one case, Wolf (1993) describes a patient who spoke of being so eager to attend his session that he had driven through red traffic lights. Rather than interpreting this behavior in terms of transference love or aggressive acting out, Kohut's quick response—"you're a damn fool!"— was effective precisely because it communicated the analyst's genuine concern for the patient's well-being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Wolf, 1993）

> This moment exposes a curious reversal: the understanding phase works not by understanding in any conventional sense, but by providing something that resists understanding altogether—a spontaneous eruption of genuine concern that breaks through the interpretive machinery.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

> This suggests that what Kohut calls "empathic immersion" might be better understood as a kind of productive misunderstanding—a willingness to be surprised by the patient rather than to comprehend them.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】这是本页最重要的内在张力：科胡特把共情立为**方法**（一种更好的观察），但他的最佳临床时刻却来自**方法的失效**——一句脱口而出的关切。Govrin 由此把「共情沉浸」重读为一种**丰产的误解（productive misunderstanding）**：分析师必须放弃精神分析 traditionally 提供的理解工具，才真正进入病人的主观世界。

## 三、理解阶段与解释阶段

科胡特的分期（Kohut, 1984, p. 96）被 Govrin 完整转述：

> This recognition led Kohut to develop his famous distinction between "understanding" and "explaining" phases of interpretation (Kohut, 1984, p. 96). The understanding phase, he argued, involved the analyst's empathic immersion in the patient's subjective experience. Only after this foundation of empathic connection had been established could the explaining phase—offering genetic and dynamic interpretations—be therapeutically effective.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「From Data-Gathering Tool to Therapeutic Experience」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 96）

但 Govrin 立刻指出这一分期**仍然臣服于古典目标**：

> Yet even this more sophisticated model reveals Kohut's continued attachment to traditional therapeutic goals. The understanding phase, however valuable, remained in service of the explaining phase, which was where "real" therapeutic change supposedly occurred. As Kohut put it, the analyst's empathic understanding enables the patient to "become more objective vis-à-vis himself and his problems" (Kohut, 1984, p. 185).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 185）

| 阶段 | 操作 | 科胡特赋予的地位 | Govrin 的诊断 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **理解（understanding）** | 共情沉浸、替代性内省，接受病人的心理现实为有效 | 必要条件、准备工作 | 实际承担了几乎全部治疗行动 |
| **解释（explaining）** | 发生学与动力学诠释 | 「真正的」改变发生之处 | 其实只是**为已经完成的理解立碑** |

## 四、解释阶段作为「追溯性虚构」——一处必要的归属澄清

Govrin 第五章第四节的**标题**即为「The Explaining Phase as Retrospective Fiction（解释阶段作为追溯性虚构）」。其论证如下：

> Kohut insists that these explanations must follow the establishment of empathic connection, yet his clinical material suggests that explanation often serves to memorialize an understanding that has already done its work. The interpretation becomes not a tool for change but a monument to change that has already occurred—a way of making the ephemeral experience of being understood available for conscious reflection.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Explaining Phase as Retrospective Fiction」

Govrin 为解释阶段另行赋予的功能是**见证性词汇（witnessing vocabulary）**：

> This distinction matters because it relocates the therapeutic action of interpretation. The explaining phase succeeds by offering what might be called a "witnessing vocabulary"—words that allow the patient's self-protective achievement to be recognized for what it truly is.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

其临床依据是律师病人「思考机器」的例证：智力化本被古典分析视为防御—阻抗，科胡特却把它读作病人早年**保护自体**的伟大成就；当科胡特把这个解释说出来时，诠释并不揭露隐藏内容，而是改变病人与某件他一直知道却从未被承认之事的关系（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章，参 Kohut, 1984, p. 116）。

> **【推断】** 必须如实标注：称「**科胡特本人承认**解释只是追溯性的虚构」在本批资料中**没有文本依据**。检索显示：（一）「retrospective fiction」这一措辞是 **Govrin 第五章第四节的标题**，不是科胡特原文；（二）科胡特在该节被引的原话只有 `"become more objective vis-à-vis himself and his problems"（1984, p. 185）` 与 `"perception of his psychic reality and accepts it as valid"（p. 173）`，均无「虚构」之意；（三）Govrin 的措辞是 `his clinical material suggests that...`——这是分析者的推断，不是被分析者的自陈。准确的表述应为：**在 Govrin 的读法中，解释阶段退化为追溯性的追认**；而 Govrin 判断科胡特本人并未走完这一步（见第六节）。

## 五、分析师作为自体客体

科胡特在晚期工作中列出三种自体客体移情——镜像（mirroring）、理想化（idealizing）、孪生（twinship）：

> In his final work, he delineates three principal selfobject transferences—mirroring, idealizing, and twinship, which represent "the self's active attempt at healing and finishing its interrupted development" (Kohut, 1984, p. 103).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 103）

**注意这句定义的取向**：自体客体移情不是退行，不是阻抗，而是**自体的主动尝试**——它试图治愈并完成被打断的发展。这是科胡特与古典移情观最根本的决裂。

由此，**诠释本身被重新定义为一种自体客体功能**：

> This reconceptualization transforms interpretation itself into a selfobject function. When the analyst offers an interpretation, they provide what Kohut calls a responsive selfobject experience. The therapeutic power lies less in the interpretation's accuracy than in its demonstration that the patient exists in the analyst's mind as someone worth sustained, careful thought. Each interpretive effort, whether correct or mistaken, performs a mirroring function—confirming that the patient's inner life merits deep engagement and persistent effort to understand.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

【综合】这是一个彻底的功能性翻转：**诠释的价值不在其真假，而在其「镜像功能」**——它对错都行，只要它证明了病人的内在生活值得被持续、仔细地思考。这直接取消了「诠释作为真理载体」的古典定义（参见 [[concepts/concept-interpretation]]、[[concepts/concept-truth-axes]]）。

更进一步，分析师**反复失败又回来**的过程，比完美的共情更有价值：

> When the analyst struggles to understand, revises their understanding, acknowledges their failure and tries again, they demonstrate something more valuable than perfect empathy: they show that the patient is worth the ongoing effort of understanding, worth returning to after failure, worth thinking about even when they are at their most difficult or attacking.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

治愈的定义也随之改写——**不是脱离自体客体，而是学会使用自体客体**：

> The cure, then, is not independence from selfobjects but what he describes as "the patient's newfound ability to seek, recognize, and draw on selfobjects outside therapy" (Kohut, 1984, p. 120).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 120）

> The most radical implication of Kohut's theory (1984) may be its suggestion that psychological structure itself develops through relationship—that what we call the "self" is not an autonomous entity but a dynamic organization that exists only within what he terms the "matrix of selfobjects."

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

## 六、Govrin 的核心批评：前门迎进主体性，后门送出去

这是本页的核心论断，Govrin 的原文极为凝练：

> However, Kohut's revolution was fundamentally clinical, not epistemological. While he transformed therapeutic practice by insisting that the analyst's subjectivity was essential to understanding and treating patients, he remained theoretically conservative, clinging to the scientific aspirations and truth-seeking ambitions of classical psychoanalysis. His concept of empathy as "vicarious introspection" attempted to preserve the objective, observational stance of traditional analysis even as his clinical work demonstrated that such objectivity was neither possible nor desirable. This tension between radical practice and conservative theory runs throughout his work—he brought the analyst's subjectivity through the front door clinically while trying to usher it out the back door theoretically.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology's Approach to Interpretation」

这一保守性的文本证据有三条：

**（一）科胡特至死坚持科学客观性的框架**：

> Even in his final writings, when he acknowledged that "objective truths must always include an assessment of the observer" (Kohut, 1984, p. 40), he continued to frame this insight as an advance in scientific understanding rather than a fundamental challenge to the scientific model itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Kohut's Early Formulations」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 40）

**（二）1978 年的私信坦白了策略性的渐进主义**：

> In a 1978 letter Kohut wrote: "I am very eager not to encourage a break in the development of psychoanalysis and am therefore proceeding with theory change in a gradual way" (cited in Cocks, 1994).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Cocks, 1994）

> Even as late as 1978, three years before his death, Kohut was insisting that he was working "within psychoanalysis, not outside of it" (Cocks, 1994).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Cocks, 1994）

**（三）体制位置**——科胡特是芝加哥研究所的训练分析师、美国精神分析协会前主席，深嵌于精神分析建制之中（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章）。

Govrin 的最终判词：

> As I wrote elsewhere (Govrin, 2025), "as he refuses to relinquish the truth of interpretation and in his efforts to base this truth on subjective element, Kohut exposes himself to irresolvable contradictions" (p. 81).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Conclusion: The Unfinished Revolution」（引 Govrin, 2025, p. 81）

> Kohut opened this door but couldn't quite walk through it, perhaps sensing that what lay beyond would require abandoning too much of what had made psychoanalysis what it was.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Beyond Interpretation」

## 七、后继者的认识论谦逊：Wolf 与 Lichtenberg

科胡特去世后的自体心理学**自行放弃了**他坚持的观察客观性：

> Where Kohut had maintained that empathy provided a relatively objective mode of observation—"vicarious introspection"—Wolf and Lichtenberg acknowledge that even empathic perception is shaped by the observer's subjectivity.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Co-Creative Nature of Interpretation」

> This integration of multiple perspectives represents a more epistemologically humble stance than Kohut's—an acknowledgment that no single mode of observation, however empathic, can capture the full complexity of human experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

并且把自体客体经验明确改写为**双向**的：

> "During an ongoing treatment both patient and therapist communicate verbally and nonverbally, thereby providing a basis for selfobject experiences for the other" (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533）

其人性论前提也一并被改写——婴儿**先天是强壮的**：

> "Self psychology takes the stand that the baby is innately ‘strong.’ We are born preadapted to respond to an empathically sensitive family life that reliably provides soothing, vitalizing, and need-fulfilling experiences" (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 536).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 536）

## 八、从共情到悲悯：埃谢尔的延伸与批评

埃谢尔处理共情的方式是**从另一侧越过科胡特**。她引 Orange（2006）把共情推到悲悯，但随即指出所有这些工作仍是**互动的、关系的、两人心理学**的：

> In her view, compassion is that part of empathy that makes the analyst willing and able to descend into the patient's realms of suffering and shattered life. This enables emotional understanding and integration of the patient's suffering, as opposed to dissociation and fragmentation, and affirms the human dignity of the patient. The above articles, however, relate to compassion from interactive, relational, two-person psychology perspectives.

— Eshel, *Withness*，COMPASSION（引 Orange, 2006）

> But while the term *witness* applies to an interaction between two subjectivities (Stern 2012; see also Reis's attempt to broaden the conception of witnessing, 2009a, 2009b), I wish to propose here the notion of *psychoanalytic* "*withness*"—the being there, within the experience of suffering, becoming at-one with it, in deep patient–analyst interconnectedness.

— Eshel, *Withness*，COMPASSION

【综合】埃谢尔的批评方向与 Govrin 相反却互补：Govrin 说科胡特**太保守**（认识论上退回科学客观主义）；埃谢尔则说**共情—悲悯这条线本身不够**（它仍停留在两个主体性的互动中，而她要的是「在受苦经验**之内**在那里、与之合一」）。两条批评共同指向：共情如果仍被理解为「一人对另一人的感知」，就不够激进。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：共情（替代性内省）究竟是一种**相对客观的观察模式**，还是**被观察者主体性塑造的**？
> **A 方立场**：科胡特——共情即替代性内省，是可产出客观数据的观察方法；连「客观真理必须包含对观察者的评估」这一让步，他也要框定为**科学理解的进步**而非对科学模型本身的挑战——「he continued to frame this insight as an advance in scientific understanding rather than a fundamental challenge to the scientific model itself」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章）
> **B 方立场**：Wolf 与 Lichtenberg（1997）——「even empathic perception is shaped by the observer's subjectivity」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章），并承认来自婴儿研究、记忆形式、阈下知觉、情感沟通、非反思性认知与神经生理学的多重信息源会**充实**共情模式所得的信息
> **备注**：这是**科胡特本人与其直接后继者之间的公开分歧**，非外部批评。它不可调和：A 方的整个理论策略（在保全科学客观性的前提下引入主体性）被 B 方放弃。Govrin 明确判定 B 方「a more epistemologically humble stance than Kohut's」。这一分歧的临床后果是：若取 B 方，则「共情沉浸」不能再被当作更精确的**取数工具**，而只能是**主体间的共构事件**——这实际上把科胡特送回了他试图回避的结论。
> **补充**：此矛盾正是 Govrin 所说「前门／后门」断裂的**具体发生点**——科胡特在临床上已经按 B 方工作（第二节的「丰产的误解」），却在理论上坚持 A 方。
