# 分析性第三方（the analytic third / thirdness）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**分析性第三方（the analytic third）** 是托马斯·奥格登（Thomas Ogden）自 1994 年起发展、并由杰西卡·本杰明（Jessica Benjamin）在其「第三方性（thirdness）」名下推向承认理论的核心概念。它要回答的是一个看似简单的问题：会谈中那些最有转化力的诠释，**究竟来自谁**？关系取向的答案是：既不来自病人的无意识（等待被发现），也不来自分析师的心智（等待被送达），而是来自二者无意识共同创造的**第三方主体性**——一个位于二者「之间」、又超出二者的共享无意识空间。

由此，诠释的性质被彻底改写：它不再是**解码**，而是分析关系的**涌现属性（emergent property）**。分析师的职能也随之改变——不是从外部把诠释施加于第三方，而是**充当第三方的「宿主」（host）**，维护其存在条件，同时开放自己被其中涌现之物所改变。

本页的另一半是本 Wiki 必须澄清的**核心区分**：奥格登的第三方位于患者与分析师**「之间」（between）**；而奥芙拉·埃谢尔（Ofra Eshel）的**同在（withness）**则主张其经验位于病人**「之内」（within）**，且明确「**甚至超越主体间性**」。这不是措辞之争，而是关于分析师主体性应当朝向何处的范式分歧。

## 一、定义：既非病人、亦非分析师的第三方主体性

Govrin 把这一概念称为「对传统精神分析认识论最激进的背离」，因为它重新规定了**诠释的产地**：

> The concept of the analytic third represents perhaps the most radical departure from traditional psychoanalytic epistemology, fundamentally reconceptualizing where interpretation originates and how therapeutic meaning emerges.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章「The Analytic Third: Interpretation as Emergent Property」

定义本身由三句递进构成。第一句给出**位置**：

> Neither residing in the patient's unconscious waiting to be discovered, nor in the analyst's mind waiting to be delivered, interpretation in this model emerges from what Thomas Ogden (2004) calls the analytic third—a jointly created unconscious space that exists between and beyond both participants.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

第二句给出**功能的改写**——诠释从解码动作变为涌现属性：

> This third space transforms interpretation from an act of decoding into an emergent property of the analytic relationship itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

第三句给出**本体论地位**——这个第三方有自己的特征、动力与生成潜能：

> Ogden's formulation of the analytic third posits that patient and analyst create something that transcends their individual psychologies—a shared unconscious space with its own characteristics, dynamics, and generative potential (Ogden, 1994, 1997).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

关键在于它不是「两个主体性之和」，而是**辩证的产物**——同时**保存又否定**二者的分离性：

> This third is not simply the sum of two subjectivities, but rather a dialectical creation that simultaneously preserves and negates the separateness of each participant.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

> The analyst and patient remain distinct individuals while also becoming something else together—participants in a jointly created unconscious life that belongs fully to neither yet profoundly influences both.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】这一「既不全属、又深刻影响双方」的表述，是第三方概念区别于一切「二人心理学」的判据。

## 二、分析师如何捕捉第三方：遐想（reverie）

第三方不可被直接观察，只能被**间接经验**。承接比昂（Wilfred Bion）的**遐想**概念，奥格登把分析师最平凡的日常念头确立为捕捉第三方的器官。奥格登本人在其本体论精神分析论文中写道：

> Reverie (waking-dreaming) is a state of being that entails making oneself unconsciously receptive to experiencing what is so disturbing to the patient (or infant) that he is unable to "dream" (to do unconscious psychological work with) the experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，论 Bion 的遐想

> The analyst's (or mother's) reveries, waking dreaming—which often take the form of his most mundane, quotidian thoughts (Ogden 1997a, 1997b)—constitutes a way in which the analyst (or mother) unconsciously experiences something like the patient's (or infant's) unthinkable, undreamable experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，论 Bion 的遐想

而**如何把遐想说出来**，是技术上最要紧的一句——分析师要**从（from）**遐想经验说话，而**不是关于（about）**它说话：

> In the analytic setting, the analyst makes available to the patient the transformed (dreamt) version of the patient's "undreamt" or partially dreamt experience by speaking (or relating in other forms) from, not about, reverie experience (Ogden 1994).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，论 Bion 的遐想

注意该句的自引年份正是 **Ogden 1994**——即《The analytic third: Working with intersubjective clinical facts》发表的同一年。Govrin 则把这一机制概括为本体论（而非认识论）的操作：

> Bion's conception of reverie—a state of unconscious receptivity to the patient's undreamable experience—exemplifies ontological thinking. The analyst doesn't interpret the patient's projective identifications but becomes temporarily transformed by them, offering back a "dreamt" version of what was previously unthinkable.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节「From Knowing to Being—The Ontological Turn」

埃谢尔则引奥格登（2009）对「比昂心智功能第二原则」的提炼，把第三方的功能压缩为一句：

> Recent contributions include Ogden's (2009) formulation of what he views as Bion's second principle of mental functioning: "It takes two minds to think one's most disturbing, previously unthinkable thoughts" (p. 100).

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

【原文校勘】原文此处因脚注插入而断词：「previously unthink- 3 Elsewhere (Eshel 2002)…[脚注正文]…able thoughts」 (p. 100)。引号内的句子在去除脚注标记后为连续形式，本页照此引录。

【综合】三方文本合起来给出一条完整链条：**遐想是接收器 → 第三方是被接收之物 → 诠释是从遐想中说出的、「被梦过」的版本。**

## 三、理论亲缘之一：温尼科特的过渡空间

第三方并非凭空出现。它最直接的先祖是唐纳德·温尼科特（Donald Winnicott）的**过渡空间（transitional space）**——那个既非纯粹内在、亦非纯粹外在的第三区域。Govrin 在温尼科特章中记录了该术语在文献中的标准表述：

> Communication takes place in what he calls the "transitional space between analyst and patient" (Phillips, 1988, p. 138)—a shared area of creative possibility where meanings can emerge collaboratively rather than being imposed by interpretive authority.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

以及温尼科特技术清单中「过渡客体」的位置：

> Sometimes this means interpretation, sometimes holding, sometimes surviving the patient's use of the analyst as a transitional object.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

更重要的亲缘在于**「共同经历一个经验」**这一表述。埃谢尔转引奥格登（2001）对温尼科特的放大——奥格登认为，正是「live」这个动词暴露了温尼科特正在改造精神分析：

> Ogden (2001) beautifully amplifies this aspect of Winnicott's thinking: "'*Live an experience together*'—what makes the phrase remarkable is the unexpected word 'live.' They do not 'take part in,' 'share,' 'participate in,' or 'enter into' an experience together: they live an experience together. In this single phrase, Winnicott is suggesting (though I think he is not fully aware of this as he writes this paper) that he is in the process of transforming psychoanalysis, both as a theory and as a therapeutic relationship" (pp. 226-227, italics in original).

— Eshel, *Withness*，注释 2

【推断】奥格登在此处对温尼科特的阅读，几乎可以视为他对自己第三方理论的一次「认祖」：二人共同生活（live）一个经验，正是「第三方」的动态版本。**但需注意**：本批资料中没有任何一句原文把 transitional space 与 analytic third 直接连写，此处的亲缘属跨文献推断，无直接引文支撑。

## 四、理论亲缘之二：比昂的 O 与本体论转向

第二条亲缘线通向比昂的 **O（终极实在）**。Govrin 把奥格登归入**本体论精神分析（ontological psychoanalysis）**——与认识论精神分析（弗洛伊德、克莱因）相对：

> Ogden's distinction between epistemological and ontological psychoanalysis captures a transformation in therapeutic sensibility that had been quietly developing over decades (Ogden, 2019, p. 663). Epistemological psychoanalysis, associated primarily with Freud and Klein, focuses on knowing and understanding unconscious meanings. Ontological psychoanalysis, championed by Donald Winnicott and Wilfred Bion, centers on being and becoming—on the struggle to come into existence as a person who feels real and alive to oneself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节

其推论是：诠释之所以重要，不是因为它揭示隐藏的真理，而是因为它**创造存在的新可能性**：

> Our interpretations matter not because they reveal hidden truths but because they create new possibilities for being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节（引 Ogden, 2019）

埃谢尔则提供了一个**层次定位**，把奥格登明确放置在韦尔莫特（Rudi Vermote）三分模型的**第二区**（知识中的转化），与比昂、费罗、博泰拉夫妇（Botella and Botella）同列：

> 2. Transformation in Knowledge—container-contained, reverie, dream-work, alpha-function (Bion, Marty, de M'Uzan, Bollas, Botella and Botella, Ogden, Ferro);

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」（引 Vermote 2013）

【原文】这一归类极为重要：在埃谢尔的分层里，**奥格登的第三方属于「知识中的转化」层，而非「O 中的转化」层**。她把自己放在第三区，把奥格登放在第二区——这是她与奥格登分歧的分层依据，也是下一节的铺垫。

## 五、★ 核心区分：奥格登的「之间」 vs 埃谢尔的「之内」

这是本 Wiki 必须澄清的区分。埃谢尔在同一节内做了**两处划界**，必须连读。

### 5.1 先承认亲缘，再点出相异

她明言自己的「二而一」新实体与奥格登的**主体间性分析性第三方**「密切相关」，并共享奥格登对移情／反移情的看法；随后立刻指出分歧——分歧不在位置，而在**分析师主体性的朝向**：

> This new entity transcends the duality of patient and analyst, transference and countertransference. In this respect, it is closely related to Ogden's (1997) conception of the *intersubjective analytic third,* and I share his thinking that "do[es] not view transference and countertransference as separate entities that arise in response to one another; rather, I understand these terms to refer to aspects of a single intersubjective totality" (p. 25). However, I differ from Ogden in the emphasis that he places on the analyst's own subjective past experiences and memories, which are evoked in the analyst's mind in the session. I reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality, especially as a necessary, functioning presence.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

### 5.2 位置之争：不在「之间」，而在「之内」

第一次划界之后，埃谢尔在讨论费罗时**回指**奥格登，用括号明确这是一次统一纠正：

> While I completely agree with this fundamental point, the analytic experience of "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness is not located *between* patient and analyst, but is as much as possible *within* the patient's world. (I have pointed this out earlier with regard to Ogden's intersubjective third.)

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

「之内」的极端形态，是分析师退向消失点：

> The analyst thus becomes part of the patient's state of being and experiencing, to the extent of becoming psychically akin to a transplant or to chimeric antibodies (Eshel 2012); or, put differently, of receding toward the vanishing point in a perspective drawing.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

### 5.3 「甚至超越主体间性」

第三处划界最具决定性——埃谢尔不只是在「之间／之内」的坐标上移动，而是**主张**一个位于主体间性**之外**的层次：

> "Presencing" and interconnectedness go beyond the level of interactions and patient–analyst relationships (object or subject relationships), even beyond intersubjectivity, to offer the opportunity for getting in touch with basic (environmental) relatedness and formative experiences of being and becoming

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

> In my view, the emerging dimension of the analyst's "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness that I am proposing reaches down to this third, most undifferentiated and fundamental analytic functioning and transformation in O.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：分析性经验的**位置**——第三方／场域位于二人「之间」，还是位于病人「之内」？分析师应唤起自己的过去，还是嵌入病人的主观现实？
> **A 方立场**：**奥格登**（经 Govrin 转述）。第三方是「a jointly created unconscious space that exists between and beyond both participants」；Govrin 并指出「If the most significant unconscious dynamics exist not within either participant but in the space between them, then interpretation cannot be a unidirectional delivery of insight.」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章）。分析师在其中是第三方的「host」。
> **B 方立场**：**埃谢尔**。经验「is not located *between* patient and analyst, but is as much as possible *within* the patient's world」；且她与奥格登相异之处在于「the emphasis that he places on the analyst's own subjective past experiences and memories」，她则「reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality」（Eshel, *Withness*，首节）。
> **备注**：**不可通约的范式差异，非措辞之争**。二人对「移情—反移情是单一主体间性整体的诸面向」完全一致；分歧在于（a）位置的拓扑学（之间 vs 之内）与（b）层次（Vermote 第二区 vs 第三区）。埃谢尔自述其维度「even beyond intersubjectivity」，而奥格登的第三方正是主体间性范式内的核心概念——故她并未把第三方当作错误，而是当作**不够深**。

## 六、本杰明：第三方性与承认

在本杰明（Jessica Benjamin）手中，第三方从**诠释的产地**转为**承认（recognition）之所以可能的条件**：

> Jessica Benjamin's concept of thirdness extends this understanding into the realm of recognition and intersubjective space (Benjamin, 2004, 2018). For Benjamin, the third represents the principle of mediation that makes genuine recognition possible—the space where both participants can simultaneously maintain their own subjectivity while recognizing the other's separate existence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

第三方的脆弱性由此成为临床核心议题——它随时可能崩塌为**「施动者与受动者」（doer and done to）**的互补结构：

> This third space is fragile and constantly under threat of collapse into what Benjamin terms "doer and done to" dynamics, where complementarity replaces mutuality and recognition fails.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

而崩塌时的临床假象尤其重要——**此时看起来像诠释的东西，其实是活现**：

> When the third collapses—when the analytic pair falls into complementary dynamics where one person becomes subject and the other object—interpretation in any meaningful sense becomes impossible. What masquerades as interpretation in such moments is actually enactment, with one person imposing meaning on the other rather than both participating in the creation of shared understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

【原文】这一句是理解第三方与活现关系的枢轴：**第三方在场 = 诠释；第三方缺席 = 活现**。详见 [[concepts/concept-enactment]]。

## 七、临床后果：诠释作为涌现属性

第三方的引入，直接改写了解释行为的**时间结构**与**作者归属**：

> Rather than the analyst formulating an interpretation internally and then deciding whether and how to deliver it, interpretations in the model of the third emerge through a more collaborative process. The analyst might begin a thought that the patient completes, or the patient might offer an association that suddenly crystallizes something the analyst had been sensing but couldn't articulate. The interpretation belongs to neither and both simultaneously.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

作者的权威来源因此被重写——不是知识优势，而是**倾听与表述的能力**：

> But it does mean that the analyst's interpretive authority derives not from superior knowledge or objectivity but from their capacity to attend to and articulate what emerges in the third. The analyst serves as what Ogden calls a "host" to the analytic third, maintaining conditions for its existence while remaining open to being transformed by what emerges within it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

「阻抗」的概念随之改变——拒斥诠释，可能不是阻抗，而是**第三方缺位**：

> When a patient rejects or seems unable to use an interpretation, this might indicate not resistance in the traditional sense but the absence of a viable third space where the interpretation could take root and grow. The therapeutic task becomes not overcoming resistance but creating conditions where thirdness can emerge and sustain itself long enough for interpretive work to occur.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

对创伤病人而言，任务先于诠释——**必须先重建第三方的能力**：

> Traumatic experience often destroys the capacity for thirdness, trapping individuals in rigid self-other configurations that preclude genuine intersubjective meeting. The interpretive task with traumatized patients involves not primarily explaining or understanding trauma but gradually rebuilding the capacity for thirdness—creating conditions where interpretation becomes possible rather than retraumatizing.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

> The patient experiences not just being understood but participating in the creation of understanding—a fundamentally different therapeutic action from receiving insight from another.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

## 八、第三方是隐喻还是实在？

Govrin 没有回避这一概念的本体论尴尬，并给出了一个**以临床效用为准**的实用主义回答：

> Yet questions remain about the ontological status of the third. Is it a metaphor for describing complex relational dynamics, or does it refer to something that actually exists in some form? Different theorists take different positions on this question, but what matters clinically is that working as if the third exists—attending to it, nurturing it, interpreting from within it—produces different therapeutic effects than working from a two-person psychology that doesn't recognize this emergent dimension.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章
