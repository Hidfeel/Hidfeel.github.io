# 求真传统与创伤疗愈传统（truth-seeking tradition and trauma healing tradition）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

阿纳·戈夫林（Aner Govrin）在《诠释：当代导论》中把精神分析的治疗遭遇史读作**两大竞争传统**的历史。**求真传统（truth-seeking tradition）**从弗洛伊德经克莱因延伸到早期比昂：分析师被想象为科学家，病人是研究客体，症状是线索，梦是加密的信息，**诠释的洞见是首要的治疗因子**；病理被理解为**否认（disavowal）**的后果——病人系统性地拒绝承认自己的内在真理，治疗任务是**收复**被驱逐出意识的部分。**创伤疗愈传统（trauma healing tradition）**由费伦齐（Sándor Ferenczi）发端、温尼科特（Donald Winnicott）以特有的微妙加以发展、科胡特（Heinz Kohut）在美国成为其最忠实的代表：它以**母婴关系为模型**，把治疗关系重构为环境供给，治疗在于**修复发展性中断**，而非考古式挖掘被压抑的真理。

这一对立在临床上结晶为**两种治疗文化**：对病人的沟通是怀疑还是相信、是否刻意避免安慰、在感激背后诠释攻击还是庆祝真实联结、严守设置还是为发展需要调整设置。戈夫林本人并不接受这一二分的字面形式——他把问题转为"**出现的是哪一种真理、通过何种方式出现**"，从而在 [[concepts/concept-truth-types]] 与 [[concepts/concept-truth-axes]] 处收口。

## 一、被压制的另一种可能：费伦齐

戈夫林以费伦齐的失败作为叙事起点：

> When Sándor Ferenczi’s approach—which held that the therapeutic essence of psychoanalysis resided in profound emotional attunement rather than intellectual interpretation—was forcefully marginalized by Freud and his circle, the field crystallized around a singular conviction: that interpretation yielding insight constituted the paramount curative force

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈：温尼科特对古典精神分析的革命性背离」

他评价温尼科特的历史作用时指出，这场革命"**伪装成了常识**"：

> Winnicott quietly dismantled the entire edifice of psychoanalytic authority while appearing to do nothing more than pay closer attention to what mothers actually do. His revolution came disguised as common sense, which may be why it took so long for the field to recognize how thoroughly he had transformed the nature of therapeutic action itself

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

## 二、求真传统：分析师作为科学家

> The history of psychoanalysis reveals two competing traditions for the therapeutic encounter, each carrying profound implications for how we understand healing, knowledge, and human relationship. The first, the *truth-seeking tradition*, stretching from Freud through Klein and early Bion, imagined the analyst as scientist and the patient as research subject—a dyad engaged in the methodical excavation of psychical truth. Here, symptoms were clues, dreams were encrypted messages, and the analyst’s interpretive acumen was the primary instrument of cure

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

病理学的内核是**否认**，治疗任务是**收复**：

> In this classical framework, pathology was understood primarily as the consequence of disavowal—the patient’s systematic refusal to acknowledge their inner truth. People preferred to live in illusions, constructing elaborate defensive narratives rather than confronting the reality of their desires, aggression, and vulnerability. Most crucially, they projected unwanted aspects of themselves onto others, unable or unwilling to claim ownership of these disavowed parts. The therapeutic task was therefore one of reclamation: helping patients to recognize and reintegrate what they had expelled from conscious awareness

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

> Healing meant knowing oneself— not in some abstract philosophical sense, but in the concrete recognition of the specific truths one had been hiding from consciousness

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

戈夫林引弗洛伊德 1917 年对神经症病人的劝告作为这一"认识论信仰"的浓缩：

> “The guilt … It is in you … look inward, look into your own depths, learn first and foremost to know yourself!”

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」（引 Freud, 1917, pp. 142–143）

> Knowledge, in this view, is inherently curative. The patient suffers not from what they don’t have but from what they won’t see. The analyst’s role is to illuminate these hidden truths through interpretation, gradually dismantling the patient’s defensive structures until they can tolerate knowing what they have always unconsciously known

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

## 三、创伤疗愈传统：母婴关系作为模型

> The second metaphor, *the trauma healing tradition* which Winnicott developed with characteristic subtlety, reconceived the therapeutic relationship as fundamentally like that between parent and infant. Here, healing occurs not through the acquisition of knowledge but through the provision of a particular kind of relational environment

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

转折点被定位在温尼科特对"求知事业"本身的怀疑。戈夫林以汉娜·西格尔（Hannah Segal）的名言作为对照物：

> Where Freud had proclaimed that neurosis resulted from repressed truth, and Klein had argued with Hannah Segal (1962) that “the goal of the analyst is solely to obtain knowledge and to convey it” (p. 212), Winnicott took a different approach

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

> He used the model of preverbal mother-infant communication as the foundation for understanding the analytic relationship, which consequently redefined the role that interpretation plays in psychoanalytic therapy

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

由此产生一个革命性推论——**如果病人已经拥有他需要知道的东西，那么分析师的职能究竟是什么？**

> This seemingly modest observation contains a revolutionary implication—if the patient already possesses what they need to know, then what exactly is the analyst’s function? Winnicott’s answer involves a complete reconceptualization of interpretive authority

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

> Winnicott emphasizes that it is crucial for the analyst to avoid knowing the answers in advance, except when the patient has regressed to the earliest infancy

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

## 四、治疗目标与移情理解的分歧

| 维度 | 求真传统 | 创伤疗愈传统 |
|---|---|---|
| 治疗目标 | 通过诠释与洞见**归还被否认的自体部分**，借理解达成更大的整合 | 促成**持续存在（going-on-being）**——环境失败可以击碎的生存连续性 |
| 目标的重心 | 自我认识 | 本真地生活、创造性地与现实交往的能力 |
| 对移情的预期 | **期待并鼓励**强烈的移情反应，以便诠释与修通 | 不回避负性移情，但**不把它当作治疗的必经阶段** |
| 分析师作为"坏客体" | 承接并存活病人的负性投射，被视为治疗进展的必要条件 | 非必要 |
| 分析师的心智姿态 | 诠释的敏锐是首要治疗工具 | 避免预先知道答案；等待病人自己抵达 |

原文依据：

> The truth-seeking tradition aims to return disavowed parts of the self through interpretation and insight, helping patients to reclaim projected aspects of their personality and achieve greater integration through understanding. The trauma healing tradition, by contrast, seeks to facilitate what Winnicott (1954) called “going-on-being,” that is, the basic continuity of existence that environmental failure can shatter. The goal is not primarily self- knowledge but the capacity for authentic living and creative engagement with reality

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

> Perhaps nowhere is this difference more evident than in each tradition’s understanding of transference. The truth-seeking tradition expects and encourages the development of intense transference reactions that can be interpreted and worked through. The analyst’s willingness to function as a “bad object”—to receive and survive the patient’s negative projections—is seen as essential to therapeutic progress. The trauma healing tradition, while not avoiding negative transference, does not require it as a necessary phase of treatment

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

## 五、两种治疗文化的四重对照

这是全书对这一对立最密集的表述：

> Nevertheless, this distinction remains crucial, and when one reads the case studies of Klein’s followers versus those of Winnicott’s disciples, the difference is striking: the former approach the patient’s communications with suspicion, searching for hidden meanings, while the latter believe and validate the patient’s experience; the former carefully avoid any display of reassurance or comfort, while the latter embrace, contain, and hold; the former interpret aggression behind apparent gratitude, while the latter celebrate genuine connection; the former maintain strict boundaries, while the latter adapt the frame to meet developmental needs— creating two fundamentally different therapeutic cultures that, despite theoretical convergences, continue to shape divergent clinical practices

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

拆成对照表：

| 临床姿态 | 求真传统（克莱因学派为型） | 创伤疗愈传统（温尼科特学派为型） |
|---|---|---|
| 对病人沟通的态度 | 带着**怀疑**接近，搜寻隐藏意义 | **相信并确认**病人的经验 |
| 安慰 | 刻意**避免**任何安慰或舒适的表示 | **拥抱、涵容、抱持** |
| 感激 | 在表面的感激**背后**诠释攻击 | **庆祝**真实的联结 |
| 设置 | 维持**严格的边界** | **为发展需要调整**设置 |

创伤疗愈一侧对分析师的要求被表述为：

> Winnicott argued that what truly matters to the patient is the analyst’s genuine desire to be helpful, their ability to empathetically connect with the patient, their belief in what the patient requires, and their readiness to respond to those needs as soon as they are communicated through verbal, non-verbal, or preverbal means

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

## 六、两派如何互相反驳，以及戈夫林的再表述

戈夫林明确记录了两方的相互反驳：**求真派主张他们也在疗愈创伤**，**创伤疗愈派主张他们的关系进路不可避免地揭示真理**。

> Critics from each camp rightly argue that the dichotomy creates false polarizations. Truth-seeking analysts contend that they too heal trauma, pointing out that their interpretive work addresses the psychological wounds created by early object relationships and constitutional vulnerabilities. Trauma healing practitioners argue that their relational approach inevitably uncovers important truths about the patient’s psychological development

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

他由此把问题**从"有没有真理"转为"出现的是哪一种真理、通过何种方式出现"**：

> The question becomes not whether truth emerges in treatment, but what kind of truth and by what means

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「从求真到创伤疗愈」

这一转向正是 [[concepts/concept-truth-axes]] 与 [[concepts/concept-truth-types]] 的问题域。

## 七、科胡特：美国最忠实的创伤疗愈传统代表

戈夫林把自体心理学放在这一谱系的延长线上：

> Heinz Kohut emerged as the most faithful representative of what I have termed in the previous chapter the “trauma healing” tradition in American psychoanalysis—that therapeutic lineage which prioritizes the restoration of developmental arrests and the repair of early environmental failures over the archaeological excavation of repressed truth

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「自体心理学进路的革命性」

但他随即给出一个重要的限定——**科胡特的革命是临床的，不是认识论的**：

> However, Kohut’s revolution was fundamentally clinical, not epistemological. While he transformed therapeutic practice by insisting that the analyst’s subjectivity was essential to understanding and treating patients, he remained theoretically conservative, clinging to the scientific aspirations and truth-seeking ambitions of classical psychoanalysis

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「自体心理学进路的革命性」

## 八、对创伤疗愈传统的批评：Blass 与 Etchegoyen

戈夫林列出对温尼科特式背离的系统性批评。**雷切尔·布拉斯（Rachel Blass）的三点质疑**：

> Rachel Blass presents a systematic critique challenging whether Winnicott’s techniques remain within the psychoanalytic framework or fundamentally transgress its boundaries (Blass, 2012). She identifies three critical concerns: First, the transformation of the analyst from neutral interpreter to active, maternal figure providing holding and surviving attacks challenges traditional analytic neutrality. Second, his emphasis on non-symbolic, pre-object relating—“being together” rather than interpreting unconscious material—represents a fundamental departure from established technique. Third, the blurring of boundaries between psychoanalysis and supportive psychotherapy undermines the specificity of psychoanalytic intervention

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「温尼科特背离古典分析的批评视角」

**埃切戈延（Horacio Etchegoyen）**提供了补充的理论根据：

> Horacio Etchegoyen provides additional theoretical grounding for these concerns, emphasizing the necessity of maintaining the analytic contract through a delicate balance between firmness in setting and responsiveness in style

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「温尼科特背离古典分析的批评视角」

批评者的共同让步与保留：

> Both critics acknowledge that Winnicott’s techniques address important clinical phenomena, particularly with severely disturbed patients unresponsive to classical interpretation. However, they argue that the modifications required by his approach may compromise the essential characteristics distinguishing psychoanalysis from other therapeutic modalities

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「温尼科特背离古典分析的批评视角」

## 九、判据的转移：从"知道什么"到"从内还是从外"

第四章的结论把这一对立收束为一条关于**诠释时机与来源**的判据：

> The classical tradition emphasized what the analyst knows and when to say it. Winnicott shifted the question entirely: not what does the interpretation contain, but does the patient experience it as coming from within or as an intrusion from without? An interpretation that is accurate but premature violates the very capacity for experience it claims to illuminate. The analyst who knows too much too soon becomes, paradoxically, useless—or worse, becomes a seduction, inviting compliance rather than growth

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「结论」

> This is why Winnicott could suggest that a brilliant interpretation, perfectly timed by the analyst’s standards, might be the most anti-therapeutic intervention of all

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「结论」

## 十、⚠️ 矛盾标注：二元对立 vs 感受力

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：求真／创伤疗愈之分，究竟是**两个可辨识的学派传统**，还是**两种治疗感受力**？
> **A 方立场**：戈夫林——主张这是两种**治疗文化**，可以在案例报告层面被辨识，并明确指出该二分"造成虚假的两极分化"；但他仍坚持"这一区分依然至关重要"（*Interpretation*，第四章）。
> **B 方立场**：奥格登——拒绝任何学派式划分：「They are ways of thinking and being—sensibilities, not “schools” of analytic thought or sets of analytic principles or analytic techniques」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节）。与之呼应，温尼科特本人对自己的"个人诠释需要"有严厉自责：「It appalls me to think how much deep change I have prevented or delayed … by my personal need to interpret」（引自 Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节）。
> **备注**：可调和。二者处理的是不同层次的问题：戈夫林描述的是**治疗文化的社会学事实**（诊所里确实存在两套做法），奥格登限定的是**概念的逻辑地位**（不应被硬化为宗派标识）。亚德林-加多特提供了第三种表述——诸理论"不是相互竞争，而是构成一条与心灵的认识论多元性平行的理论弧"（*Truth Axes*，「结论」）。
