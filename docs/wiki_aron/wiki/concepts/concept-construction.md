# 建构（construction）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**建构**是弗洛伊德（Sigmund Freud）在 1937 年论文《分析中的建构》（Constructions in Analysis）中提出的概念，用来填补诠释所覆盖不到的临床地带。诠释处理的是**当下浮现的无意识衍生物**——口误、梦的片段、抗拒的瞬间；建构则进入一个**没有直接衍生物存在**的领域——婴儿期遗忘在心理记录中造成的真实缺口。

分析师面对的不是被压抑的完整记忆，而是**碎片**：性格特质、症状、移情模式、抗拒本身的形状。建构就是对这些碎片所作的**考古学式重构**。它的关键特征是**推测性**：分析师提供的不是确定的真理，而是关于病人被埋藏历史的一份**工作假设**。建构无法被病人的记忆所证实——病人的「是」可能是虚伪的顺从，「否」可能不表示错误而只表示不完整；它的真理只能**间接地**显现于随后的联想、环绕建构情境的梦、以及分析氛围的微妙变化。

Govrin 因此指出，建构的宿命是退居**叙事真理（narrative truth）**：它指向历史真理，却必须满足于叙事真理。而 Yadlin-Gadot 的分析进一步显示，弗洛伊德在《建构》中同时动用了**融贯真理（Coherent truth）**与**实用真理（Pragmatic truth）**两套判准，并把「信念的实践后果」与「其假定的客观真值」划上了新的等号。

## 一、为什么需要建构：诠释覆盖不到的临床现实

Govrin 把建构的提出定位为弗洛伊德对两个前期发现的回应——自我的防御具有适应功能，而有些病人的最早经验**根本没有可及的记忆痕迹**。

> But if the ego’s defenses served adaptive functions, and if interpretation needed to support rather than assault these psychological structures, what happened when analysts encountered patients whose earliest experiences had left no accessible memory traces? Freud’s late work on construction represents his most ambitious attempt to address this clinical reality—the recognition that some therapeutic work requires building new psychic structures rather than simply uncovering old ones.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「From Interpretation to Construction: Freud’s Archaeological Vision」

【原文】定义性的转折在这一句：治疗工作有时要求**建立**新的心理结构，而非**揭开**旧的结构。这使建构从一开始就不是「更大规模的诠释」，而是**另一种**工作。

在后记中，Govrin 把考古学模型的内在张力概括为：

> Freud’s archaeological model, with its emphasis on making the unconscious conscious, coexisted uneasily with his growing recognition that insight alone rarely produces lasting change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记「The Interpretive Paradox」

## 二、诠释与建构的分界

这是本概念最核心的划界。Govrin 的表述是双重的：范围上的分界，与性质上的分界。

### 2.1 范围：单个材料 vs. 整段被遗忘的历史

> Reading Freud’s 1937 paper “Constructions in Analysis” reveals a profound shift in his thinking about psychoanalytic technique— one that moves beyond the familiar territory of interpretation into something more ambitious and architectonic. Where interpretation deals with the accessible derivatives of the unconscious, construction attempts something far more audacious: the reconstruction of an entire forgotten world.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

两者的对象被分别刻画：

> Interpretation, he suggests, addresses what emerges in the here and now of the session—the slip, the dream fragment, the moment of resistance. It works with what surfaces, with what the unconscious permits to escape through its various disguises.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

> Construction, by contrast, ventures into territory where no direct derivatives exist, where infantile amnesia has created genuine gaps in the psychic record. The analyst becomes not merely a decoder of symbols but an archaeologist of the mind, piecing together fragments to reconstruct entire civilizations of experience that have been buried beneath layers of repression.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】「诠释」的动词是 **deal with / works with**（处理浮现之物）；「建构」的动词是 **ventures into**（进入无路之地）。这一动词差异是本页区分二者的文本依据。

### 2.2 性质：技术之分还是哲学之分

> Freud’s distinction between these two modes of analytic work is not merely technical but philosophical.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】这一点决定了后续两节：建构的推测性（认识论）与建构的真理类型（真理理论）都不是技术细节，而是哲学承诺。

### 2.3 与考古学的同与异

弗洛伊德自己使用了考古学隐喻，但他同时指出了一个关键的**不对称**：

> Yet Freud notes a crucial difference: unlike the archaeologist who deals with ruins where essential pieces have been irretrievably lost, the psychoanalyst works with a psyche where everything is somehow preserved, waiting to be reconstructed.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】这个不对称是双刃的：一方面它保证了重构的**可能性**（一切都被保存）；另一方面它取消了「残损」这一现成的借口——既然一切都在，无法重建就只能归因于**方法**，而非材料。

## 三、建构的方法：侦探式重构

既然没有直接衍生物，建构只能从**间接迹象**出发：

> This preservation, however, doesn’t mean easy access. The analyst must work like a detective assembling a case from circumstantial evidence—character traits, symptoms, patterns of transference, the very shape of the patient’s resistances. From these indirect signs, the analyst constructs a narrative of what must have been, offering it to the patient not as definitive truth but as a working hypothesis about their buried history.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】此处即「推测性」的文本根据：**不是确定的真理，而是一份工作假设**。

Govrin 用「历史小说家」这一比喻进一步刻画这种工作的虚构维度：

> The analyst must then become a kind of historical novelist, creating plausible narratives that, while they may never achieve the status of recovered memory, can nonetheless produce therapeutic effects through their explanatory power.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「The Living Legacy」

## 四、建构为什么必然是推测性的：病人的回应不可作为判准

如果建构可以被病人的记忆证实，它就不成其为建构。弗洛伊德的处理方式被 Govrin 概括为**双重不可信**：

> What’s particularly fascinating is Freud’s handling of the patient’s response to constructions. Neither simple agreement nor disagreement can be taken at face value. A “yes” might be hypocritical compliance; a “no” might indicate not error but incompleteness.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

既然直接判准失效，真理只能**间接地**显现：

> The truth of a construction reveals itself obliquely—through subsequent associations, through dreams that circle around the constructed scenario, through subtle shifts in the analytic atmosphere.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

### 4.1 「超清晰」记忆的悖论

弗洛伊德描述了一类特殊时刻：建构触发了所谓「超清晰（ultra-clear）」的记忆——但记住的**不是**被建构的事件本身，而是**外围细节**：

> Freud describes those uncanny moments when a construction triggers what he calls “ultra-clear” memories—not of the constructed event itself, but of peripheral details: the wallpaper in a room, the face of a bystander, the furniture arrangement. These vivid but displaced recollections suggest that the construction has touched something real, even if that reality can only be approached sideways, through its atmospheric residue rather than direct recall.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】「只能从侧面接近」是本节的核心：证据的强度与证据的**相关性**成反比——记忆越鲜明，它离被建构的核心事件越远。

## 五、建构与真理类型

### 5.1 Govrin：历史真理 → 叙事真理

Govrin 把弗洛伊德在《建构》中的认识论立场概括为一次**退守**，并称之为「认识论上的谦逊」：

> The epistemological modesty of Freud’s position here is striking. Constructions aim at historical truth but must settle for narrative truth—for stories that make sense of symptoms and suffering even if they cannot claim to represent exactly what happened. The analyst offers these constructions tentatively, ready to revise them as new material emerges, understanding that the goal is not archaeological accuracy but therapeutic efficacy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

> In this vision, psychoanalysis becomes a collaborative act of world-building, where analyst and patient together construct a past that can make sense of the present. The construction doesn’t recover the past so much as create a usable version of it—one that allows the patient to understand their suffering as historical rather than simply irrational, as having origins even if those origins can never be fully verified.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】「建构与其说是**恢复**过去，不如说是创造出过去的一个**可用版本**」——这是本页对「建构无法被记忆证实，只能退居叙事真理」最直接的引文支撑。

### 5.2 Yadlin-Gadot：融贯真理与实用真理的双重动用

关于用户所问的「弗洛伊德在《建构》中同时动用了融贯真理与实用真理」——**这一具体区分在 Govrin 书中并未出现**；Govrin 只给出「历史真理 / 叙事真理」这一对。该区分出自 Yadlin-Gadot 的真理轴框架，其依据正是弗洛伊德 1937 年原文中的一句话：

> 6. As with many contemporary ideas, both referenced Freud in the development of their thinking. In 1937 Freud states that “an assured conviction of the truth of [the] construction... achieves the same therapeutic result as a recaptured memory” (p. 265). Freud engages here two notions of truth: the Coherent truth, appertaining to the fit between a newly formed construction and earlier conceptions held by the patient (“assured conviction”), and the Pragmatic truth, in which the construction is examined according to its therapeutic consequences (“the same therapeutic results”). Freud forms here a new equation between the practical implications of a belief and its presumed objective truth-value.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，注释 6

（说明：原始 PDF 转换文本在 development、result、assured、therapeutic 四处有跨行断词连字符，此处按通行引文惯例连写，未改动任何字母。）

【原文】三个要点：
1. **融贯真理（Coherent truth）**——判准是新形成的建构与病人既有观念之间的**契合度**，即引文中的「assured conviction（确证的信念）」。
2. **实用真理（Pragmatic truth）**——判准是建构的**治疗后果**，即「同样的治疗结果」。
3. 弗洛伊德在此**建立了一个新的等式**：把信念的**实践意涵**与其**假定的客观真值**等同起来。

第三条是 Yadlin-Gadot 的批评性观察，也是本概念最容易被误读之处：弗洛伊德并非放弃了历史真理，而是**用疗效为信念的真值背书**。

### 5.3 Spence：建构如何滑向重建

Yadlin-Gadot 引 Spence（1982）说明这一滑动的心理机制：

> To the extent that a narrative is persuasive and compelling, it ac- quires features of what might be called *narrative truth*. Goodness of fit seems particularly significant in bringing about this change.

— Spence (1982, p. 181)，转引自 Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，注释 7

> As soon as that step is taken, the *construction* becomes a *reconstruction*—a piece of the past that is taken to be as real as the name of the patient’s father or the date of his birth. (p. 181)

— Spence (1982, p. 181)，转引自 Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，注释 7

【原文】「建构一旦迈出那一步，就变成了**重建（reconstruction）**」——被当作与病人父亲的名字或出生日期一样真实的过去片段。这是建构的推测性被**遗忘**的机制，也是其最大的认识论风险。

## 六、历史真理的回归：弗洛伊德并未放弃

Govrin 特别强调：即使在建构中，弗洛伊德也没有放弃历史真理的诉求。

> This again emphasizes that even in constructions, Freud did not give up on historical truth and claimed that if we don’t unearth it, it will make its own comeback through repetition-compulsion.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【原文】这句话是理解《建构》之**双重性**的钥匙：退守叙事真理是一种方法论让步，但不是本体论放弃。被压抑之物若不被发掘，会通过**强迫性重复**自行回归。

### 6.1 妄想与幻觉中的历史真理

论文最富挑衅性的一步，是把建构与妄想、幻觉联系起来：

> The paper’s most provocative turn comes when Freud connects constructions to delusions and hallucinations. Perhaps, he suggests, delusions contain fragments of historical truth—not personal history but the “archaic heritage” of the species. The delusional person, like the analysand responding to a construction, may be struggling to articulate a truth that has no other means of expression. This isn’t to validate the delusion’s manifest content but to recognize that even in madness, there may be method—and memory.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

### 6.2 集体妄想

论文结尾的类比：

> This is perhaps why Freud ends with that enigmatic connection to collective delusions—those shared human beliefs that, despite contradicting reality, hold extraordinary power over us. These too, he suggests, may derive their force from fragments of historical truth, from the species’ own repressed memories. The implication is that construction, whether individual or collective, serves a fundamental human need: to create narratives that connect us to our origins, even when—especially when—those origins remain forever beyond the reach of direct recollection.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

## 七、时机问题：建构之前的技术条件

建构虽是「大动作」，但弗洛伊德的技术论文显示他对**时机**近乎偏执：

> In his technical papers, Freud became almost obsessive about timing. The analyst must wait, he insisted, until the patient “has come so near to the repressed material that he has only a few more steps to take under the lead of the interpretation you propose” (Freud, 1913, p. 140). Too early, and the interpretation bounces off the patient’s defenses like a tennis ball off a brick wall. Too late, and you’ve missed the moment when insight might have made a difference.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「The Question of Timing」

剂量原则与「淹没」警告：

> Freud warned against “flooding” the patient with interpretations. It was preferable to interpret in small, manageable doses, focusing on surface material and resistance before delving into deep content. “It is … advisable to begin the interpretation of the resistance at the point where it is most easily and clearly manifested” (Freud, 1923, p. 256).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

## 八、伦理后果：建构与认识论谦逊

Govrin 明确指出，从诠释到建构的演化「具有弗洛伊德本人只是刚开始探索的深刻伦理意涵」：

> Yet questions of technique inevitably raise questions of ethics. The evolution from interpretation to construction, from archaeological excavation to collaborative meaning-making, reflects a fundamental shift in how psychoanalysis conceives the relationship between analyst and patient. Freud did not go that far, but his shift had profound ethical implications that Freud himself was only beginning to explore.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

> This ethical stance requires what we might call “epistemological humility”—the confidence to offer interpretations combined with the humility to hold them lightly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

【综合】建构是这一伦理要求的最尖锐场景：当分析师提供的是**整段历史的重构**而非单个材料的说明时，其权力不对称被放大到最大；而「工作假设」的自我定位正是唯一的约束机制。参见 Epistemological Humility。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：建构的真理主张究竟应归还给历史真理，还是应满足于叙事真理？
> **A 方立场**：弗洛伊德（据 Govrin 读法）——建构**指向**历史真理，只是**必须满足于**叙事真理；若不去发掘，被压抑之物会通过强迫性重复自行回归——「even in constructions, Freud did not give up on historical truth and claimed that if we don’t unearth it, it will make its own comeback through repetition-compulsion」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章）
> **B 方立场**：弗洛伊德（据 Yadlin-Gadot 读法）——他在《建构》中实际上用**融贯真理与实用真理**替换了历史真理，并把信念的实践后果与其客观真值划上等号——「Freud forms here a new equation between the practical implications of a belief and its presumed objective truth-value」（Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*）
> **备注**：这不一定是逻辑矛盾，而可能是**同一文本的两个层面**：Govrin 描述的是弗洛伊德的**意图**（仍以历史真理为目标），Yadlin-Gadot 描述的是其**论证结构**（实际以融贯与实用为准）。Spence 的观察（建构滑向重建）为 B 方提供了心理机制上的支持。属**可部分调和**的分歧，但调和需以承认「弗洛伊德在此处的论证弱于其宣称」为代价。
