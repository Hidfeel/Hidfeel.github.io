# 突变性诠释（mutative interpretation）

> **类型**: concept
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

**突变性诠释**是詹姆斯·斯特雷奇（James Strachey）在 1934 年论文《精神分析的治疗行动之本质》（The Nature of the Therapeutic Action of Psychoanalysis）中提出的概念，用以区分两类诠释：一类只是**告知**病人其无意识内容（斯特雷奇贬称为「字典式诠释（dictionary interpretation）」），另一类则真正引起**结构性改变**。突变性诠释属于后者。

其机制核心在于：在移情的**紧急关头（point of urgency）**，分析师被病人内摄为一个与其严苛超我运作原则**不同**的客体，即「**辅助超我（auxiliary superego）**」。由于辅助超我的「建议始终基于真实且当下的考量」，病人得以在「古旧期待」与「分析师的实际回应」之间感知到落差，从而在神经症的恶性循环中打开一个缺口。斯特雷奇强调，改变由无数微小修正累积而成，而非由戏剧性洞见造成；诠释必须遵守「最适量」原则。

本概念是「诠释如何带来改变」这一问题在古典框架内最精密的回答，也因此成为后世所有修正与批评的参照点：它预设了分析师能够相对中立地站在病人投射系统之外，而这一预设在关系取向与场域取向中已不再成立。

## 一、问题的提出：从「告知」到「转化」

斯特雷奇 1934 年的论文被 Govrin 列为诠释思想史上五座里程碑中的第一座，其定位是「隔离出治疗改变之精确机制」的尝试：

> What actually happens in the moment when interpretation transforms suffering into understanding, repetition into recognition, deadness into aliveness? This question—deceptively simple yet endlessly complex—has haunted psychoanalysis since its inception.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Essential Writings on Interpretation」开篇

> These contributions span nearly a century, from Strachey’s 1934 attempt to isolate the precise mechanism of therapeutic change to Stern’s 2023 reconceptualization of interpretation as the voice of an interpersonal field. Together, they trace a remarkable journey: from certainty to uncertainty, from technique to relationship, from knowing to being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章开篇

斯特雷奇论文的持久价值，被 Govrin 概括为一个根本悖论——分析师必须**同时**进入与独立于病人的内在世界：

> James Strachey’s 1934 paper “The Nature of the Therapeutic Action of Psychoanalysis” stands as one of the most influential works in psychoanalytic literature. What makes this contribution enduring is not merely its systematic approach, but its recognition of a fundamental paradox: therapeutic change requires the analyst to become temporarily part of the patient’s internal world while simultaneously remaining sufficiently separate to offer something genuinely new. This delicate balance lies at the heart of what Strachey termed “mutative interpretation.”

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节「The Classical Foundation—Strachey’s Mechanical Precision」

这个「暂时的内部性 + 足够的分离性」的双重位置，正是「辅助超我」这一机制得以成立的前提。

### 1.1 「字典式诠释」与突变性诠释之分

斯特雷奇的原创性首先在于一条否定性的划界：告诉病人他无意识地做了什么，本身毫无治疗力。

> The originality of Strachey’s contribution lies in his distinction between interpretations that merely inform and those that actually transform. Anyone can tell a patient what they are doing unconsciously—this is what he dismissively called “dictionary” interpretation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】这一区分构成了本概念的定义性边界：突变性不是「更正确的诠释」，而是**另一种运作方式**的诠释。

## 二、核心机制：辅助超我（auxiliary superego）

### 2.1 什么是辅助超我

突变性诠释之所以能带来结构改变，在于它引入了一个被暂时内摄的、按不同原则运作的内在形象：

> But mutative interpretation operates through a more complex process involving what Strachey called an “auxiliary superego,” a temporarily introjected figure who operates according to different principles than the patient’s harsh internal critics (Strachey, 1934, p. 127).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

其生成条件是分析师的**实际行为**与病人残存的现实感：

> Given the analyst’s real behavior and the patient’s minimum contact with reality, the patient will incorporate the analyst as an object that differs from the rest—the auxiliary superego.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

而辅助超我区别于病人古旧超我的关键特征，Govrin 直接引用了斯特雷奇原文：

> This auxiliary superego differs from the patient’s archaic superego in crucial ways. Most importantly, as Strachey notes, “the most important characteristic of the auxiliary super-ego is that its advice to the ego is consistently based upon real and contemporary considerations and this in itself serves to differentiate it from the greater part of the original super-ego” (Strachey, 1934, p. 140).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】换言之：辅助超我的权威来源不是禁忌与惩罚，而是**现实与当下**。

### 2.2 「你可以说」对「你不许说」

斯特雷奇以基本规则（fundamental rule）本身作为辅助超我运作的范例：

> The fundamental rule itself exemplifies this difference. The auxiliary superego authorizes the patient to say everything that comes into his head—what might be called the abolition of rejection. In this way the new superego (“thou may say”) functions in the opposite direction to the old (“thou must not say”).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】这一对偶式（thou may say / thou must not say）是理解「松动严苛内在结构」的具体路径：改变发生在**禁令的中止**，而非**内容的替换**。

### 2.3 神经症的恶性循环与「缺口」

斯特雷奇借克莱因关于投射与内摄的洞见，同时解释了神经症的维持机制与疗愈机制：

> Drawing on Melanie Klein’s insights about projection and introjection, Strachey explains both the mechanism of neurosis and its cure. The neurotic becomes trapped in what he calls a “vicious circle” where projected sadism creates increasingly dangerous internal objects, which in turn provoke more defensive sadism.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

突变性诠释的作用点，是在这个循环上「打出一个缺口」：

> During the second phase, the patient recognizes the difference between their archaic expectations and the analyst’s actual response, creating what Strachey called “a breach in the neurotic vicious circle” (Strachey, 1934, p. 159).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

## 三、两阶段过程

斯特雷奇把突变性诠释描述为两个阶段，但二者不必按顺序发生：

> Strachey describes mutative interpretation as consisting of two phases, though these need not occur sequentially and can happen simultaneously. Neither phase is simple and both can be complex, but from a genetic point of view, they will always exist.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

整个理论的枢纽被表述为「两件事同时被意识到」：

> The key to the theory is that the patient should become conscious of two things: an instinctive impulse and an object that does not tally with that impulse.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

**阶段一**：先前的无意识本我冲动或其衍生物，指向分析师而成为意识。

> During the first phase, a previously unconscious id impulse or its derivative becomes conscious, directed toward the analyst. This may occur directly and spontaneously, but more often the analyst intervenes with a series of interpretations. The ego defense, the superego censure, and the instinctive impulse must be interpreted in various ways until the derivative reaches consciousness and anxiety is mobilized in quantities which must always be moderate.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】注意「焦虑的量必须始终是**中等的**」——这是剂量原则在阶段一的具体化。

**阶段二**：依赖病人的现实感，使其能够比较真实客体与古旧（被移情的）客体。

> The second phase depends crucially on the patient’s sense of reality, enabling comparison between the real object and the archaic (transferred) one. This comparison is always extremely uncertain—the patient may at any moment turn the real object (analyst) into the archaic one.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】第二阶段因此是**结构上脆弱**的：病人随时可能把分析师重新变成古旧客体。

### 3.1 设置（setting）的决定性地位

正因第二阶段如此脆弱，分析设置的稳定性成为突变性诠释的必要条件：

> Here the decisive importance of the analytic setting becomes apparent. If the analyst strays from the setting and behaves inappropriately, the immediate response will be the patient’s inclusion of the analyst in the series of good or bad archaic objects, thereby disqualifying the analyst and preventing the second phase.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】这把「设置」从技术惯例提升为**治疗机制的结构性部件**：分析师一旦越界，就丧失了作为「不同于其余者的客体」的资格。

## 四、突变性诠释的三项形式特征

斯特雷奇给出三条判准，构成古典技术的操作定义。

### 4.1 即时性（immediate）

> Strachey outlines three essential characteristics of mutative interpretation. First, it must be immediate—applied to an impulse in a cathected state. An interpretation which informs a patient of the existence of an impulse which is not present can never be mutative, although it may be useful in preparing the ground. A necessary condition will always be that the interpretation relates to an emotion that the patient experiences as actual.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】此处「即时」的含义是**冲动正处于被投注的状态**，而非「抢先说出」。这与克莱因学派的「即时诠释（immediate interpretation）」共享词汇但语义不同——后者的「即时」指不必等待关系准备期即可直指深层焦虑。参见 [[concepts/concept-immediate-interpretation]]。

### 4.2 具体性（specific）

> Second, the interpretation must be specific—detailed and concrete rather than vague and general. While broad interpretations may be necessary when tackling new subjects, until we manage to circumscribe the material and put into focus all the relevant details, we can never expect a mutative effect. The interpretation must adapt itself exactly to what is happening; it must be concrete and limited to the point in question.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

### 4.3 渐进性（progressive）

> Third, mutative interpretation must be progressive, proceeding in well-graduated steps. The interpretation must hold to the principle of optimum quantity, because otherwise either the first phase will not be reached or the second will become impossible. Strachey teaches us to distrust hasty interpretations that attempt to skip stages. Upheavals are not mutations, and great changes turn out to be short-lived and suggestive in nature.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】「激变不是突变（Upheavals are not mutations）」是本节最关键的一句：戏剧性的情绪爆发被明确排除在结构性改变之外，因其本质是暗示性的（suggestive）。

剂量原则的双向约束被表述为：

> We can only integrate as much change as our psychic equilibrium can bear. Push too hard, and the patient’s defenses mobilize to preserve the status quo. Offer too little, and nothing shifts at all. The interpretation must achieve optimal dosage—enough to mobilize unconscious material but not so much as to overwhelm the patient’s capacity to process the experience. As Strachey emphasizes, the mutative effect depends entirely on this careful calibration of psychological forces.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

也因此，改变的基本单位是**微小的**：

> True therapeutic change, he argued, occurs through countless small modifications rather than dramatic insights.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

## 五、为什么只有移情性诠释才有突变性

这是斯特雷奇立场中最强硬、也最具争议的部分。他的原话被 Govrin 完整引出：

> One of Strachey’s most significant contributions lies in his evaluation of extra- transference interpretation. His position is unequivocal: “Is it to be understood that no extra-transference interpretation can set in motion the chain of events which I have suggested as being the essence of psychoanalytical therapy? That is indeed my opinion” (Strachey, 1934, p. 154).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

理由是双重的：缺少情绪荷载，且**客体不在场**。

### 5.1 情绪荷载的缺失

> Strachey’s emphasis on transference interpretation as the only truly mutative form has profoundly influenced psychoanalytic technique. Extra-transference interpretations, while useful as “feeders,” cannot produce the same structural changes because they lack the immediate emotional charge necessary for genuine transformation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

### 5.2 客体在场与「紧急关头」

真正的理由更为结构性——只有在移情诠释中，本我冲动的**对象**才在场：

> The essential difference lies in the fact that it is only in transference interpretation that the object of the id impulse is present. This makes it difficult for an extra-transference interpretation to be given at the point of urgency, and even if accomplished, it remains problematic whether the patient will be able to establish the difference between the real (absent) object and the fantasy object. An extra-transference interpretation will therefore always be less effective and more risky.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】这是「紧急关头（point of urgency）」一词在本书中的出处之一。它同时出现在比昂处——比昂把诠释定义为「在紧急关头说出的话（a spoken interpretation at the point of urgency）」。两者共享一个结构：**只有当冲动正在被投注于分析师的此刻，比较才有可能发生**。参见 [[concepts/concept-containment-reverie-alpha]]。

> Only in the here-and-now relationship with the analyst can the patient experience the crucial discrepancy between expectation and reality that enables new internal structures to form.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

## 六、当代的批评与修正

### 6.1 机制论语言与科学乐观主义

Govrin 记录了两类主要批评：

> However, contemporary analysts have also noted limitations in Strachey’s approach (Caper, 1995; Hepburn, 2021). Some argue that his mechanistic language reflects the scientific optimism of his era and may not capture the full complexity of the therapeutic relationship. Others suggest that factors beyond interpretation—including the analyst’s personality and the quality of the therapeutic relationship—play more significant roles than Strachey’s theory acknowledges.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

【原文】第二类批评直接冲击了本概念的核心主张：如果治疗行动的主要来源是**分析师的人格与关系品质**，那么「诠释的形式特征（即时 / 具体 / 渐进）」就不再是充分条件。

相关文献（第七章参考文献）包括 Caper 的《论作出突变性诠释之困难》（On The Difficulty Of Making A Mutative Interpretation, 1995）与 Hepburn 的《斯特雷奇的阴影》（Strachey’s Shadow: A Re-Examination of the Use of the Mutative Interpretation, 2021）。

### 6.2 中立性与外部性预设的瓦解

斯特雷奇模型隐含一个位置：分析师能够**同时**进入病人的内在世界、又足够分离以提供新东西。关系取向与场域取向拒绝的正是这个「足够分离」的可实现性。

> The approach challenges classical notions of interpretive neutrality, positioning the analyst as embedded participant rather than detached observer.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节「The Relational Synthesis—Interpretation as Emergent Property」（论 Stern）

> Drawing on Edgar Levenson’s insight that psychoanalysis is the attempt to grasp what analyst and patient are “up to” in their relationship, Stern recognizes that analysts cannot separate themselves from the patterns they observe (Stern, 2023, p. 1132). Every time they speak, they are interacting from within the same configurations they wish to name. This challenges the traditional view of the analyst as objective observer, positioning them instead as participant-observer caught up in the very dynamics they seek to understand.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节

【综合】若分析师不可能站在其所命名的配置之外，那么「辅助超我」就失去了其成立条件：它要求病人内摄的是一个**按其自身运作原则行事、而非被病人的投射系统所捕获**的客体。在场域理论中，分析师恰恰持续地被「套索」进病人的投射系统（参见 [[concepts/concept-analytic-act-of-freedom]] 中 Symington 的 x-phenomenon）。

### 6.3 第二个里程碑的反转：诠释之前的东西

Govrin 把斯特雷奇与西明顿（Neville Symington）的对照，写成了对突变性诠释最尖锐的内部批评——最重要的治疗行动可能发生在**任何诠释被说出之前**：

> Strachey showed us the precision of mutative interpretation, only for Symington to reveal that the most important therapeutic action might occur before any interpretation is spoken.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章结语「The Paradox of Therapeutic Action」

> If Strachey provided the mechanics of therapeutic change, Neville Symington revealed something more elusive yet perhaps more fundamental: the analyst’s internal freedom as the precondition for any meaningful interpretation. This shift from technique to the analyst’s state of mind marked a crucial turn in psychoanalytic thinking, one that would eventually lead to the relational revolution.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第二节「The Turn to Subjectivity—Inner Freedom as Therapeutic Action」

【原文】转向的实质是：治疗行动的载体从「诠释的形式」移到了「分析师的主体状态」。

### 6.4 五座里程碑的并列：辅助超我只是其中之一

Govrin 的结语把「辅助超我」降格为**对同一过程的五种命名之一**，这是最彻底的修正姿态：

> What remains constant across these contributions is the recognition that something happens in psychoanalysis that exceeds our theoretical understanding. Whether we call it the auxiliary superego, the act of freedom, the reading of breakdown, ontological recognition, or the voice of the field, each theorist points toward a process that is simultaneously precise and mysterious, technical and deeply human.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章结语

> Perhaps this is interpretation’s essential paradox: it works best when we understand least why it works. The moment we think we’ve mastered interpretive technique, we’ve likely lost touch with what makes interpretation transformative.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章结语

### 6.5 五十周年与九十周年：持续的效力

尽管有上述批评，斯特雷奇框架的影响力被证实为长期的。Etchegoyen 在原始论文发表五十年后写道：

> Strachey’s framework has continued to exert a remarkable level of influence over decades of psychoanalytic development. Etchegoyen (1983), writing fifty years after the publication of the original paper, notes that “together with many other psychoanalysts, I consider Strachey’s contribution to be of very real importance, and that his influence is still extremely strong”

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节

而 Govrin 本人的收束判断是：

> Despite these critiques, Strachey’s fundamental insights remain clinically relevant. His careful distinction between interpretations that inform and those that transform continues to guide analytic practice. His emphasis on the primacy of transference interpretation has shaped generations of analysts’ understanding of therapeutic technique. Most importantly, his recognition that structural change occurs through countless small modifications rather than dramatic breakthroughs provides essential guidance for clinical work.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节
