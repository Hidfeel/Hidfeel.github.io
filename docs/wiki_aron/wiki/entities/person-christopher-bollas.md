> **创建时间**: 2026-08-28
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-28

## 摘要

在「诠释」议题上，克里斯托弗·博拉斯给出了本批资料中**最极端也最不设防的立场**：**崩溃（breakdown）不是有待消除的病理，而是有待诠释的文本**。看似精神崩溃的现象，实则是心智最精密的交流尝试——一种把从未被精神表征过的东西说出来的绝望努力。因此，诠释的对象不只是患者说了什么，更是**崩溃本身**在诉说什么。

他的技术后果是三项互相锁定的主张。**其一，抱持即诠释**：分析师为精神组织正在崩解的患者营造抱持环境的那种全然投入，其本身就是诠释在运作，而非诠释的前奏。**其二，设置即诠释**：治疗契约本身（每日会谈两次、不额外收费）传达着分析师对事态严重性的承认，是诠释的一部分。**其三，诠释即一种爱**：被知晓就是在生命的关键时刻被爱；每一个诠释性步骤都构成他所谓「精神分析性抱持（psychoanalytical holding）」的一部分。

他据此**反对住院与强化药物治疗**——这些路径把崩解当作需要抑制的病理，而非需要理解的交流，从而否定了崩溃的转化潜能。这使他的立场在本 Wiki 的真理问题上占据一个特殊位置：他既不属于求真传统（他不发掘被压抑的真相），也不属于温尼科特式的「不诠释」，而是把**诠释的赌注推到极限**——主张「有些患者需要的是愿意赌上一切的分析师」。

## 一、资料定位：第七章五篇「核心文献」之一

博拉斯在本批资料中**没有专章**。他作为 Govrin 第七章所细读的五篇经典文献之一出现，另四篇是斯特雷奇（1934）、西明顿（1983）、奥格登（2019）与斯特恩（2023）：

> Chapter 7 steps back to examine five essential writings that have shaped contemporary thinking about interpretation: James Strachey, “The Nature of the Therapeutic Action of Psychoanalysis” (1934); Neville Symington, “The Analyst’s Act of Freedom as Agent of Therapeutic Change” (1983); Christopher Bollas, “Catch Them Before They Fall: The Psychoanalysis of Breakdown” (2013); Thomas Ogden, “Ontological Psychoanalysis or ‘What Do You Want to Be When You Grow Up?’” (2019); and Donnel Stern, “Interpretation: Voice of the Field” (2023).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论（章节概览）

Govrin 对这五篇的整体判断是：**它们不是在回答问题，而是在层层叠加问题的复杂性**——从确定性到不确定性、从技术到关系、从知到在。

博拉斯在这一序列中的**具体位置**，由 Govrin 转入第三部分时的一句话给出：西明顿关心的是把分析师从投射性纠缠中解放出来以便清晰思考，而博拉斯把这份解放推往一个**激进的方向**：

> While Symington focused on freeing the analyst from projective entanglements to think clearly, Christopher Bollas takes this liberation in a radical direction: the analyst’s freedom enables complete devotion to creating a holding environment for patients whose psychic organization is collapsing, with this devoted containment itself functioning as interpretation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**「那份投入的涵容本身就在起着诠释的作用（this devoted containment itself functioning as interpretation）」**——这是「抱持即诠释」命题在 Govrin 文本中的原始表述，也是本页第三节与第六节全部论证的出发点。

就篇幅而言，博拉斯在 Govrin 全书中位于第 128–130 页（索引条目：「Bollas, Christopher <u>128</u>, <u>129</u>, <u>134</u>」；「breakdowns, interpretation during <u>128–130</u>」），属**中等密度的一节**。本页因此只记录他在「诠释」议题上的立场，不构成对其全部著作的概述。

## 二、★核心命题：崩溃是有待诠释的文本

这是博拉斯在「诠释」议题上交出的、也是本页的**关键定义**：

> Bollas’s approach to patients on the verge of breakdown fundamentally challenges conventional psychiatric wisdom. What appears as mental collapse, he suggests, may actually constitute the psyche’s most sophisticated attempt at communication—a desperate effort to make known what has never been mentally represented. The breakdown becomes not pathology to be eliminated but text to be interpreted, not a failure to function but the beginning of a profound conversation that has been waiting decades to occur.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

这一命题的三个分句各自承担一项理论工作：

| 分句 | 对「诠释」议题的意义 |
|---|---|
| 崩溃是「心智最精密的交流尝试」 | 症状／崩解被重新归类为**能指**，而非**故障**；诠释的适用范围被大幅扩张 |
| 「把从未被精神表征过的东西说出来」 | 诠释的对象是**前表征的**经验，因此诠释不是解码，而是**首次赋形** |
| 「有待诠释的文本」而非「有待消除的病理」 | 治疗目标从**消除**改为**阅读**；诠释成为**与崩溃合作**而非对抗它的手段 |

【推断】这一命题与 [[concepts/concept-interpretation]] 页所记「诠释不是解码而是创造」的主线一致，但把它推到了**危机临床**这一最不利的检验场：如果连崩溃都能被读成文本，则诠释的边界实际上等于精神生活的边界。（本节表格与推断无直接引文支撑，属【推断】。）

## 三、★设置即诠释：治疗契约作为诠释行为

博拉斯的技术创新不在语词层面，而在**框架层面**。当崩溃威胁到已在分析中的患者时，他提出一份非凡的治疗契约：

> This involves offering patients already in analysis an extraordinary therapeutic contract when breakdown threatens. He proposes meeting twice daily at no extra cost—a radical departure from conventional practice that reflects his understanding that patients on the verge of collapse cannot cope with additional stressors, particularly the anxiety of accumulating debt during their most vulnerable moments (Bollas, <u>2013</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**关键的一步是把经济安排本身读作诠释**：

> The financial arrangement itself becomes part of the interpretation: it communicates that the analyst recognizes the gravity of the situation and is willing to match the patient’s desperation with an equally desperate commitment to understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

契约要求**双方的完全投入**，而博拉斯在患者抗议时的坚持，被 Govrin 明确地**从「权威控制」一方摘出**：

> The contract demands total commitment from both parties. Patients must abandon all other obligations and give absolute priority to the treatment. When patients protest about missing important engagements, Bollas remains firm. The gravity of their condition requires absolute seriousness—a form of sanctuary where psychoanalysis can be intensified and the character of the breakdown allowed full articulation. This stringency is not authoritarian control but recognition that breakdown operates according to its own temporal logic that cannot be subordinated to social conventions.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

这一节的论证结构值得记下：**边界的改变不是权力的行使，而是对崩溃自身时间逻辑的承认**。换言之，设置不是诠释的容器，设置**就是**诠释。

## 四、★临床示例：Anna（pp. 46–59）

博拉斯 2013 年书中的 Anna 案例，是**「崩溃即文本」这一命题的临床执行方式**：

> Bollas’s (2013) clinical work demonstrates how interpretation during breakdown must operate simultaneously at multiple levels. In the case of Anna (pp. 46–59), a patient who had maintained a facade of perfection while harboring violent hatred toward her mother, Bollas offers interpretations that address both the content of her psychological crisis and the form of the breakdown itself. He tells her slowly and calmly that her breakdown was inevitable, that it represents the return of dissociated aspects of self that could no longer be kept at bay.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**诠释在此必须同时在两个层面运作**：危机的**内容**（对母亲的暴烈仇恨），与崩溃的**形式**（何以是此刻、何以以此种方式崩解）。

为什么形式层面的诠释不可或缺？Govrin 给出的理由是**二级恐慌**的存在：

> This communication proves essential because patients in breakdown must deal not only with the traumatic material that triggered the collapse but also with secondary panic over the fact of breaking down itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

【推断】这使得博拉斯的诠释在结构上**双轨**：一轨处理触发性创伤材料（求真传统的遗留），一轨处理「我正在崩溃」这一事实本身所引发的恐慌（温尼科特传统的遗留）。他不取消求真传统，而是把它嵌进一个更大的抱持框架之中。（本节末句为【推断】。）

## 五、★与温尼科特的分岔：从「存活」到「诠释性阅读」

博拉斯明确承继温尼科特关于**崩溃与突破（breakdown and breakthrough）**的思想，但把重心移了一格。Govrin 用一句话标出了这一分岔：

> Building on Winnicott’s revolutionary understanding that breakdown can contain the seeds of breakthrough, Bollas develops a more systematic approach to the interpretive challenges posed by patients in extremis. Where Winnicott emphasized the analyst’s survival of the patient’s destructive attacks, Bollas focuses on the interpretive dimension—reading the breakdown as meaningful text requiring specialized hermeneutic skills. The analyst must interpret not only what the patient says but what the collapse itself communicates about chronically unmet needs.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

| 维度 | 温尼科特 | 博拉斯 |
|---|---|---|
| 崩溃的意义 | 崩溃可含突破的种子 | 崩溃是有待诠释的文本 |
| 分析师的核心行动 | **存活**于患者的破坏性攻击 | **阅读**崩溃所传达的长期未被满足的需要 |
| 所需能力 | 抱持、持续存在、甘于不完美 | 「专业化的诠释学技能（specialized hermeneutic skills）」 |
| 与诠释的关系 | 诠释常是**不该使用**的工具 | 诠释被**推到极限**：连设置与崩溃本身都成为诠释对象 |

（表格为【综合】，引文见上文。）

**这是本批资料中关于「温尼科特之后诠释还能走多远」的最清楚的一次分岔记录**：同一位先驱，一位读出「少诠释」，另一位读出「诠释一切」。

## 六、★「诠释即一种爱」与精神分析性抱持

博拉斯在「诠释」议题上最被反复引用的命题，是 Govrin 转述的这一句：

> For Bollas, interpretation becomes a form of love—being known is being loved at a crucial time in one’s life. Each interpretive step constitutes part of what he calls “psychoanalytical holding.” People feel understood not simply through empathic presence but through the intelligent grasp the analyst has of why this particular person is in their particular predicament.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

这一命题包含**两个不可互换的成份**，其区分对理解博拉斯至为关键：

1. **被知晓即被爱**——赋予诠释一种**伦理性**而非仅仅技术性的地位；
2. **智性把握（intelligent grasp）**——被理解**不只是**通过共情性在场，而是通过分析师对「为什么偏偏是这个人处在偏偏这一种困境中」的把握。

**第二项成份使博拉斯区别于科胡特式的共情模型**（详见 [[entities/person-heinz-kohut]]）：他并未以共情**替换**诠释，而是坚持**理解的具体性**本身就是抱持。这正是他把「抱持」改写为「**精神分析性**抱持（psychoanalytical holding）」的用意——抱持不是一般的温情，而是**经由理解达成的**抱持。

Govrin 接着把这一立场的性质点明：它是一种**信（faith）**。

> This perspective requires extraordinary faith in the interpretive process itself—a conviction that understanding alone possesses sufficient therapeutic power to prevent psychological collapse.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**「单是理解就具有足以阻止精神崩解的治疗力量」**——这是本批资料中对诠释之效力所作的最强主张，也因此在下一节引出了它的代价。

## 七、★反对住院与强化药物治疗

博拉斯立场的**否定性一面**（他反对什么）与其肯定性命题同样规定着他的诠释观：

> His opposition to hospitalization and intensive medication reflects his belief that these approaches fundamentally misinterpret the nature of breakdown. By treating collapse as pathology requiring suppression rather than communication requiring understanding, conventional psychiatric responses negate the breakdown’s transformative potential.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**「根本性地误解了崩溃的性质（fundamentally misinterpret the nature of breakdown）」**——注意 Govrin 在此使用的是「误解（misinterpret）」一词：常规精神科并不是**不作诠释**，而是**作了错误的诠释**（把交流读成故障）。这是博拉斯与精神医学之间分歧的确切位置。

他的正面主张则是**抢先的诠释性干预**：

> Bollas positions himself as someone who can recognize the signs of impending collapse and intervene interpretively before the breakdown becomes irreversible—catching them, as the title of his 1983 book suggests, before they fall.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：崩溃面前，诠释性理解与精神科处置（住院、强化药物治疗）何者优先？
> **A 方立场**：博拉斯——把崩解当作**需要抑制的病理**而非**需要理解的交流**来对待，常规精神科的回应「**否定了崩溃的转化潜能**」；分析师应当抢在崩溃不可逆之前**诠释性地介入**。
> **B 方立场**：常规精神科共识（conventional psychiatric wisdom）——崩溃是需要被消除的病理；博拉斯的进路「**根本性地挑战**」了这一共识。
> **备注**：Govrin 的文本**只呈现博拉斯一方**，未给常规精神科任何代言性引文，因此这不是一场被对等地记录的争论，而是**一项单边立场的记录**。此外，本节所引句中「Bollas’s (2013) clinical work」与「the title of his 1983 book」在**同一节内前后不一致**（参考文献与索引均作 2013），疑为 Govrin 笔误；本页保留原文不作统一，标记为**存疑点**。

## 八、★代价：诠释性责任的边界

Govrin 没有把博拉斯写成英雄，而是立刻追问这一立场的**代价**——这是本页必须记录的限制性评论：

> This approach raises profound questions about the limits of interpretive responsibility. Some patients, Bollas suggests, require analysts willing to risk everything—their conventional technique, their professional boundaries, their personal comfort—in service of interpretive understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

**「愿意赌上一切——他们的常规技术、他们的专业边界、他们个人的舒适」**。就本 Wiki 的核心议题（分析师主体性与真理）而言，这句话把问题推到了极致：**当诠释被赋予无限的价值，分析师的边界就成了可以随时被献祭的东西**。

Govrin 的结论句把这一立场定性为**信的行为**：

> His work represents a profound act of faith in the transformative power of being deeply understood, even when understanding must be achieved at the very edge of psychological collapse.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」

【推断】「act of faith」这一措辞与 Govrin 为比昂所立的「诠释性信念（interpretive faith）」形成呼应——两条路径（一位主张放弃记忆与欲望以接近 O，一位主张赌上一切以读懂崩溃）在**「诠释最终不能被技术担保」**这一点上汇合。参见 [[entities/person-wilfred-bion]] 第九节。（本节末句为【推断】。）

## 九、与奥格登的对位：极限条件对不诠释

博拉斯在第七章中的位置，由他与紧随其后的奥格登之间的**对位关系**所规定。Govrin 在转入第四部分时写道：

> Where Bollas demonstrates interpretation under extreme conditions, Ogden questions whether interpretation, as traditionally conceived, might sometimes be precisely what prevents genuine therapeutic change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part IV: From Knowing to Being—The Ontological Turn」

而在全章结论中，这一对位被压缩成一句：

> Bollas demonstrated interpretation at the edge of breakdown, while Ogden suggested that sometimes not interpreting might be the most profound intervention.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Conclusion: The Paradox of Therapeutic Action」

**两人在第七章的叙事弧线中各占一端**：斯特雷奇给出机制，西明顿指出最关键的行动发生在任何诠释**之前**，博拉斯把诠释推到**最极端的条件**下，奥格登则怀疑诠释本身可能是**阻碍**。Govrin 明确拒绝把这四者综合为一个进步故事：

> Each paper discovers something essential that the others miss, and what emerges is not a synthesis but a productive tension— a recognition that interpretation works in multiple ways simultaneously, often contradicting our conscious intentions.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章（开篇段）

Govrin 为这五篇给出的收敛句，也与博拉斯「诠释即一种爱」的命题遥相呼应：

> This is perhaps why former patients remember not our brilliant interpretations but the moments when they felt truly seen—because in those moments, interpretation transcends technique and becomes recognition, witness, and ultimately, love.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Conclusion: The Paradox of Therapeutic Action」

## 十、Eshel 视角：博拉斯在「知识的转化」轴上

除 Govrin 外，本批资料中 Eshel 两篇文献各有一处提及博拉斯，足以定位他的学派坐标。

**其一，转化序列中的第二轴**。Eshel 把当代精神分析排成三条转化序列，博拉斯被归入**第二轴「知识的转化（Transformation in Knowledge）」**，与比昂、Marty、de M’Uzan、Botella 夫妇、奥格登、费罗同列：

> 2. Transformation in Knowledge—container-contained, reverie, dream-work, alpha-function (Bion, Marty, de M’Uzan, Bollas, Botella and Botella, Ogden, Ferro);

— Eshel, *Withness*，第一节（转化序列）

这一归类把博拉斯置于**涵容—被涵容、遐想、梦工作、α功能**这一簇概念之中，与他在 Govrin 处的形象（抱持、诠释、崩溃）高度一致；同时也意味着：在 Eshel 的三轴图式中，博拉斯**不在**最高的第三轴「转化于 O」之列。

**其二，作为温尼科特与比昂的整合者**。Eshel 在自述其思想养成时写道：

> With time, I added Racker’s and Grotstein’s contributions, and, more recently, Ogden, Bollas, and Eigen, who evocatively inte- grated Winnicott’s and Bion’s ideas into their own compelling writings.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*，第一节（个人与学派养成）

【推断】这两处共同支持本页第五节的判断：博拉斯的理论位置是**温尼科特与比昂之间的整合者**，其独特之处在于把这一整合落在**崩溃的临床**与**诠释的扩张**之上。（本节末句为【推断】。）

## 十一、另一个博拉斯：两种移情体裁与「不诠释」

Govrin 呈现的博拉斯是**积极、密集、以理解为核心**的。但在 Eshel, *Withness* 所引的博拉斯 1987 年文本中，出现了几乎相反的技术指示。Eshel 在开篇注释中调用博拉斯关于**两种移情体裁（two fundamental genres of transference）**的划分：

> Bollas’s (1987) distinction between two fundamental genres of transference. One genre involves the patient and his objects; the other derives from a receptive capacity (in both analyst and patient). It is a state of being in which the analyst functions as a part of the patient’s mental process, which facilitates the creation of new internal objects and self experiences. “The psychoanalyst’s counter- transference task within this transference is to allow himself to be assumed by the patient and not to interpret unless the patient needs it” (p. 256).

— Eshel, *Withness*，注释 1（引 Bollas, 1987, p. 256）

（原文中的连字符断裂为 PDF 转写痕迹，逐字保留。）

这里的技术指令是：**允许自己被患者「假定／征用」（allow himself to be assumed by the patient），并且除非患者需要，否则不诠释**。分析师在此**作为患者心理过程的一个部分**运作，促成新的内部客体与自体经验的生成。Eshel 随即把这一立场与自己的概念接通：

> I find that working with this kind of countertransference is closely related to my idea of “presencing.”

— Eshel, *Withness*，注释 1

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：面对深度困境中的患者，分析师应当主动密集地诠释，还是应当不诠释、只让自己被征用？
> **A 方立场**：博拉斯（2013，经 Govrin 转述）——「For Bollas, interpretation becomes a form of love—being known is being loved at a crucial time in one’s life.」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Part III: Crisis as Communication—The Radical Promise of Breakdown」）
> **B 方立场**：博拉斯（1987，经 Eshel 转引）——「The psychoanalyst’s counter- transference task within this transference is to allow himself to be assumed by the patient and not to interpret unless the patient needs it」（Eshel, *Withness*，注释 1，引 Bollas, 1987, p. 256）
> **备注**：**同一作者内部的两阶段／两情境张力**，而非人际争论。可能的调和路径是：1987 年文本针对的是第二种移情体裁（接受性移情），2013 年文本针对的是崩溃迫近的急诊情境——**是否需要诠释，取决于移情的体裁而非患者的严重程度**。但本批资料未提供博拉斯本人对这一张力的说明，此调和性阅读**属【推断】**。

【推断】博拉斯 1987 年著作的标题 *The Shadow of the Object: Psychoanalysis of the Unthought Known*（见 Govrin 第六章参考文献）中的「未思之知（the unthought known）」，与第二节「把从未被精神表征过的东西说出来」的命题高度契合；但 Govrin 正文未展开该书内容，两者的关联为本页【推断】，详见第十三节资料局限声明。

## 十二、施受虐与「自体的濒死」：崩溃作为慢性结构

博拉斯的另一个可引用面向见于 Eshel 对早期创伤与施受虐结构的讨论。Eshel 在描述一位被母亲灾难性情感淹没的患者之后，引博拉斯为佐证：

> Bollas (1995) similarly notes:
>
> The sadomasochists are still trapped by their need continuously to remaster an early trauma, although they have converted the anxiety of annihilation into the excitement of its representation.... These sadomasochistic alliances enact the near-death of the self, in which the child self avoids its killing but forever feels the near-hit as a kind of narrow escape.... That the self did indeed

> once nearly meet its end, that there *was* something awful in the environment that caused such mental intensities, is an unexam- ined feature of the sadomasochist’s life. [pp. 209-210]

— Eshel, *Withness*，正文（引 Bollas, 1995, pp. 209–210）

（原文中的连字符断裂与分页为 PDF 转写痕迹，逐字保留。）

【推断】这段的诠释学含义是：施受虐并非驱力满足，而是**对一段从未被检验过的环境的记录**——「自体确实曾经几乎走向终结，环境中确实曾经有过某种可怕的东西」。症状在此被读作**证词**：尚未被检验的，正是崩溃曾经发生过的证据。这与第二节「崩溃是有待诠释的文本」是同一命题在**慢性结构层面**的版本，而非危机层面。（本段为【推断】。）

Eshel 还在同一篇中直接使用博拉斯的术语：

> terminal object (in Bollas’s sense, 1995, p. 76)—indeed, a terminal and annihilating object and the despair of his first year of life.

— Eshel, *Withness*，正文（引 Bollas, 1995, p. 76）
