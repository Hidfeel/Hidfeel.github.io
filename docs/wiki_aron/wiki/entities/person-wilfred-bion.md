## 摘要

比昂的诠释理论**起点是一次临床失败**，这一点在精神分析史上几乎是独特的。在《对联结的攻击》（"Attacks on Linking", 1959）题为「对正常程度投射性认同的否认」一节中，他坦承：一位患者试图把淹没性的死亡恐惧投射进他，希望分析师的头脑能涵容、改造并以可承受的形式归还它们；而比昂在事后才意识到，自己回以的是他所谓的「**例行诠释（routine interpretations）**」——不带情感在场、不提供真正涵容的干预，被患者体验为**彻底的拒绝（outright rejection）**。

由此确立了比昂全部诠释理论的公理：**诠释与涵容不可分割**。分析师的具体行动不是传递理论洞见，而是在**紧迫性之点（point of urgency）**上说出的一句话，其前提是分析师已经**承受、承载并代谢**了患者无法承受的状态。命名比昂这一立场的关键词是一组连锁概念：涵容（containment）、遐想（reverie）、α功能（alpha-function）、选定事实（selected fact）、负性能力（negative capability）、O 与合一（at-one-ment）、以及**诠释性信念（interpretive faith）**。

比昂在「诠释」议题上的位置因此是**双重的**，也是本页要强调的张力所在。一方面，他在 Govrin 的谱系中被归入「**求真传统**」（从弗洛伊德经克莱因一直到**早期比昂**）——他把真理置于分析事业的中心：「健康的心智成长似乎依赖真理，正如活的有机体依赖食物」。另一方面，Yadlin-Gadot 判定：客体关系理论中从实在论到唯心论的**第一次激进转变**可归于比昂——O「能『成为』，但不能被『知道』」，因此「**实在论认识论在此崩塌**」。

## 一、★起点：例行诠释与一次被承认的临床失败

1959 年《对联结的攻击》中，比昂提供了一份异常坦率的临床失败记录，它成为其诠释理解的基础。在「对正常程度投射性认同的否认」（312）一节中，他叙述道：一位患者试图把无法承受的死亡恐惧投射进他，期待分析师的头脑能涵容、改造并最终以可忍受的形式归还。

> In the section entitled “Denial of Normal Degrees of Projective Identification,” (312) Bion recounts how a patient attempted to project overwhelming fears of death into him, in the hope that the analyst’s mind could contain, modify, and eventually return them in a tolerable form. Instead, Bion realized in hindsight, he had responded with what he calls “routine interpretations”— interventions that carried no emotional presence, offered no true containment, and were thus experienced by the patient as an outright rejection.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇（引 Bion, 1959, p. 312）

失败的关键在于：这一分析失误**再生产而非转化**了婴儿期创伤——一个不可得的、不接受的客体的创伤。比昂写道（原文着重）：

> *This patient had had to deal with a* *mother who could not tolerate such feelings and evacuated them instead* (Bion, <u>1959</u>, p. 312, emphasis added).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇（引 Bion, 1959, p. 312）

临床后果是剧烈的：患者没有经由涵容获得缓解，反而感到自己的情感痛苦在被分析师「排空」之后**加剧**了。

> The clinical consequences of this failure, Bion explains, were dramatic: instead of finding relief through containment, the patient felt that his emotional pain had intensified after being “evacuated” by the analyst. The urgent communication of unbearable psychic states met with an obstruction, and this led the patient to escalate the forcefulness of his projective identifications—pushing more desperately and violently into the analyst in order to be felt and held.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇

**关键的理论判断**：这种升级**不是**破坏性攻击（尽管它可能看似如此），而是一个**绝望的、要通过涵容求生的诉求**。比昂坚持，真正的错误在于他的**误认（misinterpretation）**：他把患者的投射当作攻击，而实际上它是经由涵容求生存的紧急呼吁（Bion, 1959, pp. 312–313）。

> For Bion, the crucial distinction was that this escalation was not a destructive attack, though it might have resembled one, but rather a desperate attempt to overcome the analyst’s denial of entry. The true error, Bion insists, lay in his misinterpretation: he had treated the patient’s projection as aggression, whereas in fact it signaled an urgent appeal for survival through containment (Bion, <u>1959</u>, pp. 312–313).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇

## 二、★公理：诠释与涵容不可分割

这一认识促使比昂**重构诠释本身的性质**。

> This realization led Bion to reformulate the very nature of interpretation in psychoanalysis. For him, the analyst’s specific action is not the delivery of theoretical insight, but *a spoken interpretation at the point of urgency*, following the patient’s projection of raw, unbearable anxiety into the analyst.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇

分析师必须**在一种放弃期待、记忆或欲望的状态中等待**（Bion, 1992/1967），以便接收患者被投射出来的部分并涵容它们，而非防御性地拒绝它们。

> The analyst must wait in a state that abandons expectation, memory, or desire, in order to receive these projected parts of the patient and contain them, rather than defensively rejecting them (Bion, 1992/1967). Only in this receptive stance can the analyst’s interpretation function as a true *link*—restoring the patient’s capacity to think and to experience continuity of being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇

Govrin 由此给出比昂的公理式表述——**要在正确的时刻有效言说，分析师必须首先承受、承载并代谢患者无法承受的状态**：

> In this way, Bion’s own clinical failure crystallized his conviction that interpretation is inseparable from containment: to speak effectively at the right moment, the analyst must first suffer, bear, and metabolize the patient’s unbearable states, transforming what would otherwise remain destructive evacuations into the beginnings of meaning.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章开篇

「无记忆、无欲望」这一方法论要求的另一表述来自奥格登的转引：

> We must “cultivate a watchful avoidance of memory” (Bion 1967,p. 137) because memory is what we think we know based on what no longer exists, and is no longer knowable. And we must renounce “desires for results, ‘cure,’ or even understanding” (p. 137).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节（引 Bion, 1967, p. 137）

而奥格登把这两个否定精确地解释为**本体论思维的条件**：记忆与对理解的欲望都是对分析师「**直觉**」当刻发生之事之实在的「**阻碍**」。

> Memory of what we think weknowanddesireforunderstandingofwhathasnotyetoccurred(and consequently unknowable) are both a “hindrance to the psychoanalyst’s intuition of the reality [of what is occurring in the present moment of a session] withwhichhemustbeatone” (1967,p.136). This is Bion’sbrandofonto- logical thinking: being has supplanted understanding; the analyst does not come to know or understand or comprehend or apprehend the reality of what is happening in the session, he “intuits” it, he becomes “at one” with it, he is fully present in experiencing the present moment.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节（引 Bion, 1967, p. 136）

## 三、选定事实（selected fact）

涵容之后紧接着的问题：在投射与碎片化沟通的混乱之中，分析师如何辨识那个**将创造意义的精确诠释**？这把比昂引向「选定事实」概念。

比昂从数学家庞加莱（Henri Poincaré）那里借来这个概念，并非只是为了跨学科的理论杂交，而是在表述某种关于人类心智的根本事实——面对淹没性的碎片化时**寻找连贯性的绝望需要**。

> When Bion borrowed the concept of the “selected fact” from the mathematician Henri Poincaré, he was not merely engaging in theoretical cross-pollination between disciplines. Rather, he was articulating something fundamental about the human mind’s desperate need to find coherence in the face of overwhelming fragmentation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

选定事实诞生于**偏执—分裂位的认识论危机**：心智面对一片互不关联、抗拒整合的散乱元素的景观。分析师像庞加莱的数学家面对散乱的数学元素一样，寻找那可能突然「**在无序统治之处引入秩序**」的东西（Bion, 1962）。

> The analyst, like Poincaré’s mathematician confronting disparate mathematical elements, searches for that which might suddenly “introduce order where the appearance of disorder reigned” (Bion, <u>1962</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」（引 Bion, 1962）

比昂的洞见比简单移植数学思维更为激进：**选定事实代表一种特定的情绪体验**——创造性的综合所带来的振奋，与对仍然未被测绘的广大领域的**同时觉察**。

> He recognized that the selected fact represents a particular kind of emotional experience—one that combines the exhilaration of creative synthesis with the simultaneous awareness of vast territories that remain uncharted. This is not merely an intellectual exercise but a profoundly emotional encounter with the unknown.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

### 3.1 选定事实与诠释的治疗功能之别

这是本节最精细的一点。选定事实属于**发现（discovery）**的领域——它是患者必须**自己找到**的东西，是一种促发从偏执—分裂位向抑郁位运动的内在认出。

> The distinction Bion draws between the selected fact proper and its cousin, interpretation, reveals a subtle but crucial difference in therapeutic function. The selected fact belongs to the realm of discovery—it is something the patient must find for themselves, an internal recognition that precipitates movement from paranoid-schizoid to depressive position.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

### 3.2 因果性作为「有用的虚假」

比昂对因果性的处理揭示了他的深刻洞见：**人类倾向于把解释上的便利误认为真理**。借用休谟关于恒常联结的洞见，他主张**原因与选定事实都属于信念的领域，而非经验事实的领域**。

> Drawing on Hume’s insights about constant conjunction, he argues that both cause and selected fact belong to the realm of belief rather than empirical fact.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

精神病性的因果使用展示了这一过程的病理极端：当患者把原因体验为「**作为自在之物的存在（existence as a thing in itself）**」（Bion, 1992, p. 276）时，我们所见证的是心智试图通过全能控制创造连贯性的绝望尝试。

> When the patient experiences cause as having “existence as a thing in itself” (Bion, 1992, p. 276), when objects appear to cohere voluntarily and independently of the patient’s volition while simultaneously being part of their personality, we witness the mind’s desperate attempt to create coherence through omnipotent control.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

对非精神病性人格而言，原因起的是比昂所谓「**有用的虚假（useful falsehood）**」的作用——正因为**其局限被承认**，它才有效。

> For the non-psychotic personality, cause functions as what Bion might call a “useful falsehood”—effective precisely because its limitations are acknowledged.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

### 3.3 时间维度的区分

选定事实在一个**时间被排除**的领域中运作，在被体验为同时的元素之间创造综合；因果性则组织散布在时间中的客体，把**序列叙事**强加于经验。这一区分塑造了分析技术：选定事实揭示线性时间之外的模式，因果性则追溯跨时间的发展序列。

> The selected fact operates in a realm where time is excluded, creating synthesis among elements experienced as contemporaneous. Cause, by contrast, organizes objects scattered in time, imposing sequential narrative on experience. This temporal distinction shapes analytic technique: selected facts reveal patterns outside linear time, while causality traces developmental sequences across temporal ga…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

比昂最终**放弃了**选定事实概念，但核心洞见保留下来：

> The selected fact, even in its abandonment, continues to illuminate something essential about how meaning emerges in the analytic encounter.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Selected Fact」

## 四、★遐想（reverie）与 α功能（alpha-function）

### 4.1 对弗洛伊德梦理论的颠倒

比昂 1962 年提出：**没有任何东西能在未经事先的无意识加工（elaboration）的情况下成为意识**。这描述的是心智的一种根本架构，挑战了关于经验与理解之关系的最基本假设。

> When Bion <u>(1962)</u> proposed that nothing can become conscious without prior unconscious elaboration, he was describing a fundamental architecture of mind that challenges our most basic assumptions about the relationship between experience and understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

这是对弗洛伊德著名命题「本我曾在之处，自我应当生成」的**方向反转**：不是把无意识材料译为意识，而是**意识材料必须经受梦工作（dream-work）才能以精神的方式（psychically）储存与转化**。梦不再是通往无意识的「王道」，而成为**实在本身得以被精神消化的机制**。

> This reversal of the traditional psychoanalytic formula challenges the directional assumption embedded in Freud’s famous dictum “where id was, there ego shall be.” Instead, Bion suggests a circular process whereby conscious material must be subjected to dream-work to render it suitable for psychic storage and transformation. The dream is no longer simply the “royal road” to the unconscious but becomes the very mechanism by which reality itself becomes psychically digestible.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

### 4.2 做梦（dreaming）而非为梦作诠释

dream-work-α 概念来自比昂对一类患者的临床观察：他们似乎**无法做梦**，却又迫切需要在分析会谈本身之中从事类梦的过程。分析师面临的特殊挑战是：这些患者要求分析师**执行他们自己无法触及的做梦功能**。

> These patients, often displaying psychotic features, presented the analyst with a peculiar challenge: they required the analyst to perform the dreaming function that they themselves could not access. Yet Bion recognized the inherent danger in this situation. The analyst must not dream for the patient— such an approach would constitute a form of psychic intrusion that replicates the very projective identification processes that keep the patient imprisoned in their pathology.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

**为患者做梦 vs. 做梦这场会谈（dreaming the session）**——这是比昂最微妙的临床洞见之一：分析师的遐想必须**真正是他自己的**，同时又提供使患者碎片化材料得以凝聚的涵容功能。

> This distinction—between dreaming for the patient and dreaming the session—represents one of Bion’s most subtle clinical insights. The analyst’s reverie must remain genuinely their own while simultaneously providing the containing function that allows the patient’s fragmented material to cohere.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

奥格登对遐想的定义同样强调其**存在状态**性质：

> Bion’s(1962a, 1962b) conception of “reverie” also reflects his onto- logical bent. Reverie (waking-dreaming) is a state of being that entails making oneself unconsciously receptive to experiencing what is so dis- turbing to the patient (or infant) that he is unable to “dream” (to do unconscious psychological work with) the experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

分析师（或母亲）的遐想——醒着的梦，常以其**最平凡、最日常的想法**的形式出现（Ogden 1997a, 1997b）——构成分析师（或母亲）无意识地体验到某种类似于患者（或婴儿）**不可思考、不可梦的**经验的方式。

> The analyst’s (or mother’s) reveries, waking dreaming—which often take the form of his most mundane, quotidian thoughts (Ogden 1997a, 1997b)—constitutes a way in which the analyst (or mother) unconsciously experiences something like the patient’s (or infant’s) unthinkable, undreamable experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

**关键的技术指令**：分析师通过**从**遐想经验言说（或以其余形式关联），而非**关于**遐想经验言说，把被转化的（被梦过的）版本提供给患者（Ogden 1994）。

> In the analytic setting, the analyst makes available to the patient the transformed (dreamt) version of the patient’s “undreamt” or partially dreamt experience by speaking (or relating in other forms) from, not about, reverie experience (Ogden 1994).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

### 4.3 α功能作为本体论主张

奥格登把 α功能理论明确读为**一个隐喻**：β元素（raw sense impressions，是对经验的身体性反应，尚不构成意义，更不构成成为自己）被转化为 α元素，后者构成**无主体之存在（subjectless being）**的组分——很像温尼科特的「持续存在（going on being）」。

> I view Bion’s(1962a) theory of alpha-function as a metaphor for the transformation of beta-elements (raw sense impressions that are bodily responses to experience, but which do not yet constitute meaning, much less being oneself) into alpha-elements, which comprise compo- nents of subjectless being, much like Winnicott’s “going on being.”

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

α元素在产生「梦—思（dream-thoughts）」的过程中相互联结，后者又被用于做梦的过程。**做梦是个体成为体验其自身存在的主体这一精神事件**。当 α功能停止处理感官印象时（在严重精神病理中），个体不只失去创造意义的能力，也**失去把自己体验为活着且真实的能力**。

> Dreaming is the psychic event in which the individual becomes a subject experiencing his own being. When, in severe forms of psychopathology (which I will describe in the clinical portion of this paper), alpha-func- tion ceases to process sense impressions, not only does the individual lose the capacity to create meaning, he also loses the capacity to experi- ence himself as alive and real.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

Govrin 的表述则更强调其本体论断言：**思维先于思想者（thoughts without a thinker）**——存在着一个寻求精神形式的**原初经验场**。做梦因此不只是一个心理过程，更是**经验作为经验得以生成的活动**。

> The notion of “thoughts without a thinker” suggests that thinking precedes the thinker, that there exists a primordial field of proto-experience in search of psychic form. Dreaming, therefore, is not only a psychological process but also the activity through which experience comes into being as experience—the moment when raw impressions are granted symbolic existence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

### 4.4 帕森斯（Parsons）的诠释内容 vs. 出发点之辨

同一个诠释内容，依据分析师的心智状态，可以构成完全不同的干预。帕森斯以教师—患者为例：诠释 A——「你大概把我看作竞争对手，你害怕我会破坏你的创造力」——与「这个房间里有我们两个人」根本不同，后者**从分析师的遐想中涌现**，而非理论知识的机械应用。

> The clinical implications become vivid in Parsons’ examination of how the same interpretive content can constitute entirely different interventions depending on the analyst’s state of mind. … The difference, Parsons <u>(2021)</u> demonstrates, lies not in manifest content but in what he calls the point of departure in the psyche of the analyst (p. 75).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」（引 Parsons, 2021, p. 75）

当分析师通过遐想运用 α功能时，诠释必须从 A 演化到他所谓的 **B2**——**同样的语词**，但出自根本不同的转化过程。这一演化「**只能通过启用一种更大更深的能力，即遐想的能力，才能发生**」（p. 75）。

> When the analyst employs alpha-function through reverie, the interpretation must evolve from A to what he terms B2—the same words as the mechanical formulation but emerging from a fundamentally different transformational process. This evolution “can only occur by bringing into play a larger and deeper capacity, namely, the capacity for reverie” (p. 75).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」（引 Parsons, 2021, p. 75）

这与本 Wiki 中 [[concepts/concept-interpretation]] 页所记「诠释不是解码而是创造」的命题直接呼应：**差异不在内容，而在涌现它的存在品质**。

### 4.5 卡索拉（Cassorla）的隐式 α功能

卡索拉（2008）对边缘型患者的临床工作揭示了这些转化过程在最困难的治疗情境中如何运作。他观察到的「**慢性活现（chronic enactments）**」——分析师与患者看似被困在无生产性的共谋中的停滞期——展示了**隐式 α功能（implicit alpha-function）**如何在意识觉察的阈限之下运作。

> His observations of “chronic enactments”—those seemingly stagnant periods when analyst and patient appear trapped in unproductive collusions—demonstrate how implicit alpha-function operates below the threshold of conscious awareness. During these periods, “unconscious exchanges occur between the dyad, in which the analyst provides implicit alpha-function to the patient, little by little recovering the traumatized parts” (p. 161).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」（引 Cassorla, 2008, p. 161）

### 4.6 真理的地位

比昂对真理的强调再怎么强调也不为过：

> His assertion that “healthy mental growth seems to depend on truth as the living organism depends on food” (Bion, <u>1965</u>, p. 38) places truthfulness at the center of the analytic enterprise.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」（引 Bion, 1965, p. 38）

但 Govrin 立即限定：**这不是与外部实在相对应的真理，而是与精神实在的真实遭遇（authentic engagement with psychic reality）**。分析师必须发展对歧义的耐受力，同时维持比昂所谓的**对真理的承诺**——这是精神分析最苛刻的要求之一：**在保持观察严格性的同时对转化保持开放**。

> The analyst must develop tolerance for ambiguity while maintaining what Bion calls commitment to truth. This represents one of psychoanalysis’s most demanding requirements: remaining open to transformation while preserving observational rigor.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Reverie and Alpha-Function」

## 五、★O、at-one-ment 与语言的限度

### 5.1 O 的定义

在比昂的著作中，或许没有任何概念比「**O**」更居于其诠释进路的中心——这个符号代表分析情境中**不可言说、终极的实在**。

> Perhaps no concept in Bion’s work is more central to understanding his approach to interpretation than what he designates simply as “O”—a symbol that represents the ineffable, ultimate reality of the analytic situation. O represents the thing-in-itself of the analytic encounter, the ultimate reality, or “invariance”—the unknowable essence that underlies all psychoanalytic phenomena but can never be directly apprehended (Sandler, <u>2005</u>, p. 178).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

Yadlin-Gadot 给出的、直接引自比昂的权威定义：

> "O... the ultimate reality, represented by terms such as absolute truth, the godhead, the infinite, the thing-in-itself... does not fall in the domain of knowledge or learning save inci- dentally; O can 'become,' but it cannot be 'known'" (Bion, 1970, p. 25).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 25）

以及她据此作出的论断——**实在论认识论在此崩塌**：

> The relevant truth and reality for psychoanalysis (O in Bion's case) can be experienced but cannot be known. Here is the down- fall of realistic epistemology, which is predicated on the assump- tion of a knowable reality.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

Yadlin-Gadot 还记录了比昂把 O 与**柏拉图形式论及基督教道成肉身教义**的关联：

> "The Platonic theory of Forms and the Christian dogma of the Incar- nation imply absolute essence which I wish to postulate as a universal quality of phenomena such as 'panic,' 'anxiety,' 'fear,' 'love.' In brief, I use O to represent this central feature of every situation that the psycho- analyst has to meet. With this he must be at one" (Bion, 1970, p. 88).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，注释 9（引 Bion, 1970, p. 88）

因此精神分析必须是「一门不受其源于知识与感官背景所限制的科学（science）……它必须是一门 **合一（at-one-ment）的科学**」。接近 O 的状态建立在**离开 K**之上，涉及丧失意义与意识连续性的风险。

### 5.2 从 K 到 O

传统诠释运作在比昂所谓的「**K 联结（Klink）**」——知晓、学习与理解的领域之内。分析师收集患者历史的信息、观察模式、应用理论框架、提供旨在增加洞见的诠释。

> Traditional interpretation operates within what Bion calls the “Klink”—the domain of knowing, learning, and understanding. The analyst gathers information about the patient’s history, observes patterns, applies theoretical frameworks, and offers interpretations designed to increase insight. This approach, while valuable, ultimately remains trapped within the realm of what can be consciously grasped and verbally articulated.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

**从 K 到 O 的运动不只是一个技术调整，而是分析注意力的根本重定方向。** K 寻求**知晓关于**患者的什么；O 则要求我们**与患者正在成为的东西同在**。

> The movement from K to O represents more than a technical adjustment; it constitutes a fundamental reorientation of analytic attention. Where K seeks to know about the patient, O requires that we become present with what the patient is becoming.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

### 5.3 诠释即 O 之演化中的实际事件

这是比昂在「诠释」议题上最激进的命题。

> the interpretation is an actual event in an evolution of O that is common to analyst and analysand (Bion, <u>1970</u>, p. 27).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

其后果：诠释**不描述也不解释实在——它参与实在的演化**。它不是关于真理的陈述，而是真理涌现的**演出**。

> This transforms our understanding of what happens when we speak in the consulting room. The interpretation does not describe or explain reality—it participates in reality’s evolution. It is not a statement about truth but an enactment of truth’s emergence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

由此导出一个技术判据：**会谈中所作的表述，其价值必然不同于会谈外所作的表述**（p. 26）。活的诠释，在分析时刻的高温中被给出，**在 O 之中进行转化**，而非仅仅提供关于 O 的知识。

> This is why Bion insists that “formulations of the events of analysis made in the course of analysis must possess value different from that of formulations made extra-sessionally” (p. 26). The living interpretation, offered in the heat of the analytic moment, conducts transformation in O rather than mere knowledge about O.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 26）

**这解释了为什么理论上正确的诠释可能完全落空，而另一些从分析师的合一（at-one-ment）状态中自发涌现的诠释却能转化双方**——差异不在语词的内容，而在它们所由之涌现的**存在品质**。

> This distinction helps to explain why interpretations that seem theoretically correct can fall completely flat, while others that emerge spontaneously from the analyst’s state of at-one-ment can transform both participants. The difference lies not in the content of the words but in the quality of being from which they emerge. An interpretation that arises from genuine contact with O carries something that cannot be manufactured through theoretical understanding or technical skill alone.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

### 5.4 语言的限度与「低饱和」诠释

语言的限度并不使诠释归于无效，而是把它置于**言说与消言之辩证（a dialectic of saying and unsaying）**之中。分析师的语词的价值不在于作为最终真理，而在于作为**邀请进一步探索的请柬**、作为指向超出语言捕捉之经验的标记。

> The limitation of language does not render interpretation futile but places it within a dialectic of saying and unsaying. The analyst’s words are valuable not as final truths but as invitations to further exploration, as markers pointing toward experiences that exceed linguistic capture. Every interpretation carries within it an acknowledgment of its own inadequacy, an implicit recognition that something essential always remains beyond the reach of words.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

Govrin 给出的临床例子：面对深度抑郁的患者，分析师可能发现传统诠释语言错失了患者经验的本质性质，于是开始更试探性地说话——「像是存在一种无法用语词表达的黑暗」，或「有什么重要的事情似乎正在沉默中发生」。这些**较低饱和（saturated）的诠释**能为患者自己的语言创造空间。

> These less saturated interpretations can create space for the patient’s own language to emerge, allowing them to approach their experience without the constraint of predetermined meanings. The analyst’s reticence becomes not an abdication of interpretive r…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

## 六、负性能力（negative capability）与诠释性信念

比昂借用济慈的「**负性能力**」——「当一个人能够处于不确定、神秘、怀疑之中，而不焦躁地追求事实与理由」的能力。

> When a man is capable of being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact and reason (Bion, 1970, p. 125; 引 Keats)

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」（引 Bion, 1970, p. 125）

这不意味着放弃理论知识或临床判断，而是**把这些工具握得轻一些**，随时准备在它们成为真正遭遇的障碍时把它们放到一边。

> This does not mean abandoning all theoretical knowledge or clinical judgment, but rather holding these tools lightly, ready to set them aside when they become obstacles to genuine encounter.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

**训练层面的后果被推向极端**：如果目标是与 O 的合一（at-one-ment）而非获得关于无意识冲突的洞见，那么成功分析的标准必须相应转移。进展不能只用症状缓解或自我理解的增加来衡量，而必须关注更微妙的指标：**患者容忍不确定性的能力增长、与困难情绪同在的能力增强、对超越直接理解的真理之可能性的信念发展**。

> Progress cannot be measured simply in terms of symptom reduction or increased self- understanding, though these may certainly occur. Instead, the analyst must attend to subtler indicators: the patient’s growing capacity to tolerate uncertainty, their increasing ability to remain present with difficult emotions, their developing faith in the possibility of truth beyond immediate understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Mystery of O」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：比昂究竟属于「求真传统」还是「本体论转向」？真理在他那里是可知的还是只能「成为」的？
> **A 方立场**（求真传统）：Govrin 把比昂明确列入求真传统——「从弗洛伊德经克莱因一直到早期比昂」，并引比昂：**「健康的心智成长似乎依赖真理，正如活的有机体依赖食物」**（Bion, 1965, p. 38）；分析师必须在容忍歧义的同时**维持对真理的承诺**。
> **B 方立场**（唯心论转向）：Yadlin-Gadot 判定客体关系理论中从实在论到唯心论的**第一次激进转变可归于比昂**——O「不落在知识或学习的领域之内，除非是偶然地；O 能『成为』，但不能被『知道』」；因此「**实在论认识论在此崩塌**」。
> **备注**：两说指向比昂的两个时期与两个层面，奥格登的「感受力而非学派」限定在此适用：早期比昂（选定事实、α功能、真理作为精神成长的食物）仍在 K 的领域内工作，晚期比昂（O、at-one-ment、无记忆无欲望）要求**离开 K**。真正不可调和之处在真理定义本身——不是「对应外在实在的真理」，而是「与精神实在的真实遭遇」。这一重定义本身即是 Yadlin-Gadot 所说的「崩塌」。

## 七、比昂作为本体论思想家：奥格登的读法

奥格登的断言极为干脆：

> As I read Bion, throughout his opus, he is principally an ontological thinker. Just as Winnicott shifted the focus of analysis from play to play- ing, Bion shifted the analytic focus from (the understanding of) dreams to (the experience of) dreaming (which, for Bion, is synonymous with doing unconscious psychological work [cf. Ogden 2007a]).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

**从游戏（play）到游戏着（playing）、从梦（dream）到做梦（dreaming）**——这一「名词对动名词」的转换是奥格登区分认识论与本体论精神分析的第四条标准，而比昂与温尼科特各占一边、互为镜像。

比昂的本体论思维在他描述精神健康与精神病理时**以存在状态（states of being）**出之：例如，精神病是一种个体「**既不能入睡也不能醒来**」的存在状态（Bion 1962a, p. 7）。

> Bion speaks in terms of states of being when he describes psychic health and psychopathology, for example, psychosis is a state of being in which the individual “cannot go to sleep and cannot wake up” (Bion 1962a,p.7).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

**临床研讨班中最生动的例子**：一位报告者说他的精神病患者告诉他做了一个梦。比昂问——「**他为什么说它们是梦？**」（p. 142）。报告者困窘地回答——「他就是这样告诉我的」。稍后，比昂描述了他可能如何对患者说话，一种针对患者**存在状态**的方式：

> So why does the patient come to see a psycho-analyst and say he had a dream? I can imagine myself saying to a patient, “Where were you last night? What did you see?” If the patient told me he didn’t see anything—he just went to bed—I would say, “Well, I still want to know where you went and what you saw.” [p. 142]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节（引 Bion, 1987, p. 142）

奥格登的评价：这里比昂所想象的说话方式，聚焦的不是患者称之为梦的**内容**，而是患者的**存在状态**——「你去了哪里？」「你在哪里？」「你是谁？」「你上床时**成为了谁**？」

> Here, Bion is imagining talking with a patient in a way that focuses not on the content of what the patient is calling a dream, but on the state of being of the patient—“Where did you go?”“Where were you?” “Who were you?”“Who did you become when you got into bed?”

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

### 7.1 奥格登的划界：比昂不是客体关系理论家

> I do not view Winnicott and Bion as object-relations theorists (reference to internal object relationships is rare in the work of both of these authors). They are not primarily concerned with understanding and interpreting the pathological internal object relationships in which the patient is ensnared. Their focus is primarily on the range of states of being experienced by the patient (and the analyst) and the states of being the patient (or analyst) is unable to experience.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第四节

奥格登还给出一项文本证据学层面的观察：

> Not only are unconscious internal object relationships rarely mentioned by Winnicott and Bion, Winnicott rarely makes mention of the unconscious and Bion creates a new conception of the nature of the unconscious.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第四节

## 八、Symington 引比昂：成为你自己之所是的分析师

在研讨会报告中，比昂对一位担心自己与患者犯了「错误」的报告者评论说：**只有「在你获得资格、完成了自己的分析之后——你才有机会发现你真正是谁［作为分析师］」**（1987, p. 34）。

> To a presenter who is worried by the “mistakes” he made with a patient, Bion comments that only “after you have become qualified and have finished your own analysis—then you have a chance to find out who you really are [as an analyst] (1987,p.34, italics in the original…). Here, Bion is differentiating between learning how to “do analysis” and the experience of being and becoming “who you really are” as an analyst.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节（引 Bion, 1987, p. 34）

由此推出的替代方案是**分析风格（analytic style）**而非**承袭的技术**，并「**为每位病人发明精神分析**」：

> I would add that becoming an analyst involves developing an “analytic style” (Ogden 2007b) that is uniquely one’s own, as opposed to adopting “a technique” handed down from previous generations of analysts. In so doing, we “invent psychoanalysis” (Ogden 2018) for each patient and develop the capacity to respond spontaneously in the moment, sometimes in words, at other times non-verbally.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第三节

## 九、结论：诠释作为信念（interpretation as faith）

第三章的结论节把比昂的重构定位为**对分析工作所能成就之事的彻底重想**，而不只是技术修正：通过从内容破译转向**真理取向**，他把诠释从一种技术技能变成一种**要求分析师全然在场的伦理实践**。

> Bion’s reconceptualization of interpretation represents more than a modification of psychoanalytic technique—it amounts to a complete reimagining of what analytic work can accomplish. By shifting from content-deciphering to truth-orientation, he transformed interpretation from a technical skill into an ethical practice demanding the analyst’s full presence.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Conclusion: Interpretation as Faith」

比昂在洛杉矶研讨班上的结论：

> I think that it’s a good thing to bet on that if you listen enough, after a time, something evolves. Something pushes out towards you, you begin to notice it, and then an interpretation will come with it (Bion, <u>2013a</u>, p. 10–11).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Conclusion」（引 Bion, 2013a, pp. 10–11）

这一转化要求的是**诠释性信念（interpretive faith）**——**在不确定性中信任分析过程，为与未知的直接遭遇而放弃理论的安全感**。

> This transformation requires interpretive faith—trusting the analytic process despite uncertainty, and abandoning theoretical security for direct encounter with the unknown.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Conclusion」

以及本页的收束命题：

> In Bion’s vision, the analyst doesn’t apply psychoanalysis but becomes psychoanalysis through disciplined attention to the unfolding truth of the analytic encounter. This framework transforms interpretation from technical act to ethical stance, whereby the pursuit of emotional truth takes precedence over therapeutic ambition. The most profound interpretive interventions are those that create space for the unknown to emerge rather than foreclosing mystery through premature understanding, making interpretation an act of faith in the human capacity for transformation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Conclusion」

## 十、后记中的比昂

Govrin 在全书中对比昂最精炼的一句定位：

> Wilfred Bion’s discovery that withholding interpretation could be more transformative than offering it emerged from his deeper understanding of containment and the analyst’s function as a thinking apparatus.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记「The Interpretive Paradox」
