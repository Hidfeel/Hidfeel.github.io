# 唐纳德·温尼科特（Donald Winnicott）

> **创建时间**: 2026-08-28
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-28
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

温尼科特在「诠释」议题上的位置，可以用他自己的一句话来锚定——**「我想我作诠释主要是为了让我知道我的理解的界限」**（"I think I interpret mainly to let the patient know the limits of my understanding"，1963, p. 189）。这句话把治疗行动的重心从**知（knowing）**移向**在（being）**：诠释不再是分析师向患者传递关于患者的知识，而是分析师**标示自己的理解的边界**，从而保留患者的分离心灵，并把「不理解也可以被维持在关系之中」这件事**演示**出来。

由此派生温尼科特最反直觉的技术主张：**他宁可被体验为来自内部的发现，甚至宁可出错**。他诠释，恰恰因为「如果我什么都不诠释，患者会得到一个印象，以为我理解一切。换句话说，我通过不完全切中要害——或者甚至是错的——保留某种外在的性质」（1969, p. 718）。

在 Govrin 的谱系中，温尼科特是**「创伤疗愈传统（trauma healing tradition）」的开创者**，与弗洛伊德—克莱因—早期比昂的「**求真传统（truth-seeking tradition）**」相对；同时他是被弗洛伊德及其圈子边缘化的**费伦齐（Sándor Ferenczi）**那一条被压抑传统的继承者。在奥格登的谱系中，他与比昂并列为**本体论精神分析**的两位主要缔造者。在 Yadlin-Gadot 的谱系中，他是**第一个把「过渡领域」定义为自主实体**的人，这一第三领域「**坍塌了**内—外／主体—客体之分」，而这一区分正是对应（correspondence）概念所固有的。

本页也必须记录围绕温尼科特的**持续争议**：Rachel Blass 与 Horacio Etchegoyen 质疑其创新究竟是精神分析的合法发展，还是**对精神分析本质的背离**。

## 一、谱系定位：费伦齐被压抑传统的继承者

Govrin 第四章开篇即给出这一谱系叙述，也是本批资料中关于费伦齐的**唯一实质段落**：

> When Sándor Ferenczi’s approach—which held that the therapeutic essence of psychoanalysis resided in profound emotional attunement rather than intellectual interpretation—was forcefully marginalized by Freud and his circle, the field crystallized around a singular conviction: that interpretation yielding insight constituted the paramount curative force. Yet, as with all pendulums swung too far in one direction, the inevitable countermovement was destined to emerge. How fortunate for the evolution of psychoanalytic thought that the figure who would shepherd this transformation was Donald Winnicott…

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

费伦齐主张**治疗的本质在于深度情绪调适（profound emotional attunement）而非智性诠释**；这一进路被弗洛伊德及其圈子强力边缘化，于是整个领域围绕一个单一的信念结晶成型：**产生洞见的诠释是最高的治疗因子**。温尼科特则是这一反向运动（countermovement）的引领者。

Govrin 对温尼科特为何适合承担这一角色的解释，落在三种罕见品质的结合上——**穿透性的临床直觉、富想象力的理论成熟度、以及深刻的知识谦逊**：

> …a clinician whose rare amalgam of qualities—penetrating clinical intuition, imaginative theoretical sophistication, and profound intellectual humility—rendered him uniquely suited to articulate what had been silently gestating in the consulting rooms of countless analysts. His contribution was not merely to offer an alternative, but to weave a tapestry of understanding that honored both the interpretive tradition and the relational depths that Ferenczi had glimpsed.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

## 二、★两条传统：求真 vs. 创伤疗愈

这是 Govrin 全书组织性的对立，也是理解温尼科特全部技术主张的框架。

**求真传统**「从弗洛伊德经克莱因一直到早期比昂」：分析师是科学家，患者是研究对象，二者从事对心理真理的系统发掘。症状是线索，梦是加密信息，分析师的诠释敏锐是治愈的首要工具。病理主要被理解为**否认（disavowal）**的后果。

> The first, the *truth-seeking tradition*, stretching from Freud through Klein and early Bion, imagined the analyst as scientist and the patient as research subject—a dyad engaged in the methodical excavation of psychical truth.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

**创伤疗愈传统**（温尼科特发展）：其目标是促成温尼科特（1954）所谓的「**持续存在（going-on-being）**」——即环境失败可以击碎的**存在的基本连续性**。

> The trauma healing tradition, by contrast, seeks to facilitate what Winnicott <u>(1954)</u> called “going-on-being,” that is, the basic continuity of existence that environmental failure can shatter. The goal is not primarily self- knowledge but the capacity for authentic living and creative engagement with reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

两条传统的关键差异：

| 维度 | 求真传统 | 创伤疗愈传统 |
|---|---|---|
| 分析师的位置 | 科学家 | 环境供给者、抱持者 |
| 病理的核心机制 | 否认（disavowal） | 环境失败、发展性中断 |
| 治疗目标 | 收复被投射的部分、经由理解达成整合 | 持续存在、真实地生活与创造性地介入实在 |
| 移情 | 期待并鼓励强烈移情以便诠释与修通；分析师须甘当「坏客体」 | 不回避负性移情，但**不要求**它作为治疗的必要阶段 |
| 诠释的地位 | 首要的治愈工具 | 众多工具之一，且常常是**不该使用**的工具 |

（表格为【综合】，依据第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」正文归纳。）

Govrin 也标注了这一对立的**自我复杂性**：两个阵营的批评者都正当地指出，这种二分制造了虚假的两极对立。求真派分析师主张他们也在疗愈创伤（healing trauma）；创伤疗愈派则主张其关系进路不可避免地揭示关于患者心理发展的重要真理。

> The question becomes not whether truth emerges in treatment, but what kind of truth and by what means.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

## 三、★诠释标示理解的界限

温尼科特在「诠释」议题上最核心的命题，由 Bonaminio（2012）引出的温尼科特自述给出：

> Bonaminio <u>(2012)</u> also illuminates this through Winnicott’s radical insight: “I think I interpret mainly to let the patient know the limits of my understanding” (Winnicott, <u>1963</u>, p. 189). This transforms interpretation from omnipotent knowing to humble recognition of boundaries. The analyst’s incomplete understanding becomes, paradoxically, the very thing that enables authentic contact.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Winnicott, 1963, p. 189）

Govrin 在后记与第四章结论中把这一命题进一步推进：**诠释作为与无知的陪伴（companionship with ignorance），而非知识的传递**。分析师的语词变得不如它们**演示「不理解可以在关系中被维持」的能力**来得重要。这是作为**游戏**而非**教导**的诠释。

> When Winnicott notes that he interprets mainly to let the patient know the limits of his understanding, he points toward interpretation as a form of companionship with ignorance rather than a delivery of knowledge. The analyst’s words become less important than their capacity to demonstrate that not-knowing can be sustained within relationship. This is interpretation as a form of play rather than instruction.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

## 四、★宁可出错：对分析性全知的有意破坏

这一关切促使温尼科特发展出一种**看似刻意不完美的诠释技术**。

> This concern about analytic omniscience led Winnicott to develop what might seem like a deliberately imperfect interpretive technique. He notes, with characteristic playfulness, that he interprets because “if I make none the patient gets the impression that I understand everything. In other words, I retain some outside quality by not being quite on the mark—or even by being wrong” (Winnicott, <u>1969</u>, p. 718).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Winnicott, 1969, p. 718）

其发展性功能是明确的：**这种甘愿不完美、甚至是刻意的不完美，保存了患者拥有独立心智的感觉，同时仍提供被理解的体验**。

> This willingness to be imperfect, even deliberately so, serves a crucial developmental function. It preserves the patient’s sense of having a separate mind while still providing the experience of being understood.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

同一个逻辑在《客体的使用》（"The Use of an Object", 1971a）中给出另一条表述：**分析师想要诠释，但这会破坏过程，而且对患者而言可能看起来像一种自我防御**（p. 92）。

> In “The Use of an Object” (Winnicott, <u>1971a</u>), he writes: “The analyst feels like interpreting, but this can spoil the process, and for the patient can seem like a kind of self-defence” (p. 92).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Winnicott, 1971a, p. 92）

### 4.1 无条件的撤回

温尼科特认为分析师应当「**等待**」，看患者如何回应处于分析情境之中。这建立在他的信念之上：**只有患者才有答案**。

> Winnicott thought that the analyst should “wait” to see how the patient was responding to being in the analytic situation. This was founded on his belief that it was only the patient who had the answers.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

他承认有时患者的阻抗表明他给出了一个正确的诠释而患者正在否认它。但他坚持：**当一个诠释未能生效时，这总是意味着他在不恰当的时间或以不恰当的方式给出了它，于是他无条件地、彻底地撤回它**。

> Winnicott acknowledges that sometimes patient resistance indicates that he has offered a correct interpretation which the patient is denying. However, he maintains that when an interpretation fails to be effective, it invariably means that he delivered it at an inappropriate time or in an unsuitable manner, and he withdraws it completely without conditions.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

对儿童分析的伦理含义：

> Winnicott <u>(1971a)</u> argues that dogmatic interpretations place children in an impossible position where they must either accept what the analyst says as mere propaganda or reject both the interpretation and the entire therapeutic relationship. He believes it is essential that children feel they have the legitimate right to refuse what he says or how he approaches their material.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Winnicott, 1971a）

### 4.2 过渡空间中的诠释

温尼科特的解决之道是**把诠释重新构想为游戏内部的一个元素，而非关于游戏的权威宣告**。沟通发生在他（经 Phillips, 1988, p. 138 表述）所谓的「**分析师与患者之间的过渡空间（transitional space between analyst and patient）**」——一个创造性的共享可能性的区域，其中意义可以**协作地涌现**，而非由诠释权威强加。

> Winnicott’s solution involves reconceptualizing interpretation as an element within play rather than an authoritative pronouncement about it. Communication takes place in what he calls the “transitional space between analyst and patient” (Phillips, <u>1988</u>, p. 138)—a shared area of creative possibility where meanings can emerge collaboratively rather than being imposed by interpretive authority.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

游戏的对立面：

> The insight that “the opposite of play is not work but coercion” (Phillips, <u>1988</u>, p. 138) transforms how we understand interpretive intervention. Language, when used interpretively, can easily become coercive—imposing meaning rather than facilitating discovery. This danger is particularly acute given the inherent power differential in the analytic situation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Phillips, 1988, p. 138）

## 五、★语言的限度：对「谈话疗法」的背离

Govrin 的判断：如果温尼科特对精神分析权威的挑战是以其特有的**间接方式**进行的，那么他对**语言本身**的怀疑则更加微妙——或许也更具颠覆性。**在一个把自己的声誉押在作为「谈话疗法（talking cure）」之上的领域里，温尼科特安静而持续地质疑：最重要的治疗性转化是否能够仅仅用语词捕捉。**

> If Winnicott’s challenge to psychoanalytic authority was conducted with characteristic indirection, his skepticism about language itself was even more subtle—and perhaps more subversive. In a field that had staked its reputation on being a “talking cure,” Winnicott quietly but persistently questioned whether the most significant therapeutic transformations could be captured solely in words. Adam Phillips devotes an extensive discussion to this aspect (Phillips, <u>1988</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

### 5.1 身体作为精神的基础

温尼科特给予**身体**以作为精神发展之基础的中心重要性——这使他区别于精神分析主流。传统精神分析当然承认身体在精神发展中的作用，但它一般把身体当作**意义的处所**：转换症状中无意识冲突的位点、本能驱力的容器、心身表现与无意识幻想的基底。

> Winnicott, in contrast, emphasized the body as a lived reality, one that cannot be separated from the psyche during the earliest phases of development.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

在《心智及其与精神—躯体的关系》（"Mind and its Relation to the Psyche-Soma", 1954）中，温尼科特描述：在生命之初**只有身体**——而精神与躯体（soma）之间的任何区分都是一个**视角问题**。围绕身体感觉、部位与功能而发展起来的**想象**，对他而言是精神的起点。

> In his essay “Mind and its Relation to the Psyche-Soma” (1954), Winnicott described how, at the beginning of life, there is simply the body—and that any distinction between psyche and soma is a matter of perspective. The development of imagination around bodily sensations, parts, and functions is, for him, the starting point of the psyche.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

「**人格化（personalization）**」这一早期精神发展的支柱：在生命之初，自体与身体之间**没有本质的同一性**，但为了健康，这种整合最终必须达成。渐渐地，精神与身体「**达成协议**」，形成一种身体边界被体验为精神边界的状态。

> What makes personalization possible is precisely this process of the psyche grounding itself in the li…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

Yadlin-Gadot 在注释 4 中给出同一命题的另一种表述，并指出其**与二元本体论的冲突**：

> Winnicott's (1949) concept of psyche–soma echoes the same sensibilities. Whereas in binary ontology the psyche and soma fall into the respective categories of inner and outer, for Winnicott this division is a distortion, similar to the one eventuating in a dominant false self that takes over space and functions from the true self.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，注释 4

### 5.2 厌食症案例：诠释的时机由躯体决定

在《心身疾病的积极与消极方面》（"Psycho-somatic Illness in Its Positive and Negative Aspects", 1966）中，温尼科特描述了他对一位严重神经性厌食年轻女性的治疗。

> For years, the patient’s communications centered exclusively on somatic experiences—belly pains, sensations of objects falling from her body, sharp-edged filing cabinets causing acute physical distress. These could not be interpreted as psychological material or internal objects because they existed precisely in the realm of bodily experience, split off from mental life. The analyst’s role during this phase was not to interpret but to survive—to provide an environment within which such primitive communications could be sustained without forcing premature integration.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

**关键转折**：当患者报告头痛时。这个看似微小的转变代表了根本性的治疗突破，因为**头痛可以被接受为与精神混乱相关联，而非纯粹的躯体扰动**。

> The crucial moment came when the patient reported a headache. This seemingly minor shift represented a fundamental therapeutic breakthrough, for a headache could be accepted as associated with mental confusion rather than purely somatic disturbance. As Winnicott <u>(1966)</u> noted, “I now interpreted that she was telling me about an illness of her mind, and I therefore slipped over from being part of a psycho-somatic team into the role of psychotherapist” (p. 512).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」（引 Winnicott, 1966, p. 512）

只有在这时，腹部症状才消失，取而代之的是**可以用精神分析方式处理的材料**。Govrin 由此给出结论：**在解离阶段，传统的诠释技术不但无用，而且主动有害**。患者的防御性组织恰恰依赖于这个分裂。

> This clinical vignette reveals something profound about the nature of interpretation in such cases. During the dissociated phase, the traditional interpretive technique would have been not merely useless but actively harmful.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Language and the Limits of Interpretation」

## 六、临床鉴别：当分析必须成为「别的什么」

什么样的临床判断会让一位临床医师认定，精神分析的熟悉工具实际上会**伤害而非帮助**？这个问题萦绕了温尼科特整个职业生涯，促使他发展出一种可称为**对诠释工作本身之限度的诊断敏感性（diagnostic sensitivity for the limits of interpretive work itself）**。

> His answer was neither theoretical nor ideological but profoundly practical: some patients require something other than interpretive work precisely because their developmental arrests occurred before symbolic thinking became possible.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Art of Clinical Discrimination」

温尼科特识别出五项具体状况，标志着需要他委婉称之为「**别的什么（something else）**」：

1. 对疯狂的恐惧主导临床图景；
2. 一个成功的**假性自体（false self）**造成了分析必然要摧毁的门面；
3. 反社会倾向反映的是**剥夺的遗留**而非冲突；
4. 内在精神实在仍**未与外部经验相连**；
5. 一位患病的父母形象继续支配患者的内在世界（Winnicott, 1965c）。

> These are not diagnostic categories in any conventional sense, but rather clinical recognitions of developmental predicaments that interpretation cannot reach. Each represents a different form of environmental failure that occurred before the patient possessed the psychological equipment to process trauma symbolically. For such patients, the analyst’s interpretive expertise becomes irrelevant— not because interpretation is wrong, but because it addresses psychological capacities that never had the chance to develop.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Art of Clinical Discrimination」

Govrin 指出这里的根本分歧：**古典分析假定所有患者都拥有诠释工作所必需的基本心理结构**（一个观察性自我、象征思维的能力、精神与躯体的某种整合程度）；温尼科特则认识到，**环境失败可以在这些发展成就固化之前就把它们打断**。

> Where classical analysis assumes that all patients possess the basic psychological structure necessary for interpretive work—an observing ego, the capacity for symbolic thought, some degree of integration between psyche and soma—Winnicott recognized that environmental failure can interrupt these developmental achievements before they solidify. The analyst’s task becomes not interpretation but the provision of conditions within which such basic psychological equipment might finally develop.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Art of Clinical Discrimination」

## 七、★根本悖论：创造那一直在那里的东西

温尼科特背离古典思维最明显之处，在于他对**心理生命如何开始**的理解。传统精神分析从**冲突**开始——欲望与实在之间、本我与超我之间不可避免的冲撞；温尼科特从**悖论**开始。

> As Abram <u>(2012)</u> notes, this centers on what Winnicott called “the essential paradox”: the object that the infant finds was created by the infant, yet existed all along waiting to be found (Winnicott, <u>1989</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Paradox of Psychological Birth」

婴儿的第一个任务不是处理冲突，而是达成某种更基本的东西：**经验可以是有意义的、内在生命与外在实在可以对应、这个世界终究值得投入**的感觉。这需要温尼科特所谓的「**幻觉（illusion）**」——**创造一个人所找到的东西、找到一个人所创造的东西**的暂时体验。

> This requires what Winnicott called “illusion”—the temporary experience of creating what one finds, of finding what one creates. Such illusion depends entirely on environmental responsiveness.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Paradox of Psychological Birth」

Ogden 转引的温尼科特原文（italics 为原文所有）：

> In health the infant creates what is in fact lying around waiting to be found. But in health the object is created, not found … This has to be accepted as a paradox, and not solved by a restatement that, by its cleverness, seems to eliminate the paradox. [Winnicott 1963,p.181, italics in the original]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二节（引 Winnicott, 1963, p. 181）

Abram 的分析揭示了其临床运作：当母亲在婴儿开始需要的那一刻呈上乳房，婴儿就体验到自己**创造了那个满足饥饿的东西**。这是一个**要被保护的发展成就**——一切后来的创造性、自发性以及对实在的投入的基础。

> when the mother presents the breast at the moment the infant begins to need it, the infant experiences having created the very thing that satisfies hunger. This is a developmental achievement to be protected—the basis of all later creativity, spontaneity, and engagement with reality. Without this early illusion of omnipotence, the infant may achieve intellectual understanding of the world while remaining fundamentally unengaged with it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Paradox of Psychological Birth」

**失败时**：从未体验过「创造那被找到的东西」的婴儿，可能发展出温尼科特所谓的「**假性自体**」——一种对环境要求的顺从性适应，把自发自体隐藏起来且不投入。这类患者可以在世界中有效运作，同时感到**根本的不真实，与自己的活生生切断**。**对这些患者，分析师的诠释会感觉像是强加。**

> For these patients, the analyst’s interpretations can feel like impositions r…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Paradox of Psychological Birth」

## 八、过渡性客体与现象：不提问的约定

奥格登把过渡性客体与现象视为温尼科特对本体论精神分析**最重要的贡献**，并完整引述其定义：

> … an intermediate state of experiencing, to which inner reality and external life both contribute. It is a area that is not challenged, because no claim is made on its behalf except that it shall exist as a resting-place for the individual engaged in the perpetual human task of keeping inner and outer reality separate yet interrelated. [p. 2]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二节（引 Winnicott, 1971c, p. 2；原文为 “It is an area”）

婴儿／儿童发展出与过渡性客体和现象相系的「**存在状态**」（Winnicott 1971c, p. 14），要求母亲（或分析师）方面有一个对应的存在状态，其中：

> … it is a matter of agreement between us and the baby that we will never ask the question: ‘ Did you conceive of this [object] or was it presented to you from without?’ The important point is that no decision on this point is expected. The question is not to be formulated. [Winnicott 1971c,p.12, italics in the original]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二节（引 Winnicott, 1971c, p. 12）

**「这个问题不该被提出（The question is not to be formulated）」——这是对诠释最彻底的限制条款**：在过渡领域中，「是你创造的还是它被给予你的」这一问题被悬置，而精神分析的诠释工作恰恰习惯于提出这个问题。

这一存在状态支撑着「**属于艺术、宗教与想象性生活**的强烈体验」（Winnicott 1971c, p. 14）。

## 九、持续存在与非沟通性的中心自体

位于自体核心的存在状态（Ogden 第二节引述）：

> the non-communicating central self, for ever immune from the reality principle [immune to the need to respond to anything external to the self], and for ever silent. Here communication is not non-verbal; it is, like the music of the spheres, absolutely personal. It belongs to being alive. And in health, it is out of this that communication naturally arises. [1963,p.192]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二节（引 Winnicott, 1963, p. 192）

奥格登的评论指出：位于我们核心的、使这一存在状态**不可想象**的，是它的沉默**既非言语性的，也非「非言语性的」**。**既非言语亦非非言语的沉默，超出人类理解之外。**

> The silence at the core of the self is not verbal in nature, but what makes the state of being at our core unimaginable is the fact that it is also “not non-verbal.” Silence that is neither verbal nor non-verbal is beyond human comprehension.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第二节

其他温尼科特式存在状态（Ogden 第二节列举）：持续存在（going-on-being，1949, p. 245——「全然是动词、没有主语，从而捕捉到某种非常早期的无主体的存在状态」）、在母亲被婴儿摧毁时存活（1969）、原初母亲专注（primary maternal preoccupation，1956）。

## 十、★我因需要诠释而阻止了改变：奥格登引温尼科特

这是奥格登区分认识论与本体论精神分析的**第三条标准（分析师的角色：诠释 vs. 等待）**所依赖的核心引文：

> It appalls me to think how much deep change I have prevented or delayed … by my personal need to interpret. If only we can wait, the patient arrives at understanding creatively and with immense joy, and I now enjoy this joy more than I used to enjoy the sense of having been clever. [Winnicott 1969, p. 86]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节（引 Winnicott, 1969, p. 86）

奥格登随即点出重点的转移——**不是所得的知识，而是「抵达理解」的那个体验**：

> From the perspective of ontological psychoanalysis, it is not the knowledge arrived at by patient and analyst that is the central point; rather, it is the patient’s experience of “arriv[ing] at understanding creatively and with immense joy,” an experience in which the patient is engaged not predominantly in searching for self-understanding, but in experiencing the process of becoming more fully himself.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

同一逻辑在「名词 vs. 动名词」之别上重演——温尼科特本人的自述：

> It is obvious that I am making a significant distinction between the meanings of the noun ‘play’ and the verbal noun ‘playing.’ [pp. 39-40]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节（引 Winnicott, 1971a, pp. 39-40）

奥格登的划界：

> Arriving at understandings of the symbolic meaning of play is the domain of epistemological psychoanalysis; working in and with the state of being involved in playing is the domain of ontological psychoanalysis.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

治疗的场域由那句名言给出，其推论是：**当游戏不可能时，治疗师的工作方向是把病人从不能游戏的状态带到能够游戏的状态**。

> Psychotherapy takes place in the overlap of two areas of playing, that of the patient and the therapist. The corollary to this is that where playing is not possible then the work done by the therapist is directed towards bringing the patient from a state of not being able to play into a state of being able to play. [Winnicott 1971a, p. 38, italics in the original]

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节（引 Winnicott, 1971a, p. 38）

由此派生出一条临床判据：**若分析师无法进入游戏状态，他必须区分这究竟是与病人之间发生之事的反映，还是他自己的无力**（后者可能要求他重返分析）。

> If the analyst is unable to engage in playing, he must determine whether his inability to engage in this state of being (playing is not simply a state of mind, it is a state of being) is a reflection of what is occurring between him and the patient (possibly a profound identification with the patient’s lifelessness) or a reflection of his own inability to genuinely engage in playing, which would likely require that he return to analysis.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

**奥格登的划界结论**：他不把温尼科特与比昂算作**客体关系理论家**——二人著作中甚少提及无意识内部客体关系，其首要关切是存在状态的范围；且温尼科特甚少提及无意识。

## 十一、Yadlin-Gadot：过渡领域的认识论转向

Yadlin-Gadot 把温尼科特视为本文**最重要的思想资源**，并给出其历史地位判定：**温尼科特是第一个把「过渡领域」定义为自主实体的人——它「立于」内在与外在现实「之间」**。

> Winnicott’s (1969) move from the experiential realm of “relating” to “usage” (1969) reflects the same sensibilities as regards the delineation of inner and outer realities. However, Winnicott (1951) is the first to define as an autonomous entity the transitional realm that which stands between the inner and the outer realities. Winnicott’s third area of intermediateness collapses the internal–external/subject– object divide that is inherent in the notion of Correspondence.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

**但这一认识论转向只在这一第三经验领域之内发生**——在此之外，我们仍可清晰区分内在与外在、精神与外部现实。虽然温尼科特维持世界与自体的传统二元论，但**价值开始了一次倒转运动**：内在的「真」被评价得比以前更高。

> In this sense he is effecting an epistemological shift, but only within this third area of experience. Outside of it, we may still make clear the differentiation of inner and outer, psychic and external real- ity. Whereas Winnicott maintains the traditional dualism of world and self, values begin a movement of reversal because the inner "true" is valued more highly than before.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

最凝练的对照句：

> Freud wanted ego where there was id. Winnicott, endowing the id with the respect- ed adjective of "true," offers it vindication. Winnicott's chaperon- ing of Subjective-Existential truth and the transitional realm he created might well have played a part in the gradual legitimation of idealistic trends in psychoanalysis.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

## 十二、★批评：Blass 与 Etchegoyen

温尼科特的贡献广受赞誉，但也引发了**实质性争议**——其创新究竟代表精神分析的合法发展，还是**构成对其本质的背离**。

**Rachel Blass（2012）的系统性批评**提出三重关切：

1. **分析师从中立诠释者转变为主动的、母性的形象**（提供抱持、承受攻击），这挑战了传统的分析性中立；
2. **他对非象征性的、前客体关联（pre-object relating）的强调**——「在一起」（being together）而非诠释无意识材料——代表了对既定技术的**根本背离**；
3. **精神分析与支持性心理治疗之间边界的模糊**，侵蚀了精神分析干预的**特异性**。

> She identifies three critical concerns: First, the transformation of the analyst from neutral interpreter to active, maternal figure providing holding and surviving attacks challenges traditional analytic neutrality. Second, his emphasis on non-symbolic, pre-object relating—“being together” rather than interpreting unconscious material—represents a fundamental departure from established technique. Third, the blurring of boundaries between psychoanalysis and supportive psychotherapy undermines the specificity of psychoanalytic intervention.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Limits of Innovation」（引 Blass, 2012）

**Horacio Etchegoyen（1991）**提供了额外的理论依据，强调**通过分析设置中的坚定性与风格上的回应性之间的微妙平衡来维持分析契约**的必要性。

> Horacio Etchegoyen provides additional theoretical grounding for these concerns, emphasizing the necessity of maintaining the analytic contract through a delicate balance between firmness in setting and responsiveness in style (Etchegoyen, <u>1991</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Limits of Innovation」

**两位批评者的共同让步**：他们都承认温尼科特的技术回应了重要的临床现象，特别是那些**对古典诠释无反应的严重障碍患者**。

> Both critics acknowledge that Winnicott’s techniques address important clinical phenomena, particularly with severely disturbed patients unresponsive to classical interpretation. However, they argue that the modifications required by his approach may compromise the essential characteristics distinguishing psychoanalysis from other therapeutic modalities.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「The Limits of Innovation」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：温尼科特的技术创新是精神分析的合法发展，还是对精神分析本质的背离？
> **A 方立场**：Blass（2012）与 Etchegoyen（1991）——三重关切（中立性的丧失、对非象征性前客体关联的强调、与支持性心理治疗的边界模糊）；Etchegoyen 强调维持分析契约需要设置上的坚定与风格上的回应之间的微妙平衡。二者承认其对严重障碍患者的临床价值，但主张其修正**可能损害精神分析区别于其他治疗模态的本质特征**。
> **B 方立场**：Govrin / 奥格登 / Yadlin-Gadot——温尼科特是被弗洛伊德圈子边缘化的**费伦齐传统**的正当继承者；是「创伤疗愈传统」的开创者（Govrin 第四章）；是**本体论精神分析的两位主要缔造者之一**（Ogden, *Ontological*）；是**把「过渡领域」定义为自主实体、并「坍塌了」内—外／主—客体之分的第一人**（Yadlin-Gadot）。
> **备注**：属**范式层面的不可通约分歧**，而非可调和的技术之争。争论的真正焦点是：**精神分析的特异性究竟在于「诠释无意识材料」这一操作，还是在于「促进存在与生成」这一结果**。奥格登的「感受力而非学派」限定在此可防止把对立做成教条：他明言**弗洛伊德与克莱因的著作中有许多是本体论性质的，温尼科特与比昂的著作中也有许多是认识论的**。

## 十三、结论：揭示性时间 vs. 成熟性时间

第四章的结论节给出温尼科特位置的最完整表述。

**核心转移**：古典传统强调分析师**知道什么**以及**何时说出**；温尼科特把问题整个地移开了——**不是诠释包含什么，而是患者把它体验为来自内部还是来自外部的侵入**。

> The classical tradition emphasized what the analyst knows and when to say it. Winnicott shifted the question entirely: not what does the interpretation contain, but does the patient experience it as coming from within or as an intrusion from without?

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

**一个准确但过早的诠释，侵犯了它声称要照亮的那种经验能力本身。** 分析师知道得太多太快，就变得悖论性地无用——或者更糟，变成一种**诱惑（seduction）**，邀请顺从而非成长。这就是为什么温尼科特可以主张：**一个以分析师的标准看来完美定时的辉煌诠释，可能是所有干预中最反治疗的**。

> An interpretation that is accurate but premature violates the very capacity for experience it claims to illuminate. The analyst who knows too much too soon becomes, paradoxically, useless—or worse, becomes a seduction, inviting compliance rather than growth. This is why Winnicott could suggest that a brilliant interpretation, perfectly timed by the analyst’s standards, might be the most anti-therapeutic intervention of all.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

**时间的维度在此变得关键**：古典诠释依据的是「**揭示性时间（revelatory time）**」——分析师知道患者尚不知道的东西，诠释逐步缩小这一差距；温尼科特的进路遵循「**成熟性时间（maturational time）**」——患者自己的发展时间表，它**不能通过洞见被加速**，并且可能需要一些根本没有任何可诠释之事发生的时期。

> Classical interpretation operates according to what might be called “revelatory time”—the analyst knows something the patient doesn’t yet know and interpretation gradually closes this gap. Winnicott’s approach follows “maturational time”—the patient’s own developmental schedule, which cannot be accelerated through insight and may require periods where nothing interpretable occurs at all.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

**身体之所以成为中心**，是因为他认识到心理发展始于精神与躯体的渐进整合——**一个如果时机错误就会被诠释打断的过程**。厌食症患者的腹痛不是需要解码的**符号**，而是**寻求一个关系语境以便整合最终成为可能的实际躯体经验**。

> This transforms our understanding of what psychoanalytic expertise involves. Rather than interpretive virtuosity, it requires what we might call “environmental attunement”—the capacity to provide the kinds of relational conditions within which the patient’s own developmental processes…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

**温尼科特所引发的争议不是技术性的，而是生存性的（existential）**：他迫使精神分析面对它自己的排除，它自己**对人类实际上需要什么才能繁盛的无知的激情（passion for ignorance）**。

> The controversy Winnicott generated was not merely technical but existential: he forced psychoanalysis to confront its own exclusions, its own passion for ignorance about what human beings actually require in order to thrive.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「Conclusion」

## 十四、后记中的温尼科特

Govrin 全书开篇所列的三种互不相容的诠释现象中，第三种——**诠释不通过语词而通过「在场的品质」本身发生**——在温尼科特处得到最早的表述。全书结尾则把温尼科特的问题（「你长大以后想成为什么？」）与诠释的命运相连：

> The deepest answer to Winnicott’s question—“What do you want to be when you grow up?”—may simply be: “Myself, but more so.”

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节（关于 Ogden, 2019）
