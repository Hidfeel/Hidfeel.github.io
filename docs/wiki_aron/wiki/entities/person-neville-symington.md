> **类型**: entity
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-28
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-28

## 摘要

内维尔·西明顿在 *The Analyst's Act of Freedom as Agent of Therapeutic Change* 一文中提出并命名了「**x-phenomenon**」：分析师身上一次**内在的自由行动（act of inner freedom）**。其核心主张是时序性的，而非替代性的——**内在自由行动先于并促成诠释**：诠释是必要的，因为它把已经发生的转变**表达**出来并使其可及于意识，但「改变的必要动因是分析师的**内在**行动」，而这个内在行动会被患者感知到并引起改变。

为支撑这一主张，西明顿建构了一套关于分析情境的元心理学：患者与分析师共同构成一个**团体性人格（corporate personality）**；移情与反移情是「分析工作缓慢加以解除的**共享错觉**」；而阻抗不是一个位于患者身上的东西，而是一个**双方都卷入其中的系统**，以错觉与文化神话为工具。精神分析的功能被界定为催化**自我对自我的接触（ego to ego contact）**——人格中非团体性、个人性与个体性的区域；由此它与**宗教**在功能上正相反，后者的中心社会功能是把人结合进团体性实体。

## 一、x-phenomenon：命名与第一个临床例证

文章开篇即声明其对象是「所有分析师都熟悉的一个现象」，并预告论述顺序：先描述，后考察其理论意涵。

> In this chapter I intend to explore a phenomenon with which all analysts are familiar. I will first describe it and then examine what its implications are for theory. I shall refer to it as the ‘x-phenomenon’.

— Symington, *Freedom*，引论

第一个例证是 Miss M.（收费案例）。分析师长期把「可怜的 Miss M.，£X 是我能向她收取的最高额」当作「我心灵的家具（the furniture of my mind）」加以默认，直到一个念头闯入：

> So the analysis went on and on with that assumption as its unquestioned concomitant until one day a startling thought occurred to me: ‘Why can’t Miss M. pay the same as all my other patients?’ Then I remembered the resentment she frequently expressed towards her boss who always called her *‘Little Mary’*. A certainty began to grow in me that I was the prisoner of an illusion about the patient’s capacities. I had been lassoed into the patient’s self-perception and I was just beginning to extricate myself from it.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证一

后续发展被归因为这一**时刻**：

> I think the source of these beneficial changes was in that moment of inner freedom when I had the unexpected thought: ‘Why can’t Miss M. pay the same as my other patients?’ I am calling this act of inner freedom the ‘x-phenomenon’.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证一

第二个例证借比昂《四次讨论》中一段话获得解放——比昂反问：病人想要你给出正确的诠释，这**同样是一种限制**：

> In view of what you are saying I think I would try to draw her attention to the way in which she wishes to limit my freedom about what I call her. It is just as much a limitation if the patient wants you to give the correct interpretation. Why shouldn’t I be free to form my own opinion that she’s a whore or that she is something quite different? Why be angry with me because in fact I am free to come to my own conclusions?

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证二（引 Bion, 1978, pp. 15–16）

由此产生的干预只有一句——「但我完全有自由那样想」：

> But I am quite free to think that.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证二

西明顿的回溯判断是：

> Again the source of all this interpretative work and insight started from the moment of my own inner act of freedom. So this was another case of the ‘x-phenomenon’.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证二

## 二、★核心主张：内在自由行动先于且促成诠释

这是全文的论证中枢，必须在四个例证全部给出之后才被正面陈述：

> My contention is that the inner act of freedom in the analyst causes a therapeutic shift in the patient and new insight, learning and development in the analyst. The interpretation is essential in that it gives *expression* to the shift that has already occurred and makes it available to consciousness. The point though is that the essential agent of change is the *inner* act of the analyst and that this inner act is perceived by the patient and causes change.

— Symington, *Freedom*，x-phenomenon 的界定

其认识论前提是：最内在的心智行动也有可感知的外显相关物。

> Even the most inner mental act has some manifest correlate that is perceptible, though this perceptibility may be unconscious and probably is. The psychotic is particularly sensitized to these minute changes.

— Symington, *Freedom*，同上节

两个支持性观察（均出自幻觉性精神病女性患者的治疗）。其一，分析师因另一位病人自杀而心绪不宁，作了诠释，患者回应：

> I am not taking stick for your bad experience.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证三

其二，分析师在静默中暗自决定结束于某个周二，患者恰在那一刻说：

> You have interrupted my thoughts, you have just stolen something from me.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证三

西明顿的总括是：

> The most inward mental act reverberates through the sensational and perceptual spheres.

— Symington, *Freedom*，同上节

### 2.1 「诠释」的重新定位：表达，而非动因

这一主张把诠释从「改变的首要动因」位置上撤下，但撤法是**时序性的**（先有转变，后有表达），而非替代性的。结论部分的对应表述是：

> The ‘x-phenomenon’ implies that there is a knowledge that is pre-verbal and that it is anterior to speech and therefore to interpretation. At this level of knowledge the patient knows unconsciously the analyst’s internal attitudes. If, for example, the analyst is unconsciously envious of the patient in some particular way then the patient perceives it and only a change in the analyst’s inner attitude will enable the patient to move forward psychically. The moment the analyst becomes aware of his or her attitude and is freed from it then the patient perceives it. That is to say he or she perceives a change within the self and may make declarations to that effect without knowing the cause. The interpretations that follow the x-phenomenon become conscious articulations of a change that has already occurred unconsciously at the ego to ego level.

— Symington, *Freedom*，结论段

诠释还有第二重功能——**重建超我**，使其神话与价值观变得与自我中已经发生的变化相调谐：

> The interpretations help them to re-establish the superego so that its myths and values change and become tuned in to the changes that have occurred within the ego.

— Symington, *Freedom*，结论段

## 三、团体性人格（corporate personality）

为解释「内在行动如何能被患者感知」，西明顿提出：患者与分析师共同构成一个单一系统与一个实体。

> I think at one level the analyst and patient together make a single system. Together they form an entity which we might call a corporate personality. From the moment that patient and analyst engage in what we call an analysis the two are together part of an illusory system. Both are caught into it.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

他并顺带批评了近期文献的保守：

> Recent literature stresses that the analyst is not just a mirror but this is a gross understatement. The analyst is lassoed into the patient’s illusory world. He is more involved in it, more victim to it than the average social contact. As the analytical work proceeds the analyst slowly disengages himself from it.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

融合的**部位**由超我承担：

> When the patient first comes to the analyst’s consulting room it is probable that a fusing takes place of the analyst and patient via the superegos of each. Transference and countertransference are emotional expressions of this fusion.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

超我概念在此被帕森斯（Talcott Parsons）式的社会学论证放大——不只是道德标准，**共同文化的全部成分**都被内化为人格结构的一部分：

> The inescapable conclusion is that not only moral standards, but *all the components of the common culture* are internalized as part of the personality structure. Moral standards, indeed, cannot in this respect be dissociated from the *content* of the orientation patterns which they regulate.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段（引 Parsons, 1952, p. 23）

## 四、共享错觉与共享阻抗

> transference and countertransference are two parts of a single system; together they form a unity. They are the shared illusions which the work of analysis slowly undoes.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

阻抗被重新描述为一个**系统**，而非患者身上的属性：

> At the same time there is another process at work, in both the analyst and the patient, whose goal is to sabotage the analysis. This process is located in the superego and makes use of illusions and cultural myths as its instrument. We call this process resistance but I have wanted to emphasize that this is a system in which both analyst and patient are involved, not something that is just located in the patient.

— Symington, *Freedom*，结论段

团体实体内部的双重过程被并置：

> So in the corporate entity there is a shared illusion or delusion (transference/countertransference) and shared resistance and there is also a process which we call psychoanalysis which fights a slow but persistent battle in both against the shared resistance and illusion. The analytic process catalyses the individual to individual existent reality. The x-phenomenon is a product of the analytic process. The latter works at a deep level, at a pre-verbal, primary process level. It finds its verbal expression in interpretation. Interpretation expresses this deep change and effects the final consummation of it at the conscious and manifest level.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

一个后果是：既有的理论立场可能被阻抗过程所用，也可能被良性的精神分析过程所用。

> Received theoretical positions may be used by the resistant process, as they also may be used by the benign psychoanalytical process. The patient and analyst as a corporate entity are involved in these two processes.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

### 4.1 对「古典技术」的批评（同时声明并不轻蔑技术本身）

> I hope it will not be inferred that I am scorning analytic technique; this would be the very opposite of what I am intending to say. After all the soul of analytic technique is to free analyst and patient from the normal social constraints and so favour development of the inner world. The problem is when ‘classical technique’ becomes the agent of a new social constraint.

— Symington, *Freedom*，临床例证三之后的反思段

## 五、自我对自我的接触（ego to ego contact）

> Psychoanalysis is a process which catalyses the ego to ego contact: that area of the personality that is non-corporate, personal and individual. In this way psychoanalysis is working in the opposite way to religion, whose central social function is to bind people together into corporate entities.

— Symington, *Freedom*，理论建构段

### 5.1 ★与宗教的功能对立

需要注明：这是**社会功能**上的类型学对照，而非对信仰内容的批评。宗教的中心社会功能是**把人结合进团体性实体**；精神分析则相反，催化人格中**非团体性、个人性与个体性**的区域中的自我对自我接触。维持患者与分析师结合的那个虚假信念，被比作社会黏合剂的微观形态：

> This belief that held both together is that social glue in microcosm that binds together the numerous communities and groupings of society. This type of togetherness is quite different from the ego to ego contact that occurs at particular moments in analysis.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

自我对自我的接触被赋予近乎政治性的意义：

> This type of contact is a revolution because new reality, new growth begins. In fact, it is the only true revolution that does occur within human affairs.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

### 5.2 患者需要触及分析师的**核心感受**

> In order to separate, the patient needs to get access to the analyst’s core feelings. His interpretations need to flow from here to as great an extent as possible if the patient is to be able to separate. This is most especially true for the psychotic patient whose fusion at the superego level is greatest and whose need for ego to ego contact is also greatest.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

由此产生一条可被患者检验的判准——患者在意的是分析师所说的究竟**是他自己所想所感**，还是精神分析传统的「**既定语录**」（即只是超我内容）：

> It greatly concerned one patient whether what I said to him was what *I* thought or felt or was just a received dictum of the psychoanalytical tradition and therefore just a superego content.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

> My greatest problem in his analysis was to reach those feelings which were most truly mine. In the case of that patient the problem was particularly acute but on reflection I think this may be a central problem in every analysis.

— Symington, *Freedom*，同上节

## 六、认识论辩护：一种不经由感官、也不经由意识的特殊知识

西明顿承认自己的说法看似与弗洛伊德相矛盾，然后引弗洛伊德**自己**的两段话反证：

> What I have said so far may seem to contradict Freud’s view that our only knowledge of the external world is through perception, mediated consciously by the ego. In nearly all Freud’s writings he followed the scientific view of his day which was that man’s knowledge of his fellow man is via his senses and does not differ essentially from his knowledge of the nonhuman world.

— Symington, *Freedom*，认识论辩护段

> I have had good reason for asserting that everyone possesses in his own unconscious an instrument with which he can interpret the utterances of the unconscious in other people. (1913i, p. 320)

— Symington, *Freedom*，认识论辩护段（引 Freud）

> It is a very remarkable thing that the *Ucs*. of one human being can react upon that of another, without passing through the *Cs*. This deserves closer investigation, especially with a view to finding out whether preconscious activity can be excluded as playing a part in it; but, descriptively speaking, the fact is incontestable. (1915e, p. 194)

— Symington, *Freedom*，认识论辩护段（引 Freud）

他的评论点明了与诠释的时序关系：

> He is here talking about a special type of knowledge that exists between human beings that does not pass through the normal sense organs or through that conscious part of the personality inhabited by word-presentations. This particular type of knowledge therefore antedates any interpretation that the analyst may give.

— Symington, *Freedom*，认识论辩护段

其哲学谱系被追至维柯（Vico）——在形而上学／神学、演绎、知觉三种知识之外，加上第四种：人对自己与他人所具有的知识，即 *per causas* 的「从内部」所知——以及韦伯（Max Weber）的 *Verstehen* / *Wissen* 之分。他也诊断了这一传统在人文社会科学与学院心理学中的边缘地位：

> Although this special type of knowledge has been central to clinical work in psychoanalytic practice there does not seem to be a metapsychology to account for it. The idea that a scientist might take this type of knowledge seriously is also scorned by almost all schools of thinking within academic psychology.

— Symington, *Freedom*，认识论辩护段

本文即为弥补这一元心理学缺口而作。

## 七、洞见行动的类比：读康德

为说明「理解如何发生」，西明顿用读《纯粹理性批判》作类比：不理解是因为有一个错误的观念遮蔽了理智；理解发生于驱逐这一错误观念之际——而抗拒驱逐，往往是因为它意味着放弃许多与自己的生活方式或思维习惯相契合的珍爱的观念。

> At the moment of understanding I become Kant, as it were, through an action of the ego, whereby I dispel my superego contents and because of this I remain separated and become slightly more of an individual. At the moment of understanding activity and passivity come together and form a single psychological event.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

映射到分析情境：

> The analyst does not only have his own false ideas to clear away but needs to be passive to the analytical process and combating the resistance that he and the patient are locked into. The attempt to understand is being continually sabotaged by a parallel process that stimulates and fosters false ideas.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

> Belief in the psychoanalytical process seems to be the essential ingredient for both parties. However, it seems that it may be the special role of the analyst to carry this belief for the patient as well as for himself, especially early on in treatment. The act of understanding is rooted in what is most personal, in the ego, but the false ideas are located in the superego. At the moment of insight, expressed in interpretation, the illusions or false ideas are banished both in analyst and in patient. A personal, ego to ego, contact is established.

— Symington, *Freedom*，洞见行动段

## 八、对投射性认同的批评：遗漏了第三词项

西明顿预先回应一种可能的归类，然后拒绝之：

> It could be argued that what I am describing is a particular instance of projective identification but I do not think this does justice to those psychological events which, for want of a better term, I have called the x-phenomenon. Projective identification means that feelings which belong to the patient are projected into the analyst and lodge there like a foreign body. What I am describing is a joint process in which the real feelings of analyst and patient are aroused by the resistant process. The analyst’s feelings are *his* feelings even though they may have been stirred up by the patient. Patient and analyst are responsible for the feelings that are generated in the situation. Often the patient is ‘blamed’ for feelings experienced by the analyst and this is called projective identification. This type of description implies that there are only two blameable objects in the room: patient and analyst. There is a third term: the process in which both are involved.

— Symington, *Freedom*，批评投射性认同段

## 九、结论：个体性 + 团体性实体的双重存在

全文最后一段给出其形而上学结论，并批评了整个领域的默认假设：

> With the exception of Winnicott I think that most analysts operate on the assumption that people are separate entities. I think that the x-phenomenon and the particular form of knowledge that it must imply means that people are individuals and yet part of a corporate entity. Because we are parts of a corporate entity then as soon as analyst and patient come together in the same room there is an immediate adaptation and fusing. The corporate entity instantly establishes itself. Socially this occurs when two people meet, but, in this case, ego-to-ego contact is kept to a minimum, so also the joint illusion is kept to a minimum. In psychoanalysis the latter is enhanced but only, so to speak, so as to hypercathect it and work through it and give place to the personal.

— Symington, *Freedom*，结论段（全文末段）

### 9.1 关于精神病患者的知觉特殊性

文中另有一处对精神病性知觉的正面刻画，可与其「内在行动可被感知」的主张互证：

> The psychotic is not cut off from reality but rather one minute aspect of reality is enlarged so that the rest of the mental or emotional field is crowded out. It is like the zoom lens on a television camera that swoops down on one object of interest and that one object then takes up the whole television screen.

— Symington, *Freedom*，x-phenomenon 的界定段

## 十、与奥格登的对照（综合）

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：诠释在治疗行动中的位置
> **A 方立场**：奥格登——在本体论维度中，分析师在传达理解之前最好「等待」；重点不在所得的知识，而在病人**创造性抵达理解的体验**——「It appalls me to think how much deep change I have prevented or delayed … by my personal need to interpret」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节，引 Winnicott）
> **B 方立场**：西明顿——诠释「是必要的」，因为它把已经发生的转变表达出来并使其可及于意识；但「改变的必要动因是分析师的**内在**行动」——「The interpretation is essential in that it gives *expression* to the shift that has already occurred」（Symington, *Freedom*，x-phenomenon 的界定）
> **备注**：可调和。两文都把诠释从「改变的首要动因」位置上撤下，但撤法不同：奥格登是**替代性**的（以存在状态取代理解），西明顿是**时序性**的（转变在先，表达在后）。二者在对「分析师最属己之物」的强调与技术非规则化上高度一致。详见 [[topics/topic-therapeutic-action-debate]]。
