## 摘要

什洛米特·亚德林-加多特在 *Truth Axes and the Transformation of Self*（*Psychoanalytic Review*, 104(2), April 2017）中提出**真理轴（truth axes）**模型，试图为当代精神分析的多元认识论与多元真理开辟理论空间，而不在诸认识论之间作取舍。

其论证由三个原创步骤构成。第一步是**心理学化的真理定义**：哲学史的考察表明真理问题的根源是心理性的——人有确定性、恒常性与心智控制的需要——因此「真理所满足的并非**事态**，而是**心境**」。由此在实在、命题、人类意识的约束这三个秩序之外，加入第四个秩序：作为思考者的主体的心理需要。第二步是辨析认识论与真理的关系：二者相互关联却不等同，每种认识论「自然产生」一种对它而言原初的真理，并**只偏爱那一种**；这一赋值决定了治疗目标与方法。第三步是提出「真理轴」：每条轴把**认识论预设 + 特征性自体状态 + 现实意象 + 被体验的真理**四者绑定为一个多维心理领域，为主体在生命关键维度上提供**确定性的阿基米德点（Archimedean point of certainty）**。六种范式真理——对应（Correspondent）、融贯（Coherent）、理想（Ideal）、主观-存在（Subjective-Existential）、主体间（Intersubjective）、实用（Pragmatic）——即六条轴。

其临床后果是权威基础的迁移：后现代放弃单一真理之后，分析师的权威不再建立在「知道真理」上，而建立在**认识论专长（epistemic expertise）**上。

## 二、起点：唯一真理的碎裂

> Freud adhered to the idea that psychoanalysis is a science and that truth is one. The transition from a realistic epistemology to an epistemology of subjective idealism in psychoanalytic thought was accompanied by the splintering of the “one” realistic truth into a multiplicity of truths: realistic-correspondent, ideal, subjective-existential, intersubjective, coherent, and pragmatic truths.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

本文并不打算在这些认识论之间作取舍：

> The present paper, employing the concept of “truth axes,” explores these truths as they relate to basic human needs, self-states, and the structuring of subjectivity. Truth axes are posited as organizing principles of the psyche aimed at achieving stable images of reality across critical dimensions of the subject’s life. Personality and experience render some axes dominant, while others remain foreclosed and dissociated.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

> In this construal, the psychoanalytic process concerns the detailing, depicting, and understanding of the various truth axes. The psychological definition of truth illuminates its relation to clinical objectives and methodologies and emphasizes the ethical dimension involved in prioritization of truths.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

最后一句是本页的枢轴：对真理的**优先排序**本身携带**伦理**维度——这正是她与 [[entities/person-aner-govrin]] 的「认识论谦逊」可以对接之处。

## 三、★核心翻转：真理满足的是「心境」而非「事态」

在「TRUTH AS NEED」一节，她综述了把真理还原为需要的现代路线（尼采、实用主义者、福柯、德里达等），然后给出结论：

> Notwithstanding the differences in content, all of the preceding views suggest that the pertinence of truth is of a psychological nature: Human beings have a need for certainty, constancy and mental control. Contrary to naive intuition, and many philosophical positions, this implies that what truth satisfies are not states of affairs, but rather states of mind. If until now the search for truth involved three orders—reality, propositions, and the constraints of human consciousness—we now add a fourth order: that of the psychological needs of the subject-as-thinker.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「TRUTH AS NEED」

她随即引拉康（Jacques Lacan）的格言作结：

> This is neatly captured in the clever aphorism proposed by Jacques Lacan (1998): For the physicist, where there is smoke there is fire, but for the psychoanalyst where there is smoke there is a smoker. The need for truth is an overarching need for certainty and it expresses itself across the different dimensions of the subject’s life.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「TRUTH AS NEED」

这一翻转的后果是：真理不再是一个静态的参照点（关于某个特定事实或陈述），而是一个漫长的**排序过程**的动态产物。

> First, one must give up the idea that truth is one and absolute. Second, one must keep in mind that truth is inextricably tied up with the subject and determined by his or her needs. Third, truth is not static; it is not a specific point of reference regarding a particular fact or statement. Instead, it is a dynamic product of a long and gradual process of ordering. Fourth, different notions of truth do not invalidate each other. Each notion relies, in one way or another, on the way that another notion captures a different aspect of reality.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「FROM TRUTH NOTIONS TO TRUTH AXES」

## 四、认识论与真理：自然产生与偏爱

> Epistemology and truth are not one and the same, but they are interrelated. Each epistemology has a notion of truth that it naturally gives rise to. Thus, realistic epistemology is the natural home of Correspondent truth.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Relation Between Truth and Epistemology and the Question of Values」

> Epistemologies may recognize various truths, but they favor the truth that is primary to them. In the context of psychoanalysis, this valuing of truths is reflected in the definition of therapeutic objectives and methodologies.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

由此得出她对学派之争的诊断：诸学派其实**承认同样的真理**，差别在于赋值，以及对「追求某一种真理时所启动的过程」的解释。

> What is clear from this short discussion is that analysts from different psychoanalytic schools acknowledge the different truths that influence mental life generally and analysis particularly. But they ascribe their value differently, often as dictated by their epistemological convictions, and interpret differently the processes that are set in motion when pursuing a particular truth.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

这与戈夫林的判断高度接近：戈夫林在 [[raw/govrin-interpretation]] 中写道，问题「不是治疗中是否出现真理，而是出现何种真理、以何种方式出现」。

## 五、六种范式真理

> In a comprehensive review of the history of the idea of truth, I identify six paradigmatic notions of truth (Yadlin-Gadot, 2016): Correspondent, Coherent, Ideal, Intersubjective, Subjective-Existential, and Pragmatic truths (for clarity, I shall denote them with capital letters).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Six Truth Notions」

**定义与认识论前提（原文逐条）**

| 真理 | 判定方式 | 认识论归属 |
|---|---|---|
| 对应（Correspondent） | 命题与外在于心智的事实之间的**对应关系**（亚里士多德） | 实在论：假定主体能借感官认识独立于心智的世界 |
| 融贯（Coherent） | 一个信念与整个信念系统的**相容性**（斯宾诺莎）；一个信念在**它与其他信念相融贯的程度上**为真（亨佩尔） | 唯心论；导致真理的「本质性软化」 |
| 理想（Ideal） | 特定信念或经验个案与相应**永恒形式／理型**之间的相容性（柏拉图） | 「客观唯心论」：存在一个感官不可及、只能借艰苦心智过程部分认识的、独立于心智的秩序 |
| 主观-存在（Subjective-Existential） | 真理是高度个人化的，体现主体的**所经验到的本真性**；经验先于任何本质或概括（克尔凯郭尔） | 主观唯心论：以「我」／自我为现实的构成性来源 |
| 主体间（Intersubjective） | 客观性由**人际协议**建立，有时明示，通常是隐含的 | 主观唯心论 |
| 实用（Pragmatic） | 按**未来收益**组织经验；在达成目标上感到自己是有效的 | 实用主义 |

关键的结构性区分是客观唯心论与主观唯心论：

> Subjective Idealism shares the premise of absolute and objective idealism, that all known reality resides within the mind. But whereas the latter assumes a principle external to the system that creates and unifies it, the former assumes the “I” or the Ego as the constituting source of reality. Subjective and Intersubjective truths are embedded in the assumptions of subjective idealism (Orange, Stolorow, & Atwood, 1998).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Six Truth Notions」

## 六、★真理轴：定义、阿基米德点与四要素

> The history of Western philosophical thought has given rise to six paradigmatic notions of truth, truths that frequent our language as well as clinical experience, philosophical theory, and psychoanalytic meta-theory. I will present each of these truths as reflecting an organizing principle in the psyche and as constituting its Archimedean point of certainty. Each organizing principle selects and organizes disparate elements of perception, emotion, and thought, and enforces certain rules of combination among them. Every organization of this kind produces a distinct image of reality, together with a definable self hood that resides within it. These images sometimes overlap but are often incompatible and generate tensions within the psyche. I term these organizational principles “Truth Axes.”

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

四要素的严格绑定：

> The joining together of epistemic assumption, characteristic self-state, image of reality, and experienced truth form a truth axis. In that sense, a truth axis is a multidimensional mental domain. The truth axes, their products, and their motivational forces exist on all levels of mental activity, from basic experiences of sensation and emotion, to ground-level representations (memory, imagination, etc.), and then to outlooks on life, both intuitive and formulated (e.g., in language).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「FROM TRUTH NOTIONS TO TRUTH AXES」

诸轴同时运作，其构型由个体发展史塑造——某些轴被增强，另一些被抑制与**封禁（foreclosed）**：

> In each person’s psyche, these principles of organization function simultaneously, embodied in distinctive cognitive, experiential, and emotional modes. Their unique configuration is shaped by a singular developmental history that enhances certain axes, inhibits and forecloses others and, in that way, determines their interrelations.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，引言

> The six organizing axes exist in the psychic space of every person in different forms and degrees of dominance. Each person may have one or two dominant axes, whose relation to other axes may vary considerably.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

### 6.1 诸轴并存如棱镜

> The various truth axes coexist in consciousness as prisms through which various realities are construed, each giving rise to a different experience of self and world.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

值得注意的是她与关系／后现代作家的分歧：自体状态**不是**零散的、无限的、纯情境性的，而是服从于与真理轴相对应的**普遍排列**。

> Rather, the different self-states are understood as subject to general alignments that correspond to the different truth axes; these alignments are universal and may be defined and described.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

## 七、六条轴的需要根源与发展挑战

> Each organizational principle provides for a deep emotional need that motivates its formulation. These needs unify each axis in a particular logic and motivate its development.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Psychological Needs, and Developmental Challenges」

| 轴 | 需要根源 | 生成的现实 | 发展挑战（须克服的危机） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 对应 | 与外部现实保持接触以提升生存机会 | 「事实的」现实，依赖感官信息、观察与对现实约束的掌握 | 内在现实对立于外在现实的建立与区分（Freud, 1923） |
| 融贯 | 信念、期待与行为倾向之间的相容与和谐；身份、连续性与规律性 | 在个人身份与被感知的外部性两个层面上的凝聚一致的现实；常贬低不融贯的事实 | 在思想之间建立连接的能力——比昂的「连结（link）」；须对抗「让思想保持分裂与混乱」以消解痛苦的防御倾向 |
| 理想 | 对完美与完整、对赋予指导与意义者的需要；克服可厌、可憎与任意之物 | 所向往并信仰的现实，提供指导与动机 | 须曾经历允许正向镜像与理想化、并能被内化为人格功能的关系（Kohut, 1959） |
| 主观-存在 | 忠诚于自己，把自己置于中心舞台作为规划人生与决定意义的标准 | 私人的、常常隐藏的真理所构成的主体-本真现实 | 依赖使「真」自体得以生成的复杂过程；唯有相对这一本真自体，该轴才能形成（Winnicott, 1975） |
| 实用 | 按未来收益组织经验；感到自己在达成目标上是有效的；体现主动施为 | 容纳目标与利益的现实意象；在其中作为主动的、发起性的施动者存在 | 灵活的良心（按自利原则行事）；须曾有「影响现实」的体验，加上功能弹性与处理模糊性的能力（James, 1907） |
| 主体间 | 与周遭之人的联结、共享现实的深刻需要 | 与周遭之人共享的现实 | 需要一个其存在与构成现实的能力等同于自己的主体在场；须放弃海洋性自恋、把他人从客体地位释放出来（Benjamin 承 Winnicott 1969 与黑格尔主奴辩证法） |

**需要根源（原文逐条）**

> At the root of the Correspondent axis is the need to be in touch with external reality so as to enhance the chances of survival, through explaining, responding to, and anticipating events that influence the exchanges between a person and the mind-independent world around him or her.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Psychological Needs, and Developmental Challenges」

> At the root of the Coherent axis of truth is the need for compatibility and harmony among a person’s beliefs, expectations, and behavioral tendencies. Coherence allows the smooth transition among the various activities that the subject is engaged with and allows him or her to create a sense of identity, continuity, and regularity across time (Dewey, 1929; Festinger, 1957; Swann & Brooks, 2012).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> Humanity’s need for perfection and completeness, for that which bestows guidance and meaning, and allows overcoming the repulsive, the abhorred, and the arbitrary, is at the root of the Ideal axis of truth. Ideal truths give people a sense of harmony and beauty, serving as both directives and sources of motivation (James, 1897; Kohut & Wolf, 1978).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> The Subjective-Existential axis arises in the subject’s need to be loyal to himself or herself, to be positioned at center stage as a criterion for planning his or her life and determining meaning without subordinating himself or herself to a universal concept of subject or truth (Laing, 1960; Sartre, 1956; Winnicott, 1960).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> The organization of experience in terms of future benefit is the basis for the Pragmatic axis of truth. This axis addresses the need to feel effective in one’s ability to achieve one’s goals in the world. It embodies active agency and overcomes the experience of helplessness and inevitability (Bandura, 1997; James, 1912; Seligman, 1975).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> At the root of the Intersubjective axis of truth is the profound need for a connection with those around us and a shared reality with them (Baumeister & Leary, 1995; Fairbairn, 1946; Fromm, 1970; Shaw, 2003). At the center of this axis resides the representation of the psychoanalytic object and the ability of the subject to cohere with the inner dynamics of this representation. This axis also generalizes the need for a singular object to the gratifying experience of belonging and cohering with a whole social field.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

**发展挑战（原文逐条）**

> The establishment of a Coherent axis demands, first and foremost, the ability to connect between thoughts. A key concept here is Bion’s (1970) “link,” central in his theory of the development of thought. Linking concerns the ability to connect between a thing and a thought and between one thought and another. Linking is possible when an individual is prepared and able to fight against a defensive tendency to annul pain by leaving thoughts split and in disarray (Bion, 1970).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> The establishment of an Intersubjective axis demands the presence of a subject whose existence and ability to constitute reality is equivalent to one’s own. Jessica Benjamin, following Winnicott (1969) and the dialectic of Hegel’s (1807) Master and Slave, formulates the complex emotional process required in order to renounce oceanic narcissism, release the other from his or her status as object, and establish him or her as a subject.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

> A Pragmatic axis of truth necessitates a flexible conscience that allows a person to behave in accordance with principles of self-benefit. In order for this axis to exist it is essential to have had the experience of influencing reality, together with functional flexibility and the ability to deal with vagueness (James, 1907).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

## 八、诸轴的语法差异

诸轴各有其独特语法、规则与优先性，这是它们不可互相还原的理由：

> Thus, in the Coherent theory, elements are ordered and checked for truth value not by correspondence to reality, but by inner consistency. The Subjective-Existential axis, on the other hand, is not subject to rules of logic. It not only allows for paradoxes, but also establishes itself through the tensions within them. The present is dominant in the Subjective-Existential axis, negligible in the Ideal axis, and is but a step toward the future in the Pragmatic axis of truth. Sense data is pertinent in the Correspondent axis, is irrelevant for the Ideal axis, and provides a base for common signification in the Intersubjective axis.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Truth Axes, Realities, and Self-States」

情境性激活的规律：

> Every organizing axis can have constructive or pathological expressions but, in both cases, the underlying logic of truth will be apparent. Of course, a person does not always respond to events with the same logic of truth. Different contexts arousing different needs may activate one or another of the axes at particular times. Usually, one lives according to directives of various different truth axes simultaneously. Life, as we experience it, eclipses any one register, and is perpetually given in multiple significations.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

## 九、★临床：从「知真理」到「认识论专长」

> Traditionally, the analyst based his or her authority on a truth which he or she knew, mastered, and employed. The analyst’s authority was undermined in the postmodern era by the relinquishing of a single truth. I ascribe here the analyst’s authority to his or her epistemic expertise, that is, his or her ability to know the epistemic layout of the psyche, recognize particular configurations in it, and make democratic and pluralistic use of a variety of languages. The analyst identifies the different appearances of each epistemic language on the levels of sensation, syntax, and encounter with the world. He or she recognizes the standing and relevance of any language in the entirety of psychic economy and structure, irrespective of his or her feelings, preferences, and identifications.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「TRUTH AXES AND THE PSYCHOANALYTIC CLINIC」

关键限定：分析师**不被要求中立**。

> The analyst is not expected to be neutral in his or her relation to the different languages, to equally identify with them, or to be devoid of natural preferences and tendencies. The analyst’s challenge consists in being aware of these tendencies and their significance (Hoffman, 2009). For understanding the analytic stance I advocate here, it is pertinent to remember that, beyond preferences and identifications, all truth axes express basic needs and are determined by them.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

治疗目标因此被重新表述——不是判定哪种真理为真，而是让分析二元不**坍缩**到单一轴上，转而分析**轴间运动**：

> I suggest that, among other factors, this space may be constituted by the evolving shared ability of the analytic dyad not to gravitate toward and collapse into one particular axis of truth. Instead, patient and analyst develop the ability to analyze the way in which movement between axes is enabled, whether it “flows” or rather gets “blocked.”

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

轴不是歇息处，而是**运动的发生器**：

> Axes of truth are not conceptualized as resting places, even if they may in fact fulfill such a function from time to time. Rather, the axes are viewed as having the potential to create continuous movement of images and meanings.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，同上节

## 十、结论：治疗与伦理的合流

> I have stressed that these truths are not in competition with each other. Rather, they are complementary, arising from different needs and expressing their associated dreads and desires.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「CONCLUSION」

> The therapeutic objective here is not necessarily to resolve conflicts. Our work often consists of creating a space that may contain various versions of world, self, and experience, acknowledging and recognizing their validity, though they often do not cohere.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「CONCLUSION」

学派关系的最终定位：诸理论不是竞争对手，而是一条**平行于心智之认识论多元性的理论弧**。

> From this vantage point, the different psychoanalytic theories are not seen as competing, but as creating a theoretical arc that parallels the epistemic multiplicity of the mind. Acknowledging that all truth axes express basic needs and are determined by them draws together the dimensions of the therapeutic and the ethic.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「CONCLUSION」

## 十一、对 Symington「自由行动」的重读（跨文献连接）

亚德林-加多特把 [[entities/person-neville-symington]] 的 Miss M 案例读作**轴间运动**的临床例证：以对应真理**解构**一条根深蒂固的实用真理。

> The case cited in Symington’s (1983) “The Analyst’s Act of Freedom as Agent of Therapeutic Change” illustrates once again the therapeutic effect involved in movement among truth axes, triggered by the analyst’s intervention. The case illustrates the potency of deconstructing an entrenched, Pragmatic truth by means of Correspondence.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Act of Freedom」

她也概述并保留了西明顿本人的解释，然后给出替代读法：

> Symington suggests an elaborate explanation for the “x-phenomenon” involving unconscious communication and an illusory corporate personality system shared by patient and analyst and dominated by elements of superego. The analyst’s act of freedom brings to consciousness the imperatives of this system and the ability to question them. This vignette can be alternatively understood through the prism of multiple epistemic perspectives that structure our world. Symington describes “Little Mary,” his patient, as construed along the Pragmatic axis, obviously a crucial one in her life. Little Mary inhabits a reality she cannot influence.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Act of Freedom」

> **A 方立场**：西明顿（1983）——改变由分析师**内在自由行动**（x-phenomenon）引起，诠释只是把已经发生的转变表达出来；机制被解释为无意识沟通与由超我元素主导的「团体性人格」错觉系统——「Symington suggests an elaborate explanation for the “x-phenomenon” involving unconscious communication and an illusory corporate personality system」（Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Act of Freedom」）
> **B 方立场**：亚德林-加多特——可从**多认识论视角**的棱镜另行理解：患者被建构在实用轴上，治疗效应来自**轴间运动**（以对应解构实用）——「This vignette can be alternatively understood through the prism of multiple epistemic perspectives that structure our world」（同上）
> **备注**：可调和，属**解释层级**差异而非事实争议：西明顿给出的是元心理学机制，亚德林-加多特给出的是认识论描述；她本人明言这是「alternative」读法，而非取代。此标注另为 [[raw/symington-act-of-freedom]] 的出版信息（1983, p. 283）提供了唯一的跨源佐证。
