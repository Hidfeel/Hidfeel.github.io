> **类型**: entity
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-28
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-28

## 摘要

在「诠释」议题上，克莱因的位置可用一句话概括：**诠释是精神滋养（psychic nourishment）**。它与弗洛伊德共享一个前提——无意识幻想（unconscious phantasy）的首要性与诠释在治疗中的中心地位——但她把弗洛伊德的发展时间表与理论框架作了三处激进修订：复杂客体关系**自出生即存在**、俄狄浦斯情结**被移置到生命第一年**、超我**以原始形式在最初数月形成**。第三处修订直接产生了她的技术结论：既然如此复杂的心理结构自婴儿期就存在，那么**即使是很小的孩子也能从深度诠释中获益**。

由此诞生了她最具争议的技术创新——**即时诠释（immediate interpretation）**：不以节制（sparingly）的方式给诠释，而是在焦虑出现的当下直接命名它。延迟诠释就是让儿童独自面对其无法管理的恐惧；而**准确的诠释创造而非依赖治疗关系**。

克莱因对「诠释」议题的第二项贡献是**代谢模型**：诠释不是认知过程，而是一种形式的精神营养（psychic nutrition）。儿童不需要在理智上理解诠释，他们需要能够**把它摄入体内并让它从内部起作用**。分析师因此不只是符号的解码者，而是患者最原始焦虑的**活容器（living container）**。

第三项贡献是移情观的激进化：移情是分析工作的「**芝麻开门**」；诠释必须锚定在**实际情境**与由此激发的感受之中；「**总情境（total situation）**」概念要求把整体构型（entire constellations）的情感、防御模式与客体关系方式纳入诠释。

但本页也必须记录克莱因的**两面性**。她对**反移情（countertransference）**持明确的警惕立场：反移情只是理解患者内在世界的**工具**，不是探索对象本身——「我从未发现反移情帮助我更好地理解我的患者；如果可以这样说的话，我发现它帮助我更好地理解了我自己」。这一立场与她自己的投射性认同概念之间存在需要标注的张力，也构成了贝蒂·约瑟夫后来转向的出发点。

## 一、婴儿期的剧场：克莱因的人类学前提

克莱因要求我们想象的，不是玛多纳与圣婴式的原初安宁，而是一出**战场上的激情戏剧**：婴儿被超出理解的力量所驱动，攻击、吞食、掏空、占有那个喂养他的乳房，随后又被自己所做之事引发的内疚与恐惧淹没，试图修复、恢复、复活被自己的贪婪所摧毁的东西。

> According to Klein, the baby has from birth an innate relationship with the mother (although focusing primarily on her breast) which is imbued with the fundamental elements of an object-relation, that is, love, hatred, phantasies, anxieties, and defences (Klein, <u>1952</u>, p. 53). Notice what Klein is claiming here: not that the baby develops these capacities, but that they’re there from the start, as if human nature came fully equipped with its own tragedy and redemption.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Theatre of the Body」

Govrin 用一句极为凝练的话概括这一修订的意义：**她让婴儿变得有趣（she made the baby interesting）**——不是可爱、不是无辜、甚至主要不是匮乏的，而是复杂的、已经投入到「试图把一个在自身强度压力下威胁要碎裂的自体聚合起来」这一根本人类工作之中的。

> This was Klein’s scandalous revision of psychoanalysis: she made the baby interesting. Not cute, not innocent, not even primarily needy—but interesting, complex, already engaged in the fundamental human work of trying to hold together a self that threatens to fragment under the pressure of its own intensity.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Theatre of the Body」

与威廉·詹姆斯的对照点出了克莱因的独特之处：詹姆斯看到「巨大的、嗡嗡作响的混乱」，克莱因看到的是**组织**——一种原始的、幻想性的组织，但仍是组织。

## 二、对弗洛伊德的三处修订与其技术后果

> Klein’s relationship to Freudian theory represents both continuity and radical departure. Like Freud, she believed in the primacy of unconscious phantasy and the centrality of interpretation in psychoanalytic treatment. However, Klein fundamentally revised Freud’s developmental timeline and theoretical framework in three crucial ways.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Theoretical Foundation: Klein’s Extension of Freud」

三处修订：

| 维度 | 弗洛伊德 | 克莱因 |
|---|---|---|
| 客体关系 | 原初自恋，客体关系渐进发展 | 复杂客体关系**自出生即存在** |
| 俄狄浦斯情结 | 3–5 岁的发展阶段 | 移置到**生命第一年**，约六个月时与抑郁位一同浮现 |
| 超我 | 较晚形成 | 以原始、严厉、迫害性的形式在**最初数月**形成，源自婴儿自身投射的攻击性 |

（表格为【综合】，依据「The Theoretical Foundation」段正文归纳，见上文引文。）

Govrin 明确指出第三点**对技术而言最为重要**：

> Third, and most significantly for technique, Klein believed that the superego forms much earlier than Freud proposed, emerging in primitive form during the first months of life. This early superego is harsh and persecutory, derived from the infant’s own projected aggression. This theoretical revision had immediate technical implications: if such complex mental structures exist from infancy, then even very young children could benefit from deep interpretation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Theoretical Foundation」

**与安娜·弗洛伊德的争论**就发生在这里。Govrin 强调，这场争论从来不只是技术之争，它反映的是关于「童年是什么、儿童需要分析师给予什么」的根本不同愿景：

> Klein’s revolutionary approach to child interpretation sparked intense debate, most notably with Anna Freud. The dispute between these two pioneers was never simply about technique; it reflected fundamentally different visions of what childhood is and what children need from analysts (Aguayo, 2011). Where Klein saw sophisticated unconscious phantasy life demanding immediate interpretation, Anna Freud saw developmental immaturity requiring careful preparation and gradual introduction to self- understanding. Anna Freud argued that children required a preparatory phase before analysis proper could begin—a period of relationship-building during which the analyst w…[原文此处被截断]

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Theoretical Foundation」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：儿童分析是否需要一个「准备期」，深度诠释对幼儿是否适当？
> **A 方立场**：克莱因——幼儿拥有复杂的无意识幻想生活，**要求即时诠释**；延迟诠释等于让儿童独自面对无法管理的恐惧，而准确的诠释会**创造**出治疗关系。
> **B 方立场**：安娜·弗洛伊德——儿童存在发展性不成熟，**需要准备期**（关系建立阶段，让分析师逐步引导儿童走向自我理解），之后才能进入正式分析。

## 三、技术的演化：从「解释」到直呼其名

克莱因的诠释技术始于她对儿子弗里茨（Fritz）的分析——这在早期分析师中并不罕见，但今天已被认为在伦理上存在问题。她著作中第一次真正的诠释出现在弗里茨讲述大发动机与小发动机撞上电动汽车的梦—幻想时：

> The first true interpretation in Klein’s writings appears when Fritz related his dream-fantasy of the big motor and little motor running into the electric car. Klein explained that “the big motor is his papa, the electric car his mamma and the little motor himself, and that he has put himself between papa and mamma because he would so much like to put papa away altogether and to remain alone with his mamma and to do with her what only papa is allowed to do” (Klein, <u>1975a</u>, Vol. 1, p. 35).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Evolution of Klein’s Interpretive Technique」

值得注意的是，克莱因把这一次称为「**解释（explanation）**」并置于括号之中，暗示她最初对这一新技术路径的**不确定**。

> Significantly, Klein calls this an “explanation” and places it in brackets, suggesting her initial uncertainty about this new technical approach.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Evolution of Klein’s Interpretive Technique」

从这一试探性的开端，到克莱因成熟技术的演化是戏剧性的。在诠释弗里茨的**负性俄狄浦斯情结**时，她从象征性暗示走向**直接命名**：

> But he is afraid (as he imagines his mamma to be too) that if this stick—papa’s wiwi—gets into his wiwi he will be hurt and then inside his belly, in his stomach, everything will be destroyed, too (Klein, <u>1975a</u>, Vol. 1, p. 41).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Evolution of Klein’s Interpretive Technique」

Govrin（引 Etchegoyen, 2005）指出，这一进路**同时定义了三者**：

> This approach defines simultaneously a theory (that the child understands the semantic value of interpretation), a technique (that symbols must be brought back to their origin), and an ethic (the necessity of telling the child the truth without hiding things) (Etchegoyen, <u>2005</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Evolution of Klein’s Interpretive Technique」

## 四、★即时诠释原则与丽塔案例

克莱因最具争议的技术创新是**即时诠释**。她在 1955 年回忆道：

> At the time I began to work it was an established principle that interpretations should be given very sparingly. With few exceptions, psychoanalysts had not explored the deeper layers of the unconscious—in children, such exploration was considered potentially dangerous. (Klein, <u>1975a</u>, Vol. 3, p. 122)

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Principle of Immediate Interpretation」（引 Klein, 1975a, Vol. 3, p. 122）

而她的立场是：

> Yet Klein recognized interpretation as the essential instrument of psychoanalysis and applied it unhesitatingly whenever she deemed it necessary.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Principle of Immediate Interpretation」

**丽塔（Rita）案例**（1923 年，克莱因在自己家中分析，年龄两岁零九个月）是范式性的。丽塔要求去花园，克莱因不把这看作对新鲜空气的简单请求，而是看出**负性移情**支配了整个图景。看到丽塔在花园中较为平静后，克莱因作出了一个历史性的诠释：她告诉丽塔，丽塔害怕克莱因会在她们独处时对她做可怕的事，正如她夜里害怕坏女人会在床上袭击她。

> Upon seeing Rita more tranquil in the garden, Klein made a historic interpretation: she told Rita that she feared Klein would do something terrible to her while they were alone in the room, just as she feared at night that a bad woman would attack her in her bed. Within minutes of this interpretation, Rita returned confidently to the playroom.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Principle of Immediate Interpretation」

Govrin 指出这一诠释成为**历史里程碑**的几个理由：其精确年代为克莱因早期技术提供了确定性，并且例示（exemplifies）了她进路的显著特征——**直接处理焦虑、对移情（包括其负性表现）的考量、以及把当下行为与症状及潜在冲突连接起来**（Etchegoyen, 2005, p. 415）。

**即时诠释的理论根据**有二，二者互相支撑：

> The theoretical justification for immediate interpretation rests on Klein’s understanding of anxiety as present from the beginning of life. To delay interpretation is to leave the child alone with terrors they cannot manage. Moreover, Klein believed that accurate interpretation creates rather than depends upon the therapeutic relationship. When a child feels understood at the deepest level, trust follows naturally.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Principle of Immediate Interpretation」

「**准确的诠释创造而非依赖治疗关系**」——这是克莱因在「诠释与关系孰先」这一本 Wiki 核心争论上给出的、与温尼科特／科胡特截然相反的答案。

## 五、★诠释作为精神滋养

这是克莱因对「诠释」概念最具原创性的贡献。她相信**诠释本身就是一种喂养**：正如婴儿摄入母亲的乳汁，儿童患者摄入分析师的语词；正如乳汁可以被体验为好的或坏的、滋养的或有毒的，诠释也可以被感受为有帮助的或迫害性的。

> Klein believed that interpretation itself was a kind of feeding. Just as the infant takes in the mother’s milk, so the child patient takes in the analyst’s words. And just as milk can be experienced as good or bad, nourishing or poisonous, so interpretations can be felt as helpful or persecutory. The analyst’s task isn’t simply to be correct but to offer interpretations that can be digested, metabolized, made use of.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Interpretation as Psychic Nourishment」

这一消化隐喻揭示的洞见是：诠释**主要不是认知过程，而是精神营养的一种形式**。

> This digestive metaphor reveals a profound insight into Klein’s understanding of interpretation. It’s not primarily a cognitive process—it’s a form of psychic nutrition. The child doesn’t need to understand the interpretation intellectually; they need to be able to take it in and let it work on them from the inside. This is why Klein could interpret to very young children in language that might seem far beyond their years. She wasn’t appealing to their rational understanding but to something more primitive and more powerful.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Interpretation as Psychic Nourishment」

**约翰（John）案例**展示了这一过程：约翰玩扮演狮子、吞食分析师的游戏。克莱因不只是观察这个游戏，她把他害怕被吃掉**诠释为其自身攻击愿望的投射**，并把它与吞食母亲、乃至更早吞食乳房的幻想连接起来（Klein, 2017a, p. 17）。诠释过程**最初加剧**约翰的焦虑——他变得更激动、游戏更碎片化——但随着工作继续，某种东西转变了：缓解达成，新的、更信任的游戏形式出现。**诠释被代谢了。**

> The process of interpretation initially heightens John’s anxiety—he becomes more agitated, his play more fragmented—but as the work continues, something shifts. Relief is achieved and new, more trusting forms of play emerge. The interpretation has been metabolized.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Interpretation as Psychic Nourishment」

## 六、克莱因式诠释的四个特征

### 6.1 具体的解剖学语言

克莱因的诠释极其**具体**，使用 Aguayo（2011）所称的「解剖学诠释语言（anatomical interpretative language）」（p. 1117）来描述无意识幻想。弗洛伊德可能说「力比多投注」或「移置」，克莱因则直接告诉儿童：母亲体内被制造与被摧毁的婴儿、被咬掉与被吞食的阴茎、迫害与下毒的乳房。

> This concreteness reflects Klein’s theory that the unconscious speaks in bodily metaphors rather than abstract concepts.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Nature and Function of Kleinian Interpretation」

### 6.2 锚定于实际情境

克莱因坚持诠释必须始终锚定在「**实际情境以及在此情境中被激起的感受**」之中，即分析遭遇的此时此地（Klein, 2017b, p. 89）。分析师的任务是澄清当下的体验，**然后**才建立与过去的连接。

> Second, Klein insists that interpretation must always be anchored in the “actual situation and the feelings aroused in this situation,” that is, in the here-and-now of the analytic encounter (Klein, <u>2017b</u>, p. 89). The analyst’s task is to clarify the present experience, and only then to make links to the past. Interpretation, for Klein, is never mere guesswork or theoretical imposition but must be grounded in the material currently being explored.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Nature and Function of Kleinian Interpretation」

### 6.3 象征化作为杠杆

1927 年儿童分析研讨会上，克莱因宣称象征化「是我们在儿童分析中必须使用的**杠杆**」（Klein, 1975a, Vol. 1, p. 147）。

### 6.4 对「热心的初学者」的警告

克莱因特别警惕那些**「热心的初学者」（enthusiastic beginners）**（Klein, 2017a, p. 15）——他们缺乏患者材料中的充分根据，就跳到无意识经验的最深或最早层级。

> Klein is particularly wary of “enthusiastic beginners” (Klein, <u>2017a</u>, p. 15) who leap to the deepest or earliest levels of unconscious experience without sufficient justification in the patient’s material. She illustrates this with a clinical example: a patient disappointed by being assigned a junior analyst is not to be immediately interpreted as suffering from “breast disappointment”; rather, the disappointment should be linked to more proximate experiences of loss and idealization, such as the disappointment in the father or in subsequent love objects.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Nature and Function of Kleinian Interpretation」

## 七、投射性认同：诠释作为情绪代谢

投射性认同（projective identification）是克莱因对诠释技术最具原创性的贡献之一。它描述无意识幻想如何**无需语词、亦无需意识觉察**地从一个人传达到另一个人。在克莱因的咨询室里，诠释成为一种**接收这些投射并以改造过的形式归还它们**的方式。

> In Klein’s consulting room, interpretation became a way of receiving and returning these projections in modified form. When a child made her feel frightened, angry, or confused, Klein didn’t simply note these as countertransference feelings to be analyzed later. She used them as immediate data about what the child was experiencing and trying to communicate.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Projective Identification」

关键区别在于：诠释**归还**投射，但同时**改造**它，使它成为可思考的而非仅仅可演出的。

> The interpretation returns the projection but transforms it, making it thinkable rather than merely enactable. This understanding of interpretation as a form of emotional metabolism revolutionized psychoanalytic technique. The analyst wasn’t simply a decoder of symbols but a living container for the patient’s most primitive anxieties. Interpretation became a way of demonstrating that these anxieties could be survived, thought about, and ultimately transformed.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Projective Identification」

分析师的条件被明确列出：必须对患者的投射**保持接受**，必须**摄入并处理**它们，同时也必须**维持足够的自我强度**以可消化的形式归还它们。

> The analyst must be receptive to the patient’s projections, must take them in and process them, but must also maintain sufficient ego strength to return them in a form that can be psychically digested.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Projective Identification」

## 八、★移情作为通往无意识的「芝麻开门」

对克莱因而言，移情现象是分析工作的「**芝麻开门（Open Sesame）**」（Klein, 2017b, p. 34）。移情情境——患者最早的客体关系、冲突与幻想在与分析师的关系中被复活并上演——使无意识的最深层变得可及。

> For Klein, the phenomenon of transference is the “Open Sesame” of analytic work (Klein, <u>2017b</u>, p. 34). The transference situation —whereby the patient’s earliest object relations, conflicts, and fantasies are revived and enacted in the relationship with the analyst —makes the deepest layers of the unconscious accessible.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」

技术信条：

> Klein’s technical approach is thus rooted in the conviction that “the transference situation and the exploration of the unconscious are the two fundamentals which should continuously guide our technique, and that they are actually interconnected” (p. 34).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」（引 Klein, 2017b, p. 34）

克莱因敏锐地意识到：分析师不是患者真实父母的简单替代，而是被体验为**多个内在客体的复合体**，常常迅速从仁慈的形象转为迫害者、从真实的人转为幻想的图像。而她的创新在于坚持这些冲突的感受是**动态地交织在一起的**：

> Klein’s innovation is to insist that these conflicting feelings are dynamically intertwined: “sorrow, guilt and anxiety are part and parcel of the complex relation to objects which we call love” (Klein, <u>2017a</u>, p. 8).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」

### 8.1 总情境（total situation）

克莱因革命性贡献的核心是「**总情境**」概念：

> in unraveling the details of the transference, it is essential to think in terms of total situations transferred from the past into the present as well as emotions, defenses and object relationships (Klein, <u>1952</u>, p. 437).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」（引 Klein, 1952, p. 437）

这意味着移情**不只是**孤立的感受或记忆，而是**整个情感星丛、防御模式与既定客体关系方式**——在分析遭遇中被现实化的完整内在世界。

### 8.2 但她的临床实践更保守

Govrin 明确标注了这里的**悖论**：尽管克莱因在理论上阐述了总情境概念，她的临床实践却显示出**更为保守的进路**。她主要在传统弗洛伊德框架内使用移情诠释，并对 Heimann 的反移情构想保持怀疑（Grosskurth, 1986, p. 382）——而这些要素恰恰是理解总情境的关键。

> Yet paradoxically, despite Klein’s theoretical articulation of the total situation concept, her clinical practice revealed a more conservative approach. She primarily employed transference interpretations within a traditional Freudian framework and maintained skepticism toward Heimann’s counter-transference formulations (Grosskurth, <u>1986</u>, p. 382)—elements crucial to understanding the total situation.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」

即使在《儿童分析的故事》（Klein, 1961）中，针对总情境的诠释也**极为稀少**。正如汉娜·西格尔（Hanna Segal）所观察到的，克莱因本人对探索反移情关注有限，把总情境概念的充分临床应用留给了后继几代分析师。

## 九、★反移情：工具，而非探索对象

克莱因对反移情的态度，是她既延伸又背离弗洛伊德之处，也是本页最需要标注的立场。

> Klein’s attitude toward countertransference reveals another area where she both extends and departs from Freud. While acknowledging that the patient’s projections can profoundly affect the analyst, she maintains a cautious stance: “I have never found that the countertransference has helped me to understand my patient better; if I may put it like this; I have found that it has helped me to understand myself better” (Klein, <u>2017b</u>, p. 103).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

Govrin 立即指出：这一立场**看似与克莱因对投射性认同的理解相矛盾**。但她的论点很微妙——分析师必须对患者的投射保持接受，但这些投射必须**以诠释的形式被处理与归还，而不是被演出（enactment）**。

> This position might seem to contradict Klein’s understanding of projective identification. However, Klein’s point is subtle: while the analyst must be receptive to the patient’s projections, these must be processed and returned in the form of interpretation rather than enactment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

并且分析师必须维持**对投射性认同说「不」**的能力（Klein, 2017b, p. 105）——对那些威胁要淹没分析工作的投射性认同说「不」。

> The analyst must maintain a capacity to say “no” to projective identifications that threaten to overwhelm analytic work (Klein, <u>2017b</u>, p. 105).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

结论性的一句：

> Klein criticized contemporary uses of projective identification that focus on analyzing the analyst’s feelings, insisting instead that the analyst’s central task is to use countertransference only as a tool for understanding the patient’s internal world, not as an object of exploration in its own right.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：分析师的反移情在其自身权利上是否应成为探索对象？
> **A 方立场**：克莱因——反移情只是理解患者内在世界的**工具**；「我从未发现反移情帮助我更好地理解我的患者……我发现它帮助我更好地理解了我自己」（Klein, 2017b, p. 103）；她批评那些聚焦于分析分析师感受的投射性认同用法（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」）。
> **B 方立场**： Paula Heimann 与后继的克莱因学派（约瑟夫、比昂、奥格登、费罗、埃谢尔）——反移情／遐想／场是首要的临床数据。Govrin 指出，克莱因的这一怀疑立场恰恰使她自己提出的总情境概念无法在临床上充分展开（第二章「Transference as the Gateway to the Unconscious」）。
> **备注**：这是克莱因**内部**的张力，而非两个外部学派之争——她的投射性认同理论在逻辑上要求分析师以自己的感受为数据，她的技术信条却禁止把它当作探索对象。贝蒂·约瑟夫的「从内容到过程」转向（见 [[entities/person-betty-joseph]]）正是这一张力的历史性解决尝试。

## 十、分析态度：严格与接受性

克莱因的技术进路要求一种特定的**分析态度（analytic attitude）**，其特征是她所谓的「**对确定真理的不懈渴望（unfailing desire to ascertain the truth）**」（Klein, 2017b, p. 5）。这需要在批判能力与情感开放之间取得平衡：分析师的感受必须「**充分活跃，尽管被很好地控制着**」（p. 30）。

> This orientation requires a balance between critical faculties and emotional openness. The analyst’s feelings must be “fully active, though they are kept well under control” (p. 30). Only an analyst who approaches the patient with “deep and true respect for the workings of the human mind and the human personality in general” can hope to facilitate genuine analytic work (p. 30).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Analytic Attitude」

克莱因警告**权力感与优越感**（可能来自分析师的特权位置），其解药是一种「**谦卑而同时自信的精神**」，其根据是认识到理解另一个人是极其困难的事业（Klein, 2017b, p. 31）。

在未发表的技术讲座中，她把「分析态度」描述为一种**悖论性的心境**：

> Our whole interest is focused on one aim, namely, on the exploration of the mind of this one person who for the time being has become the center of our attention … This rather curious state of mind, eager and at the same time patient, detached from its subject and at the same time fully absorbed in it, is clearly the result of a balance between different and partly conflicting tendencies and psychological drives. (Klein Archive, cited in Spillius et al., <u>2011</u>)

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Analytic Attitude」

Govrin 的评价是：这一表述揭示出，有效的诠释需要的**不只是理论知识，而是一种特定的注意力品质**——能够持有矛盾而不过早地解决它们。

## 十一、对批评的回应

克莱因的诠释进路面对来自同代人的大量批评，主要集中于三点：**理论先入之见的强加、暗示的可能性、以及幼儿深度诠释的适当性**。

1. **关于强加先入之见**——克莱因以经验方式回应：诠释通过**其效果**证明其有效性。当诠释准确时，儿童的游戏变得更自由、焦虑减轻、症状改善。

> To the charge of imposing preconceptions, Klein responded empirically: interpretations prove their validity through their effects. When interpretations are accurate, children’s play becomes freer, their anxiety diminishes, and their symptoms improve.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Responding to Criticisms」

2. **关于暗示**——克莱因论证说，她的技术实际上**最小化了暗示影响**，因为它直接处理无意识材料，而非通过教育性或安抚性措施。验证或否证诠释的是**儿童随后的联想，而非有意识的同意**。

> Regarding suggestion, Klein argued that her technique actually minimizes suggestive influence by addressing unconscious material directly rather than through educative or reassuring measures. The child’s subsequent associations, not conscious agreement, validate or invalidate interpretations.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Responding to Criticisms」

3. **关于幼儿深度诠释**——克莱因的临床经验表明，儿童不仅耐受而且常常**欢迎**那些处理其最深层焦虑的诠释。经由诠释达成的焦虑缓解，为精神生活开辟了新的探索领域。

她把焦虑比作必须小心处理的「**爆炸性材料（explosive material）**」（Klein, 2017b, p. 16），但同时也断言：以可管理的分量**释放**它，对分析过程而言是根本性的。

> Klein likens anxiety to “explosive material” (Klein, <u>2017b</u>, p. 16) that must be handled with care, but also asserts that releasing it in manageable quantities is essential to the analytic process.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Responding to Criticisms」

## 十二、学派内部此后的技术演化（含对约瑟夫的引子）

克莱因死后，克莱因学派技术继续显著演化。1960—70 年代出现了三个重要趋势（Spillius et al., 2011）：

1. 对破坏性的诠释变得更平衡，不再只聚焦于攻击性；
2. 即时使用具体的「部分客体」身体语言的做法，开始被**心理功能（看、听、思考、排空）的描述**所取代——从解剖学语言到功能语言的转变使诠释更可及，同时保持其深度；
3. 投射性认同概念开始被**更直接地用于分析移情**，且日益强调分析师—患者关系中的**活现（enactment）**。

> This development was particularly important in Betty Joseph’s work, which emphasized how patients put pressure on analysts to join in their defensive patterns (Spillius et al., <u>2011</u>).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Later Technical Developments in the Kleinian School」

Govrin 强调，这些变化反映的是**对诠释如何被接收与被处理**的理解日益精细，而非仅仅关注其内容。正是这些悬而未决的问题——患者如何能够在同意诠释的同时**不被它触动**？为什么某些准确的诠释无法产生转化？——把克莱因学派带向了贝蒂·约瑟夫。

> How do patients manage to agree with interpretations while remaining untouched by them? Why do some accurate interpretations fail to produce transformation? It was Betty Joseph, working within the Kleinian tradition, who would address these questions by shifting attention from the content of interpretation to the process of how interpretations are received and used—or neutralized—by patients.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Later Technical Developments in the Kleinian School」

## 十三、真理与仁慈：斯坦纳的批评

第二章的结论节把从克莱因到约瑟夫的旅程诊断为：**真理与仁慈（truth and kindness）之间的关系问题**。克莱因派分析师约翰·斯坦纳（John Steiner）指出：

> truth can be cruel in its unrelenting logic and it is easy for those who idealise truth to overlook the human limitations that make us depend on illusion in order to survive (Steiner, <u>2020</u>, p. 9).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Conclusion: Truth, Kindness, and the Limits of Interpretation」

这一批评**特别适用于**某些克莱因式进路：分析师揭示攻击性幻想与负性移情的热忱，可能遮蔽对**人类脆弱性**的认识——患者的，以及分析师自己的。

> This critique applies particularly to certain Kleinian approaches where the analyst’s zeal for uncovering aggressive phantasies and negative transference might overshadow recognition of human frailty—both the patient’s and the analyst’s own.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Conclusion: Truth, Kindness, and the Limits of Interpretation」

危险不只在于真理潜在的残酷，更在于**被过于僵化地（rigidly）追求时的不完整**：当分析师成为某一版本真理的热忱者时，他就冒着斯坦纳所指出的**视野狭窄、无法认识自身无意识幻想**的风险。

> The analyst convinced of their interpretive accuracy may be denying their own sadism, their own need for omnipotent understanding, their own difficulty tolerating the patient’s pace of change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「Conclusion: Truth, Kindness, and the Limits of Interpretation」

斯坦纳引 E. M. Forster 指出：仁慈不只是用来软化真理的严酷，**它也使真理本身更真实、更有意义**（p. 10）。

## 十四、Yadlin-Gadot 视角下的克莱因：位置与进行中的现实检验

Yadlin-Gadot 把克莱因放在「实在论—对应元理论」谱系中弗洛伊德的继承者位置：克莱因的婴儿与弗洛伊德的婴儿一样，起始于一个**完全是幻想性的、内在的**现实；通过分裂、投射、内摄与认同等机制，内外世界的分化才逐渐演化。而克莱因的「**位置（position）**」概念意味着能够**进出**抑郁性的现实检验。

> The Kleinian infant, like the Freudian one, begins life in a real- ity that is wholly phantasmatic and internal (Klein, 1930, 1932). Gradually, through such mechanisms as splitting, projection, in- trojections, and identification, the differentiation of internal and external worlds evolves. Klein's concept of "position" implies the ability to move in and out of depressive reality-testing.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

同一作者在论及**理想真理（ideal truth）**时给出了克莱因学派与科胡特的分歧：

> Kleinians, on the other hand, find fault in the ac- ceptance of ideals as truth, suggesting the influence of splitting that acts as a defense in the face of aggression.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Relation Between Truth and Epistemology and the Question of Values」

## 十五、奥格登的划界：克莱因作为客体关系理论家

奥格登在第四节把克莱因明确列为**客体关系理论家**，并精准刻画其版本：患者的焦虑源自**被幻想的内在客体关系**所发出的危险；无意识幻想（生本能与死本能的心理表现）常常关涉母亲／分析师身体内部正在发生的事——例如对母亲体内的婴儿或父亲阴茎的攻击。

> For Klein (1961, 1975), who is an object-relations theorist of a sort different from Fairbairn, the patient’s anxieties are derived from the dangers emanating from phantasied internal object relationships. Unconscious phantasies (the psychic manifestations of life and death instincts) are often concerned with what is occurring inside the body of the mother/analyst, for instance, attacks on the babies or the father’s penis inside the mother.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第四节

这些原始焦虑在移情中显现，并被以**对患者为真（ring true）且有助于减轻其迫害性与抑郁性焦虑**的方式加以诠释。奥格登同时指出克莱因与费尔贝恩的根本差异：费尔贝恩视内在客体关系为母婴关系中实际不满足经验的**内化**，克莱因则视其为源自婴儿**嫉羡（envy）**经验的无意识幻想——嫉羡是死本能的主要心理表现。

> Their primary difference lies in the way Fairbairn views internal object relationships as internalizations of actual unsatisfactory experience in the mother-infant relationship, while Klein views internal object relationships as unconscious phantasies derived from the infant’s experience of envy (the principal psychic manifestation of the death instinct).

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第四节

同时，奥格登引温尼科特对克莱因的**隐含批评**——就其与游戏的关系而言，克莱因「几乎完全关注游戏的用途」（作为儿童内在世界的象征化形式）：

> This is not a criticism of Melanie Klein or of others who have described the use of the child’s play in the psychoanalysis of children. It is simply a comment on the possibility that … the psychoanalyst has been too busy using play content to look at the playing child, and to write about playing as a thing in itself.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节（引 Winnicott, 1971a, pp. 39-40）
