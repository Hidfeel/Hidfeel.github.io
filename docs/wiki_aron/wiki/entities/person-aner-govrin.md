# 阿纳·戈夫林（Aner Govrin）

> **类型**: entity
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-28
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-28
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

阿纳·戈夫林是以色列精神分析师、哲学家与临床心理学家，常驻特拉维夫；现任巴伊兰大学「精神分析与诠释学」博士点主任，并为特拉维夫当代精神分析研究所成员。他是 Routledge「当代精神分析导论」丛书的主编，也是其中 *Interpretation: A Contemporary Introduction*（2026）一书的作者。

戈夫林在本批文献中的枢纽地位来自三点：第一，他对「诠释（interpretation）」这一术语的语义过载作出诊断，称之为学科内部的「巴别塔」；第二，他提出了一对原创的对立轴——**求真传统（truth-seeking tradition）**与**创伤疗愈传统（trauma healing tradition）**，用以重写精神分析治疗行动观念史；第三，他把当代各家在诠释问题上的收敛，归结为一种伦理立场，即**认识论谦逊（epistemological humility）**——有信心给出诠释，同时有谦逊轻握它。他对海因茨·科胡特（Heinz Kohut）的批评构成了这一立场最锋利的样本：科胡特的革命「根本上是临床的，而非认识论的」，他在临床上把分析师主体性从前门迎进来，却在理论上试图把它从后门送出去。

## 一、身份与著述定位

据原书作者简介：

> **Aner Govrin** is a psychoanalyst, philosopher, and clinical psychologist based in Tel Aviv, Israel. He is the director of the doctoral track, ‘Psychoanalysis and Hermeneutics’, at Bar-Ilan University and is a member of the Tel Aviv Institute for Contemporary Psychoanalysis.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，作者简介

他同时是 Routledge「当代精神分析导论（Routledge Introductions to Contemporary Psychoanalysis）」丛书的**丛书主编（Series Editor）**；该丛书的编辑方针被表述为「成为一部精神分析百科全书，每个词条各占一书」：

> Routledge Introductions to Contemporary Psychoanalysis is one of the most prominent psychoanalytic publishing ventures of our day. The series’ aim is to become an encyclopedia of psychoanalysis, with each entry given its own book.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，丛书主编前言

全书结构按人物编排：弗洛伊德（第 1 章）、克莱因（第 2 章）、比昂（第 3 章）、温尼科特（第 4 章）、科胡特（第 5 章）、关系取向（第 6 章），第 7 章为「诠释之重要文献：精神分析思想演进中的五个里程碑」，另有后记「诠释的悖论」。这一编排本身就体现他的方法论判断：诠释的语义分歧无法靠定义消解，只能靠**谱系**呈现。

## 二、诊断：术语的巴别塔

戈夫林在导论开篇即指出，「诠释」已成为一个「如此弹性、如此被竞争意义所过载」的词，以至于「它威胁着要同时意味着一切与一无所有」：

> Interpretation, like the unconscious, the couch, or the Oedipus complex, functions as one of our field’s defining brands—a concept so central to psychoanalytic identity that we invoke it worshipfully, as if the mere act of naming it grants therapeutic power. Yet this worship masks a troubling reality: interpretation has become a term so elastic, so overburdened with competing meanings, that it threatens to mean everything and nothing at once.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

他随即给出经验证据：同一场会议上，三位分析师用同一个词描述根本不同的现象——精确说出无意识内容、在关系遭遇中创造新意义、以及不借语词而借「在场的品质」起作用。其结论是：

> We have created a Tower of Babel within our own discipline, where the same word carries radically different meanings depending on who speaks it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

值得注意的是，戈夫林并未试图用重新定义来终结这一混乱。他明确把这种「增殖」重新描述为价值而非缺陷：

> This proliferation of perspectives might seem like theoretical chaos, but I prefer to think of it as creative ferment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论「The Organization of This Book」

## 三、机制判断：诠释不是解码，而是创造

戈夫林与当代各家共享的核心立场是：诠释改变它所描述的对象。

> This is the enigma at the heart of psychoanalytic interpretation: it doesn’t simply decode what already exists but participates in creating new forms of psychic reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

其工作方式因此不是消除歧义，而是**让歧义变得丰产**：

> It works not by eliminating ambiguity but by making ambiguity fertile, transforming confusion from a problem to be solved into a creative space where new meanings can emerge.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

他甚至借用量子观察作比喻：诠释行为改变了它试图理解的东西。这一判断直接决定了后文对治疗目标的改写——治疗的目的不再是「让病人知道关于自己的真理」，而是扩展其可经验、可承受、可生成的心理空间。

## 四、原创对立轴：求真传统 vs 创伤疗愈传统

这是戈夫林在此书中最具建构性的贡献，正式提出于第 4 章（论温尼科特）。他宣称：

> The history of psychoanalysis reveals two competing traditions for the therapeutic encounter, each carrying profound implications for how we understand healing, knowledge, and human relationship. The first, the *truth-seeking tradition*, stretching from Freud through Klein and early Bion, imagined the analyst as scientist and the patient as research subject—a dyad engaged in the methodical excavation of psychical truth.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

求真传统的病理学前提是**否认（disavowal）**：症状是线索，梦是加密信息，疗愈即「认识你自己」；知识本身就是疗效。

> Knowledge, in this view, is inherently curative. The patient suffers not from what they don’t have but from what they won’t see. The analyst’s role is to illuminate these hidden truths through interpretation, gradually dismantling the patient’s defensive structures until they can tolerate knowing what they have always unconsciously known.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

相对的一极由温尼科特代表：

> The second metaphor, *the trauma healing tradition* which Winnicott developed with characteristic subtlety, reconceived the therapeutic relationship as fundamentally like that between parent and infant. Here, healing occurs not through the acquisition of knowledge but through the provision of a particular kind of relational environment.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

两传统在**治疗目标**上的分歧被概括为：

> The implications of this shift extend to the very goal of treatment. The truth-seeking tradition aims to return disavowed parts of the self through interpretation and insight, helping patients to reclaim projected aspects of their personality and achieve greater integration through understanding. The trauma healing tradition, by contrast, seeks to facilitate what Winnicott (1954) called “going-on-being,” that is, the basic continuity of existence that environmental failure can shatter. The goal is not primarily self-knowledge but the capacity for authentic living and creative engagement with reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

### 4.1 差异的四个层面（综合）

| 层面 | 求真传统 | 创伤疗愈传统 |
|---|---|---|
| 治疗目标 | 通过诠释与洞见归还被否认的自体部分 | 促成持续存在（going-on-being），恢复存在的连续性 |
| 病理学前提 | 否认：患者系统性地拒绝承认内在真理 | 环境失败：发展性需要未被满足 |
| 移情的地位 | 期待并鼓励强烈移情反应以便诠释与修通 | 不回避负性移情，但不以其为治疗的必经阶段 |
| 疗效来源 | 认识自己 | 关系性环境的供给 |

### 4.2 自我限定：区分会被批评为虚假极化

戈夫林并未把这一对立轴做成教条。他主动承认：

> Yet this neat distinction between truth-seeking and trauma healing contains its own complications. Critics from each camp rightly argue that the dichotomy creates false polarizations.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

并随即给出让步后的最低限度主张——问题不再是「治疗中是否出现真理」，而是「出现何种真理、以何种方式出现」：

> The question becomes not whether truth emerges in treatment, but what kind of truth and by what means. Nevertheless, this distinction remains crucial, and when one reads the case studies of Klein’s followers versus those of Winnicott’s disciples, the difference is striking: the former approach the patient’s communications with suspicion, searching for hidden meanings, while the latter believe and validate the patient’s experience; the former carefully avoid any display of reassurance or comfort, while the latter embrace, contain, and hold.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：求真 / 创伤疗愈二分是否制造虚假极化
> **A 方立场**：求真阵营——「我们也疗愈创伤」，其诠释工作正处理早期客体关系与体质性脆弱所造成的心灵伤口——「Truth-seeking analysts contend that they too heal trauma」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章）
> **B 方立场**：创伤疗愈阵营——「我们的关系取向不可避免地揭示了关于患者心理发展的重要真理」——「Trauma healing practitioners argue that their relational approach inevitably uncovers important truths about the patient’s psychological development」（同上）
> **备注**：可调和。戈夫林保留了这一区分的**临床可辨识性**（案例写作风格的差异），而放弃其**排他性**；这与 [[topics/topic-therapeutic-action-debate]] 中「治疗行动来源之争」属同一问题域。

## 五、伦理立场：认识论谦逊

戈夫林把诠释的不对称性定性为权力行为：

> Interpretation is fundamentally an act of power: one person claims to know something about another that they cannot know themselves. This asymmetry could easily become authoritarian, even abusive.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「The Living Legacy」

对应的伦理规范被命名为认识论谦逊：

> This ethical stance requires what we might call “epistemological humility”—the confidence to offer interpretations combined with the humility to hold them lightly. The analyst must believe enough in their perceptions to speak them, yet doubt enough to remain open to correction. It’s an ethics of proximity without possession, intimacy without intrusion.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

这一伦理立场在后记中收束为一个普遍命题：

> This brings us to interpretation’s final paradox: it becomes most powerful when we hold it most lightly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记「The Interpretive Paradox」

它也改写了训练观——既然最具转化力的诠释发生在「技术暂时消融」之处，教学便只能是**培养能力**而非**积累技能**：

> We cannot master interpretation as technique because interpretation, at its most transformative, transcends technique entirely. This reframes psychoanalytic training around developing capacities rather than accumulating skills.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

## 六、对科胡特的批评：临床激进，认识论保守

在第 5 章，戈夫林把科胡特定位为美国精神分析中「创伤疗愈」传统最忠实的代表，然后给出全书最锋利的一句判断：

> However, Kohut’s revolution was fundamentally clinical, not epistemological. While he transformed therapeutic practice by insisting that the analyst’s subjectivity was essential to understanding and treating patients, he remained theoretically conservative, clinging to the scientific aspirations and truth-seeking ambitions of classical psychoanalysis.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

其机制在于「替代性内省（vicarious introspection）」这一概念的功效：

> His concept of empathy as “vicarious introspection” attempted to preserve the objective, observational stance of traditional analysis even as his clinical work demonstrated that such objectivity was neither possible nor desirable.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

由此产生一个贯穿其全部著作的结构性张力——分析师主体性的「前门 / 后门」：

> This tension between radical practice and conservative theory runs throughout his work—he brought the analyst’s subjectivity through the front door clinically while trying to usher it out the back door theoretically.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

戈夫林进一步指出科胡特本人已在临床材料中走到了自己理论的边界之外，却未能穿越：

> Kohut opened this door but couldn’t quite walk through it, perhaps sensing that what lay beyond would require abandoning too much of what had made psychoanalysis what it was. Kohut’s institutional embeddedness and theoretical commitments prevented him from fully embracing this insight’s radical implications: that interpretation as traditionally conceived is obsolete, and what patients need is not explanation but recognition—having their adaptive intelligence witnessed and confirmed by another mind.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Conclusion: The Unfinished Revolution」

其结果是戈夫林所说的「未完成的革命」：治疗行动的观念从「解码无意识」转向「承认始终可见却被系统否定的智慧」。

> This unfinished revolution points toward a fundamental reconceptualization of therapeutic action: from decoding the unconscious to recognizing the wisdom in what was always visible but systematically invalidated.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章

## 七、收敛结论：关系而非准确性

戈夫林在后记中给出的全书收敛性判断是：

> This convergence points toward something fundamental: interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

他随即自设反驳——若疗效来自关系而非准确性，精神分析岂不沦为单纯的情绪疗愈？其回答是把二者重新绑定：

> The answer may lie in recognizing that the two are inseparable. Genuine understanding changes the person who understands, just as being truly understood transforms the one who is known. The analyst’s knowledge becomes therapeutic when knowing becomes a way of being with the patient that demonstrates new possibilities for being with oneself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

## 八、分类学的自我瓦解

戈夫林承认所有诠释类型学在临床现场都会崩解——这一点构成他方法论上的自我限制，也是他把全书组织为谱系而非体系的原因：

> Perhaps what we’re discovering is that every taxonomy of interpretation reveals more about our need to organize mystery than about the mystery itself. We divide interpretations into categories—epistemological, transformative, developmental, enactive, aesthetic—but in the consulting room they blend and blur like watercolors in the rain.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记
