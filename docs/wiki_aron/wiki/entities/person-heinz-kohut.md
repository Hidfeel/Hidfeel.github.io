## 摘要

科胡特在「诠释」议题上的核心操作是：**以共情（empathy）替代诠释作为首要工具**。在 1970—80 年代的美国精神分析语境中，共情成为精神分析中**第一个真正打破「治疗行动＝产生洞见的诠释」这一保守大厦**的概念。这不是一次小调整，而是对整个分析事业的根本重构：**共情被承认为心理存在的根本媒介（fundamental medium of psychological existence）**。

他的技术主张建立在理解阶段与解释阶段（understanding / explaining phases）的区分之上：前者是分析师对患者主观经验的**共情沉浸（empathic immersion）**，后者才是遗传学与动力学诠释。而 Govrin 的关键读法是——**解释阶段其实是一种追溯性的虚构**：当解释发生时，理解已经完成了它的工作，诠释于是成为「**一座纪念已经发生的改变的纪念碑**」，而非改变的工具。

科胡特体系的其余构件围绕这一重排展开：**最佳挫折（optimal frustration）与蜕变内化（transmuting internalization）**解释了结构如何被铺设；**自体客体（selfobject）**概念把分析师重新定位为患者发展所需的「功能」而非无意识内容的诠释者；三种自体客体移情（镜映、理想化、孪生）则被视为「**自体主动尝试治愈并完成其被中断的发展**」。

**但本页必须记录 Govrin 对科胡特的核心批评**：科胡特的革命**是临床的，不是认识论的**。他在临床上把分析师的主观性从前门请进来，却在理论上试图把它从后门送出去。Govrin 的自我引述给出了最锋利的表述——「**由于他拒绝放弃诠释的真理，并努力把这一真理建立在主观要素之上，科胡特把自己暴露于不可解决的矛盾之中**」。

## 一、革命的性质

Govrin 强调，今天很难充分把握科胡特的理论框架在当年有多么革命性。

> The concept of empathy became the one of the first notions in psychoanalysis to genuinely rupture the conservative edifice that had long equated therapeutic action with interpretation leading to insight. This was no minor adjustment but a fundamental reconceptualization of the analytic enterprise itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

由此释放出的连锁洞见：共情被承认为**心理存在的根本媒介**；自体只能在其**自体客体（selfobjects）**的基质之内被理解——我们唯有通过与他人的连接才作为完整的存在而存在，体现着一种深刻的主体间实在；人类发展本身被重新想象为**在这种他性的本质语境之内展开**。

> This reconceptualization unleashed a cascade of transformative insights: empathy was recognized as the fundamental medium of psychological existence. The self could only be understood as functioning within its matrix of selfobjects—we exist as complete beings solely through our connection with others, embodying a profound intersubjective reality. Human development itself was reimagined as unfolding within this essential context of otherness.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

### 1.1 历史语境：美国 1970—80 年代

Govrin 提供了一个关键的历史定位：**在 1970 与 1980 年代的美国，温尼科特的著作在专业圈子之外大体上不为人知**。在这一智识版图中，科胡特成为他前一章所称「创伤疗愈传统」在美国精神分析中最忠实的代表——**优先修复发展性中断与早期环境失败，而非考古式发掘被压抑的真理**。

> It is crucial to understand that in the United States of the 1970s and 1980s, Donald Winnicott’s work remained largely unknown outside specialized circles. In this intellectual landscape, Heinz Kohut emerged as the most faithful representative of what I have termed in the previous chapter the “trauma healing” tradition in American psychoanalysis—that therapeutic lineage which prioritizes the restoration of developmental arrests and the repair of early environmental failures over the archaeological excavation of repressed truth.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

## 二、★Govrin 的核心批评：临床激进，认识论保守

这是本页最重要的判断，必须优先于所有技术细节。

> However, Kohut’s revolution was fundamentally clinical, not epistemological. While he transformed therapeutic practice by insisting that the analyst’s subjectivity was essential to understanding and treating patients, he remained theoretically conservative, clinging to the scientific aspirations and truth-seeking ambitions of classical psychoanalysis.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

机制被精确命名：他把共情构想为「**替代性内省（vicarious introspection）**」，试图以此**保存传统分析的客观、观察性立场**——**即便他的临床工作已经证明这种客观性既不可能也不可取**。

> His concept of empathy as “vicarious introspection” attempted to preserve the objective, observational stance of traditional analysis even as his clinical work demonstrated that such objectivity was neither possible nor desirable.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

Govrin 给出的意象极为锋利：**他把分析师的主观性在临床上从前门请了进来，同时在理论上试图把它从后门送出去**。

> This tension between radical practice and conservative theory runs throughout his work—he brought the analyst’s subjectivity through the front door clinically while trying to usher it out the back door theoretically.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology’s Approach to Interpretation」

## 三、早期构想：共情科学的悖论

科胡特 1959 年的论文《内省、共情与精神分析》标志着他终其一生的尝试——**调和两种根本不相容的心理理解愿景**。一方面，他论证共情是研究心理现象的**唯一合法方法**；另一方面，他坚持这一方法是彻底科学的，能够以其他科学仪器产生关于物理世界的客观数据**完全相同的方式**产生关于人类经验的客观真理。

> On the one hand, Kohut argued that was the only legitimate method for studying psychological phenomena. On the other hand, he insisted that this method was thoroughly scientific and capable of yielding objective truths about human experience in precisely the same way that other scientific instruments yield objective data about the physical world.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Kohut’s Early Formulations: The Paradox of Empathic Science」

Govrin 指出：**科胡特的理论创新——承认共情为心理观察的根本模式——受制于他坚持这一模式能产生科学客观性**。

> The tension here is revealing: Kohut’s theoretical innovation—recognizing empathy as the fundamental mode of psychological observation—remained constrained by his insistence that this mode could yield scientific objectivity.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Kohut’s Early Formulations」

即使在他最后的著作中，当科胡特承认「**客观真理必须始终包含对观察者的评估**」（Kohut, 1984, p. 40）时，他仍继续把这一洞见框定为**科学理解上的进步，而非对科学模型本身的根本挑战**。

> Even in his final writings, when he acknowledged that “objective truths must always include an assessment of the observer” (Kohut, <u>1984</u>, p. 40), he continued to frame this insight as an advance in scientific understanding rather than a fundamental challenge to the scientific model itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Kohut’s Early Formulations」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 40）

**体制位置的解释**：与当代关系取向分析师不同（他们大多在传统精神分析机构之外工作），科胡特**深嵌于精神分析建制之中**——他是芝加哥研究所的培训分析师、美国精神分析协会的前主席。这种体制嵌入性有助于解释他为何如此小心地呈现自己的创新。

> This institutional embeddedness may help to explain why Kohut was so careful to present his innovation…

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Kohut’s Early Formulations」

## 四、★从数据收集工具到治疗体验本身

在早期工作中，科胡特主要把共情呈现为一种**数据收集工具**——一种更精细的收集患者内在世界信息的方式，这些信息随后经由传统诠释框架被处理。

> In his early work, Kohut presented empathy primarily as a data-gathering tool—a more sophisticated way of collecting information about the patient’s inner world that would then be processed through traditional interpretive frameworks. The analyst’s empathic understanding was valuable, but it remained subordinate to the larger goal of providing accurate explanations of the patient’s psychological functioning.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「From Data-Gathering Tool to Therapeutic Experience」

这一认识的演化把他引向著名的**「理解」与「解释」阶段之分**（Kohut, 1984, p. 96）：

> This recognition led Kohut to develop his famous distinction between “understanding” and “explaining” phases of interpretation (Kohut, <u>1984</u>, p. 96). The understanding phase, he argued, involved the analyst’s empathic immersion in the patient’s subjective experience. Only after this foundation of empathic connection had been established could the explaining phase—offering genetic and dynamic interpretations—be therapeutically effective.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「From Data-Gathering Tool to Therapeutic Experience」

**但 Govrin 立即指出科胡特的保守残留**：即便在这个更精细的模型中，理解阶段无论多么有价值，**仍然服务于解释阶段**——后者才是「真正的」治疗改变据称（supposedly）发生之处。最终目标本质上仍是古典的：帮助患者获得一种更客观、基于实在的、对其心理运作的视角。

> Yet even this more sophisticated model reveals Kohut’s continued attachment to traditional therapeutic goals. The understanding phase, however valuable, remained in service of the explaining phase, which was where “real” therapeutic change supposedly occurred. As Kohut put it, the analyst’s empathic understanding enables the patient to “become more objective vis-à-vis himself and his problems” (Kohut, <u>1984</u>, p. 185). The ultimate goal remained essentially classical: helping the patient to achieve a more objective, reality-based perspective on their psychological functioning.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「From Data-Gathering Tool to Therapeutic Experience」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 185）

## 五、理解阶段：共情沉浸与「生产性的误解」

在理解阶段，分析师的首要任务**不是挑战防御或揭露无意识愿望，而是共情地进入患者的主观世界**。这需要科胡特（1984）所谓的对患者经验的「**共情沉浸（empathic immersion）**」——一种**替代性内省（vicarious introspection）**的过程，使分析师能够**从内部**而非从外部观察地把握患者的内在实在。

> Here, the analyst’s primary task is not to challenge defenses or to expose unconscious wishes but to enter empathically into the patient’s subjective world. This requires what Kohut <u>(1984)</u> describes as “empathic immersion” in the patient’s experience—a process of vicarious introspection that allows the analyst to grasp the patient’s inner reality from within rather than observing it from without.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」

### 5.1 「你这该死的傻瓜！」

Ernest Wolf（1993）的临床小品展示了这一共情理解的深刻影响。一位患者说到自己如此急于赶来参加会谈，以至于**闯了红灯**。科胡特的快速反应不是把它诠释为移情爱或攻击性的行动化，而是——「**你这该死的傻瓜！（you’re a damn fool!）**」。

> Rather than interpreting this behavior in terms of transference love or aggressive acting out, Kohut’s quick response—“you’re a damn fool!”— was effective precisely because it communicated the analyst’s genuine concern for the patient’s well-being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」（引 Wolf, 1993）

Govrin 的读法极为精到：**这一时刻之所以成功，恰恰因为它未能成为传统意义上的诠释性的**。这是一种奇特的反转——理解阶段的运作**不是通过任何常规意义上的「理解」，而是通过提供某种彻底抗拒理解的东西**：一种自发涌现的真切关切，击穿了诠释机器。

> This moment exposes a curious reversal: the understanding phase works not by understanding in any conventional sense, but by providing something that resists understanding altogether—a spontaneous eruption of genuine concern that breaks through the interpretive machinery. The patient recognizes his excessive need to please not through the analyst’s empathic grasp of this need, but through experiencing himself as someone who matters enough to provoke genuine alarm.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」

由此 Govrin 提出一个重构：**科胡特所谓的「共情沉浸」，或许更宜被理解为一种「生产性的误解（productive misunderstanding）」——一种甘愿被患者所惊讶，而非去理解患者的意愿**。

> This suggests that what Kohut calls “empathic immersion” might be better understood as a kind of productive misunderstanding—a willingness to be surprised by the patient rather than to comprehend them. The analyst who truly enters the patient’s subjective world must paradoxically abandon the very instruments of understanding that psychoanalysis has traditionally provided.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」

科胡特自己的表述（1984, p. 173）——分析师的任务是把握患者的「**对其精神实在的感知，并接受它为有效**」——指向的正是**超出其所用空间隐喻**的东西：分析师的「在患者世界之中」所指的**与其说是一个内在位置，不如说是一种姿态——理论掌控被搁置，甘愿被患者的经验所惊讶与迷失方向**。

> When Kohut <u>(1984)</u> describes the analyst’s task as grasping the patient’s “perception of his psychic reality and accepts it as valid” (p. 173), he points toward something that exceeds the spatial metaphor he employs. In this sense, the analyst’s “being within” the patient’s world refers less to an inner location than to a stance in which theoretical mastery is set aside in favor of a willingness to be surprised and disoriented by the patient’s experience.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Understanding Phase in Clinical Practice」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 173）

## 六、★解释阶段作为追溯性的虚构

这是 Govrin 对科胡特最具原创性的读法，也是本页对「诠释」议题最直接的贡献。

> If the understanding phase operates through a kind of blessed incomprehension, the explaining phase—those genetic and dynamic interpretations that Kohut still considers essential—becomes something altogether more peculiar. Kohut insists that these explanations must follow the establishment of empathic connection, yet his clinical material suggests that explanation often serves to memorialize an understanding that has already done its work. The interpretation becomes not a tool for change but a monument to change that has already occurred—a way of making the ephemeral experience of being understood available for conscious reflection.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Explaining Phase as Retrospective Fiction」

**律师—患者案例**（Kohut, 1984, p. 116）说明了这一点。患者的理智化曾保护他免受母亲侵入性的非理性之害。科胡特逐渐认识到：传统上被视为「理智化」（一种阻碍分析的防御—阻抗）的东西，**实际上是患者早期生命的一项伟大成就**。这个患者把自己构想成的「**思考机器（thinking machine）**」身份，成功地保护了他的自体，免受其「有点疯狂的」母亲试图接管他的企图之害。

> Kohut came to recognize that what might traditionally be seen as “intellectualizing”—a defense-resistance impeding analysis—was actually a great achievement of the patient’s early life. This “thinking machine” identity, as the patient conceived of himself, had successfully protected his self against his “a-little-crazy” mother’s attempts to take him over.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Explaining Phase as Retrospective Fiction」

当科胡特向患者解释这一点时，**诠释并不揭示隐藏的内容，而是转变患者与某种他一直知道但从未能够充分认出的东西之间的关系**。

> When Kohut explains this to the patient, the interpretation does not reveal hidden content but transforms the patient’s relationship to something he had always known but never been able to fully recognize. This distinction matters because it relocates the therapeutic action of interpretation. The explaining phase succeeds by offering what might be called a “witnessing vocabulary”—words that allow the patient’s self-protective achievement to be recognized for what it truly is.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Explaining Phase as Retrospective Fiction」

**「见证性词汇（witnessing vocabulary）」**——这一命名把诠释的功能从「揭示」重定为「见证」，与 [[concepts/concept-interpretation]] 页所记「诠释不揭示隐藏真理而创造新的存在可能性」的命题直接汇合。

## 七、最佳挫折与蜕变内化

科胡特（1984）的表述：**「精神分析通过铺设心理结构而治愈」**（p. 98）——经由最佳挫折与蜕变内化。

> Kohut’s (1984) formulation that “psychoanalysis cures by the laying down of psychological structure” (p. 98) through optimal frustration and transmuting internalization offers a theory of therapeutic action that fundamentally reconceives the nature of psychic change.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 98）

机制：患者带着未被满足的发展需要进入分析，向分析师寻求古老形式的支持——**确认其夸大性的镜映（mirroring）、提供力量与平静的理想化（idealization）、提供本质相同性的孪生（twinship）**。分析师通过共情调谐**暂时满足**这些需要，但不可避免地无法完美地做到。

> According to this view, the patient enters analysis with unmet developmental needs, seeking archaic forms of support from the analyst—mirroring that confirms their grandiosity, idealization that provides strength and calm, twinship that offers essential sameness. The analyst, through empathic attunement, temporarily fulfills these needs, yet inevitably cannot do so perfectly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」

**蜕变内化（transmuting internalization）**：通过在共情连接之后反复出现的**可承受的失望**的循环，患者逐渐把外部的自体客体功能**转化为内在的心理结构**。

> The mechanism Kohut proposes is transmuting internalization: through repeated cycles of empathic connection followed by tolerable disappointment, the patient gradually converts external selfobject functions into internal psychological structure. When the analyst’s understanding falls short, the patient must momentarily provide their own self-soothing. When the idealized analyst proves human and fallible, the patient begins to access their own sources of strength. Each optimal frustration catalyzes a small act of self- provision that gradually accumulates into autonomous capacity.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」

### 7.1 一次诠释作为「额外的创伤」

科胡特（1984, p. 182）的临床小品照亮了这一过程的微妙性质。一次缺席之后，患者出现严重恶化——痛苦的头部感觉、准偏执的指控（关于人们声音的刺耳）、甚至把电视机拿到修理店去检查是否被做过手脚。

> Kohut initially offered what seemed like empathic interpretations: he suggested that the patient’s worsening was paradoxically part of his improvement, that increased emotional openness had exposed him to new anxieties. While cognitively correct, these interpretations only temporarily relieved the patient’s distress before he returned to attacking Kohut as lacking all understanding.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」

**科胡特逐渐把握到的是：他的诠释，无论多么准确，都构成了一次额外的创伤。** 患者体验到它们**「只是从外面来的」**——有语词而无真正的理解，从而**重复了他早期生命的本质创伤**。

> What Kohut gradually came to grasp was that his interpretations, however accurate, constituted an additional trauma. The patient experienced them as coming “only from the outside”—words without real understanding, thereby repeating the essential trauma of his early life.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」

**关键的治疗转变发生在科胡特停止捍卫其诠释的正确性，而允许自己「完全以他的方式、而完全不以我的方式」看待事物**（1984, p. 182）。这要求科胡特经历自己的痛苦自我审视过程，考察那些阻碍他充分感受患者所感的**内在屏障**。

> The crucial therapeutic shift occurred when Kohut <u>(1984)</u> stopped defending the correctness of his interpretations and instead allowed himself to see things “exclusively in his way and not at all in my way” (p. 182). This required Kohut to undergo his own painful process of self-scrutiny, examining the inner barriers that prevented him from fully feeling what the patient felt.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 182）

Govrin 判定这是**对分析技术的激进修正**：**分析师必须接受患者的批评，不是作为有待诠释的移情扭曲，而是作为有待拥抱的心理真理**。

> This represents a radical revision of analytic technique. The analyst must accept the patient’s criticism not as transference distortion to be interpreted but as psychological truth to be embraced.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Optimal Frustration and Transmuting Internalization」

## 八、结构建造的悖论

最优挫折如何变成心理结构，在科胡特的叙述中**根本上是神秘的**。他说「蜕变内化」，但这个术语**命名而非解释**了关系经验变成自主能力这一炼金术。

> The process by which optimal frustrations become psychic structure remains, in Kohut’s account, fundamentally mysterious. He speaks of “transmuting internalization,” but this term names rather than explains the alchemy by which relational experiences become autonomous capacities.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Paradox of Structure Building」

而科胡特的临床材料提示：结构建造**与其说是内化，不如说是发现那些始终在场、但没有分析关系的安全就无法被触及的能力**。律师—患者的笑的能力、表达情绪的能力、放松的能力——**这些不是新被创造出来的能力，而是为了心理生存而被牺牲掉的、被重新找回的可能性**。

> Kohut’s clinical material suggests that structure building might be less about internalization than about discovering capacities that were always present but couldn’t be accessed without the safety of the analytic relationship. … This reconceptualization has profound implications for how we understand therapeutic change. The analyst doesn’t give the patient something new but creates conditions in which the patient can reclaim what was always theirs. The selfobject functions that the analyst provides don’t become internalized; rather, they make it possible for the patient to recognize and utilize selfobject experiences that were always available but couldn’t be trusted.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Paradox of Structure Building」

## 九、★分析师作为自体客体

科胡特认识到分析师**作为自体客体起作用，而不仅仅作为无意识内容的诠释者**——这革新了我们对治疗行动的理解。在最后的著作中，他划分了三种主要的自体客体移情——镜映、理想化与孪生，它们代表「**自体主动尝试治愈并完成其被中断的发展**」（Kohut, 1984, p. 103）。

> In his final work, he delineates three principal selfobject transferences—mirroring, idealizing, and twinship, which represent “the self’s active attempt at healing and finishing its interrupted development” (Kohut, <u>1984</u>, p. 103).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 103）

### 9.1 诠释本身成为一种自体客体功能

这一重新构想**把诠释本身转化为一种自体客体功能**。当分析师提供一个诠释时，他提供的是科胡特所谓的**回应性的自体客体经验**。治疗力量**较少在于诠释的准确性，而更多在于它演示了：患者在分析师的头脑中作为某个值得持续、仔细思考的人而存在**。

> This reconceptualization transforms interpretation itself into a selfobject function. When the analyst offers an interpretation, they provide what Kohut calls a responsive selfobject experience. The therapeutic power lies less in the interpretation’s accuracy than in its demonstration that the patient exists in the analyst’s mind as someone worth sustained, careful thought.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」

**每一次诠释努力，无论对错，都执行着一种镜映功能**——确认患者的内在生命值得深度投入与持续的理解努力。

> Each interpretive effort, whether correct or mistaken, performs a mirroring function—confirming that the patient’s inner life merits deep engagement and persistent effort to understand.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」

### 9.2 首要工具的定义

科胡特最明确的技术宣告：

> This is why Kohut insists that “the analyst’s primary tool must be sustained, finely attuned empathy—not interpretation as revelation of hidden drive conflicts” (Kohut, <u>1984</u>, p. 115).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 115）

在科胡特的框架中，诠释通过**提供那缺失的发展性经验**而运作——被准确感知、被深度考量、在一个人攻击、退缩或崩溃时仍被耐心地持留在另一个人的注意力之中的经验。

> In Kohut’s framework, interpretation works through providing the missing developmental experience—the experience of being accurately perceived, deeply considered, patiently held in another’s attention even when one is attacking, withdrawing, or falling apart.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」

**当分析师挣扎着去理解、修正自己的理解、承认自己的失败并再次尝试时，他所演示的是某种比完美共情更有价值的东西**：患者**值得这持续的理解努力，值得在失败之后被回访，值得被思考**。

> When the analyst struggles to understand, revises their understanding, acknowledges their failure and tries again, they demonstrate something more valuable than perfect empathy: they show that the patient is worth the ongoing effort of understanding, worth returning to after failure, worth thinking about even when the…

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Analyst as Selfobject」

## 十、★超越诠释

科胡特临床与理论革命的最终含义——**一个他的认识论保守主义使他无法充分表述的含义**——是：**传统构想中的诠释变成了某种完全不同的东西**。

> The ultimate implication of Kohut’s clinical and theoretical revolution—one his epistemological conservatism prevented him from fully articulating—is that interpretation as traditionally conceived becomes something entirely different. If therapeutic action lies in the experience of being understood rather than in the content of understanding, if explanation works by witnessing rather than revealing, if resistance protects hope rather than hiding truth, then interpretation cannot be about uncovering objective psychological truths.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Beyond Interpretation」

科胡特的革命性概念——自体客体移情、蜕变内化、最佳挫折——**全都指向这一结论**，然而他继续把它们框定在**传统的认识论框架之内**。

> Kohut’s revolutionary concepts—selfobject transferences, transmuting internalization, optimal frustration—all point toward this conclusion, yet he continued to frame them within a traditional epistemological framework.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Beyond Interpretation」

### 10.1 从诠释到承认（recognition）

**当患者常常确切地知道出了什么问题时，他们所不知道的是他们知道这一点，或者他们的知识是重要的。** 科胡特对律师—患者与其父亲关系的处理说明了这一点：古典分析可能把患者对父亲软弱的恐惧诠释为对竞争性或同性恋愿望的防御；科胡特则把它认作**恰恰是它所看起来的那样**——一个孩子对其父亲无法提供他所需要的可理想化力量的**准确感知**。

> Kohut recognizes it as exactly what it appears to be—a child’s accurate perception that his father couldn’t provide the idealizable strength he needed. The interpretation doesn’t reveal hidden content but confirms the patient’s reality, a reality that had been systematically invalidated by those who should have confirmed it. This movement from interpretation to recognition represents more than a technical modification. It acknowledges that patients often know exactly what’s wrong; what they don’t know is that they know it, or that their knowledge matters…

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Beyond Interpretation」

## 十一、共同创造性：Lichtenberg 与 Wolf 的推进

Ernest Wolf 与 Joseph Lichtenberg 在他们 1997 年的自体心理学立场声明中，展示了自科胡特去世以来这一领域演化了多远。

**人性观**：

> “Self psychology takes the stand that the baby is innately ‘strong.’ We are born preadapted to respond to an empathically sensitive family life that reliably provides soothing, vitalizing, and need-fulfilling experiences” (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 536).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Co-Creative Nature of Interpretation」（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 536）

**治疗行动的根本主体间性**：

> “During an ongoing treatment both patient and therapist communicate verbally and nonverbally, thereby providing a basis for selfobject experiences for the other” (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Co-Creative Nature of Interpretation」（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 533）

这意味着**诠释永远不可能是从分析师到患者的单向真理传递，它总是一种协作性的创造，由双方的贡献所塑造**。

> This mutual influence means that interpretation can never be a one-way delivery of truth from analyst to patient. It’s always a collaborative creation, shaped by both participants’ contributions.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Co-Creative Nature of Interpretation」

**认识论的转移在此是激进的**：科胡特曾维持共情提供了一种相对客观的观察模式（「替代性内省」），而 Wolf 与 Lichtenberg 承认**即便是共情性感知也被观察者的主观性所塑造**。

> Where Kohut had maintained that empathy provided a relatively objective mode of observation—“vicarious introspection”—Wolf and Lichtenberg acknowledge that even empathic perception is shaped by the observer’s subjectivity. They note that “information derived from other sources such as studies of infant development, forms of memory, subliminal perception, affect communication, nonreflective cognition, and neurophysiology enriches our understanding of information derived from an empathic mode of perception” (Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 532).

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Co-Creative Nature of Interpretation」（引 Lichtenberg & Wolf, 1997, p. 532）

Govrin 判定：**这代表一种比科胡特更具认识论谦逊的立场——承认没有任何单一的观察模式，无论多么共情，能够捕捉人类经验的全部复杂性**。

## 十二、★结论：未完成的革命与 Govrin 的核心批评

科胡特的遗产是一个**精致的悖论**：**一种没有占有性的在场伦理，一种没有掌控的知晓，一种没有强制的介入**。

> Kohut’s legacy is a sophisticated paradox: it is an ethic of presence without possessiveness, of knowing without mastery, of intervening without coercion. If analysis cures, it cures not through the analyst’s superior knowledge, but through an encounter that allows the patient to borrow, for a time, the analyst’s faith that a story—any story—is worth the effort to tell.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Conclusion: The Unfinished Revolution」

而 Govrin 的批评以他自己的自我引述达到顶点：

> The tragedy and triumph of Kohut’s contribution is that he gave us the tools to dismantle the very edifice he sought to preserve. As I wrote elsewhere (Govrin, <u>2025</u>), “as he refuses to relinquish the truth of interpretation and in his efforts to base this truth on subjective element, Kohut exposes himself to irresolvable contradictions” (p. 81). His careful observations of therapeutic action revealed that what actually works in analysis cannot be grounded in the kind of interpretive truth Kohut tried to secure and instead exposes the very contradictions he could not resolve.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「Conclusion」（引 Govrin, 2025, p. 81）

最终定位：**科胡特的工作通往一种他无法完全主张的分析愿景——咨询室不是我们超越对他人的需要之处，而是我们学会好好地需要他人之处**。分析师的最终赠礼不是洞见，而是**在「认出我们一直知道的东西」这一困难工作中的陪伴**：我们最奇怪的适应曾是我们最清醒的回应，我们的阻抗曾保护我们的希望，而我们所谓的病理可能只是**在没有更好选项的处境中活下来的代价**。

## 十三、Yadlin-Gadot 视角：自体心理学的认识论归属之争

Yadlin-Gadot 指出：自体心理学家**确认**病人的现实概念，**即使它与治疗师所熟悉的「客观」现实相冲突**（Kohut, 1984）。

> The self psychologist validates the patient's concept of reality even if it conflicts with the "objective" reality the therapist is familiar with (Kohut, 1984).

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

**但与前人一样，在科胡特那里，内外现实的划界仍由分析者完成**——分析者力求巩固病人的主体性及其真理，却不失去对精神现实与物质现实之区分的清晰把握。

> The analyst strives to fortify the patient's subjectivity and its truths, but he or she does this without losing the clarity of his or her hold on the dis- tinction between psychical and material realities.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「Epistemology in Psychoanalytic Discourse」

**科胡特与理想真理**：科胡特相信**缺乏理想真理的生命是贫瘠的**（Kohut & Wolf, 1978, p. 420），这转化为方法论——分析通过与分析师的理想化关系提供发展的新可能。

> Kohut's belief that a life bereft of ideal truth is barren (Kohut & Wolf, 1978, p. 420) translates into methodology, where analysis offers a renewed possibility of development by means of idealizing relations with the analyst.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，「The Relation Between Truth and Epistemology and the Question of Values」

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：科胡特的认识论归属——他究竟是客观观察者传统的延续者，还是主观—现象认识论的开创者？
> **A 方立场**：Govrin（本批资料中的主评）——科胡特的革命「是临床的，不是认识论的」；他坚持以共情为「替代性内省」来保存客观观察立场，「**在临床上把分析师的主观性从前门请进来，同时在理论上试图把它从后门送出去**」；并引自己 2025 年的著作判定他「**暴露于不可解决的矛盾**」。
> **B 方立场**：Yadlin-Gadot 记录的学界分歧（注释 5）——Gedo 把科胡特置于**现象学—主观认识论**；Fosshage 认为他通过引入临床的观察方法**更新了**精神分析认识论；当一切都经过分析者的主观加工过滤时，「**认识论就转变为主观—现象的**」（"Thus, when all is filtered through the analyst's subjective processing, epistemology transforms into the subjective-phenomenal."）。
> **C 方立场（第三方交叉批评）**：克莱因学派**把对理想的接受当作真理视为一种缺陷**，提示这是面对攻击时充当防御的**分裂**的影响；而 Benjamin（1994）则警告，**提出一个理想或作为一个理想而存在的分析者，可能严重损害对病人关于缺失、虚弱与受损之经验的探究**。
> **备注**：A 与 B 可调和——Govrin 描述的是科胡特的**自我理解**（他想保存科学客观性），B 描述的是其工作的**客观归属**（学界如何定位他）；两者共同支持「**理论与实践之间的落差**」这一诊断。C 方则是来自外部的正面对立，与 [[entities/person-melanie-klein]] 页所记克莱因学派的立场同源。
