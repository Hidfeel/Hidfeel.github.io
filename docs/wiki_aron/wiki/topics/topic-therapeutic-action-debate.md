# 治疗行动之争：到底是什么在治愈病人（The Debate on Therapeutic Action）

> **类型**: topic
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]

## 摘要

「精神分析中到底是什么在治愈病人？」——这一问题自斯特雷奇（James Strachey）1934 年论文《精神分析的治疗行动之本质》（The Nature of the Therapeutic Action of Psychoanalysis）起被反复重述，却从未被解决。本页梳理七种相互竞争的答案：洞见与意识化、突变性诠释、涵容与代谢、关系与新客体经验、自体客体功能与内化、分析师的内在自由行动、本体论层面的「成为」与临在。

本页的核心论点是：这七种答案之间的分歧**不是技术之争，而是关于「人是什么、痛苦源于什么」的预设之争**。它们把治疗行动分别定位在**认知、结构、情绪调节、人际、存在**五个不同的层面上；由于各派对「什么叫好转」的定义本身就随其人性预设而变，这场争论在原则上无法被经验证据裁决——没有任何一组数据能同时成为七个不同「好转」定义的检验。

因此本页不给出综合，而是给出两个**收敛性判断**：Govrin 的「诠释通过关系而非准确性起作用」，以及 Ogden 的告诫——认识论与本体论不是学派，而是两种**感受力（sensibilities）**，分析师的任务不是在其中选择，而是知道自己此刻正在使用哪一种。

---

## 一、问题的成立

Govrin 在第 7 章把五篇里程碑论文并置，得出的结论是：每一篇都**增加**了复杂性，而没有一篇**减少**它——

> These five papers attempt to isolate the mechanism of therapeutic change, revealing new layers of complexity, and new paradoxes that resist resolution.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章「Conclusion: The Paradox of Therapeutic Action」

更麻烦的是，这场争论连「成功」的判准都不统一：

> it works best when we understand least why it works

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章

并由此推出一个对培训制度极具破坏力的结论——治疗行动中最关键的东西**不可教**：

> We cannot teach students to create such moments, only to recognize and avoid interfering with them when they occur. We cannot supervise the development of interpretive wisdom—we can only help clinicians to become more comfortable with the uncertainty that makes wisdom possible.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

## 三、逐一展开

### 3.1 洞见与意识化（层面：认知）

**主张**：治疗行动在于使无意识成为意识。病理的根源是**否认（disavowal）**——病人系统性地拒绝承认自己的内在真相，并把不想要的部分投射到他人身上。治疗因此是一种**收复**：帮助病人认回并重新整合被逐出意识的东西。

Govrin 对这一信念的历史定位：

> insight constituted the paramount curative force.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

求真传统的自我陈述则极简而彻底：

> Knowledge, in this view, is inherently curative. The patient suffers not from what they don't have but from what they won't see.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章「From Truth-Seeking to Trauma Healing」

这一传统的分析师角色被克莱因与西格尔（Hanna Segal）压缩为一句话：

> the goal of the analyst is solely to obtain knowledge and to convey it

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Klein & Segal, 1962, p. 212）

**定位**：认知层面。改变的中介是**知识**，判准是**是否看见**。

### 3.2 突变性诠释（层面：认知＋结构）

斯特雷奇给出了机制层面的精细说明：诠释之所以带来**结构性**改变，在于它使分析师被临时内摄为一个**辅助超我（auxiliary superego）**——一个按不同原则运作的形象，从而松动病人严苛的内在结构。

> therapeutic change requires the analyst to become temporarily part of the patient's internal world while simultaneously remaining sufficiently separate to offer something genuinely new.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节（论 Strachey, 1934）

关键在于「告知」与「转化」之分——

> Anyone can tell a patient what they are doing unconsciously—this is what he dismissively called "dictionary" interpretation. But mutative interpretation operates through a more complex process involving what Strachey called an "auxiliary superego," a temporarily introjected figure who operates according to different principles than the patient's harsh internal critics

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节（引 Strachey, 1934, p. 127）

其技术限定极严：只有移情诠释才可能是突变性的，因为唯有在移情中，本能冲动的**对象在场**。

> Is it to be understood that no extra-transference interpretation can set in motion the chain of events which I have suggested as being the essence of psychoanalytical therapy? That is indeed my opinion

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第一节（引 Strachey, 1934, p. 154）

**这一模型的软肋**：它预设分析师「足够分离」而能被当作新客体摄入。在关系与场域取向中，这一预设已不成立。

### 3.3 涵容与代谢（层面：情绪调节）

**主张**：治疗行动不在「知道」，而在**转化**——把无法被思考的 β 元素转为可被思考、可被梦的 α 元素。这是比昂路线。

机制母体是母性遐想（reverie）：

> Leaving aside the physical channels of communication my impression is that her love is expressed by reverie

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1962）

在困难临床情境中，这一过程在意识阈限之下运作：

> unconscious exchanges occur between the dyad, in which the analyst provides implicit alpha-function to the patient, little by little recovering the traumatized parts

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Cassorla, 2008, p. 161）

而 Govrin 的判断是，这已经不是诠释的改良版，而是**别的东西**：

> This is not interpretation in the classical sense but a more fundamental containing and transforming function.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

温尼科特在解离期的工作与之同构——分析师的职责不是诠释，而是**存活**：

> The analyst's role during this phase was not to interpret but to survive—to provide an environment within which such primitive communications could be sustained without forcing premature integration.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

**定位**：情绪调节层面。改变的中介是**被承受与被代谢的经验**，判准是**思考与梦的能力是否恢复**。

### 3.4 关系与新客体经验（层面：人际）

**主张**：治疗行动在于**关系本身**——在于病人获得了一个此前不可得的新客体经验，在于双方共同存活并代谢了活现。

阿隆（Lewis Aron）的奠基句：

> interpretation is best thought of as the quintessential container and purveyor of intersubjectivity between patient and analyst

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 475）

斯特恩（Donnel Stern）给出这一路线最干净的判准——诠释的价值不在于内容，而在于被知的广度与深度：

> The therapeutic value of interpretation stems primarily from widening and deepening the patient's sense of being known by someone who has become emotionally significant

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节（引 Stern, 2023, p. 1128）

布隆伯格（Philip Bromberg）则说明**为什么**必须是新的、不可预见的：

> interpersonal novelty allows the self to grow because it is unanticipated by both persons, organized by what takes place between two minds, and belongs to neither person alone

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节（引 Stern, 2023, p. 1145）

米切尔（Stephen Mitchell）的条件最苛刻——分析师必须**在情感上进入**病人的关系矩阵，并在其中发现自己：

> unless the analyst affectively enters the patient's relational matrix and discovers themselves within it—becoming charmed by the patient's entreaties, shaped by the patient's projections, and antagonized and frustrated by the patient's defenses—the treatment never fully engages and a certain depth is lost.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Mitchell, 1988）

**定位**：人际层面。改变的中介是**共同存活的活现与不可预见的人际新颖性**，判准是**是否生成双方皆无法独自产出的新意义**。

### 3.5 自体客体功能与内化（层面：结构建立）

**主张**：治疗行动在于**心理结构的铺设**——通过最佳挫折（optimal frustration）与蜕变内化（transmuting internalization），外在的自体客体功能被逐步转换为内在能力。

> psychoanalysis cures by the laying down of psychological structure

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 98）

三类自体客体移情（镜映、理想化、孪生）被重新定义为自体的自救努力，而非退行：

> the self's active attempt at healing and finishing its interrupted development

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 103）

而「治愈」的定义随之被彻底改写——**不是**脱离对自体客体的需要，而是：

> the patient's newfound ability to seek, recognize, and draw on selfobjects outside therapy

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 120）

**定位**：结构／发展层面。改变的中介是**可承受的失望**，判准是**自主功能是否增生**。

### 3.6 分析师的内在自由行动（层面：分析师的心智状态）

**主张**：治疗行动发生在**诠释之前**。真正的动因是分析师的内在自由行动（西明顿称之为 x-phenomenon），诠释只是对已经发生的转变的**表达**。

> The interpretation is essential in that it gives *expression* to the shift that has already occurred and makes it available to consciousness. The point though is that the essential agent of change is the *inner* act of the analyst

— Symington, *Freedom*，「Freedom as agent of therapeutic change」

> I am calling this act of inner freedom the 'x-phenomenon'.

— Symington, *Freedom*，「Freedom as agent of therapeutic change」

分析师并非站在场外，而是被**套索**进去：

> The analyst is lassoed into the patient's illusory world. He is more involved in it, more victim to it than the average social contact.

— Symington, *Freedom*，「Freedom as agent of therapeutic change」

他明确拒绝把这一切归为投射性认同，并给出第三项：

> There is a third term: the process in which both are involved.

— Symington, *Freedom*，「Neville Symington」

其时间结构被写在结论中——**存在一种先于言语、因而先于诠释的知识**：

> The 'x-phenomenon' implies that there is a knowledge that is pre-verbal and that it is anterior to speech and therefore to interpretation.

— Symington, *Freedom*，结论

**定位**：分析师的存在状态层面（严格说不在病人身上定位）。改变的中介是**分析师内在自由的获得**，判准是**团体性人格中的束缚是否被解除**。参见 [[concepts/concept-analytic-act-of-freedom]]、[[concepts/concept-corporate-personality]]。

### 3.7 本体论的「成为」与临在（层面：存在）

**奥格登**：治疗行动在于让此前无法想象的**存在状态**在关系中活过来，而不是让病人获得关于自己的知识。

> therapeutic action characterizing ontological psychoanalysis involves providing an interpersonal context in which forms of experiencing, states of being, come to life in the analytic relationship that were previously unimaginable by the patient

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

> Our interpretations matter not because they reveal hidden truths but because they create new possibilities for being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第四节（引 Ogden, 2019）

临床上最极端的形式是**拒绝诠释**。面对脑瘫病人 C 先生那个关于洗车与音乐的梦：

> I had not the slightest inclination to talk with Mr. C. about my understanding of the dream. I said to him, "What a wonderful dream that was"

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五节

**埃谢尔**：在最困难的分析情境中，首要的治疗手段不是诠释，而是分析师把自己交付出去，成为病人经验世界**之内**的一个嵌入性的、持续运作的在场。

> Essentially, "presencing" is the analyst's deep acceptance of the necessity of becoming an embedded, elemental, and sustaining functioning presence within the treatment process—thereby experiencing, withstanding, processing, and gradually transforming, *from within*, the repetitive cycle of pathological self–other relations and defenses.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

其极致是分析师**成为病人精神现实的一部分**：

> The analyst thus becomes part of the patient's state of being and experiencing, to the extent of becoming psychically akin to a transplant or to chimeric antibodies

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

其终点条件则被写在本批文献最沉的一句里——**不抵达谷底就没有结束**：

> And I add: there is no end unless the bottom of the trough has been reached*, un-* *less the thing feared has been experienced* in a compassionate holding, within the analyst's "presencing" and interconnecting with the patient's gripping, unbearable agony, devastation, and death. Analyst-and-patient t(w)ogether there, with-in.

— Eshel, *Withness*，结语

**定位**：存在层面。改变的中介是**新的存在状态在关系中获得生命／共同受苦与从之内的转化**，判准是**是否感到更真实、更活着／不可承受之物是否被经验过**。

### 3.8 补充：崩溃作为沟通，与诠释作为爱

博拉斯（Christopher Bollas）在崩溃这一极端情境中提出第八种说法：崩溃不是需要被消除的病理，而是**等待被阅读的文本**；而诠释在此成为一种爱——

> For Bollas, interpretation becomes a form of love—being known is being loved at a crucial time in one's life.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第三节

**定位**：存在与关系的交界。参见 [[concepts/concept-breakdown-deadness]]。

## 四、为什么这场争论难以裁决

### 4.1 它涉及的是预设分歧，而非可检验的技术差异

「洞见能治愈」与「关系能治愈」这两个命题，各自依赖于一套关于人的预设：

| | 求真／洞见路线 | 关系／存在路线 |
|---|---|---|
| **痛苦源于** | 否认、看不见自己的真相 | 环境失败、发展性中断、存在未被接住 |
| **病人缺的是** | 关于自己的知识 | 一个能被依赖的客体／一段被同在的经验 |
| **分析师的角色** | 知晓者 | 环境、存活者、同在者 |
| **治愈的标志** | 能看见并承受自己的真相 | 能成为更充分的自己 |

这两套预设无法在同一组数据上竞争，因为它们对「什么算治愈」的定义都不同。

### 4.2 分歧的根源是需要，不是证据

亚德林-加多特（Shlomit Yadlin-Gadot）的真理轴框架给出了最有力的说明：每一种真理观（以及与之配套的治疗理论）都扎根于一种**深层的情绪需要**——

> Each organizational principle provides for a deep emotional need that motivates its formulation.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，"Truth Axes, Psychological Needs, and Developmental Challenges"

而世界本身即是依需要被建构的：

> The world is construed in accordance with our needs: We create the realities we need.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，"From Truth Notions to Truth Axes"

**这意味着各理论并非在竞争同一个对象的解释权，而是在回应不同的需要。** 她的结论因此是：

> From this vantage point, the different psychoanalytic theories are not seen as competing, but as creating a theoretical arc that parallels the epistemic multiplicity of the mind.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，结论

并进一步把治疗与伦理重新连接：

> Acknowledging that all truth axes express basic needs and are determined by them draws together the dimensions of the therapeutic and the ethic.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，结论

> I have stressed that these truths are not in competition with each other. Rather, they are complementary, arising from different needs and expressing their associated dreads and desires.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，结论

### 4.3 奥格登的告诫：这是感受力，不是学派

若各派是「感受力」而非「学派」，则把它们当作可比较的技术集合来做疗效对比，本身就是一个**范畴错误**：

> They are ways of thinking and being—sensibilities, not "schools" of analytic thought or sets of analytic principles or analytic techniques

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

### 4.4 分类学在临床现场自我瓦解

即便接受某种分类，它在临床现场也不成立——

> We divide interpretations into categories—epistemological, transformative, developmental, enactive, aesthetic—but in the consulting room they blend and blur like watercolors in the rain.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

> **【推断】** 综合 4.1–4.4：这场争论难以被经验证据裁决，不是因为证据不足，而是因为**争的是人性预设**（人是求知的动物、是需要被涵容的婴儿、是关系性的存在、是需要结构的发展中自体、还是挣扎着要「成为」自己的存在）。证据只能在预设内部起作用，无法在预设之间仲裁。亚德林-加多特的「真理弧」方案因此不是解决，而是**把不可解决性重新描述为可共存性**。

## 五、两个收敛性判断

### 5.1 Govrin：诠释通过关系而非准确性起作用

> This convergence points toward something fundamental: interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

但他立刻处理了这一判断自带的反对意见——若疗效来自关系，精神分析岂不沦为一种情绪疗愈？他的回答是**二分本身不成立**：

> The answer may lie in recognizing that the two are inseparable. Genuine understanding changes the person who understands, just as being truly understood transforms the one who is known.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

并给出临床上的可操作结论——**握得越轻，越有力量**：

> This brings us to interpretation's final paradox: it becomes most powerful when we hold it most lightly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

### 5.2 Ogden：不要把它们当作学派标签

> They coexist in mutually enriching relationship with one another. They are ways of thinking and being—sensibilities, not "schools" of analytic thought or sets of analytic principles or analytic techniques

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

其规范性后果是：分析师的任务不是**选择**一种立场，而是**知道自己此刻正在使用哪一种**，并愿意被病人改变。

### 5.3 一条仍未闭合的分歧

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：治疗行动的动因在**分析师之内**（他的一次内在自由行动），还是在**二元之间**（共同生成的场域）？
> **A 方（西明顿）**：动因是分析师的一次内在自由行动，先于并促成诠释；「The point though is that the essential agent of change is the *inner* act of the analyst」（Symington, *Freedom*）。
> **B 方（关系／场域取向、埃谢尔）**：动因在二元共同生成的场域中，或在分析师**嵌入**病人经验这一状态本身；不存在「脱身」这回事——「The analyst is lassoed into the patient's illusory world. He is more involved in it, more victim to it than the average social contact.」（Symington, *Freedom*）
> **备注**：**范式差异**。A 方的「自由」以「共享错觉／团体性人格（corporate personality）」为前提，即假定分析师**能够**获得一个相对独立的观察位置；B 方的「共构」以「分析师已在其中、且对其参与部分盲视」为前提，即假定这个位置**不可得**。二者对分析师能否脱身持有相反判断。注意西明顿本人的文本同时含有两个前提（既说被套索，又说可获得自由），因此这一张力在他内部亦未完全化解。

   — Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

9. > The analyst thus becomes part of the patient's state of being and experiencing, to the extent of becoming psychically akin to a transplant or to chimeric antibodies
   — Eshel, *Withness*

10. > They are ways of thinking and being—sensibilities, not "schools" of analytic thought or sets of analytic principles or analytic techniques
    — Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

11. > interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.
    — Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

12. > the different psychoanalytic theories are not seen as competing, but as creating a theoretical arc that parallels the epistemic multiplicity of the mind.
    — Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*，结论
