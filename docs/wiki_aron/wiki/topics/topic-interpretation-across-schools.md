# 各学派诠释观横向对比（interpretation across schools）

> **类型**: topic
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/govrin-interpretation]]，并综合其余五份文献

## 摘要

本页回答一个贯穿全部原始资料的问题：**「诠释」在各个精神分析体系中究竟处于什么位置？**

在求真传统（弗洛伊德—克莱因—早期比昂）中，诠释是核心治疗工具，其价值在于**揭示**被否认的内在真理；在创伤疗愈传统（费伦齐—温尼科特—科胡特）中，诠释退居次位，治疗的核心转为**提供关系环境**；在关系与场域取向中，诠释是二元场域中**共同涌现的产物**；到了奥格登与埃谢尔，诠释甚至被视为可能**阻碍**治疗的东西——真正起作用的是「存在」本身。贯穿这一演变的有四条线索：诠释从「揭示」转向「创造」；反移情从「干扰」经「工具」升级为「共同建构」；治疗行动的重心从「洞见」移向「关系」再移向「存在」；以及整体范式从认识论转向本体论。第一条是后三条的根源：一旦承认诠释**参与创造**它所描述的精神现实，「谁在认识」「认识是否带来疗愈」都必须被重新回答。

## 一、总览对比表

| 学派／人物 | 诠释的性质 | 治疗行动来源 | 分析师角色 | 真理观 | 认识论／本体论 | 对反移情的立场 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **弗洛伊德** | 把梦工作编码的无意识信息译回意识；后期转为考古学式**建构** | 洞见、使无意识成为意识 | 知晓者、解读者 | 对应真理优先，晚期退居叙事真理 | 强认识论 | 需被克服的**干扰** |
| **克莱因** | 即刻直指最深层原始焦虑的诠释；**精神滋养** | 被摄入、被代谢；洞见本身即喂养 | 婴儿期焦虑的翻译者 | 无意识幻想的客观可知性 | 认识论 | **工具**，但不是探索对象 |
| **比昂** | 涵容不可分割的另一半；紧急时刻说出的话 | 涵容与转化（β→α），终于 O 中的转化 | 接收者、转化者、做梦者 | 拒斥对应真理，取「有用的虚假」 | **由认识论转向本体论** | 遐想是接收装置 |
| **温尼科特** | 标示分析师**理解的界限**，而非传递知识；故意不完美 | 环境供给、持续存在、被使用 | 环境、存活者 | 语言有限，诠释可能构成侵犯 | 本体论 | 相对开放，但非核心议题 |
| **科胡特** | 共情后的追溯性**虚构**；解释只是叙事重构 | 被理解、最佳挫折与蜕变内化 | 自体客体 | 解释的价值在证认病人现实 | 认识论（保守） | 共情沉浸即工具 |
| **贝蒂·约瑟夫** | 焦点从「内容」转向「过程」——病人如何**中和**诠释 | 对过程的觉察；真实改变 vs 智性顺从 | 气氛与压力的觉察者 | 真理与仁慈之间的张力 | 认识论 | **核心工具** |
| **关系／场域** | 主体间场域中涌现的**共构产物** | 新意义的共同生成、共同存活活现 | 参与者、共同作者、宿主 | 多重真理并存 | 认识论＋本体论 | **共同建构** |
| **奥格登** | 有时**不诠释**才是最深刻的干预 | 存在状态的活化；创造存在的新可能性 | 等待者、做梦者 | 真理即生成的可能性 | **本体论**（明确主张，但强调与认识论共存） | 反移情属于第三方；分析师被它转化 |
| **埃谢尔** | 被「临在」取代；治疗发生在诠释**之外** | 相互贯通、同在；治疗**创造**精神现实 | 嵌入病人经验**之内**者 | 与 O 合一 | **本体论**，且**超越主体间性** | 超越投射性认同，是双方共担的真实感受 |
| **西明顿** | 只是把**已发生**的转变表达出来 | 分析师的内在自由行动（x-phenomenon） | 自由者、自我对自我接触的一端 | — | 本体论 | 与移情同属一个系统，是**共享错觉** |
| **戈夫林** | 通过关系而非准确性起作用 | 关系 | 有信心给出、有谦逊轻握者 | 问题不在「有无真理」，而在「何种真理、以何方式出现」 | 综合 | 分析师主体性是最核心的仪器 |
| **亚德林-加多特** | 依其所栖居的**真理轴**而定 | 轴间流动而非坍缩 | 认识论专长者，但不被要求中立 | 六轴并存，互不取消 | 认识论多元论 | 需觉察自身偏好及其意义 |

【综合】本表六列的取值均由各节引文支撑；「对反移情的立场」一列中，弗洛伊德一格为【推断】——本批文献未直接引用弗洛伊德论反移情的原句，该格依据克莱因章中「克莱因对反移情的态度既延伸又背离弗洛伊德」这一对照论述推得。

## 二、分节详述

### 2.1 弗洛伊德：从解码到建构，终于「病人有最后一句话」

弗洛伊德把诠释设想为一项翻译工作——把梦工作（dream-work）编码过的无意识信息译回意识语言。戈夫林把这条路线称为求真传统：分析师是科学家，病人是研究主体，症状是线索，病理源于**否认（disavowal）**，而知识本身即疗效。他给神经症病人的劝告浓缩了这一认识论信仰：

> The guilt … It is in you … look inward, look into your own depths, learn first and foremost to know yourself!

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Freud, 1917, pp. 142–143）

他在导论中把这一视野命名为通往无意识的「royal road（王道）」，但这条路的尽头是一处失败。**朵拉案例是这一失败的原型**：

> It succeeded as correspondence truth—capturing what was factually accurate—yet failed as pragmatic truth, as something that could actually work in the lived reality of the analysis

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章（引 Yadlin-Gadot, 2016, 2017）

换言之：弗洛伊德的诠释在**对应真理**上成功——它准确说中了事实；却在**实用真理**上失败——它在分析关系的现实中没有起作用。十八岁的朵拉在两周后走出了分析室。

弗洛伊德本人对时机的执念可以读作对这一失败的迟来承认：

> One must wait until [the patient] has himself reached the neighbourhood of what he has repressed … one must allow the patient time … to become conversant with the resistance

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章（引 Freud, 1913, pp. 139–140）

1937 年《分析中的建构》是最终的退让：面对被遗忘历史的碎片，分析师只能作考古学式重构：

> Constructions aim at historical truth but must settle for narrative truth—for stories that make sense of symptoms and suffering even if they cannot claim to represent exactly what happened.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章「From Interpretation to Construction」

戈夫林由此抽出弗洛伊德全部工作的伦理内核——诠释本质上是一种权力行为：

> Interpretation is fundamentally an act of power: one person claims to know something about another that they cannot know themselves.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章

详见 [[entities/person-sigmund-freud]]、[[concepts/concept-construction]]、[[concepts/concept-mutative-interpretation]]。

### 2.2 克莱因：即时诠释、精神滋养，以及对反移情的警惕

克莱因保留了「无意识可知、诠释可译」的认识论信念，但把它推向极端：既然无意识幻想从出生起就活跃于移情之中，分析师就无须等待关系建立，而应当**即刻**就最深层、最原始的焦虑作诠释。她自己把这一创新描述为对既有原则的背离：

> At the time I began to work it was an established principle that interpretations should be given very sparingly.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Klein, 1975a, Vol. 3, p. 122）

其理论正当性在于一个方向性判断：**准确的诠释创造关系，而非依赖关系**——延宕诠释等于把孩子独自留在无法承受的恐惧里。

> Moreover, Klein believed that accurate interpretation creates rather than depends upon the therapeutic relationship.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Principle of Immediate Interpretation」

第二个转折是诠释的功能定位：它不只是传递洞见，更是**精神滋养**——被病人摄入、代谢，从而扩展其承受原始焦虑的能力。这也解释了克莱因为何能对极幼小的儿童使用看似超出其年龄的语言。参见 [[concepts/concept-immediate-interpretation]]。

与后世克莱因派不同，克莱因本人对反移情持**警惕**立场：

> I have never found that the countertransference has helped me to understand my patient better; if I may put it like this; I have found that it has helped me to understand myself better

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」（引 Klein, 2017b, p. 103）

> Klein criticized contemporary uses of projective identification that focus on analyzing the analyst's feelings, insisting instead that the analyst's central task is to use countertransference only as a tool for understanding the patient's internal world, not as an object of exploration in its own right.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Question of Countertransference」

她的分析态度由一句短语标定——「unfailing desire to ascertain the truth（不懈求真之愿）」。详见 [[entities/person-melanie-klein]]。

### 2.3 比昂：从一次临床失败出发，终于 O

比昂的整个诠释理论起源于一次坦率的失败记录。在《对联结的攻击》（1959）中，他回顾自己如何以例行诠释回应病人投射过来的死亡恐惧：

> he had responded with what he calls “routine interpretations”— interventions that carried no emotional presence, offered no true containment, and were thus experienced by the patient as an outright rejection

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（论 Bion, 1959, p. 312）

这一失误**复现而非转化**了婴儿期创伤——病人面对的又是一个无法接收的客体。比昂由此确立的原则是：**诠释与涵容不可分割**：

> the analyst's specific action is not the delivery of theoretical insight, but a spoken interpretation at the point of urgency

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章

接收装置是**遐想（reverie）**。比昂以母婴为模型：

> Leaving aside the physical channels of communication my impression is that her love is expressed by reverie

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1962）

晚期比昂把重心从「知」彻底移到「在」。他的真理观既极端又非对应论：

> healthy mental growth seems to depend on truth as the living organism depends on food

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1965, p. 38）

而他所要求的分析师姿态，是济慈意义上的**负性能力（negative capability）**：

> When a man is capable of being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact and reason

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 125）

最终，诠释的本体论地位被改写——它不再描述现实，而参与现实的演化：

> the interpretation is an actual event in an evolution of O that is common to analyst and analysand

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

【综合】比昂是本表中唯一在**单个人内部**完成认识论→本体论转向的人物，后来的奥格登正是据此把早期比昂与晚期比昂分置两侧。详见 [[entities/person-wilfred-bion]]、[[concepts/concept-containment-reverie-alpha]]、[[concepts/concept-negative-capability]]、[[concepts/concept-transformation-o-to-k]]。

### 2.4 温尼科特：诠释标示的是理解的界限

温尼科特完成了治疗行动从「知」向「在」的迁移。他那句被广泛引用的自述把诠释的功能整个翻转过来：

> I think I interpret mainly to let the patient know the limits of my understanding

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Winnicott, 1963, p. 189）

诠释不再传递知识，而是**标示分析师理解的边界**。他甚至主张诠释应当**故意不完美**：

> if I make none the patient gets the impression that I understand everything. In other words, I retain some outside quality by not being quite on the mark—or even by being wrong

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Winnicott, 1969, p. 718）

因为「游戏的对立面不是工作，而是**强制（coercion）**」——语言一旦以诠释的形式使用，极易从促进发现滑向强加意义：

> the opposite of play is not work but coercion

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Phillips, 1988, p. 138）

在治疗严重解离的病人时，把躯体症状翻译成情绪语言本身就是一种「**侵犯（impingement）**」，是对原始创伤的复现。因此温尼科特可以随时撤回诠释：

> The analyst feels like interpreting, but this can spoil the process, and for the patient can seem like a kind of self-defence

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Winnicott, 1971a, p. 92）

戈夫林用温尼科特给出全书的二分框架——求真传统 vs 创伤疗愈传统，并立即指出这一二分会制造「虚假的两极对立」，真正的分歧被重新表述为**真理的类型学问题**：

> The question becomes not whether truth emerges in treatment, but what kind of truth and by what means.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章

对照面由西格尔（Hanna Segal）提供——分析师的目標**仅仅是获取知识并把它传达出去**：

> the goal of the analyst is solely to obtain knowledge and to convey it

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章（引 Segal, 1962, p. 212）

详见 [[entities/person-donald-winnicott]]、[[concepts/concept-holding-going-on-being]]、[[concepts/concept-transitional-space]]、[[concepts/concept-truth-seeking-vs-trauma-healing]]。

### 2.5 科胡特：共情取代诠释，解释只是事后追认

科胡特把**共情（替代性内省）**而非诠释确立为首要工具。他的立场可以浓缩为一句：

> the analyst's primary tool must be sustained, finely attuned empathy—not interpretation as revelation of hidden drive conflicts

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 115）

治疗通过**最佳挫折（optimal frustration）**与**蜕变内化（transmuting internalization）**建立心理结构：

> psychoanalysis cures by the laying down of psychological structure

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 98）

他承认「解释阶段」只是**追溯性的虚构**——它描述的是已经发生过的理解过程，而非新增的知识。戈夫林对科胡特的判词极为锋利，且是全章的题眼：

> Kohut's revolution was fundamentally clinical, not epistemological.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章「The Revolutionary Nature of Self Psychology's Approach to Interpretation」

他打开了分析师主体性的大门，又在认识论层面把它关上——即使在承认观察者不可排除时，仍把这表述为科学的进步而非对科学模型的挑战：

> objective truths must always include an assessment of the observer

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Kohut, 1984, p. 40）

> as he refuses to relinquish the truth of interpretation and in his efforts to base this truth on subjective element, Kohut exposes himself to irresolvable contradictions

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第五章（引 Govrin, 2025, p. 81）

详见 [[entities/person-heinz-kohut]]、[[concepts/concept-selfobject-empathy]]、[[concepts/concept-optimal-frustration]]。

### 2.6 贝蒂·约瑟夫：从内容转向过程

约瑟夫承接克莱因传统，但把注意力从「诠释说了什么」转向「**病人如何接收、使用或中和**诠释」。其经典克莱因派前提（越深越稳）由西格尔表述：

> the deeper the layers of the unconscious reached, the richer and the more stable the therapeutic result

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章「The Shift from Content to Process」（引 Segal, in Blass, 2011, p. 1151）

约瑟夫的观察是：病人常会**同意**诠释，同时使之失效。她的注意力因此落在会谈的**气氛与压力**上：

> not only what is being said—the actual words, stories being told—but also to the atmosphere of the session; the pressures on and expectations and anxieties that are being aroused in the analyst

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 238）

她据此区分真实洞见与智性顺从：

> If, however, the patient loses contact with his experiencing self, and projectively identifies with, and takes over, the analyst's understanding … then that very understanding becomes an intellectual thing

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Joseph, 1992, p. 243）

这一转向的实质是：病人正在**邀请分析师共同演出**，从而维持其内在动力于既有平衡态：

> the way in which the patient is inviting the analyst to enact and thus maintain his (the patient's) inner dynamics in their current state of equilibrium

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Blass, 2011, p. 1150）

【推断】约瑟夫在此事实上已经站在「反移情＝共同建构」的门槛上——她要求分析师监听自己**被施加的压力**，这与克莱因「反移情只是工具」的禁令方向相反，只是她未把它上升为本体论主张。详见 [[entities/person-betty-joseph]]、[[concepts/concept-vertex]]、[[concepts/concept-enactment]]。

### 2.7 关系与场域取向：诠释作为共同涌现的产物

关系取向的转折由阿伦（Lewis Aron）一句话完成：

> interpretation is best thought of as the quintessential container and purveyor of intersubjectivity between patient and analyst

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 475）

如果诠释本质上是主体间的，那么分析师的 unconscious、理论偏好与个人历史就不可避免地塑造了他听到什么、又如何诠释；而且他对自己的这一角色**部分盲视**：

> The analyst is not only impacted by his own unconscious process in the formulation of any interpretation, not only a full participant in any analytic enactment, but partially blind to the role he plays in helping the patient to understand both of these processes.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 654）

**分析师的权威由此被结构性瓦解。** 巴斯（Anthony Bass）把「失误、口误与盲点」重新评价为通道：

> mistakes, slips, and blind spots that serve as doors through which the analyst and the patient are transported into realms of personal encounter and self-experience that might otherwise remain inaccessible

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Bass, 2003, p. 660）

场论（Baranger、Ferro、Civitarese）把这一点推到更彻底：诠释不再是分析师对病人输出的东西，而是**双人际场**中涌现的叙事衍生；好的诠释应当**未饱和**，为病人留下填充意义的空间：

> premature interpretive closure—what he describes as attempts to reveal true truth—functions as a form of intellectual violence that forecloses possibility rather than opening it

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（论 Ferro, 2006b, p. 1）

斯特恩（Donnel Stern）给出最极端也最简洁的版本——诠释根本不是分析师的自觉创造：

> interpretations are not the analyst's conscious creations, but rather they are “the voice of the field”—expressions of the dynamic interpersonal space between analyst and patient

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章（论 Stern, 2023, p. 1136）

详见 [[concepts/concept-analytic-field]]、[[concepts/concept-unsaturated-interpretation]]、[[concepts/concept-analytic-third]]、[[concepts/concept-dissociation-self-states]]。

### 2.8 奥格登：本体论转向——有时不诠释才是最深的干预

奥格登 2019 年提出「本体论精神分析」，把弗洛伊德—克莱因一线归为认识论取向（关乎**知晓与理解**），把温尼科特—比昂一线归为本体论取向（关乎**存在与生成**）：

> The author discusses differences between what he calls epistemological psychoanalysis (having to do with knowing and understanding), for which Freud and Klein are principal authors, and ontological psychoanalysis (having to do with being and becoming), for which Winnicott and Bion are principal architects.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，摘要

> I view Freud and Klein as the founders of a form of psychoanalysis that is epistemological in nature, and I consider Winnicott and Bion as the principal contributors to the development of ontological psychoanalysis

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

**必须强调**：奥格登反复申明，这两种取向从来不以纯粹形态存在，它们是「**感受力（sensibilities）**」而非学派或技术：

> there is no such thing as ontological psychoanalysis or epistemological psychoanalysis in pure form. They coexist in mutually enriching relationship with one another. They are ways of thinking and being—sensibilities, not “schools” of analytic thought or sets of analytic principles or analytic techniques.

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

临床上，他的标杆案例是「不诠释」：面对脑瘫病人 C 先生那个洗车听音乐的梦，奥格登没有解析象征内容：

> I had not the slightest inclination to talk with Mr. C. about my understanding of the dream. I said to him, “What a wonderful dream that was”

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五节

戈夫林把这一路线压缩成一条关于诠释价值的新定义：

> Our interpretations matter not because they reveal hidden truths but because they create new possibilities for being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章

详见 [[entities/person-thomas-ogden]]、[[concepts/concept-epistemological-vs-ontological]]、[[concepts/concept-dreaming-the-patient]]。

### 2.9 埃谢尔：临在与同在——位置在「之内」，不在「之间」

埃谢尔的立场比关系取向更激进。她主张分析师的根本手段是**临在（presencing）**——深度接受自己成为病人经验世界**内部**的一个运作性在场：

> This paper focuses on the analyst's “presencing” (being there) within the patient's experiential world and within the grip of the psychoanalytic process, and the ensuing deep patient–analyst interconnectedness, as a fundamental dimension of analytic work.

— Eshel, *Withness*，摘要

她对自己的范式归属毫不含糊：

> It meant pursuing the ontological (being) dimension of experience rather than the epistemological (interpretive) dimension

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*

治疗因此**创造**而非揭示精神现实：

> As in quantum reality, treatment thus creates psychic reality, and goes beyond the exposing or deciphering of an existing repressed or concealed psychic reality.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*

其正面主张是「二而一」：

> Viewed in this way, it is not a one-person or two-person psychology, but a process whereby patient and analyst become an emergent two-in-one new entity that is fundamentally inseparable into its two participants: an entity (unit or being) of interconnectedness, “twogetherness,” or “withness,” of essential inseparability.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*

**关键分歧**在于位置。她同意费罗「场必须随病人自身的病而病」这一根本点，却纠正其**拓扑学**：

> While I completely agree with this fundamental point, the analytic experience of “presencing” and “withness” or interconnectedness is not located between patient and analyst, but is as much as possible within the patient's world.

— Eshel, *Withness*，第二节

她明确把自己的主张置于关系取向**之外**：

> “Presencing” and interconnectedness go beyond the level of interactions and patient–analyst relationships (object or subject relationships), even beyond intersubjectivity, to offer the opportunity for getting in touch with basic (environmental) relatedness and formative experiences of being and becoming

— Eshel, *Withness*，第一部分

诠释在此被降格为可有可无的「诠释性休止（interpretive caesura）」：

> It is an interconnected rather than interactive relatedness, a primary quality of presence that provides experience-near attunement, holding, containing, and protection, rather than interpreting patient–analyst relationships and especially patient–analyst separateness.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*（引 Ferro, 2005, p. 44）

详见 [[entities/person-ofra-eshel]]、[[concepts/concept-withness]]、[[concepts/concept-interconnectedness]]、[[concepts/concept-analytic-presencing]]、[[topics/comparison-withness-vs-analytic-third]]。

### 2.10 西明顿：诠释只是事后追认，动因为内在自由

西明顿把治疗动因从诠释**整个移走**。真正的动因是分析师在病人投射系统的裹挟中仍能思考自己思想的那一刻——他称之为 **x-phenomenon**。诠释的作用仅在于把这个**已经发生**的转变表达出来：

> The interpretation is essential in that it gives expression to the shift that has already occurred and makes it available to consciousness. The point though is that the essential agent of change is the inner act of the analyst and that this inner act is perceived by the patient and causes change.

— Symington, *Freedom*

他的论证核心是：存在一种先于言语、因而**先于诠释**的知识：

> The ‘x-phenomenon’ implies that there is a knowledge that is pre-verbal and that it is anterior to speech and therefore to interpretation.

— Symington, *Freedom*

支撑这一主张的是**团体性人格（corporate personality）**模型——分析师与病人经由各自超我融合为一个系统：

> Together they form an entity which we might call a corporate personality.

— Symington, *Freedom*

> In this way transference and countertransference are two parts of a single system; together they form a unity. They are the shared illusions which the work of analysis slowly undoes.

— Symington, *Freedom*

他明确拒绝用投射性认同来覆盖这一现象——因为这里没有「可归咎的异物」，只有双方共担的真实感受：

> What I am describing is a joint process in which the real feelings of analyst and patient are aroused by the resistant process.

— Symington, *Freedom*

> The interpretations that follow the x-phenomenon become conscious articulations of a change that has already occurred unconsciously at the ego to ego level.

— Symington, *Freedom*

详见 [[entities/person-neville-symington]]、[[concepts/concept-analytic-act-of-freedom]]、[[concepts/concept-corporate-personality]]。

### 2.11 戈夫林：关系而非准确性

戈夫林本人不提供新学派，而提供**收敛判断**与**伦理限定**。他的全书起点是把诠释从解码重述为创造：

> it doesn't simply decode what already exists but participates in creating new forms of psychic reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

后记给出全部七章的收敛结论：

> interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

他同时对任何分类学本身施以怀疑：

> Perhaps what we're discovering is that every taxonomy of interpretation reveals more about our need to organize mystery than about the mystery itself.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

其正面主张是一种**认识论谦逊**的姿态——有信心给出，有谦逊轻握；分析师主体性不是理解的障碍，而是最核心的仪器：

> The question is not whether to be subjective—this is impossible to avoid—but how to use one's subjectivity in service of the patient's growth rather than one's own comfort.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

详见 [[entities/person-aner-govrin]]、[[concepts/concept-interpretation]]。

### 2.12 亚德林-加多特：用真理轴重述全部分歧

亚德林-加多特提供的是一套**类型学装置**，而非又一种技术主张。她诊断当代精神分析的真理观已经整体位移：

> In the last decades of psychoanalytic thinking, the epistemological focus has shifted from realism to idealism, from a Correspondent concept of truth to an Intersubjective one.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

她列出六种真理概念——对应（Correspondent）、融贯（Coherent）、理想（Ideal）、主体间（Intersubjective）、主体—存在（Subjective-Existential）与实用（Pragmatic）——并据此指出弗洛伊德与克莱因共享一个前提：

> In the form they take in the Realistic-Correspondent meta-theory, both truths (Correspondent and Subjective-Existential) are found or revealed; they are neither constructed nor co-constructed.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

她的基本单元是**真理轴**：

> Truth axes are posited as organizing principles of the psyche aimed at achieving stable images of reality across critical dimensions of the subject's life.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

治疗目标被重写为**轴间的流动能力**，而非坍缩到某一条轴上：

> I suggest that, among other factors, this space may be constituted by the evolving shared ability of the analytic dyad not to gravitate toward and collapse into one particular axis of truth.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

她同时拒绝把「中立」当作分析师的理想：

> The analyst is not expected to be neutral in his or her relation to the different languages, to equally identify with them, or to be devoid of natural preferences and tendencies.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

详见 [[entities/person-shlomit-yadlin-gadot]]、[[concepts/concept-truth-axes]]、[[concepts/concept-truth-types]]。

## 三、四条贯穿性线索

### 3.1 线索一：诠释从「揭示」转向「创造」

古典模型把诠释看作**解码**——意义先在于无意识，诠释只是把它翻译出来。除最古典一线外，本批各家几乎一致放弃了这一假设。戈夫林把它写成全书题词：

> it doesn't simply decode what already exists but participates in creating new forms of psychic reality.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，导论

比昂把同一转向写成了关于诠释本体论地位的命题——诠释不是一个关于 O 的陈述，而是 O 演化中的一个**实际事件**：

> the interpretation is an actual event in an evolution of O that is common to analyst and analysand

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

奥格登给出最直白的表述，埃谢尔给出最强的表述：

> Our interpretations matter not because they reveal hidden truths but because they create new possibilities for being.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章

> As in quantum reality, treatment thus creates psychic reality, and goes beyond the exposing or deciphering of an existing repressed or concealed psychic reality.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*

【综合】这一转变是后面三条线索的根源：一旦承认诠释**参与创造**它所描述的精神现实，则（a）分析师的主体性无法再被排除在观察之外；（b）「洞见」不足以解释疗效；（c）「求真」与「生成」必须被区分为两种不同的治疗目标。

### 3.2 线索二：反移情的三级演进——干扰 → 工具 → 共同建构

**第一级：干扰。** 反移情被当作分析师自身的残留，需被排除在分析工作之外。埃谢尔记述自己两位督导的差别，正处在这一级的末尾：

> Anne-Marie concentrated, for example, on the interpretations and understanding of the here and now of the analytic situation and related to countertransference issues; my first supervisor had focused exclusively on transference and considered countertransference an impediment to the analytic work.

— Eshel, *Interconnectedness*

**第二级：工具。** 反移情成为捕捉病人投射的接收装置，但**不是**探索对象。克莱因的名言是这一级的界碑：

> I have never found that the countertransference has helped me to understand my patient better; if I may put it like this; I have found that it has helped me to understand myself better

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章（引 Klein, 2017b, p. 103）

**第三级：共同建构。** 分析师已在场域之内，且对自己的角色部分盲视：

> The analyst is not only impacted by his own unconscious process in the formulation of any interpretation, not only a full participant in any analytic enactment, but partially blind to the role he plays in helping the patient to understand both of these processes.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 654）

西明顿则把「移情／反移情」这一二分本身取消：

> In this way transference and countertransference are two parts of a single system; together they form a unity. They are the shared illusions which the work of analysis slowly undoes.

— Symington, *Freedom*

【推断】每升一级，分析师的「外部观察者」位置就后退一步；到第三级，「谁对谁诠释」这个问题本身已不再成立。第三级与第一条线索互为条件——只有当反移情被视为**参与**而非**观察**，「共同演出」的说法才有意义。参见 [[concepts/concept-enactment]]。

### 3.3 线索三：治疗行动的重心迁移——洞见 → 关系 → 存在

**洞见（认知层）**：弗洛伊德—克莱因—早期比昂。知识本身即疗效，诠释的价值在于说中了什么。

**关系（人际层）**：温尼科特、科胡特、关系取向。治疗的核心转为提供关系环境、修复发展性中断；诠释通过关系起作用：

> interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记

**存在（本体层）**：奥格登、埃谢尔、西明顿。终点不是「关系」，而是「成为」。奥格登用一句临床判断标出这一层：

> I had not the slightest inclination to talk with Mr. C. about my understanding of the dream. I said to him, “What a wonderful dream that was”

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第五节

西明顿则从另一侧标出：真正的动因发生在任何诠释**之前**：

> The point though is that the essential agent of change is the inner act of the analyst and that this inner act is perceived by the patient and causes change.

— Symington, *Freedom*

【综合】三层的分歧不在「哪种技术更有效」，而在「什么样的东西算作改变」——是知道自己、是与他人相处的方式变了、还是存在方式本身变了。参见 [[topics/topic-therapeutic-action-debate]]。

### 3.4 线索四：从认识论范式到本体论范式

这是奥格登给出的命名，也是本批资料中最具统摄力的框架：

> I view Freud and Klein as the founders of a form of psychoanalysis that is epistemological in nature, and I consider Winnicott and Bion as the principal contributors to the development of ontological psychoanalysis

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

亚德林-加多特从真理观一侧给出并行叙述——同一位移被描述为从实在论到观念论：

> In the last decades of psychoanalytic thinking, the epistemological focus has shifted from realism to idealism, from a Correspondent concept of truth to an Intersubjective one.

— Yadlin-Gadot, *Truth Axes*

比昂则提供了这一转移的**方法论**版本：分析师被要求悬置记忆与欲望，代之以负性能力。

> When a man is capable of being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact and reason

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Bion, 1970, p. 125）

【综合】需要保留的限定是奥格登本人的：二者不是学派而是**感受力**，且「以相互丰富的方式共存」。因此本表中的「认识论／本体论」一列应读作**重心**而非**归属**。参见 [[concepts/concept-epistemological-vs-ontological]]。

## 四、未解决的张力

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：深度即时诠释 vs 等待与不诠释
> **A 方立场**：克莱因——无意识幻想自始活跃于移情，延宕诠释等于把病人独自留在恐惧里，而准确的诠释**创造**关系而非依赖关系——「Moreover, Klein believed that accurate interpretation creates rather than depends upon the therapeutic relationship.」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第二章）
> **B 方立场**：温尼科特——诠释应当等待病人创造性地抵达理解，且语言在解离状态下本身就是侵犯——「The analyst feels like interpreting, but this can spoil the process, and for the patient can seem like a kind of self-defence」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章，引 Winnicott, 1971a, p. 92）；奥格登进一步主张有时不诠释才是最深的干预。
> **备注**：**近乎不可调和**。二者对「病人处于何种心理状态」的预设不同——一方假定意义已在等待被译出，另一方假定意义尚需生成。这更接近**范式差异**而非技术分歧，故不存在可公度的裁决标准。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：治疗经验发生在「之间」还是「之内」
> **A 方立场**：关系／场域取向与奥格登——诠释涌现于两人之间的第三空间——「interpretation in this model emerges from what Thomas Ogden (2004) calls the analytic third—a jointly created unconscious space that exists between and beyond both participants」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章）
> **B 方立场**：埃谢尔——临在与同在不位于两人**之间**，而尽可能位于病人的世界**之内**——「the analytic experience of “presencing” and “withness” or interconnectedness is not located between patient and analyst, but is as much as possible within the patient's world.」（Eshel, *Withness*，第二节）
> **备注**：**拓扑学分歧，目前无调和方案**。埃谢尔明确说自己的路径「even beyond intersubjectivity」，因此这不是同一维度上的程度之争，而是层次之争（Vermote 第三区 vs 第二区）。参见 [[topics/comparison-withness-vs-analytic-third]]。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：诠释的疗效来自准确性还是来自关系
> **A 方立场**：求真传统——诠释的价值在于它**说中了**什么；弗洛伊德对朵拉的诠释在**对应真理**上成功（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第一章）。
> **B 方立场**：戈夫林的收敛判断与创伤疗愈传统——「interpretation works through relationship rather than accuracy.」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，后记）
> **备注**：**部分可调和**。亚德林-加多特的真理轴模型提供第三种可能——问题不是「何者为真」，而是「**出现的是哪一种真理、通过何种方式出现**」；戈夫林本人也用「what kind of truth and by what means」重述了这一分歧。参见 [[concepts/concept-truth-axes]]。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：求真传统 vs 创伤疗愈传统
> **A 方立场**：分析师是科学家，病人是研究主体；症状是线索，诠释洞见是治疗因子；病理源于**否认（disavowal）**——「The guilt … It is in you … look inward, look into your own depths, learn first and foremost to know yourself!」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第四章，引 Freud, 1917, pp. 142–143）
> **B 方立场**：以母婴关系为模型；治疗在于提供关系环境、修复发展性中断；目标是温尼科特所谓「**持续存在（going-on-being）**」。
> **备注**：**戈夫林本人指出这一二分造成「虚假的两极对立」**——求真派同样在疗愈创伤，关系派同样在 uncovering 真理。故应视为**分析性抽象**而非真实对立阵营。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：诠释在「共同建构」之后是否还保留认识论职能
> **A 方立场**：戈夫林——完全放弃诠释的权威会失去精神分析事业中某种本质之物，即「提供不同视角、看出病人尚不能看见的模式」的能力（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章）。
> **B 方立场**：斯特恩——诠释根本不是分析师的自觉创造，而是「the voice of the field」（Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章，论 Stern, 2023, p. 1136）。
> **备注**：**未决，且关系取向内部亦无共识**。米尔斯（Jon Mills）与格林伯格（Jay Greenberg）的批评提示，过度强调互主体性会掩盖分析关系的结构性不对称。参见 [[concepts/concept-analytic-field]]。
