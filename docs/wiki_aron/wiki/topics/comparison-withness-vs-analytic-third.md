# 同在 vs 分析性第三方 vs 主体间性：一场位置之争（Withness vs the Analytic Third vs Intersubjectivity）

> **类型**: comparison
> **创建时间**: 2026-08-29
> **最后更新**: 2026-08-29
> **主来源**: [[raw/eshel-withness]]

## 摘要

当代精神分析有一批看似同调的理论：奥格登（Thomas H. Ogden）的**分析性第三方（analytic third）**、费罗（Antonino Ferro）与巴朗热夫妇（the Barangers）的**双人际场（bipersonal field）／分析场（analytic field）**、一般关系取向的**主体间性（intersubjectivity）**，以及埃谢尔（Ofra Eshel）的**同在（withness）**。它们共享一个直觉：治疗性的东西不发生在任何单一个体的心智内部，而发生在某种**共同的、生成性的空间**中。

本页的核心论点是：**这是一个误认。** 四家共享的是一句否定——「不在单人内部」；但他们对**肯定性位置**的回答并不相同。前三家把位置定在二元的「**之间（between）**」；埃谢尔则明确主张，她所描述的临在与相互贯通发生在病人的「**之内（within）**」，并且「**甚至超越主体间性**」，下探至比昂所说的 **O 中的转化**。

这不是措辞差异，而是**拓扑学差异**，并且具有可辨识的临床后果：它决定分析师在困难时刻应当**观察自己的遐想**，还是**进入病人的经验**；决定当病人无法进入第三方时，分析师应当**修复第三方**，还是**放弃第三方、进入病人的崩解**。

本页的裁决是：**不可通约的范式差异**，宜并列保留，不作调和。

---

## 一、共同前提：为什么它们看起来一样

四家共享三个前提，这是误认的根源。

**（一）反单人心理学。** 四者都拒绝把治疗经验定位在病人「内部」或分析师「内部」的任一单独一侧。

**（二）移情与反移情不可分。** 埃谢尔明确表示她与奥格登共享这一命题——

> In this respect, it is closely related to Ogden's (1997) conception of the *intersubjective analytic third,* and I share his thinking that "do[es] not view transference and countertransference as separate entities that arise in response to one another; rather, I understand these terms to refer to aspects of a single intersubjective totality" (p. 25).

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

**（三）治疗创造而非揭示。** 诠释／治疗不是解码既存内容，而是参与生成新的精神现实。

正是这三条共识使得**第四条上的分歧被系统性地低估**。

## 三、原文引文对照

### 3.1 奥格登：分析性第三方（「之间」且「之外」）

经由 Govrin 转述的定位句——这被描述为对传统精神分析认识论最激进的一次背离：

> The concept of the analytic third represents perhaps the most radical departure from traditional psychoanalytic epistemology, fundamentally reconceptualizing where interpretation originates and how therapeutic meaning emerges.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

定义句：诠释既不在病人的无意识里等待被发现，也不在分析师的头脑里等待被交付——

> Neither residing in the patient's unconscious waiting to be discovered, nor in the analyst's mind waiting to be delivered, interpretation in this model emerges from what Thomas Ogden (2004) calls the analytic third—a jointly created unconscious space that exists between and beyond both participants.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

这不是两个主体性的总和，而是**辩证的产物**：

> This third is not simply the sum of two subjectivities, but rather a dialectical creation that simultaneously preserves and negates the separateness of each participant.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

临床后果是诠释的**归属权**被重新分配：

> The interpretation belongs to neither and both simultaneously.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

分析师在其中不是权威，而是**宿主**：

> The analyst serves as what Ogden calls a "host" to the analytic third, maintaining conditions for its existence while remaining open to being transformed by what emerges within it.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

奥格登本人在本批资料中关于**遐想**的表述（这是第三方得以被感知的器官）：

> speaking (or relating in other forms) from, not about, reverie experience (Ogden 1994)

— Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节

而埃谢尔引述的奥格登公式则把「之间」写得最为凝练：

> Recent contributions include Ogden's (2009) formulation of what he views as Bion's second principle of mental functioning: "It takes two minds to think one's most disturbing, previously…

— Eshel, *Withness*（引 Ogden, 2009, p. 100）

**校勘说明**：该句在 PDF 抽取文本中于 `previously unthink-` 处被脚注编号「3」插入打断，后半节（当为 "unthinkable thoughts"）在本批资料中**无法逐字核实**，故依「宁可不用」原则截断于此，不补全。

### 3.2 费罗与巴朗热：双人际场／分析场

场域理论把「之间」推进为**本体论主张**：场中不存在未被共构的东西。埃谢尔的转述是中性的：

> The conceptualizations of the Barangers' *work of confluence* (2009) and Ferro's *bipersonal field* (1999, 2009, 2010; see also Ferro and Basile 2009) both emphasize the formation of a new identity—the analytic field—which is created between patient and analyst within the unit that they form in the session.

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

而她**完全同意**的那句话，是场域立场最强的一次自我表述：

> "Such a description illustrates the fundamental point that the field must get ill with the patient's own illness in order to be then cured of it" (Ferro 2010, p. 418).

— Eshel, *Withness*（引 Ferro, 2010, p. 418）

场域一脉的技术后果（经 Govrin 转述）：意义是**共同创造**而非**被发现**的——

> bipersonal field into a multi-personal framework, focusing on "functional aggregates"—affective holograms jointly constructed by patient and analyst in a shared mental space where meaning is co-created rather than discovered.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Ferro's Theoretical Foundation」（引 Ferro, 2002c）

病人的每一段叙述都是**α 元素的叙事衍生物**，是分析时刻情绪真相的实时象形文字，而非需要被解释的历史材料：

> Rather than viewing them as coded messages about past trauma or internal phantasies requiring decoding, he conceptualizes them as narrative derivatives of alpha elements—real-time pictographs of the emotional experience unfolding within what he terms the analytic field

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「Ferro's Theoretical Foundation」（引 Ferro, 2002c）

拒绝过早闭合的理由是**伦理性的**：

> premature interpretive closure—what he describes as attempts to reveal true truth—functions as a form of intellectual violence that forecloses possibility rather than opening it

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Ferro, 2006b, p. 1）

分析师的姿态随之从解释者变为**呼吸中心**：

> a breathing center that must constantly modulate the breaths of the field according to necessity

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章「The Evolution Toward Dream-Work」

奇维塔雷塞（Giuseppe Civitarese）则把倾听的对象写清楚——**不是病人真正意指什么，而是两人之间浮现了什么**：

> an attunement not to what the patient really means but to what emerges between the participants

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第三章（引 Civitarese, 2023）

### 3.3 关系取向：主体间性（两个主体性的交汇与协商）

阿隆（Lewis Aron）的奠基句：

> interpretation is best thought of as the quintessential container and purveyor of intersubjectivity between patient and analyst

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 475）

其知识论代价是分析师对自身参与的**部分盲视**：

> The analyst is not only impacted by his own unconscious process in the formulation of any interpretation, not only a full participant in any analytic enactment, but partially blind to the role he plays in helping the patient to understand both of these processes.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章（引 Aron, 1992, p. 654）

本杰明（Jessica Benjamin）把「第三」从诠释的产地转为**承认之所以可能的条件**，并给出崩塌的形态：

> This third space is fragile and constantly under threat of collapse into what Benjamin terms "doer and done to" dynamics, where complementarity replaces mutuality and recognition fails.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

> The restoration of thirdness becomes a precondition for genuine interpretive work.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

斯特恩（Donnel Stern）的场域版本把位置表述得最不含糊——**诠释既不属于分析师也不属于病人**：

> Stern's radical proposition is that interpretations are not the analyst's conscious creations, but rather they are "the voice of the field"

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第七章第五节（引 Stern, 2023, p. 1136）

### 3.4 埃谢尔：之内，且超越主体间性

**摘要中的划界**：

> This paper focuses on the analyst's "presencing" (being there) within the patient's experiential world and within the grip of the psychoanalytic process, and the ensuing deep patient–analyst interconnectedness, as a fundamental dimension of analytic work.

— Eshel, *Withness*，摘要

**划界句（本页的支点）**：

> While I completely agree with this fundamental point, the analytic experience of "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness is not located *between* patient and analyst, but is as much as possible *within* the patient's world. (I have pointed this out earlier with regard to Ogden's intersubjective third.)

— Eshel, *Withness*，「WHAT KIND OF ANALYTIC PRESENCE AND "WITHNESS" WOULD THAT BE?」

括号中那句自引至关重要：**这是一次针对整个「之间」范式的统一纠正，而非针对某一位作者的局部异议。**

**与奥格登的正面分歧点（不在位置，而在分析师用什么材料工作）**：

> However, I differ from Ogden in the emphasis that he places on the analyst's own subjective past experiences and memories, which are evoked in the analyst's mind in the session. I reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality, especially as a necessary, functioning presence.

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

**位置的极致**：

> The analyst thus becomes part of the patient's state of being and experiencing, to the extent of becoming psychically akin to a transplant or to chimeric antibodies (Eshel 2012); or, put differently, of receding toward the vanishing point in a perspective drawing.

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

**她要的不是一种关系，而是一个新的处所**：

> Thus, working within this dimension of analytic presence and interconnectedness has less to do with object relationships than with the emergence of a new *place* or psychic space-time, and a new possibility of experiencing and becoming, via the analyst's being *with-in* and staying interconnected to the patient's unbearable core of experience.

— Eshel, *Withness*，第一部分（着重号为原文所有）

**「甚至超越主体间性」**：

> "Presencing" and interconnectedness go beyond the level of interactions and patient–analyst relationships (object or subject relationships), even beyond intersubjectivity, to offer the opportunity for getting in touch with basic (environmental) relatedness and formative experiences of being and becoming

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

**二而一，而非二者之和**：

> patient and analyst forge an emergent new entity of interconnectedness or "withness" that goes beyond the confines of their separate subjectivities and the simple summation of the two.

— Eshel, *Withness*，摘要

**对诠释技术的直接后果：诠释性休止被取消。**

> This seems to me closely related to Ferro's (2005) description of "the analyst's way of being in the session... without any particular interpretive caesura" (p. 44).

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

**自我定位**：

> It is primarily an interconnected relatedness, rather than an interactive relationship, and it concentrates on the ontological (being) quality of the analytic experience that is lived through with the analyst, rather than on epistemological and interpretive qualities.

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

## 四、为什么这个差异重要：临床后果

### 4.1 分析师的意识状态不同

这是本页最具操作性的一条。

| | 「之间」模型 | 「之内」模型 |
|---|---|---|
| 分析师**观察**什么 | 自己的遐想、反移情、场域中的涌现 | 病人经验内部正在发生的东西 |
| 分析师**是**什么 | 第三方的宿主、场域的参与者 | 病人经验世界中的一个运作性在场 |
| 关键能力 | 反思性、遐想、负性能力 | 承受、冒险、与之同在的能力 |
| 当自己浮现个人记忆时 | 把它当作第三方的产物加以使用 | 视为**尚未足够深入**的标志，继续向内移动 |

**奥格登路线**：分析师的器官是**自己的遐想**。分析师注意自己身上浮现的东西，并**从**那个经验出发言说，而非**关于**它言说。

**埃谢尔路线**：分析师要抵达的是**病人主观现实中的嵌入性**。她与奥格登的分歧点正在于：奥格登强调「分析师自己被唤起的主观过去经验与记忆」，而她「reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality」。

> **【推断】** 这一分岔在**临床现场可被辨识**：当分析师在困难时刻注意到自己身上浮现某段个人记忆或意象时，奥格登路线会把它当作第三方的产物加以使用；埃谢尔路线则会把它视为尚未足够深入的标志，转而继续向病人的经验之内移动。两者并不必然冲突——分析师的记忆也可能是进入病人的通道——但它们给出的**下一步指令不同**，且这个不同不可由理论消除。

**注意：比昂传统内部已经埋着两种读法。** 格罗特斯坦（James Grotstein，经埃谢尔引述）把遐想推向埃谢尔一侧：

> the indivisibility of the transference ↔ countertransference in the analyst's reverie ... the necessity for the analyst to experience his own inner version of what the analysand is suffering from; to "*become*" the analysand's anguish and agony. Bion (1967, 1970) termed this phenomenon "transformation in O within the analyst."

— Eshel, *Withness*（引 Grotstein, 2010, p. 25）

而比昂本人的句子则更强硬：

> "With this [O] the analyst cannot be identified; he must *be* it" (p. 27, italics in original)

— Eshel, *Withness*（引 Bion, 1970, p. 27）

**因此埃谢尔并非在发明一条与比昂相反的路，而是在主张：比昂的晚期（O、at-one-ment、「必须成为它」）与奥格登的第三方不在同一层次。**

### 4.2 层次差异：Vermote 三区模型

埃谢尔为支撑「超越主体间性」引入了韦尔莫特（Ruben Vermote）的整合模型：

> Drawing heavily on Bion's writing, Vermote discerns three distinct zones of psychic functioning with varying degrees of differentiation, different major psychoanalytic models, and clinical implications for the analyst: 1. Reason—Oedipal, understanding Ucs. System (Freud, Klein); 2. Transformation in Knowledge—container-contained, reverie, dream-work, alpha-function (Bion, Marty, de M'Uzan, Bollas, Botella and Botella, Ogden, Ferro); 3. Transformation in O, when dealing with the most non-thought, unknown, undifferentiated zone of psychic functioning (Winnicott, Milner, late Bion, late Lacan).

— Eshel, *Withness*（引 Vermote, 2013）

**这是本页最重要的一条证据**：埃谢尔把奥格登与费罗**明确列在第二区**，而把自己列在第三区。她随之写道：

> In my view, the emerging dimension of the analyst's "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness that I am proposing reaches down to this third, most undifferentiated and fundamental analytic functioning and transformation in O.

— Eshel, *Withness*，同节

**含义**：她并未把分析性第三方或场域当作**错误**，而是当作**不够深**。这是「超越」而非「反驳」的关系，也是本页判定其为范式差异而非观点冲突的主要依据。

### 4.3 在极端状态下的操作分歧

这一点在**崩溃、死寂、绝望**的临床中变得决定性。

在「之间」模型中，分析师后退一步，观察自己的遐想，从中推断场域中正在发生什么，再给出未饱和的诠释。当第三方崩塌时，处置是**修复第三方**——因为第三性是诠释工作的前提条件；而创伤会摧毁进入第三方的**能力**本身：

> Traumatic experience often destroys the capacity for thirdness, trapping individuals in rigid self-other configurations that preclude genuine intersubjective meeting.

— Govrin, *Interpretation*，第六章

在埃谢尔的「之内」模型中，这还不够。她的长案例 P.（严重恋物—受虐倒错，每周四次）显示：倒错结构崩解后，**死亡本身**（而非对死亡的恐惧）成为其实存核心。此时分析师需要与之同在到「死亡本身」：

> And I add: there is no end unless the bottom of the trough has been reached*, un-* *less the thing feared has been experienced* in a compassionate holding, within the analyst's "presencing" and interconnecting with the patient's gripping, unbearable agony, devastation, and death. Analyst-and-patient t(w)ogether there, with-in.

— Eshel, *Withness*，结语

> **【推断】** 两条路线针对的**临床人群部分不同**：本杰明的第三方修复适用于第三性**曾经存在而后崩塌**的情形；埃谢尔的「之内」适用于第三性**从未形成**的情形（早期创伤、崩解、死寂）。若如此，二者在临床上是**分工**而非**冲突**——但没有任何一方文本承认这种分工，故本页仍将其列为不可通约的范式差异，并在此记录这一推断作为可能的调和发展方向。

### 4.4 这与「真理」问题的关联

「之间」模型隐含一种**认识论多元论**：意义是协商出来的，因而没有单独的裁判者。这与亚德林-加多特的**真理轴**模型亲和——诸轴并存、互不取消，治疗目标是让二元不坍缩进单一轴。

「之内」模型则更接近**本体论**：问题不是「谁的意义为真」，而是「能否与之同在、能否生成新的存在」。埃谢尔对此的自我表述毫不含糊——她所专注的是**与分析师共同经历的分析经验的「存在」性质**，而非认识论性质与诠释性质（其原文见本页 3.4 节）。

## 五、裁决

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：治疗性经验发生在二元的「**之间**」还是病人的「**之内**」？二者是可调和的程度之差，还是不可通约的范式差异？
> **A 方（奥格登的分析性第三方、费罗的场论、关系取向主体间性）**：意义在共享空间中共同生成——「a jointly created unconscious space that exists between and beyond both participants」；费罗经埃谢尔转述：场域「is created between patient and analyst within the unit that they form in the session」；阿隆：诠释是「the quintessential container and purveyor of intersubjectivity between patient and analyst」。
> **B 方（埃谢尔的同在）**：位置在「之内」——「the analytic experience of "presencing" and "withness" or interconnectedness is not located *between* patient and analyst, but is as much as possible *within* the patient's world.」；且「even beyond intersubjectivity」；在层次上自列于 Vermote **第三区**（O 中的转化），而把奥格登与费罗列入**第二区**（知识中的转化）。
> **备注**：**不可通约的范式差异，宜并列保留，不作调和。** 理由有三：（一）**共享前提充分**——三家在「反单人心理学」「移情／反移情不可分」「治疗创造而非揭示」上完全一致，故分歧**纯粹在拓扑学**；（二）**非程度之争**——埃谢尔自陈其维度「甚至超越主体间性」并把对手列入较低的功能区，这不是对同一维度的不同刻度，而是对维度的不同选择；（三）**「之间」在关系取向一侧带伦理负荷**——它是保证双方分离主体性得以相互承认的前提，取消它即沦为「doer and done to」的互补结构，因此关系取向**不能**接受「之内」而不自毁其伦理基础；而埃谢尔的「之内」正是要突破这一分离性，因为在崩解与死寂中尚无「两个主体」可供承认。
> **一处必要的保留（【推断】）**：A 方原文中「between **and beyond** both participants」也含有「超越」的维度，因此两方的距离可能比埃谢尔自我描述的更近；这一点尚缺直接的文本对质。
> **可能的调和发展方向（本页推断，无文本支持）**：二者或许针对不同的临床人群——第三性**曾经存在而后崩塌**者（第三方修复）vs 第三性**从未形成**者（进入之内）。此说目前无任何一方文本承认。

> ⚠️ **矛盾标注**
> **议题**：分析师应当**使用**自己的遐想与主观过去，还是**越过**它们、嵌入病人的主观现实？
> **A 方（奥格登）**：分析师从遐想经验「**而言说**」，而非关于它言说——「speaking (or relating in other forms) from, not about, reverie experience (Ogden 1994)」（Ogden, *Ontological*，第一节）。
> **B 方（埃谢尔）**：「I differ from Ogden in the emphasis that he places on the analyst's own subjective past experiences and memories, which are evoked in the analyst's mind in the session. I reach out toward the analyst's embeddedness in the patient's subjective reality」（Eshel, *Withness*）。
> **备注**：**部分可调和，但给出的临床指令不同。** 二者都同意分析师的主体性无可回避；分歧在于它是**抵达病人的通道**（奥格登）还是**可能挡在病人前面的东西**（埃谢尔）。比昂本人的「the analyst cannot be identified [with O]; he must *be* it」与格罗特斯坦的「to "become" the analysand's anguish and agony」站在埃谢尔一侧，说明此分歧**穿过**比昂传统内部，而非发生在传统之间。
